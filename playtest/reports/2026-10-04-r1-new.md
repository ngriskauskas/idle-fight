# r1-new: fresh character, 25 minutes, War Cry build

## Run
- Seed 601, fresh start, 25m00s game time, 12 `run` calls (two wasted: one stopped after 0s on a pending level-up, one ran with my item/spell changes not applied because of my own shell quoting mistake, so 9m06-11m21 was played in the old setup).
- Ended level 8 (level 8 points unspent, it arrived at 25:00). Highest: world 1 wave 5. Deaths 4. 267 kills, 37 item drops.
- Not tested: any magic spell, any enemy-side aura (Curse, Disarm), any tier 3+ talent. I do not rate those.

## Build
- Idea: War Cry (stacking +10% damage, lost on losing health) kept alive by a shield and fed by Bloodlust. I picked it because it was the one catalog entry at 3 points that reads like a gamble rather than a stat.
- End: Slash, Strike, War Cry, Battle Cry. Talents: Warrior Training 3, Shield Bearer 1, Mana Efficiency 1, Bloodlust. Gear: Arcane Shield (shield 8, +1/s), epic Warhammer, Plate Armor with on-hit speed boost, Ring of Elements.
- Course changes: War Cry alone ate all my mana (10 mana every 3s against 3/s regen: out of mana 86% of the time, Regeneration up 18%), so I took Mana Efficiency. Dropped Regeneration for Battle Cry at 20m.

## Level-by-level choices
- L2: Warrior Training, obvious. +3 flat on a base attack of 3 doubles damage; nothing else in tier 1 is close. Spell: Slash (1 point) only because nothing else costs 1 except Arcane Missile.
- L3: Warrior Training again, obvious. Weighed Quickstep (+3 speed, no idea what 3 speed is worth) and passed.
- L4: Shield Bearer. A real choice, made for the plan, not the numbers: +3 shield with 0 regen did nothing visible until Arcane Shield dropped.
- L5: War Cry bought (3 points saved over three levels, which felt long). Talent: Warrior Training by default.
- L6: Mana Efficiency. Forced, not chosen: +4 regen on a base of 3 was the only fix for 86% out-of-mana. Best single pick of the run.
- L7: Bloodlust. The first exciting pick. Weighed Rampage and Deep Wounds; neither fit. Battle Cry (2 points) at the same time.
- Looked exciting but out of reach in 25 minutes: Widened Focus (level 8, 2 points), everything in tier 3 (Battle Trance, Spiked Shield), Bleed Shatter and Lifesteal (6 spell points = six levels).

## How it felt
- 0-11m was dull: one or two buttons, 6-10 kills/min, 12 minutes to get past wave 2. New furthest waves at 3:01, 12:57, 14:59, 17:52; nothing new in the last 7 minutes.
- 11m-17m was the spike and the best part: gear plus Mana Efficiency took War Cry from 76% uptime/max 6-10 stacks to 98%/15 stacks. Kills/min 7.6 -> 13.2, damage per 200s 961 -> 3098, and three bosses fell in five minutes without my health dropping below 68%.
- After Bloodlust plus Battle Cry, re-clearing waves 1-4 took about 2m30s at 21.6 kills/min with health never below 100%. That is satisfying as proof of growth, but waves 1-3 are now a walk.
- Bosses: Goblin King formality every time (lowest health 78%). Bone Warden a real wall at level 5 (killed me twice, Ice Spike 17-26 into a 40 health pool), then a formality. Orc Warlord formality (first try, health 68%+). Shadow Assassin formality with Regeneration (first try), then killed me once I swapped Regeneration out. Inferno Lord: one Inferno for 77 against 62 health + 12 shield. Not a fight, a stat check.

## Enemies
- Asked something of my build: Bone Warden (burst before I had health), Shadow Assassin (202-326 damage in many small hits plus Lifesteal on itself; exactly what strips War Cry, and it is the only enemy that made me miss a spell), Inferno Lord (needs health or fire defence I do not have).
- Only numbers: Goblin, Giant Bat, Slime, Giant Rat, Orc, Zombie. Poison was the one persistent tax (225 status damage taken, more than any single enemy except Shadow Assassin).
- Orc Warlord's Fortitude was up only 8-12% of the fight and I never noticed it. Necromancer/Arcane Specter put Curse on me 5-26% of the time; I could not tell what it cost me.

## Too strong
- War Cry + Bloodlust + any shield regen: 15 stacks is +150% damage for 10 mana, 93-100% uptime on waves 1-4. Attack 43 -> 91 in status. One-shots everything below wave 4 so the "lost when you lose health" drawback never fires there.
- Mana Efficiency rank 1: +4 on a base of 3 for one point.
- Warrior Training rank 1-3: attack 3 -> 19 by level 5.

## Too weak
- Shield Bearer alone: +3 shield with no regen is invisible. All of the shield value came from one common item.
- Strike after Slash exists: same job, slower. I kept it only because the slot was otherwise empty.
- Ring of Elements: 1 fire/ice/lightning per hit gave 837 status damage of 27,664 total (3%).
- Mage gear flooded the drops (6 Mage Hoods, including the legendary) on a character with 0 mana cost use for them.

## Bugs or oddities
- `run` called with a level-up already pending returns "advanced 0m00s (stopped: level up)" and burns the call.
- With Bloodlust the manual War Cry spell still casts every 3s; I could not tell whether the spell slot was still doing anything at 100% uptime.
- Status showed speed -5 after equipping the Warhammer and swapping the Mage Hood out, then +7 after the next spell change with no item change in between. I did not find the cause.
- Summary reports "too low to cast 19%" for the whole run, all of it from the 5m46-11m21 War Cry stretch.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make early levels a choice: cut Warrior Training to +2 flat/+5% and give Quickstep and Shield Bearer a visible effect at rank 1 (Shield Bearer: +3 shield and +0.5 shield regen). Right now L2, L3 and L5 are one pick.
2. War Cry: raise cost to 15 mana or cap at 10 stacks, and make Bloodlust replace the cast (freeing the slot) so the combination changes the bar instead of only the numbers.
3. Give Inferno Lord a tell instead of a 77 opener: lower Inferno to about 45 and add burn, so health, shield and regen builds each have an answer at level 7.
4. Put one 2-point spell in reach that changes play by level 3-4 (War Cry or Raise Shield at 2). Eleven minutes of Strike/Slash is the dull part of the run.
5. More enemies like Shadow Assassin: a wave 2-3 fast, weak hitter would make War Cry's drawback matter early, and would make Fortitude/Raise Shield a real purchase.
