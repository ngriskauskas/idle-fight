# r2-world2 (2026-10-04)

## Run
- Seed 703. NOT a fresh start: level 12 at world 2 wave 1, 11 talent + 11 spell points, 20 stash drops. 30m00s game time, 8 `run` calls.
- Ended level 13 (levelled at ~29m; that point and the amulet slot were still unspent/empty when the budget ran out). Highest: world 2 wave 5. Deaths 2. 552 kills, 66 drops.

## Build
- Talents: Warrior Training 3, Iron Constitution 2, Rampage 3, Widened Focus, Deep Wounds. Spells: Strike, Slash, Rend, Whirlwind, Battle Cry, plus Health Regen (swapped for Arcane Missile at 25m to try magic).
- Items: Warhammer (132, then a world 2 one at +222), Plate Armor (def 101, hp 180, onHit speedBoost 6: my trigger piece), epic Arcane Shield from 6m (shield 248, +31/s), epic Ring of Mana from 15m (manaRegen +23, bleed +3 on every hit, damage +9%).
- Plan was "heavy hits chained by Rampage, bleed doubled by crits for bosses". It became a shield tank after the first death: the Arcane Shield was the only drop that changed how I thought, because bleed/poison ignore defense and a regenerating shield was my only answer.

## How it felt
- World 2 waves 1-3 are not a fight. First visit, in stash gear: Goblin King dead at 0m53, Bone Warden at 1m54, health never under 61%. Goblin King's hardest hit was 44 against 420 health. He is not "a little harder than Shadow Emperor"; he is a speed bump. Goblin King 3/3, Bone Warden 3/3, each in 40-60s of wave time.
- The real wall is wave 3 onward. Orc Warlord 2/3: the loss (no shield, 420 hp) was 160-168 hits plus 448 bleed damage over the fight. Shadow Assassin 2/2 (about 92s each, 874 damage taken). Inferno Lord 0/1: one Inferno for 543 and a Fireball for 305 against 506 health + 248 shield. That death felt like a number check, not something I could play around with what I owned.
- Re-clearing world 1 took 7m45s to 8m00s both times, 40-55s per wave (47, 39, 40, 42, 43, 41, 51, 53, 54, 55s). Health stayed at 100% for whole stretches; the hardest hit from any world 1 boss before Shadow Emperor was 8. It is a chore, not a speed-clear: 21-24 kills/min against 13-17 in world 2, so being hugely overgeared only bought ~40% more kill speed. Shadow Emperor (hits of 50) is the only world 1 boss that still registers. Over half my 30 minutes was spent on waves that could not hurt me.
- World 2 is world 1 again: the same bosses in the same order with bigger numbers. Nothing in waves 1-5 made me change a spell.
- Working toward at level 12: Split Mind at 14 and my first tier 3 pick. Level 13 took 29 minutes; 14 needs 20,480 xp at about 360 xp/min, so roughly an hour, with world 1 re-clears paying only ~170 xp/min.

## Enemies
- Orc Warlord and Shadow Assassin asked for something: their bleed and poison ignore my 293 defense (1,908 bleed and 1,702 poison taken over the run), which is what pushed me to a shield. Good pressure.
- Inferno Lord asks for raw health or fire resistance I never saw offered. Goblin King, Bone Warden and all trash were only health bars. The enemy mix inside a wave never mattered.

## Too strong
- World 2 item rolls. A common Warhammer "L3" is +222 damage; a world 1 legendary Plate Armor L6 is defense +15 against my common L1 at +101. Two drops in the first 6 minutes took me from dying at wave 3 to clearing wave 4.
- Ring of Mana: mana starvation went from 78% of the time to 0%, Battle Cry uptime 86% to 97%, and with bleed +3 on every hit bleed rose from 7.4k/3min (11% of damage) to 27k/5min (20%).
- Widened Focus earned its 2 points easily. Physical spells cost nothing and each has its own timer, so any extra attack is +15-30% damage (Slash alone was 34% of mine); two Warrior Training levels are +20%. Not a real choice for a physical build.

## Too weak
- Health Regen aura: up 80% of the time, dropped at 25m with no visible change. Arcane Missile on a physical build: 121 per hit, 6% of damage.
- Deep Wounds + Rend in world 1: bleed did 0-1.4k per stretch because everything dies in two hits. It only exists on world 2 bosses, which I liked.
- Every world 1 drop on a re-clear (about 40 items). Rarity means nothing next to which world an item came from.
- I could not isolate Rampage: I never ran without it. World 1 waves still took 40-55s with it at 3/3, so it did not turn re-clears into a blur.

## Bugs or oddities
- Item "L" restarts per world, so L1-L3 beats L10. Unreadable in a 78-item inventory.
- Leech showed 21, then 8 after equipping the epic Arcane Shield (healthLeech +15 and +10%) in place of the Greatsword (+13). I expected about 25; the shield's bonus leech lines look unapplied.
- `status` listed a dead enemy: "Giant Rat L4 0/1637hp". `status` shows health regen 0/s while the Regeneration aura is up.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. (Matters most) Make Rampage the re-clear talent it claims to be: let overkill carry to the next enemy, or let a kill instantly ready the next cast. World 1 should take 2-3 minutes at this gear, not 8.
2. Give Goblin King and Bone Warden in world 2 a status that ignores defense (as Orc Warlord has) or roughly triple their hits; 44 damage is no boss.
3. Add fire resistance or a "cap one hit at X% of max health" piece (shield talent or body armor) so Inferno Lord's 543 has a build answer.
4. Show a world tag or absolute item level, and cut or auto-junk drops far below equipped gear.
5. Give world 2 bosses a different order or a new aura each, so it reads as a new place; and make Health Regen scale with max health so it is a real slot.
