# r2-new: fresh character, 25 minutes, shield build that had to become a shield-caster
## Run
- Seed 701, fresh start, 25m00s game time, 10 `run` calls. Ended level 6 (145/160 xp), highest world 1 wave 4 (standing at its boss, Shadow Assassin 240/402). Deaths 1. 122 kills (4.9/min), 19 drops.
- Not done, so not rated: tier 2+ talents (5 points spent only at 19:26, so no two-piece talent combination and no trigger piece was reachable), Raise Shield, War Cry, any player aura except the starting Regeneration.
## Build
- Idea: Shield Bearer now has regen, so stack it, tick enemies down with Poison Stab, later Shield Specialist + Raise Shield. I picked it because "cannot be hurt, kills slowly" is a style, not a stat.
- End: Strike, Poison Stab, Fireball, Arcane Missile. Talents: Shield Bearer 3, Warrior Training 1, Mana Efficiency 1. Gear: Arcane Shield (shield 35, +2.5/s total), common Spellbook (+7 mana cost), epic Mage Hood (+4), Enchanted Boots (+3), Plate Leggings.
- Course change at 17:26, forced: the shield build could not kill Orc Warlord and could not die to him either. Mage drops (Spellbook, Mage Hood) were sitting in the bag, so I bought Fireball and dropped Regeneration. Kills went from 3.5/min to 12/min in the next stretch.
## Level-by-level choices
- L2: Shield Bearer over Warrior Training. A real choice now: +5 shield +0.5/s was visible at once (health lowest 91%, then 100%). Better than r1, where Warrior Training was the only pick. Saved the spell point.
- L3: Shield Bearer again (shield 12, +1/s). Poison Stab (2) over Slash plus waiting: real choice, and Poison Stab was right (poison did 154 of 317 damage in its first stretch).
- L4: Warrior Training, because 6.5 kills/min felt slow. Weak pick: attack 3 -> 5 changed nothing I could see (kills fell to 3.3/min on wave 2). After the nerf it is no longer the obvious pick, but it is now close to a wasted point for anyone not stacking it.
- L5: Shield Bearer 3. Weighed Regeneration talent (poison was my only real damage intake). Spell: nothing I wanted cost 2, saved for Raise Shield (3).
- L6 (19:26): Mana Efficiency, wrong in hindsight: mana averaged 95% before and 96% after. Arcane Affinity (+3 mana cost = +6 per magic hit) was the pick. Arcane Missile for 1 point was obvious: 43 damage per 6s for the cheapest spell in the game.
- Verdict: 3 of 5 levels were real choices against r1's 2 of 6. The catch is that the choices only matter once you know magic damage ignores your attack stat; nothing in the catalog told me that.
## How it felt
- Minutes 0-11 are still dull, and for this build 0-17 were: 3.0-4.1 kills/min, Strike doing 70-80% of direct damage at 7-9 a hit, health average 98-100%. Safe and slow with nothing to decide.
- New furthest waves at 3:54, 7:38, 22:20. The 14m42s gap was not a hard wall, it was a stalemate: from 13:26 to 17:26 I got 0 kills and 0 deaths on Orc Warlord. He took 1126 damage and went 279 -> 122 health; I soaked 1277. No xp, so no level, so no way out except re-speccing spells. This was the worst stretch of the run.
- 17:26-25:00 was the fun part: Fireball 42-58 a hit, wave 1-3 re-clear in about 4m30s, 2601 direct damage in 170s against 507 in the 240s before the switch.
- One death in 25 minutes (status damage, lightning + bleed, on Orc Warlord right after I dropped Regeneration). That is too few for a game meant to be hard; the shield made waves 1-3 riskless.
- Bosses: Goblin King formality (health 100%). Bone Warden slow formality (lowest 63%, about 3 minutes of chip). Orc Warlord the only real fight, and the wrong kind (unkillable, not deadly); with Fireball he fell with my health at 67%+. Shadow Assassin looked like a real fight (my lowest 36%, unfinished).
## Enemies
- Asked something: Orc Warlord. Fortitude was up 90% of the stalemate and 1-9% once I had burst, so he punishes low damage per hit specifically. That is a good boss idea with a bad failure state. Poison, bleed and lightning statuses (271/233/129 taken) are the only things that got through the shield, and they are what killed me.
- Only numbers: Goblin, Bat, Rat, Slime, Zombie, Spider, Goblin King, Bone Warden (Ice Spike 23 into 12 shield + 56 health is no longer a threat). Curse was on me 36% of wave 4 and I could not tell what it cost.
## Too strong
- Mana cost on common gear: one common Spellbook (+7) adds 14 damage to every magic cast; Warrior Training adds 2. Arcane Missile + Fireball did 4733 damage in 7.5 minutes; Strike did 1990 in 25.
- Magic has no mana pressure at this level: 2 magic spells, 96% average mana, never too low to cast.
- Shield Bearer 3 + one Arcane Shield: 35 shield, +2.5/s, health average 97% for the run.
## Too weak
- Warrior Training rank 1 at +2 flat. Strike and Poison Stab's direct hit (4 per hit) once anything else is equipped.
- Mana Efficiency for a two-spell caster: bought nothing. Physical drops (Greatsword, Iron Helms) were bag filler once magic was on.
## Bugs or oddities
- A fight can run forever with no kills and no deaths (4 minutes observed, no sign it would end). Status showed "mana cost 0" at 17:26 after I swapped the Spellbook out, then 11 after re-equipping, which is correct but worth knowing: the stat sits on items only.
- First stretch reported "enemy 1 -> enemy 4" with 4 kills and the next "4 -> 7" with 7 kills; the counter and kills do not line up.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. (Matters most) Break stalemates through the pieces: give Orc Warlord's Fortitude a shorter uptime or a stacking enrage, or give shield builds a cheap damage answer in reach (Spiked Shield's Riposte as a tier 1/2 talent, or Raise Shield at 2 points). A shield player should be slow, not stuck.
2. Say in the spell text that magic damage is 2 x (spell mana + your mana cost stat) and ignores attack. It is the best early discovery in the game and I found it by desperation.
3. Make mana a real limit for early casters: Arcane Missile to 2 points or 8 mana, or base regen 2/s, so Mana Efficiency and Arcane Affinity become a real pair of choices.
4. Give Warrior Training rank 1 something to feel (+2 flat and +10% is 0.5 damage on attack 5); a small crit or speed rider would keep physical openers from being a trap.
5. Add an early enemy that deals mostly status damage (a wave 2 poisoner or bleeder), so a shield is not a free pass through waves 1-3 and Regeneration stays worth a slot.
