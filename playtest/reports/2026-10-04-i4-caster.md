# i4-caster: Seed 805 Caster Playtest

## Run

- Requested run name: `i4-caster`; that ID already existed as seed 122, level 11, at 1h26m.
- I left that unrelated run untouched and ran the requested fresh seed 805 as `i4-caster-805`.
- Game time: 25m00s exactly; 8 run calls; level 8; highest: world 1, wave 5.
- 308 kills (12.3/min overall), 5 deaths, 766 XP, 36 drops.
- Average mana 65%; low mana prevented casting for 16% of the full run.
- Health averaged 91%; Health Regeneration was active 91% of the time.

## Build

- Ended with Arcane Missile, Ice Spike, Fireball, and Health Regeneration.
- Talents: Arcane Affinity 4, Mana Efficiency 3; seven points spent total.
- Kept Strike briefly as a physical control, then swapped it out for Ice Spike.
- Added Fireball at level 6; it gave the build a useful burn channel, but its high draw became costly later.
- Final combat stretch used Arcane Affinity 4 and a level-5 Spellbook; the status `mana cost` stat reached 43.
- Final gear: Wand, Spellbook, Silk Leggings, Enchanted Boots, Iron Helm, Plate Armor, Ring of Poison, Ring of Speed, and Amulet of Plague.
- The Ring of Speed and upgraded Silk Leggings were equipped after the 25-minute cap; they did not affect the run results.
- Plate Armor's on-hit `speedBoost 6` trigger was used in the last stretch only. The harness did not report proc counts, so I cannot credit it with the kill rate.

## How it felt

- This was a slow, fragile opening that turned into a genuinely fast caster once gear and the third attack slot arrived.
- Stretch kill rates: 0:00-0:35, 3 kills (5.1/min); 0:35-1:23, 5 (6.3/min).
- 1:23-2:58: 11 (6.9/min); 2:58-5:59: 19 (6.3/min), with the first death.
- 5:59-9:57: 37 (9.3/min); 9:57-13:48: 61 (15.8/min), no deaths.
- 13:48-21:32: 121 (15.6/min); 21:32-25:00: 51 (14.7/min).
- The first big click was 9:57-13:48: missile, ice, fire, and the caster gear were all online, and I cleared 61 enemies without dying.
- That spike felt earned by the build, but several upgrades landed together, so I cannot credit any single spell or item for the jump from 9.3 to 15.8 kills/min.
- Wave 2 was first reached at 2:58; I died there twice to Bone Warden Poison Stab for 8.
- Wave 3 arrived at 11:29 after Bone Warden; wave 4 at 12:36 after Orc Warlord; wave 5 at 17:48 after Shadow Assassin.
- The first wave-3/4/5 reaches felt earned: they followed a build spike, and I still died five times overall.
- I died twice at wave 4 to Shadow Assassin Slash (28, then 18) and once at wave 5 to Inferno Lord Inferno (73).
- There were no deaths in the 9:57-13:48 stretch. New-wave progress then came quickly, but the wave-4/5 deaths made the success feel provisional rather than trivial.
- All observed play was within the first 25 minutes, so there was no later-hour grind to judge.

## Fun

- Seeing magic become the whole damage profile was satisfying: the final whole-run totals were 13,313 Missile, 11,326 Ice Spike, and 6,739 Fireball damage.
- Ice Spike and Fireball made the slots feel like different status choices, not three copies of the same attack.
- Reaching wave 5 after repeatedly being stopped at wave 2 was a clear progression moment.
- The first Goblin King kill at 2:58 was a modest but real milestone; later Goblin King re-clears became routine.

## Not fun

- The build oscillated between plenty of mana and long periods unable to cast, depending on the Affinity/gear setup.
- Fireball was exciting when available, then nearly disappeared from the final stretch's damage because I could not sustain the full rotation.
- The exact talent choice is hard to evaluate from its short tooltip: `manaCost` reads like resource price, but it also tracks the caster's damage scaling.
- Health Regeneration stayed up, but did not answer burst hits from the later bosses.

## Combinations

- Mana Efficiency 1 plus Arcane Missile: 104 Missile damage over 8 hits in 48 seconds; 0% mana-limited, average mana 79%.
- Adding Arcane Affinity 1 raised Missile to 295 over 15 hits in the next stretch, with Strike contributing 131; mana remained unlimited. Levels and progression also changed, so this is suggestive, not a controlled damage comparison.
- Arcane Affinity 3 with Mana Efficiency 2 and the wand/leggings/boot set: 59% mana-limited, average mana 23%; 61 kills/minute rate was 15.8.
- Raising Mana Efficiency to 3, with the same combat gear and spell loadout: the next stretch was 0% mana-limited and averaged 84% mana; kill rate was 15.6/min. Sustain improved sharply without a comparable kill-rate gain.
- Pushing Affinity to 4 and replacing one Wand with the higher-cost Spellbook: mana limitation returned to 49% (22% average mana) and kill rate was 14.7/min. Missile supplied 53% of that stretch's direct damage; Fireball only 8%.
- Across the whole run, direct damage was 31,575 and status damage 1,065, of which fire was 991. Fire was a useful supplement, not a replacement for casting.
- The tested combination changed play when mana was ample; at high cost, the bottleneck narrowed the rotation back toward Missile and Ice Spike.

## Items

- The pair of wands, Enchanted Boots, and Silk Leggings leaned into mana and `manaCost`; they made a coherent caster set, though their bonuses were still mostly numeric.
- The level-5 Spellbook was a real choice: +12 `manaCost` and a larger mana pool, but less regeneration, speed, and crit than the Wand it replaced. It pushed the run back toward mana starvation.
- Dragon Scale Mail and Iron Helm supplied basic survivability. Plate Armor traded two health and two speed for the on-hit trigger; its effect was not separately measurable.
- Ring of Poison, Ring of Speed, and Amulet of Plague filled the accessory slots. Their status/crit bonuses fit the build, but no drop changed its core plan.
- I did not find a clear vendor-trash gear system in this short sample; most drops were small stat substitutions. The later strict upgrades were not combat-tested.

## Enemies

- Bone Warden asked the clearest early question: its Poison Stab killed me twice at the same wave-2 endpoint. Poison dealt 172 damage over the full run, so this pressure mattered beyond the killing blows.
- Shadow Assassin also asked for more than raw damage: its Slash killed me twice at wave 4, with 247 total attack damage attributed to it.
- Inferno Lord's Inferno for 73 was the run's hardest hit and final-wave death. Frost Wolf Frostbolt also hit for 47; both made defense/health feel relevant against elemental bursts.
- Orc Warlord's Lightning Bolt (22) and Rend (12) were noticeable but not fatal. Goblin King became a routine re-clear rather than a renewed challenge.
- The wave mix had recognizable attack/status variety, but the harness output did not show a clear tactical counter beyond gear and general survival. I could not tell whether Ice's slow changed enemy pressure in a measurable way.
- I wanted a mana-draining or spell-disrupting enemy that forced a choice between expensive burst and cheap control; none of the enemies I encountered clearly tested that weakness.

## Too strong

- No single piece was clearly overpowered. The complete caster package peaked at 15.8 kills/min, but still died to specific wave endpoints and was often mana-limited.
- Affinity's damage benefit looked attractive, but high Affinity plus costly gear made the rotation unreliable; it was not a free upgrade.

## Too weak or useless

- I would not call any tested spell useless. Arcane Missile was the most reliable contributor; Fireball was strong when cast, but poor uptime reduced its final-stretch share.
- Extra Mana Efficiency rank 3 restored uninterrupted casting, but did not lift kill rate over the preceding stretch. It felt like a sustain threshold, not a progression spike.
- Health Regeneration was useful enough to keep equipped, but it did not prevent boss burst deaths.

## Bugs or oddities

- No definite gameplay bug observed.
- Plate Armor's `speedBoost` trigger was equipped in the final stretch, but no proc/uptime data was printed. I did not test its shared one-second cooldown as a mechanic.
- The reported `mana cost` stat and per-spell mana prices are not self-explanatory together; the combat output does show low-mana time, but not which spell was blocked on each occasion.

## Against DESIGN.md

- The run matched the intended hard progression: deaths reset me, the early waves became faster, and waves 3-5 arrived only after the build strengthened.
- Arcane Affinity versus Mana Efficiency was a real tradeoff: Affinity raised magic scaling, while Efficiency bought casting uptime. Neither dominated across both damage and sustain.
- Gear and spell unlocks created a recognizable magic archetype, though this sample's caster identity came more from stacked stats than from a distinctive rotation mechanic.
- I did not find one obvious universal path; the best choice depended on whether I wanted maximum spell scaling or reliable casts.

## Suggestions

1. Clarify Arcane Affinity's tooltip: state that it raises the caster's `manaCost` scaling and show the resulting resource pressure, so the damage/sustain decision is legible.
2. Keep the rank-3 Mana Efficiency threshold meaningful, but make costly builds choose between more casts and higher damage more deliberately; here it removed 59% low-mana time without changing kill pace much.
3. Give Fireball a clearer reason to occupy a slot when mana is tight, such as stronger burn payoff or a named interaction with Wildfire, rather than only more direct scaling.
4. Add a readable mana-pressure enemy that challenges expensive spell rotations while leaving cheap spells or status control viable.
5. Expose trigger activations in the run output. That would let an on-hit item like Plate Armor be evaluated without confusing it with the shared speedBoost cooldown.
6. Consider an early defensive/control option that specifically answers the repeat Bone Warden poison endpoint; I had Health Regeneration, but it did not change the outcome there.
