# Comprehensive Item Suite

## Overview

The item suite has been redesigned to use all 20 available effect types thematically across 40+ items.

### Effect Type Coverage

**Offensive Effects:**

- `damage` - Physical weapons
- `critChance` - Daggers, rings
- `critMultiplier` - Axes
- `poison`, `bleed`, `fire`, `ice`, `lightning` - Status effect items and special weapons

**Defensive Effects:**

- `defense` - All armor pieces
- `health` - Armor and amulets
- `shield` - Shields and defensive amulets
- `shieldRegen` - Defensive gear

**Sustain Effects:**

- `healthRegen` - Health-focused armor and amulets
- `healthLeech` - Greatsword, boots, leech amulet
- `manaLeech` - Magic weapons and amulets

**Utility Effects:**

- `speed` - Daggers, cloth robes, leather gear, boots, rings
- `mana` - Magic weapons and mana-focused gear
- `manaRegen` - Magic weapons, helmets, amulets
- `manaCost` - Spellbook, mage gear (reduces spell costs)
- `itemDropChance` - Amulet of Luck
