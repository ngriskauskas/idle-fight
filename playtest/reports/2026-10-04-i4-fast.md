# i4-fast: fresh physical / Quick Strike, seed 804

## Run

- Fresh start, 25m00s, 12 `run` calls. Ended level 8, highest world 1 wave 5, 299 kills, 6 deaths, 29 drops.
- Final stats: health 64, attack 23, speed 14, crit 13% x1.97.
- Spells: Strike, Quick Strike, Arcane Missile, Health Regeneration. Talents: Warrior Training 3, Quickstep 3, Adrenaline Surge 1.
- Final gear: Plate Armor (onHit speedBoost 6), legendary Battleaxe, epic Shield, Mage Hood, Leather Leggings, Heavy Boots, Ring of Speed, Amulet of Health.

## Build

- Started Strike + Health Regeneration; swapped the aura for Slash, then added Arcane Missile at level 3. Quick Strike came at level 6; I restored the aura in the fourth slot.
- Quickstep and Warrior Training got me to tier 2. The speed loop only became real when a common Plate Armor dropped at 17:49; Adrenaline Surge was added at 18:49.
- The legendary Battleaxe at 23:20 made a strong final upgrade: +3 speed, +11% speed, and large crit-multiplier bonuses over the first Battleaxe.

## How it felt

- 0-9:38: slow-dangerous rather than effortless. First wave 2 at 3:50, wave 3 at 6:12, wave 4 at 13:47. Three deaths by 9:38, including Goblin and Goblin King kills. The early deaths made the first boss clears feel earned.
- 9:38-16:49: Quick Strike made re-clears exciting, but the 6m11s stretch averaged only 7.6 kills/min as enemies got tougher. I reached wave 4, killed Bone Warden and Orc Warlord, then died to Dark Mage Poison Stab.
- 16:49-20:10: the Plate Armor made weak waves collapse quickly. I re-cleared from wave 2 to wave 4 in one minute, killing Bone Warden and Orc Warlord; wave 5 arrived at 20:10. That was a satisfying spike, not the old boss deletion chain.
- 20:10-25:00: wave 5 was a real wall. I died twice to Inferno Lord Inferno and Shadow Assassin Slash, while the final 11 seconds after the legendary axe killed 14 weak enemies without losing health.
- Furthest-wave gaps were 3:50, 2:22, 7:35, then 6:23. Six deaths total; re-clearing early waves became very fast, but the wave-5 boss did not fall.

## Fun

- Quick Strike finally felt distinct once the on-hit armor arrived: lots of short attacks visibly fed the trigger and carried me through old bosses faster.
- The Orc Warlord and Shadow Assassin were satisfying checkpoints after prior deaths. Inferno Lord's 68-damage Inferno was frightening and decisive.
- Finding a legendary speed/crit Battleaxe late was a good payoff, even though there was only time for an 11-second test afterward.

## Not fun

- Health Regeneration cost 25 mana. It was up only 30% of the run; mana was too low to cast 33% overall and 92-97% of the time in the two trigger-heavy stretches.
- Without trigger gear, the early spell/talent choices were mostly small stat steps. The empty fourth slot before level 6 and the slow spell-point savings made the build take a while to come online.
- Fast hits do not save mana: the combo starved Arcane Missile and the aura while doing its best damage.

## Combinations

- Quick Strike + Quickstep/Wand/Ring of Speed, before a trigger: at level 7, 36 Quick Strike hits, 13 Strike hits, 10 Arcane Missile hits, and 26 kills in one minute (59 total spell hits, about 2.3 hits/kill).
- Plate Armor + Quick Strike: next minute, 85 Quick Strike hits, 32 Strike hits, 16 Arcane Missile hits, and 21 kills (133 hits, about 6.3/kill). Direct damage rose from 1,073 to 2,031; I killed Bone Warden and Orc Warlord and did not die. This is not a perfectly matched casts-to-kill comparison: it started at wave 2 instead of wave 1, so enemies had much more health.
- Plate Armor + Adrenaline Surge: the following minute, in wave 4, logged 68 Quick Strike, 29 Strike, and 15 Arcane Missile hits, with 14 kills and no death. The lower hit count is confounded by tougher enemies and a wave-4 start; adding the crit trigger did not recreate the old runaway rate.
- Over the following five minutes the combined build made 387 Quick Strike hits (1.29/sec), killed 83 (16.6/min), and died twice. In the final 11-second wave-1 stretch it made 15 Quick Strike hits and killed 14. Counts are aggregate spell hits, not per-target casts-to-kill; the harness does not expose exact individual kill thresholds or speedBoost activations.

## Items

- The common Plate Armor was the build-defining drop. It gives up 2 speed and some defense/health for on-hit speedBoost 6; that was a real, fun trade.
- The Ring of Speed, speed amulet, speed helmet, and speed leggings supported the plan. I kept the leeching Shield for survivability and equipped the stronger Battleaxes as they dropped.
- Tower Shields with speed -5/-6 and duplicate robes were poor fits. The Dagger's +3 speed/+5 crit was tempting, but I kept the Battleaxe's damage and crit multiplier instead.

## Enemies

- Orc Warlord asked for durability: its Lightning Bolt killed me for 21 early and later hit for 36. Dark Mage/Necromancer pressure mattered too; Poison Stab killed me, poison dealt 146 total damage, and Curse was up on me 48% of the combined-trigger minute.
- Shadow Assassin was a meaningful wave-5 gate: it killed me with a 17-damage Slash before I beat it on the retry. Inferno Lord's 68-damage Inferno killed me at wave 5 and remained undefeated.
- Goblin King and Bone Warden became repeatable checks, mostly health bars once the build had gear. Most ordinary mobs blurred together; the mixes with poison, Curse, and burst damage stood out. The harness made killing blows legible, but I did not test visual telegraphs.

## Too strong

- Plate Armor + Quick Strike still strongly accelerates hit volume: 36 to 85 Quick Strike hits/min across the observed before/after stretches. It rapidly clears weak waves, including 14 kills in the capped 11-second finish.
- The shared 10-fast-tick cooldown appears to have bounded the loop: the five-minute combined stretch averaged 1.29 Quick Strike hits/sec, versus r3-new's 942 hits in 232 seconds (4.06/sec) after its speed loop. Strong, but not the same runaway boss wipe.

## Too weak or useless

- Health Regeneration was mostly a mana sink once the fast loop was active; 30% total uptime did not prevent six deaths.
- Arcane Missile contributed 3,970 damage overall, but became an unreliable slot under the late mana shortage. I did not test another aura or magic spell deeply enough to rate it against alternatives.

## Bugs or oddities

- No description mismatch observed. The harness reports spell-hit totals but not speedBoost proc counts or actual cooldowns, so the shared cooldown's exact contribution cannot be isolated from hit totals alone.
- The final 11-second stretch's 76.4 kills/min is a tiny weak-wave sample, not a sustainable rate.

## Against DESIGN.md

- The trigger drop created a distinct fast-hit style and the speed-versus-defense trade was real. Bosses still stopped progress, so the shared cooldown kept this run closer to hard/earned than r3-new.
- Weak waves still fell without much fight after the trigger, especially on death re-clears. The design's earned-new-wave goal held better: wave 5 took until 20:10 and its boss killed me.
- Fresh pacing was slower than 2026-10-04-r3-new: this run reached wave 4 at 13:47 and wave 5 at 20:10, ending at wave 5; r3 reached waves 7-9 in the 95 seconds after its 14:28 speed-armor drop. The shared cooldown materially reduced the extreme hit rate, but did not make weak-wave re-clears engaging by themselves.

## Suggestions

1. Keep the shared speedBoost cooldown: this run's observed Quick Strike rate was about 1.3-1.4/sec after the trigger, far below r3-new's 4.06/sec, while bosses remained lethal.
2. Add proc/cast-interval telemetry to the harness so future cooldown tests can separate hit count from enemy health and time-on-target.
3. Keep a speed trigger's defense/speed cost explicit in its item roll; Plate Armor's trade made the build choice interesting instead of a free upgrade.
4. Consider reducing Health Regeneration's mana cost or extending its duration. At 25 mana, it was up only 30% of this run and nearly absent during the fast loop.
5. Preserve a dangerous wave-5 burst boss, but make the threat legible enough that a physical speed build can meaningfully prepare for it; Inferno Lord's 68 damage was earned but offered no tested counter here.
