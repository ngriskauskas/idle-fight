# i2-fresh: bleed/physical, item economy focus

**Run**: seed 2101, fresh character, 45m06s game time, 11 `run` calls. Ended level 10, world 2 wave 1 (furthest), 4 deaths, 565 kills.
**Build**: Slash + Rend + Strike + Battle Cry. Talents: Warrior Training 4, Iron Constitution 3, Deep Wounds. Gear: Plate Armor L9 (onHit speed boost), Greatsword L6 epic, Warhammer L8 common, Ring of Speed (held, then bought), **Stormcoil Ring L9 unique (bought)**, Iron Helm legendary, Heavy Boots L8, Plate Leggings L7, Life Leech amulet. I never got to a magic spell. The 5th slot opened in the last seconds and was still empty when the run ended.

## Economy: the headline
Gold now works. Kills paid about 795 gold and selling paid about 755. Buying things was a real part of the run, not a dead menu.
- Gold at each check: 0:40 **3** | 1:35 **9** | 3:00 **24** | 6:49 **42** (+15 sold, -36 bought = 21) | 9:53 **63** (+26 sold = 89) | 14:44 **137** (+42 sold, -147 bought = 32) | 20:44 **143** (+9 sold) | 30:44 **321** (+132 sold = 453) | 31:46 **469** (+43 sold) | 41:46 **707** (+488 sold = 1195, -894 bought = 301) | 45:06 **472**.
- Kill gold per minute climbed with the waves: about 5/min in waves 1-2, 10/min at wave 3-5, 17-20/min at waves 6-9, and **51/min in the last stretch** (171 gold in 3m20 crossing into world 2).
- **First buy at 6:49**: Battleaxe rare, 36 gold. With the Arcane Shield it took attack from 9 to 19. The next stretch I went from losing at wave 2 to wave 3 with no deaths (12.4 kills/min, was 4.7). That buy felt great. It was the first decision in the run that was mine.
- 14:44: Warhammer L3 (57) and the held Ring of Speed (90). Attack 24 to 36, speed -2 to 7, crit 14 to 20%. Kills/min went from 8.5 to 15.8.
- **First unique: Stormcoil Ring at 41:46 for 894.** Possible only because I sold a whole stash at once (488). Uniques were on display from 0:40 at 150, but the price follows my furthest wave: 150, then 234, 458, 572, 894. My gold chased that price for 40 minutes. The cheaper 1x pricing helped, but the price still runs away from you while you progress. The only window where I could afford one was a big sell-off right after a level-scaled loot burst.
- **Stormcoil was the best moment of the run.** At 45% crit, its onCrit lightning AoE plus +17 lightning on every attack did **10,431 lightning in 3m20 (about 3,100/min) against 7,166 bleed**. Over the 10 minutes before, bleed did about 560/min. It cleared waves 8 to world 2 wave 1 with no deaths and killed Dragon Lord and Shadow Emperor on the first try. That is too strong for one ring, but it is a "build clicked" spike of the kind DESIGN.md allows. It turned my bleed build into a crit/lightning build overnight.
- **hold-item works.** The Ring of Speed I held at 9:53 survived the ~10:05 restock and I bought it at 14:44 for its L1 price of 90, when fresh epics cost 141+. Martyr's Plate, held at 30:44, survived the ~40:10 restock still at 572. One catch: a held item keeps its old price, so holding a low-level ware is a cheap discount. Useful, possibly exploitable.
- **The smith is not worth it now.** Four forges: 4 commons (sell 11) became an uncommon (sell 6). 4 commons (sell 12) became an uncommon (sell 7). 4 uncommons (sell 33) became a rare Mage Hood (sell 20). 4 L4-5 commons became Heavy Boots L5 uncommon, worse than its inputs. I equipped one of the four results, and that was only the rare Mage Hood as a sideways helmet swap. Averaging plus random slot makes the smith a gold sink that returns 55-60% of the sell value. Selling is always better.
- **Item level beats rarity by miles.** A common Warhammer L8 (+31 damage) replaced a rare L3 (+10). A common Plate Armor L9 replaced a legendary Cloth Robes L6. Rarity colour told me almost nothing.

## How it felt
- 0-7 min: slow start. Health Regeneration starved my mana (too low to cast 93-100% of the time) and I died to Bone Warden's Poison Stab at wave 2.
- 7-15 min: the shop buy turned it around. New waves every 1.5-3 min (wave 3 at 9:24, wave 4 at 10:51, wave 5 at 12:22, wave 6 at 15:19). Satisfying.
- 15-29 min: **stuck on the Frost Queen** (wave 6 boss). She killed me twice with 62-68 Frostbolts against 85, then 124, health. It was a real wall and beating her at 29:19 felt earned. Re-clearing waves 1-5 took about 6 minutes each loop: a tolerable grind.
- 29-42 min: waves 7, 8 and 9 fell in 4 minutes. Then I died once at wave 9 to a Dark Mage Lightning Bolt (22). Not readable as a threat. It was chip on top of 161 poison I had taken that stretch.
- 42-45 min: Stormcoil spike. Waves 9, 10 and world 2 fell without a fight.
- Deaths: 4 in 45 min. That is hard enough early. The final 3 minutes were not hard.

## Combinations
- Rend + Deep Wounds: bleed went from 347/min to 432/min. Attack also rose in that window, so the combo itself was worth little. Bleed was never more than 17-20% of my damage. It made numbers bigger without changing how the build played.
- Battle Cry (+20 crit) + Deep Wounds: bleed stayed at about 441/min. Battle Cry's crit was worth far more once Stormcoil arrived.
- Crit + Stormcoil Ring: a genuine combination that changed the build (see above).

## Enemies
- The Frost Queen is the memorable one: a clear spike, and she made me go for health (Iron Constitution, then a health-heavy armour swap).
- Bone Warden's poison was the first lesson. Poison was my top source of status damage taken all run (669 total).
- Storm Titan, Shadow Emperor and Dragon Lord hit hard (79-98) but died before they mattered once I had gear.
- Mid-wave enemies blurred together. They are named in the damage table, but none changed a decision.

## Bugs or oddities
- World 2 drops show **L1** but carry huge stats: Battleaxe L1 +101 damage, Tower Shield L1 +108 defense and +197 shield (sells for 224). The level label looks like wave-in-world rather than power, which is misleading.
- Miser's Ring L6 showed **health -21%**, not the -15% in the change note. It probably scales with level. Check that this is intended.
- Restock cost jumps sharply: 50, 63, 78, 122, 153, 191, 238, 298, then **931** on reaching world 2. I never restocked, because it was never worth it.
- The status at 0:00 shows an empty shop until the first tick.

## Suggestions
1. Smith: return at least the sum of the inputs' value, or let the player choose the slot. As it is, it is strictly worse than selling.
2. Cap or slow how unique prices follow the furthest wave (for example, price by the item's own level band), so saving for one does not mean chasing a moving target.
3. Tone down Stormcoil Ring: drop the flat +17 lightning, or lower the onCrit AoE. On its own it out-damaged my whole bleed package.
4. Make rarity matter against item level, or show item power in the shop and inventory, so legendaries do not lose to commons two levels higher.
5. Add a bleed unique to the merchant pool, to pair with Rend and Deep Wounds. None of the 12+ uniques I saw fit a physical bleed build.
