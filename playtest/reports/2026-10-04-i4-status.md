# Playtest: i4-status

## Run

- Seed 806; fresh level-1 start; 24m59s of game time; level 8; highest wave 5; 5 deaths.
- Stayed under the 25-minute and 14-run-call caps. No post-run character setup was used.
- Overall: 300 kills (12.0/min), 19,175 direct damage, 4,291 status damage, average health 88%.
- The early checkpoints were slow: 3 kills by 0m45s, 9 by 1m35s, and 20 by 3m21s.

## Build

- Ended with Strike, Poison Stab, Arcane Missile, and Health Regeneration.
- Talents: Warrior Training 2, Iron Constitution 2, Quickstep 1, Epidemic 1.
- Final equipped status bonuses were poison 2, bleed 2, and fire 2 on every attack.
- Gear: Dragon Scale Mail, Mage Hood, Silk Leggings, Swift Boots, epic Warhammer, Greatsword, Ring of Poison, and Amulet of Plague.
- I started as a basic melee/aura character, added Poison Stab at level 3, and added Arcane Missile when level 6 opened a fourth slot.
- Five tier-one talent points opened Epidemic at level 7. That was the deliberate poison-trigger combination I wanted.

## How it felt

- There was no full in-game hour in this stage. The opening 3m21s produced 20 kills (6.0/min) and 111 poison damage, with no deaths.
- From 3m21s to 5m17s, I got 19 kills (9.8/min), 165 poison damage, and my first death, to Bone Warden's Poison Stab for 8.
- From 5m17s to 8m45s, the run added 39 kills (11.3/min). I reached wave 3 at 7m52s after killing Bone Warden.
- From 8m45s to 13m55s, I got 69 kills (13.4/min) and 686 poison damage. The Orc Warlord killed me twice with Lightning Bolts for 28 and 44 before I beat one at 13m46s.
- From 13m55s to 23m19s, I added 126 kills (13.4/min). The run eventually reached wave 4 at 16m42s and wave 5 at 19m07s.
- The final 90 seconds added 22 kills (14.7/min), 401 poison, 80 fire, and 28 bleed damage, plus one death to a Necromancer Ice Spike for 12.
- The final 10-second check added 5 kills and 37 poison / 6 bleed damage after equipping Ring of Poison. That rate is too short to treat as representative.
- There were 5 deaths total: Bone Warden Poison Stab (8), Orc Warlord Lightning Bolt (28 and 44), Dark Mage Lightning Bolt (18), and Necromancer Ice Spike (12).
- Furthest-wave timing was uneven: wave 2 at 3m10s, wave 3 at 7m52s, wave 4 at 16m42s, and wave 5 at 19m07s. The gap to wave 4 was the grind; wave 5 followed 2m25s later.
- I never got far enough to make the next region feel routine. Deaths sent me back to wave 1, but later stretches cleared back upward quickly.

## Fun

- Poison became a real damage stream rather than decoration: 4,005 poison damage was about 93% of all status damage at the end.
- The Amulet of Plague and Ring of Poison made the attack-by-attack status line visible in the character stats, while Epidemic gave poison a kill-triggered spread angle.
- The Shadow Assassin kill at 19m06s and wave-5 breakthrough one second later felt earned after the long wait for wave 4.
- The epic Warhammer was a satisfying late drop: it kept the weapon's damage while adding speed and critical multiplier.

## Not fun

- Poison Pool costs 5 spell points; I ended with 4 unspent, so the clearest poison-plus-aura spell combination stayed just out of reach.
- Mana was low 72% of the time. Arcane Missile still supplied 30% of direct damage, but the mana pressure made it a costly slot rather than a smooth second engine.
- Repeated Goblin King and Bone Warden fights were familiar checkpoints, but several stretches were mostly waiting for the next boss attempt.
- Health Regeneration helped sustain the build, but it did not interact mechanically with poison. The useful status/aura pairing was poison/bleed gear alongside the healing aura, not a bespoke proc synergy.

## Combinations

- Poison Stab plus Epidemic: the intended on-death poison spread. Poison Stab dealt 3,641 direct damage overall; poison dealt 4,005 status damage. The harness reports status totals, not spread-trigger counts, so I cannot isolate Epidemic's share.
- Poison Stab plus Amulet of Plague: the amulet added poison, bleed, fire, and mana leech. During the final 90 seconds after equipping it, status damage was 401 poison, 80 fire, and 28 bleed.
- Amulet of Plague plus Ring of Poison plus Health Regeneration: after the ring went on, per-attack bonuses reached poison 2 / bleed 2 / fire 2. In the final 10 seconds, poison rose by 37 and bleed by 6; this is a promising but very short check.
- Poison Stab plus Arcane Missile was not a strong mechanical synergy. Arcane Missile added magic coverage and 5,817 total direct damage, while Poison Stab remained the status source.
- I could not test Poison Pool with the available points, and did not test Poison Gas or a bleed-focused spell.

## Items

- The Plague amulet and Poison ring were the most build-defining drops; both were equipped, and together they doubled the per-attack poison and bleed bonuses.
- The Dragon Scale Mail filled an empty body slot with defense and health. Swift Boots and Silk Leggings gave speed, with the leggings trading mana cost for speed and item-drop chance.
- The epic Warhammer replaced a common Warhammer late, improving speed and critical multiplier. The Greatsword stayed in the second slot for damage and health leech.
- The Tower Shield's speed penalty made it a poor fit for this setup; I left it unequipped. Duplicate low-level armor and weapons did not change the plan.
- Item gearing felt like a build choice only once poison/bleed affixes appeared; most earlier drops were straightforward upgrades or rejects.

## Enemies

- Orc Warlord asked the most of the build: its Lightning Bolt killed me twice at wave 3, for 28 and 44, before the 13m46s boss kill. That felt like a real wall, not just extra health.
- Bone Warden's Ice Spike hit for 27 at its worst; its Poison Stab also delivered the first killing blow. It made the low-defense setup feel vulnerable.
- Dark Mage Lightning Bolt killed me for 18, and Necromancer Ice Spike killed me for 12. Those were readable attack names, but I had no targeted counter beyond more health, defense, or healing.
- Goblin King and Bone Warden returned often. Their repeated appearances made the route legible, but the intervening waves blurred together.
- Slimes, Goblins, and Giant Bats mostly read as progression numbers here. The wave mix did not make me change spells or gear.
- I would like an enemy that suppresses or consumes poison stacks, forcing a choice between fast spreading and saving stacks for a tougher target.

## Too strong

- Poison/status damage was substantial but not obviously overpowered: 4,291 status versus 19,175 direct damage, with most status damage from poison.
- No talent or spell clearly outperformed its cost in this short run. Epidemic's individual contribution is not separately reported.

## Too weak or useless

- Poison Stab's direct damage was 3,641 (19% of direct damage), but its larger value was enabling 4,005 poison damage. I would not call it useless.
- Arcane Missile dealt 5,817 direct damage, but mana was too low to cast 72% of the time. It felt constrained rather than weak.
- Poison Pool, Poison Gas, Critical Toxin, and speedBoost were not tested; I am not rating them.

## Bugs or oddities

- Several terminal responses were interleaved with output from other named playtests. I trusted only snapshots that explicitly named i4-status; this made some individual stretch output unavailable.
- No in-game description mismatch was observed. I saw no speedBoost proc in this build, so the shared one-second cooldown was not tested.

## Against DESIGN.md

- The run fit the slow-grind philosophy: it took 16m42s to reach wave 4, and deaths cost real progress.
- Poison Stab, Epidemic, and status gear made a recognizable build, though most of its identity came from affix stacking rather than a new play pattern.
- The real tradeoff was mana pressure versus adding another active spell. Gear and spell points gave me choices, but Poison Pool's cost left the intended poison/aura bridge unavailable in this budget.
- The repeated boss gates felt earned; ordinary waves were less distinctive and mostly served as time between those gates.

## Suggestions

1. Let poison-spread effects report their damage or trigger count separately, so Epidemic's value is observable.
2. Add a cheaper poison aura or a lower-cost status aura that can be reached by level 8; it would let a fresh poison run test a true status/aura build within a short stage.
3. Give wave mixes an enemy that punishes a particular status strategy, so poison spreading creates a tactical choice rather than only more damage.
4. Revisit mana pressure for hybrid spell builds: Arcane Missile dealt 30% of direct damage while casts were unavailable 72% of the time.
5. Preserve the Orc Warlord's sharp damage check; the two Lightning Bolt deaths made finally beating it feel earned.
