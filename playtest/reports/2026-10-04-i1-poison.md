# i1-poison: early-game status build, 25 minutes

## Run
- Seed 202, fresh character, 25m00s game time, 13 `run` calls. Level 8, highest world 1 wave 6, 3 deaths, 235 kills (9.4/min), 41 drops.
- Stretches (kills/min, deaths): 0-1m 3.5, 1 death | 1-2m 5.6 | 2-3m 8.7 | 3-5m 8.6 | 5-8m 7.0 | 8-9m 12.0, 1 death | 9-12m 13.0 (re-clear) | 12-15m 7.3 | 15-18m 4.7 | 18-21m 7.0 | 21-23m 22.5, 1 death | 23-25m 14.1 (re-clear).

## Build
- End: Strike, Poison Stab, Slash, Raise Shield. Talents: Warrior Training 3, Iron Constitution 1, Mana Efficiency 1, Epidemic. Gear: Battleaxe L5 + Warhammer L3, Dragon Scale Mail (fire +3), Amulet of Plague (poison/bleed/fire +1), plate legs and helm, Swift Boots. Both ring slots empty all run.
- Order: Health Regen aura (free) at 1m, Poison Stab at level 3, Raise Shield at level 6 after a one-shot death, Epidemic at level 7, then swapped Health Regen for Slash at 18m because health had sat at 100% for 9 minutes.
- I never tested Rend or Critical Toxin; the points were not there in 25 minutes.

## How it felt
- Yes, the first 10 minutes are playable with a status build. Wave 2 at 3m, wave 3 at 5m, wave 4 at 7.5m, with zero deaths between 1m and 8.5m. A slow, steady grind rather than a stall.
- Status was never the main damage. Status share of all damage per stretch: 16%, 31%, 30%, 10% (re-clear), 25%, 38% (first stretch with Epidemic), 26%, 23%. Whole run: 6164 status against 19858 direct. Strike alone did 51% of direct damage.
- Hits to kill a typical enemy: wave 1 two to three (15-18 hp, Strike 6-9), wave 2 two to three (28 hp), wave 3 three (46-78 hp), wave 4 three to four (107-170 hp, Strike about 39), wave 5 three to five (109-205 hp), wave 6 four to five (256-292 hp, Strike about 64, Slash about 40).
- Wave 5 was the slowest point (4.7 kills/min), and it was a damage problem, not a survival one. Adding Slash and a Battleaxe L5 brought it back to 7.0/min and got wave 6.
- Choice per level: levels 2-5 were not a real choice (Warrior Training three times, because attack feeds both hits and status). Level 6 was real (Raise Shield or Rend). Level 7 was real (Epidemic or Critical Toxin, though 10% crit made Toxin look bad on paper).

## Fun
- Raise Shield changing the run: lowest health went from 0% to 100% for 9 straight minutes, 98-100% uptime at 10 stacks, 90 shield and +10/s.
- Dragon Scale Mail's fire +3 on every attack: fire damage went from 82 to 285 per stretch. Gear status that rides every hit is the best item idea I saw, and it made fast Slash feel like a status spell.
- Re-clears after death were visibly faster (waves 1-3 in under 2 minutes at 22.5 kills/min against 5 minutes the first time). That goal of the design works.

## Not fun
- Poison Stab feels like a worse Strike with a tail. Per cast it did about 30 direct against Strike's 64, and poison per cast was roughly 45-55. Slash (1 point) did 1806 direct in the stretch where Stab (2 points) did 719 direct plus most of 1353 poison: about even, for half the price.
- I could not see what Epidemic was worth. Poison went from 651 to 1120 per 3 minutes, but the wave changed too, and the output never says how much spread.
- About 15 of 41 drops were caster gear (mana, mana cost) that is useless or harmful to a physical build. One ring dropped in the whole run, and it was a mana ring.

## Enemies
- Arcane Specter is the only enemy that asked something of me, and it is unfair. It dealt 746 of all damage taken (next: Giant Bat 272) and landed two of three killing blows: 108 on a 61 hp character at wave 4, and 69 through 90 shield and 138 health at wave 6. It buffs itself with Arcane Boost, which I could read in the output but could do nothing about.
- Frost Wolf (264) and Knight (169) at wave 6 were the first to dent the shield; Necromancer puts Curse on you (8% uptime), which I only noticed as speed and defense dropping in `status`.
- All five bosses (Goblin King, Bone Warden, Orc Warlord, Shadow Assassin, Inferno Lord) were only bigger numbers. Orc Warlord dealt 48 damage in total across three kills. None killed me, none made me change anything.
- Waves 1-3 blur together. Enemy poison and bleed on me (143 and 88 all run) never mattered.

## Too strong
- Health Regeneration aura: free, +12 health/s on a 40 hp character, 81-97% uptime. It makes waves 1-3 risk-free.
- Raise Shield for 3 points: worth its slot without question. From wave 4 one-shot to untouched through wave 5. Too good to skip, which makes it not a choice.

## Too weak
- Poison Stab as the status opener (numbers above). Epidemic in the early game, where most packs are one to three enemies that die at about the same time.
- Status build as a whole at this stage: 23-38% of damage after spending the first spell unlock, an amulet and a tier 2 talent on it.

## Bugs or oddities
- A fresh character starts with Health Regeneration unlocked but not equipped and slot 2 empty. I died at wave 1 enemy 3 in the first minute before noticing.
- "mana cost" on gear raised what my auras cost and gave a physical build nothing. With mana cost 8 and both auras I was too low to cast 69% of the time, and Regeneration uptime fell to 59%. One Mana Efficiency point plus dropping that gear fixed it (2%).
- Equipping a weapon with an explicit slot silently bumps the better one back to the inventory; I lost 3 attack for 3 minutes without a warning.
- `status` showed speed -8 while cursed with no hint that an enemy aura was the cause.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Poison Stab: raise poison from 6 to about 10, or cut the cast from 7.5s to 6s. Its poison should clearly beat Slash's direct damage, since it costs twice as much.
2. Arcane Specter: cap its boosted Arcane Missile or make the boost a visible wind-up a shield can answer. Right now it is the only cause of death after minute 1 and nothing in the build counters it.
3. Make Raise Shield a tradeoff (fewer max stacks, or more mana per stack) and give Health Regeneration a cost, so defense competes with a second status spell instead of being automatic.
4. Add early status rings (for example poison +2, or "poison lasts longer") and lower the share of caster gear in drops for the first waves, so a status build has something to chase before tier 2.
5. Give Epidemic an early-game hook (spread on death also adds a few stacks) and print spread damage in the run output, so the talent is felt before packs get large.
