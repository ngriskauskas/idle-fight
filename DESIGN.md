# Design Philosophy

## Pace

- Idle game. Grindy and slow. Goals take a long time.
- Stretches of little progress are fine.
- Sudden power spikes when a build clicks are also fine.

## Where power comes from

- Player power comes from item drops, levels, talents and spells. Nothing of the player's scales with the wave automatically.
- Death sends you back to world 1 wave 1. Getting stronger shows as clearing the early waves faster.
- The game is hard. Death is common, and beating a new boss or setting a new furthest wave should feel earned.
- If progress is too slow or a run gets stuck, tune the build pieces (talent, spell and item numbers and pacing), not the rules.

## Fun over balance

- If it's fun and a bit broken, keep it.

## Builds

- Depth comes from combining the simple base mechanics, not from adding new systems.
- Builds emerge from combinations of spells, talents and items.
- Builds must feel unique, with different styles: slow but reliable, strong against groups, etc.
- Tradeoffs must be real. Gaining something should cost something.
- No single obvious path. Choices should not be linear.

## Synergy

- Pieces should work together. Weak alone, strong combined is the ideal.
- Every new piece should name at least one existing piece it combines with.
- Variety matters. Prefer a new angle over a stronger version of something that exists.

## Progression

- There should always be something to work toward: a level, a spell slot, a talent tier, a rare drop.
- Unlocks should open new combinations, not just raise numbers.

## Archetypes

- Bleed: slow, sustained, shreds defense.
- Poison: stacks multiply and spread; can poison yourself for gain.
- Fire: burns groups, spreads on death, burns you too.
- Ice: slows enemies; shatter trades the slow for burst.
- Lightning: crits arc to every enemy.
- War Cry: stacking damage that is lost when you lose health.
- Shield: soaks hits, hits back, protects fragile stacks.
- Mana: spend it on spells, or hoard it for an overflow burst.
- Control: weaken enemies instead of strengthening yourself.

## Checklist for new content

- Does it combine with something that already exists? Name it.
- Does it use existing mechanics?
- Does it give up something for what it gains?
- Does it make a build play differently, not just bigger numbers?
- Is it a real choice, or would every build take it?
- Does it fit an archetype, or bridge two?
