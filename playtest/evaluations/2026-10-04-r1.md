# Evaluation r1 (2026-10-04)

## Verdict

Not yet hard in the way DESIGN.md asks, but the gap is now narrow and specific. The wave 5 and wave 10 bosses are real walls; almost everything between them is not, because two cheap pieces remove the danger: Bloodlust behind any shield, and Raise Shield on any build. Balance is close enough that most remaining complaints are single-run leads. The single most important change is to make War Cry's drawback actually fire for a shielded Bloodlust build; today's cut to 8% x 12 lowers the size of the bonus but leaves the tradeoff dead.

## The two questions asked

**Is it hard the way the owner wants?** Partly.
- Death common: no. With a build equipped, r1-new died 4 times in 25 min (level 1 to 8), r1-mid once in 22.5 min, r1-late 3 times in 27 min. Waves 1 to 7 are cleared at 100% health by both mid and late builds.
- Each new boss earned: only Bone Warden (at level 5), Storm Titan (without Raise Shield), Dragon Lord (first kill) and Shadow Emperor. Goblin King, Orc Warlord, Frost Queen, Plague Master and Inferno Lord (for the caster) fell first try.
- A build path past every wall: yes in world 1 on the evidence (QA: Dragon Lord 22/36; Emperor 2/7 for the caster before today's cut). Not known for the physical build at Shadow Emperor (0/7 in QA, 0/1 in r1-late) or for anything at world 2's Goblin King (0/19 before today's change).

**Are the remaining findings still productive?** About half. Problems 1 to 4 below are productive: each is seen by two or more sources or confirmed in source, and each has a build-piece fix. Problems 5 to 8 are single-run leads worth one cheap change each, no more. Everything else in the reports is now one of: already answered by today's edits and needing a re-run, not a new change (Warrior Training, Shield Bearer, Mana Efficiency, Magical Specialist, Shadow Emperor, Inferno Lord, world 2); harness or display problems (unreadable Curse and slow, affix rolled three times, failed `act` calls); or taste (Strike versus Slash, mage drops on a warrior). After one more short round to confirm today's seven number changes and the Bloodlust fix, short rounds will stop paying. What is left then (do builds diverge after level 12, does world 2 hold, do statuses matter in long fights, do decisions dry up) only longer runs can answer.

## Evidence base

- r1-new (fresh, War Cry, 25 min, reached wave 5), r1-mid (caster from level 8, 22.5 min built, waves 5 to 9), r1-late (physical Bloodlust/Rampage from level 11, 27 min built, waves 8 to 10). All three lost their first stretch or more to harness mistakes; mid and late started with banked points, so they say little about the early game.
- QA late-bosses recheck (36 Dragon Lord, 25 Shadow Emperor, 19 world 2 Goblin King attempts, all forked and stepped, none a fair run) and QA open-items (Raise Shield cost, Rampage levels, ice). UX review for readability only.
- All three reports and both QA files predate today's changes. Every number in them for War Cry, Warrior Training, Mana Efficiency, Shield Bearer, Magical Specialist, Shadow Emperor, Inferno Lord and world 2 is from the old values. I judged those from the source and did not run anything.
- Nobody tested: poison, fire, ice, lightning, control or mana-hoard builds this round; any tier 3 or 4 talent in a real run; Widened Focus; world 2 in play; anything past 30 minutes.

- The fresh run is the right shape: a 12 minute crawl, a spike when the build clicked, then a wall at Inferno Lord.
- The caster is too safe: 4 new bosses in 17.5 min, health 96 to 100% on average, one real decision after the initial build.
- Checked in source: the new Shadow Emperor is about 12% lower on attack (16 to 14, 104 at wave 10) and 14% lower on spell damage (mana cost 22 to 19). QA estimated 15 to 25% too hard, so the caster should land near even odds; the physical build probably still loses.

## Problems, ranked

1. **Bloodlust behind a shield has no drawback.** r1-new: 93 to 100% uptime on waves 1 to 4; r1-late: 100% through waves 1 to 7 behind 37 shield. Confirmed in `src/hooks/useCheckHealth.ts:43-47`: fragile auras drop only when health falls, so shield soaks everything. Today's cut (8% x 12, +96%) shrinks the bonus but not the problem, and today's Shield Bearer (5 shield, 0.5 regen at rank 1) makes the protection one talent point instead of a lucky drop. Breaks "tradeoffs must be real" and the War Cry archetype line. Confidence high. Direction: tune Bloodlust, not War Cry. Give it a real price (lower cap for kill-granted stacks, for example 6, or a cost of 2 points, or stacks from kills that do not refresh the timer), so the hand-cast spell and Battle Trance remain the way to the full 12. Shield protecting stacks is in DESIGN.md and should stay.
2. **Raise Shield is close to required and close to free.** r1-mid: died to Storm Titan without it, then 17.5 min with no death; damage unchanged with or without (5467 and 6094 against 5402 to 6659). QA: Dragon Lord 0/6 without against 3/6 with at level 10, 2/6 against 6/6 at level 12; cost measured at 5 to 14% damage for the caster, paid in mana. Confirmed: -1 speed per stack, 15 mana, 12 s (`auras.ts:364`). Breaks "is it a real choice, or would every build take it". Confidence high. Direction: raise the speed cost per stack to -2 or the mana cost toward 25. Today's Magical Specialist regen cut may already make the mana price bite; test that first, change one of the two.
3. **World 2's first boss is unverified after a large change.** QA: 0/19, dead in 6 to 10 s. By my arithmetic the new Goblin King there has 149 attack (was 186) and about 1117 health (was 4190, because health multi resets to 1 at wave 1), against Shadow Emperor's 104 attack and 4693 health. It may now swing to a 10 second coin flip that is easier to kill than the boss before it. Confidence low; no run exists. Direction: if it is still a wall, lower Goblin King's base attack rather than touch scale; if it is trivial, that is the intended breather and fine.
4. **Caster mana cost stacking had no price.** r1-mid: 41 mana/s regen against 26/s spent, mana 92 to 96% full, about 39 damage/s flat. Today's Magical Specialist (+25% regen) and Mana Efficiency (3 flat) put the same build near 25 to 27/s by my estimate, right at break-even. Likely fixed, unconfirmed. Direction: no further change until one caster run reports time out of mana. If it still never runs dry, raise Arcane Affinity's cost growth rather than cutting regen again.
5. **Mid-world bosses ask nothing.** Plague Master 3/3 with 22 to 75 poison taken; Frost Queen 2/2; Orc Warlord's Fortitude up 8 to 12% of the fight; Curse on the player 16 to 39% of the time with no noticed effect. Two reports each. Breaks "new boss should feel earned". Confidence medium. Direction: enemy lists and numbers only. Give Plague Master a second, different spell in place of the duplicate Poison Stab; raise Fortitude uptime on Orc Warlord (its 35 mana cost against his regen is why it is rarely up).
6. **Inferno Lord is a one-hit stat check for a fresh level 7.** r1-new: Inferno 77 into 62 health plus 12 shield. The level 8 caster with 44 defense and 60 shield never noticed him. Mana cost is now 26; I could not confirm the previous value, so the size of the cut is unknown. One sample. Direction: re-run before changing; if it persists, move damage from Inferno to Fireball's burn so regen and shield are answers.
7. **Poison Stab and Rend are filler in a fast-kill build.** r1-late: 9% and 14% of damage. One run, and enemies die too fast for stacks on waves 1 to 7 by design. Confidence low. Direction: leave the spells; this is what a dedicated status run should test. If confirmed, raise stacks per hit, not base damage.
8. **Percent leech with no flat leech gives 0.** r1-late: +33% leech showed leech 0 until a flat item was added. Consistent with percent-of-base in the stat system. A trap, not a balance fault. Direction: add a small flat leech to the items that roll percent leech.
9. **Physical Specialist locks out every aura.** Confirmed: mana regen set to 0 (`tier2Talents.ts:77`). r1-late could not field Fortitude or Disarm against Shadow Emperor, and QA's physical build went 0/7. This is a real, intended cost and I would keep it; the open point is whether a physical path past the Emperor exists at all. Direction: none yet; answer with a run using mana leech gear.
10. **Early talent picks.** r1-new called levels 2, 3 and 5 one pick. Today's Warrior Training (2 flat) and Shield Bearer changes address this; Quickstep (+3 speed) is unchanged and still unreadable. Direction: re-run fresh; touch Quickstep only if it is still never taken.

## What is working

- The fresh run's shape: slow start, a spike at 11 to 17 min when gear and Mana Efficiency met War Cry, then a wall.
- Re-clears as proof of growth (21 to 30 kills/min on early waves). Nothing on the player scales with the wave; `getScaleMulti` is used for enemies and drops only.
- Wave 9 trash killing the physical build with bleed and lightning ticks, which taught "statuses ignore defense, bring leech". That is a readable, fair death.
- Dragon Lord: 22/36 in QA and felt earned in play. Shadow Assassin: the one enemy that strips War Cry.
- Rampage with a slow heavy spell (Bludgeon 29% of damage) is a build that plays differently. Keep it, even if strong.

## Against DESIGN.md

- **Pace:** matched early. Mid-world is too fast for a built caster (4 bosses in 17.5 min).
- **Where power comes from:** matched. No player-side wave scaling found. "Death is common" is not matched in waves 1 to 7.
- **Fun over balance:** Rampage plus Bludgeon and the War Cry spike are fun and should stay.
- **Builds:** partly. Three builds tested, three different feels. Tradeoffs fail on Bloodlust and Raise Shield; Magical Specialist's health cut is covered by plate, Physical Specialist's cost bites hard.
- **Synergy:** matched. Bloodlust plus shield, Rampage plus Bludgeon, cost stacking plus Specialist all emerged from simple pieces.
- **Progression:** matched to level 8. Thin after: r1-mid ended with 2 spell points and nowhere to put them; r1-late skipped Widened Focus for lack of a sixth spell point.
- **Archetypes:** see below; most are untested this round.

## Archetypes

- Bleed: untested as a build; filler in r1-late.
- Poison: untested this round (m4-poison is the last evidence).
- Fire: untested; Fireball was 9% status damage on the caster.
- Ice: untested; QA measured an 11% damage cut from slow on one pair.
- Lightning: untested.
- War Cry: working and strong; drawback missing behind shield.
- Shield: working as a support piece (Raise Shield, Arcane Shield); "hits back" (Spiked Shield) untested.
- Mana: spend side working; hoard and overflow untested.
- Control: untested by players; enemy Curse and Disarm are invisible to them.

## Enemies

- Ask something: Bone Warden (burst before health), Shadow Assassin (many small hits against War Cry), Storm Titan (needs shield), Dragon Lord, Shadow Emperor, wave 9 status packs.
- Health and damage only: Goblin King, Orc Warlord, Frost Queen, Plague Master, and all trash on waves 1 to 7.
- Invisible: Curse (-30% defense, -12 speed in source, so it does matter) and ice slow. Players cannot see either, which makes the deaths they cause unreadable. That is a display problem, not a numbers one.

## Risks in the mechanics

- Health multi now resets to 1 at each world's wave 1, so world 2 waves 1 to 4 have less health than world 1 wave 10 while hitting harder. Bloodlust and Rampage both feed on fast kills and will run hottest exactly there.
- Warrior Training, Arcane Affinity and Shield Bearer have max level 200 with 10% per level; nothing stops a long run from putting every point into one.
- Shield Bearer rank 1 now carries its own regen, so every fragile-stack build gets its protection for one point.
- Enemy crit after player defense is 1.75 to 1.94 times a normal hit (QA), so high-defense builds die to crits more than the raw 1.5 suggests; Inferno crits decide Dragon Lord and Emperor losses.

- Then longer runs with builds nobody has played: poison or bleed as the main damage, ice with Shatter, mana hoard.
- Rule, for the owner: fragile auras drop on health loss only, never on shield loss. It is the cause of problem 1 and also what DESIGN.md means by "protects fragile stacks". I ranked a Bloodlust fix that leaves the rule alone.
- Rule, for the owner: percent bonuses apply to base only, which is why percent leech alone gives 0.
