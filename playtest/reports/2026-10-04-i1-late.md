# i1-late: physical build at the end of world 1 (late start)
## Run
- Seed 203, 30m00s game time, 14 `run` calls. NOT a fresh start: level 9 at world 1 wave 8, 8 talent + 8 spell points, 30 drops. Ended level 10, highest wave 10 (enemy 7 of 10), 5 deaths.
- My mistake, not the game's: my first setup script failed silently, so the first 2m30s were played naked with only Strike (3 deaths: Frost Wolf Frostbolt 52.5, Goblin Slash 3 x2). Numbers below are from 2m30s on, which works as a clean "died at wave 8, re-clear from wave 1" test. Deaths with the build: 2, both on wave 10.
## Build
- Talents: Warrior Training 5, Deep Wounds, Bloodlust, Adrenaline Surge; Iron Constitution at level 10. Spells: Strike, Rend, Bludgeon, Health Regeneration; Slash added at level 10 (5 slots).
- Items: Battleaxe L8 + Greatsword L7 (attack 97), epic Iron Helm, two Vitality rings, Amulet of Health. Body went Plate Armor L2 (onHit speedBoost 12) -> Cloth Robes (shield 40) -> Dragon Scale Mail L8 (def 30, health 65) after wave 10 one-shot me.
- Setup choices: talents were interesting (three tier-2 picks or Widened Focus). Spells were tight in a good way: 8 points bought Rend + Bludgeon and nothing fast, so I could not hold fast and slow together until level 10. Items were obvious, one pick per slot. The stash felt right for weapons and armour, poor for rings and amulets (no damage or crit option), and about half of it was mana gear I could not use.
## How it felt
- Yes, a sensible level 9 fights at waves 8 and 9: both cleared first try, 0 deaths, health averaging 99% (lowest 59%). Wave 10 is the wall, and it kills by burst, not attrition.
- Kills per minute: waves 1-4 23.2, waves 4-6 18.4, waves 6-8 12.8, waves 8-9 13.0, waves 9-10 8.8. Second re-clear, waves 2-5: 19.6.
- Hits to kill: waves 1-4 about 1.1 (66 hits, 58 kills), waves 6-8 about 2.8, waves 9-10 about 5 (109 hits, 22 kills, including the 2145 hp Dragon Lord). A Bludgeon (about 500) one-shots wave 8 trash (429-500 hp).
- Re-clear: wave 1 to wave 8 took about 7 minutes (2m30s -> 9m23s), wave 10 at about 10 minutes. Second time 7.9 and 12.8 minutes because I was swapping spells. Waves 1-3 take 35-45 seconds each and feel like speed-clearing; waves 6-8 take 90-100 seconds each and are a chore: nothing there can hurt me and nothing drops that I want.
## Fun
- Bloodlust was my best piece. On waves 1-5 War Cry sat at 15 stacks and attack read 195 instead of 97. It makes the re-clear the fun part, which is what the design asks for.
- Shield protecting War Cry is real: with 40 shield from Cloth Robes, War Cry was up 100% of the time on waves 2-5, against 82-86% without. Dragon Scale Mail putting fire 4 on every attack was a nice surprise.
## Not fun
- Wave 10 deaths arrive out of nowhere. Health averaged 97-99% in both stretches I died in; there was no slide to react to.
- Bloodlust stops working exactly where I need help: on waves 8-10 it peaked at 2-5 stacks, 50-60% uptime.
- Mana does nothing for a physical build (average 80-90%, never short). Health Regeneration is free and always up, so that slot was not a decision.
- Level 10 took 20 minutes (about 100 xp per minute for 1280), so the build I picked at the start is the build I played.
## Enemies
- Arcane Specter (waves 8-10): the one I learned to fear. 642 damage over the run, one Arcane Missile for 162 against 139 health and 63 defense. It asked for health and I re-geared for it (139 -> 200 -> 295). Fair once known, unreadable the first time.
- Archmage (wave 10): 381 damage in one stretch against 295 health and 80 defense. More defense did not seem to help against either caster; if magic ignores defense, the game does not say so.
- Frost Lord (413 total), Dread Knight (217) and Frost Wolf were noticeable but only bigger numbers. Something puts Curse on me (19-49% of the time on waves 6-9); I never worked out who, or what it cost me.
- Bosses of waves 6-9 (Frost Queen, Storm Titan, Plague Master, Dragon Lord) all died first try and blurred together. Plague Master's poison was the only trace (166 poison damage that stretch). Dragon Lord was a 2145 hp bag.
- Wished for: a wave 8-10 enemy that punishes slow heavy hits (dodge or shield) and one that punishes fast weak hits (thorns or high defense), so fast or slow matters.
## Too strong
- Plate Armor L2 (onHit speedBoost 12): Strike landed 35-41 times per 150s with it, 25 without. About 40-60% more casts from a level 2 uncommon, best in slot at wave 8.
- Bludgeon: 36% of all damage. Same stretches, five slots: Bludgeon 6083 and 7016 against Slash 3576 and 3927. Dropping it with nothing in its place took a 150s stretch from about 16700 damage to 7750, and kills from 12.8 to 8.8 per minute.
## Too weak
- Slash against Bludgeon: 110 per hit and 24-27 damage per second against 497 per hit and 33-47. Fine for 1 point, but fast spells give nothing a slow spell does not. Quick Strike and Execute not tested (no points).
- Rend with Deep Wounds: bleed was 11433 of 181000 total damage, about 6%. Rend's direct hits did 39924; bleed added about a quarter on top. Deep Wounds never felt like it fired.
- Widened Focus is not a real choice at level 9-10. Spell points are the limit, not slots: I had 4 slots and points for 2 spells, and at level 10 a fifth slot arrived with exactly 1 point to fill it. Three talent points for a slot I cannot fill loses to Deep Wounds + Bloodlust + Adrenaline Surge.
## Bugs or oddities
- Killing blow can mislead: my last death reads "Skeletal Crawler Ice Spike for 4" when Archmage had done 381.
- `status` shows attack with War Cry included (97, 110, 123, 195 at different moments), which makes gear comparisons confusing.
- Level 9 shows 4 spell slots and level 10 shows 5; the stated unlocks (3, 6, 10, 15) suggested 3 and 4 to me.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Give fast spells a job: let Deep Wounds add bleed on every hit and double on crit, or Bloodlust reward hit count, so Slash and Quick Strike feed something Bludgeon cannot.
2. Raise Rend's bleed (6 to about 12) or let Deep Wounds triple. At 6% of damage, bleed is not "slow, sustained"; it is a rounding error next to Bludgeon.
3. Make Widened Focus come with a spell point, or cost 2. As it stands the slot arrives empty.
4. Cut Plate Armor's speedBoost (12 to about 5) or scale it with item level, and put a damage or crit option on rings and amulets so those slots are a build decision.
5. Make wave 10 casters readable: say on the enemy that magic ignores defense (if it does), and give physical builds one answer, such as Fortitude granting a short magic ward, so the response is a build piece and not only more health.
