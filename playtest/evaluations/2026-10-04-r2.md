# Evaluation r2 (2026-10-04)

## Verdict

The game is hard at its walls and too safe between them, and that is now mostly a question short runs cannot settle. Balance is close: the remaining faults are one stalemate, one free stat (mana cost) and one piece that today's edit may have pushed from too weak to runaway (Rampage at 30 per level). It matches DESIGN.md on where power comes from and on pace; it misses on "death is common" for any shielded build in waves 1 to 3 and on re-clears for anyone without Rampage. The most important thing to change is nothing new: confirm today's fifteen edits with one last short round, with Rampage and the Orc Warlord stalemate first, then switch to long runs.

## The questions asked

**Time to switch to longer runs? Yes**, after one confirmation pass on today's edits (three short runs, no new changes in between). All three r2 reports and QA predate those edits, so half of what they say is already answered or stale; another short round of changes on top would be tuning blind.

**Is it hard the way the owner wants?**
- Death common: no. 1 death in 25 min (fresh shield), 2 in 25 (caster), 2 in 30 (world 2 physical). 2.4 to 4.8 per hour. All five deaths that had a named cause were status damage or one boss spell; none was trash hitting hard in waves 1 to 7.
- Each new boss or wave earned: yes at Storm Titan (13 min wall, r2-mid), Orc Warlord in both worlds, Shadow Emperor (caster about 5/8, QA), world 2 wave 3 onward. No at Goblin King, Bone Warden, Inferno Lord and Frost Queen for a built character.
- A build path past every wall: yes on the evidence, with two exceptions. The physical build is 0/8 at Shadow Emperor in QA (0/7 last round), yet r2-world2 started past him, so nobody has shown a physical character beating him in play. World 2 Inferno Lord (543 in one hit against 506 health plus 248 shield) is 0/1 with no answer the tester owned.
- Opening minutes neither dull nor safe: not met in r2-new. Minutes 0 to 17 were 3 to 4 kills/min at 98 to 100% health. Today's Strike at 5 s, Warrior Training 2 plus 15% and base mana regen 2 all aim at this; unmeasured.

**Re-clear time (the design question).** r2-world2 measured 40 to 55 s per wave, 21 to 24 kills/min, with damage far beyond what world 1 needs. So the limit was never damage. From the code, a wave is 10 steps: 9 trash groups averaging 16.5 enemies (`ENEMY_SPAWN_LIMITS`, uniform 1 to max) plus the boss. Three things set the time:
1. Casts. A single-target spell kills one enemy per cast however hard it hits. Strike, Slash, Rend and Whirlwind at speed 10 give about 0.67 casts/s, so 17.5 enemies cost about 25 s before any pause. Overkill is wasted.
2. The pause after each group: 10 steps at 0.5 s is 5 s per wave, 50 s per world. This is now the fixed floor.
3. Rampage. `triggerActions.ts:62-76` pushes every spell by value times level (`talentActions.ts:53`), capped one tick short of ready. At 90, every spell with a cast bar of 91 or less (Strike 50, Slash 40, Rend 75, Poison Stab 75, Quick Strike 25) is ready on the next 0.1 s tick after any kill. If three casts make at least one kill, the chain never stops: about one kill per tick. A trivial wave becomes about 5 s of pauses plus 2 to 3 s of casting plus the boss.

My estimate for an overgeared physical build with Rampage 3: world 1 in roughly 1.5 to 3 minutes, most of it the pause floor and the wave 9 and 10 bosses (3218 and 4693 health). That closes the gap for that build, through a build piece, with no rule change. It does not close it for anyone else: the caster's re-clear (about 60 s per wave, r2-mid) is limited by 6 to 8.5 s single-target casts and by mana, and a character under level 6 or so has no Rampage at all. Build pieces can close the caster's gap too (Rampage is not limited to physical spells, and mana becomes its price; area spells exist). What build pieces cannot change is the 5 s per wave floor and the fact that overkill is discarded; those are rules and the owner's call. I would not ask for either until a long run shows Rampage at 90 is not enough. Not run; all arithmetic from source.

## r1 problems: closed or not

1. Bloodlust behind a shield: changed (2 points), untested; nobody played War Cry in r2. Open.
2. Raise Shield near-free: changed to 25 mana after r2-mid asked for it (97 to 100% uptime at 15 mana, even at 36% mana). Likely closed for a three-nuke caster; unmeasured.
3. World 2 Goblin King: closed. 0/19 became 6/10 and 3/9 (QA). Now 2235 health and 111 attack by my arithmetic (was 1117 and 149), very close to QA's "twice the health, two thirds the attack".
4. Caster mana free: not closed at the time of the reports (QA: damage identical to the last digit at 77 and 47 regen). Halved item regen today is the right lever; unmeasured.
5. Mid-world bosses ask nothing: open. Inferno Lord and Frost Queen fell first try again.
6. Inferno Lord one-shots a fresh level 7: not reproduced (r2-new never reached him). In world 2 the same shape appears. Open.
7. Poison Stab and Rend as filler: partly answered. Poison was 154 of 317 early damage for r2-new; bleed was 20% on world 2 bosses. Fine.
8. Percent leech gives 0: not retested; r2-world2 saw a related oddity (21 leech fell to 8). Open, bug-shaped.
9. Physical path past Shadow Emperor: open, 0/8.
10. Early talent picks: improved. 3 of 5 levels were real choices, against 2 of 6.

## Still worth changing, ranked

1. **Rampage at 90 may run away; confirm before anything else.** Source, above. It now makes five physical spells instant on every kill and favours fast spells over the "slow, heavy hits" its text promises (Bludgeon 140 gets 64%). Combined with Bloodlust it is hottest in world 2 waves 1 and 2, whose trash is softer than world 1 wave 10 (QA). "Fun and a bit broken" covers re-clears; it does not cover live waves. Confidence medium, untested. Direction: if a run shows it carrying current-wave trash, lower the per-level value to about 20 before touching anything else, so only Strike and Slash chain at level 3. **Short run can tell**: one physical run, re-clear timed per wave, with and without the talent.
2. **A shield build can stall with no kills and no deaths.** r2-new: 4 minutes at Orc Warlord, 0 kills, 0 deaths, Fortitude up 90%. Source: Fortitude is 4 flat plus 20% defense, 8 shield and 0.5 shield regen; his 0.5/s shield regen alone cancels about a third of a 5 s Strike doing 8. Breaks "a wall is a fault when the wait has nothing to decide". One run. Direction: Orc Warlord's mana (regen 5 against cost 35) so Fortitude has gaps, not player damage. **Short run can tell**: replay the same shield opening to wave 3.
3. **Mana cost is still free damage for a two-nuke caster.** r2-mid: 83 to 90% mana, never too low to cast, +15% damage per Arcane Affinity point; QA: identical damage at 47 and 77 regen, with 670 mana and leech 34 hiding it. Three sources. Today's halved item regen is the right piece; if the level 11 caster still never runs dry, the next lever is mana leech on staff and shield, not regen again. Breaks "tradeoffs must be real". **Short run can tell at level 8; only a long run** tells whether it bites and stays biting as gear grows.
4. **Shield makes waves 1 to 3 riskless and the opening dull.** r2-new: 98 to 100% health for 17 minutes; only statuses got through (271 poison, 233 bleed, 129 lightning). Breaks "death is common". One run; the same shape in world 2 once the Arcane Shield dropped. Direction: enemy lists, an early trash enemy whose damage is mostly a status, as the tester asked; leave Shield Bearer alone. **Short run can tell.**
5. **One-spell boss deaths with no answer in reach.** World 2 Inferno Lord 543 into 754 total; r1's world 1 case was the same shape. Two samples across two rounds. Direction: move Inferno's damage into its burn so regen and shield regen answer it. **Only a long run** can show whether a level 13 to 14 character finds the answer in drops.
6. **Physical build has no shown path past Shadow Emperor.** 0/8 and 0/7 in QA, boss left at 34 to 62%. Today's Warrior Training (15% per level) and the Rampage change both help it; QA's fork was on stale talents. Direction: none until a rebuilt physical character is run. **Only a long run** (or a clean QA batch) can tell.

Not ranked because they are display or rules, not build pieces: magic damage not explained in spell text, Curse and slow unreadable, item level restarting per world, forks keeping old talent numbers (QA bug 1; check `saveSystem.ts`, since a real save may do the same).

## What is working

- Status damage as the counter to shield and defense stacking. All three testers found it and liked it. Protect it.
- Storm Titan as a 13 minute wall with a build answer; Shadow Emperor for the caster; Orc Warlord in world 2.
- r2-mid's swap of Health Regeneration for a third nuke: more damage, 29% average health, a death two minutes later. The best tradeoff seen so far.
- Early talent picks are now choices. The magic-ignores-attack discovery turned a stuck run around.
- World 2 as a gear wall with a path: two drops in six minutes turned wave 3 deaths into wave 4 clears.

## Against DESIGN.md

- **Pace:** matched. Level 14 is about an hour out at level 13; that is the intended grind.
- **Where power comes from:** matched; `getScaleMulti` feeds enemies and drops only. "Death is common" not matched. "Clearing early waves faster" was not matched (8 min at full gear) and is probably matched now for Rampage builds only.
- **Fun over balance:** the Fireball switch and the world 2 hammer were the fun; keep both.
- **Builds:** three styles seen. Tradeoffs fail on mana cost and on shield in early waves.
- **Synergy:** matched. Widened Focus is too good for physical builds (free spells on separate timers), a lead from one run.
- **Progression:** thin at level 9 (2 spell points, nowhere to spend) and at 12 to 13 (an hour to the next unlock).
- **Archetypes:** below.

## Archetypes

- Bleed: partial; real on world 2 bosses, nothing elsewhere. Poison: partial as an early damage source. Fire: weak next to Ice Spike (2560 against 2841 per stretch).
- Ice, Lightning, Control, Mana hoard: untested by any r1 or r2 run.
- War Cry: untested this round. Shield: working, too safe early, can stall. Mana spend: working, underpriced.

## Enemies

- Ask something: Orc Warlord (punishes low damage per hit), Storm Titan, Shadow Assassin, Shadow Emperor, wave 8 and 9 status packs.
- Health and damage only: Goblin King, Bone Warden, Inferno Lord and Frost Queen for a built character, all trash in waves 1 to 3. World 2 repeats world 1's order with nothing new; the mix inside a wave never mattered.

## Risks in the mechanics

- Rampage chain, as above. On arriving at a boss every spell is one tick from ready, so bosses open with a full burst.
- The boss health floor of 2 times 1.5 leaves world 1 Goblin King at 120 health and 6 attack: still a formality, by design or not.
- World 2 drops outclass everything from world 1 at once, so a character who reaches world 2 wave 1 and farms it will trivialise world 1 for good; re-clears then have no decisions and no useful drops.
- Warrior Training at 15% per level with max level 200 is the default sink for every spare physical point.

## Open questions for the long runs

- Three runs of 90 minutes or more, from fresh, each on a different archetype nobody has played: ice with Shatter, War Cry with Bloodlust at 2 points, and a physical Rampage build.
- Each should report: deaths per hour by 15 minute block; world 1 re-clear time each time it happens; percent of time out of mana; when the last real decision was made; first tier 3 talent and whether it changed play.
- Does a physical character beat Shadow Emperor in play? Does anyone reach world 2 from fresh, and in how long?
- Rules for the owner: the pause after each group (now 5 s per wave) and discarded overkill are the floor on re-clears for every build without Rampage; fragile auras drop on health loss only, which is why shield makes early waves safe.
