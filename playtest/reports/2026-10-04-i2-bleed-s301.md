# i2-bleed (seed 301), first hour, physical bleed with fast attacks
## Run
- Run name is `i2-bleed-s301`, not `i2-bleed`: a run called `i2-bleed` (seed 44, 1h played) already existed and its file was being written as I started, so I did not touch it. Fresh character, seed 301, 60m00s game time, 14 `run` calls.
- Level 10, highest wave 10 (world 1), 7 deaths, 836 kills (13.9/min), 108 drops.
- My mistake: I hit level 10 inside a `--no-stop` stretch at about 54m and ended with 1 talent point, 2 spell points and the fifth spell slot unspent.
- Not tested: Bludgeon, Execute, Lacerate. See "Not fun" for why. Strike (6s, 100%) stood in as the slow spell.
## Build
- End: Slash, Rend, Quick Strike, Health Regeneration. Warrior Training 5, Iron Constitution 1, Deep Wounds, Bloodlust. Two Greatswords (+27, +18 damage, leech 15), Dragon Scale Mail, heavy boots and helm. Attack 138, health 167, defense 49, crit 10%.
- Path: Strike+Slash to level 5, Rend at 5, Deep Wounds at 7, Bloodlust + Quick Strike at 8. I dropped the Dagger (crit 19% to 10%) for a second Greatsword at 48m because raw attack beat crit for Deep Wounds by a mile.
## How it felt
- 0-10m: brisk. A level every 1-2 minutes, wave 5 by 9m49s. 2.3 to 2.9 hits per kill.
- 10-23m: stuck at wave 5 for 14 minutes, 2 deaths. Casters hit 27-39 into 43-49 health. Nothing I could spend fixed it; a Warhammer (+14) and armour drops did (health 49 to 84, attack 31 to 53). Re-clears took 5 minutes each at the same 2+ hits per kill as the first time: a chore.
- 22-36m: Bloodlust turned it around. Kills went 11.9/min to 15.0/min, and 27.4/min on a re-clear where everything died in one hit. Still stuck at wave 6 enemy 9 for 13 minutes (2 deaths).
- 36-60m: took off. Wave 8 at 38m, wave 10 at 54m after a full regear (defense 19 to 45, attack 83 to 138). Re-clears settled at 45-50s per wave, about 4 minutes back to wave 6. That felt like speed-clearing, but the floor is cast time, not power: one hit per kill cannot go faster.
## Fun
- Bloodlust is the best moment of the hour. War Cry sat at 15 stacks 81-90% of the time and the attack readout jumped 53 to 123. One point, changed how the run played.
- The Greatsword pair drop at 48m: wave 5 to wave 10 in 5m40s with no deaths.
- Plate Armor's on-hit speed boost with Quick Strike was a real "two pieces click" moment (numbers under Too strong).
## Not fun
- Bleed is a side dish. Share of all damage: 23% with Rend and Slash alone, 10% once Strike filled a slot, 12% after Deep Wounds, 8.5-13% after that, 11% for the hour (19.7k of 180.8k). On re-clears it was 3%: nothing lives long enough to bleed.
- Deep Wounds did nothing I could see: bleed share 10% to 12%, and it did not drop when my crit halved.
- Spell points gate the brief. Eight points by level 9, Rend and Quick Strike cost 3 each, so a 5-point Bludgeon or Execute cannot be bought in the first hour next to a bleed kit. No real choice either: Warrior Training five times was automatic at base attack 3.
- Fast versus slow, using Strike: Strike stretch 12.9 kills/min, 15.5 damage/s from that slot, bleed 14%; Quick Strike stretch 16.5 kills/min, 19 damage/s, bleed 8.5%. Different waves, so treat as a hint: fast wins by about 20%.
- Health averaged 98-99% in every stretch and still hit 0. Every death was burst, so regen and leech 15 never mattered. Mana sat at 80% unused all hour.
- 99 items in the bag, about a third mage gear, no way to bulk clear.
## Enemies
- Nothing asked for a counter. I never changed a spell because of an enemy; the only answer was more health and defense.
- Bosses of waves 1-3 are weaker than their trash: Goblin King dealt 8 damage all hour, Bone Warden 10. I could not tell you what any boss does.
- Dangerous ones were regular casters: Storm Elemental (Lightning Bolt 41), Arcane Specter (two killing blows, 27 and 33), Necromancer (485 total, the most of anything). Flame Lord Inferno 72 at wave 8 enemy 3 and Frost Lord Ice Spike 71 at wave 10 were one hit for 40-45% of my health: unreadable spikes.
- Curse was on me 20% of the hour and the output never says who cast it.
- One change for a memorable boss: give Bone Warden a shield that only status damage gets through. Bleed would finally have a job and a direct-damage build would feel the difference.
## Too strong
- Plate Armor L4 (common), on-hit speed boost 8. Same attack, 138: with it Slash landed every 2.3s and Quick Strike every 1.7s, 134 damage/s; without it 5.2s and 3.5s, 74 damage/s. Roughly double from a common body piece. Fun, keep it, but it should cost defense.
- Bloodlust for 1 point. "Lost when you lose health" barely bites: 20 shield kept 15 stacks up over 80% of the time.
- Flat weapon damage. One Greatsword L8 (+27) is worth nine Warrior Training points of flat damage.
## Too weak
- Deep Wounds (above). Health Regeneration aura: never the reason I lived. Crit gear for a bleed build: Dagger and Ring of Speed lost to plain damage.
## Bugs or oddities
- `attack` in `status` includes live War Cry stacks (123 at 32m45s, 83 at 48m45s with the same gear), which makes gear comparisons misleading.
- A killing blow listed as "Slime Slash for 3" hides the hit that actually did it.
- Dragon Scale Mail's fire +2 put 384 fire damage on enemies in 5 minutes, about a sixth of what my whole bleed kit did.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make bleed the build, not the garnish: let Rend's bleed lose a tenth per second instead of a fifth, or have Deep Wounds also stop bleed decaying for 3s. Target 30%+ of damage on bosses.
2. Give Bloodlust its tradeoff: War Cry stacks drop when the shield is hit, not only health. Then Raise Shield or a shield item becomes a choice rather than free cover.
3. Put a price on Plate Armor's trigger (lower defense, or speed boost 5) and add a bleed twin: "on hit, add 1 bleed" for Quick Strike to feed.
4. Cut Execute and Bludgeon to 4 points, or Rend to 2, so a slow-versus-fast choice exists in the first hour.
5. Give each early boss one mechanic the build must answer (status-only shield, an enrage timer, a visible wind-up before a 70 hit) and name the source of auras like Curse in the run output.
