## How it felt
- 0-8 min: one cast per kill on wave 1 (18 hp, 17 per Missile), 10-12 kills/min. Wave 3-4 at level 5 dropped to 5.5 kills/min and 4.0 casts per kill: slow, never dangerous.
- 8-19 min: first death at wave 6. Specialist plus Lightning Bolt was the spike: 8.4 to 14.4 kills/min, casts per kill 4 to 2.1, waves 1-5 re-cleared in 4.5 min against 10m51s the first time.
- 22-43 min: stuck at wave 8 for 21 minutes and three deaths. Each re-clear back to wave 8 took about 7 minutes (8.5, 7, 7) and did not get faster. A talent point did not break it; two item swaps (+16 mana cost) did.
- 50-60 min: Raise Shield ended dying: waves 5-9 with lowest health 100%. Wave 10 is slow (5.9 kills/min, 4.3 casts per kill) but safe (lowest 61%).
- Time out of mana per point: Affinity 1: 11%. +Efficiency 1: 2%. Affinity 2: 17%. +Efficiency 2: 0%. Affinity 3 with a staff: 0%. Specialist: 0%, mana averaging 91%. Affinity 4: 0%, 91%. The talent cliff is gone if you alternate.
- The cliff moved to gear: Mage Hood, Enchanted Boots and an Arcane Shield for a staff (cost 58 to 74, regen 37 to 30) took me from 91% mana and 0% dry to 20% mana and 27% dry in one step. That stretch still did more damage (3828 direct per minute against 3470) and reached wave 9 for the first time. Dry time is not a penalty; it only means regen is now the damage stat.
- Magical Specialist matters now: cost 23 to 38, regen 15 to 35 on the point.
- Per hit at the end: Lightning Bolt 222, Ice Spike 207, Arcane Missile 198. Missile still led every stretch (36-44%) on its 6s cast, for 1 spell point.
- Lightning status: 70/min from the Bolt alone, 190/min with Chain Reaction 1 (about +4% at 10-21% crit). On wave 10, where enemies live longer, it was 4312 of 28900 damage (15%).
- Ice: the output shows nothing for the slow, so Ice Spike read as a second Missile. I cannot say what it was worth.
- Choices: levels 2-6 were a ratio (alternate Affinity and Efficiency), L7 was obvious, L8 and the L10 spell were the only real decisions.
## Fun
- The Specialist spike, and early bosses falling a minute apart on the way back up.
- Trading surplus mana for a shield (Arcane Shield for a staff, then Raise Shield) felt like building.
- A common Amulet of Plague (poison, bleed, fire +2 on every attack) quietly added 5-9% damage.
## Not fun
- The lightning kit is out of reach in hour one, so I held 1-2 dead spell points for 20 minutes at a time.
- Three identical 7-minute re-clears to die at the same spot in wave 8.
- Health Regeneration ran 95% of the time while health averaged 95-100%: a slot I never chose and never felt.
## Enemies
- Every death came from a caster mid-wave (Dark Mage, Frost Lord, Flame Lord, Arcane Specter), never a boss. Frost Wolf, a plain wave 6 enemy, hit for 56-78 with Frostbolt, more than any boss before wave 9.
- Bosses asked nothing. Goblin King's hardest hit was 7; Inferno Lord, Frost Queen and Storm Titan never appeared in my hardest hits. Dragon Lord's wave was the first I noticed (Dragon Meteor 106).
- Curse was on me 6-31% of the time and enemy Arcane Boost 1-14%; I could not see either change an outcome.
- One change: give Storm Titan a visible charge, lightning that builds to one large hit unless you kill or slow him in time. Ice would then have a job.
## Too strong
- Raise Shield (3 points): 99-100% uptime at 10 stacks, shield 57 to 149 (+9/s), zero deaths after it, mana still at 90%.
- Magical Specialist: 8.4 to 14.4 kills/min for one point, and attack set to 0 costs a caster nothing.
## Too weak
- Chain Reaction 1 at base crit: about 4% damage. Strike in a caster build: 60 hits for 370 damage, 4% of the stretch, 6 a hit against 62-70 for a spell.
- Drops: 98 items in the bag, about 12 ever worth a look (Iron Helms, Tower Shields, Battleaxes, Daggers).
## Bugs or oddities
- `new` cannot replace an existing run and nothing else can remove one.
- A level-up stop cost a `run` call that lasted 3 seconds (13m26s to 13m29s).
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make ice visible and useful: report seconds of slow or hits prevented, so Ice Spike has a job against the mid-wave casters that do the killing.
2. Lower Static Field or Chain Lightning to 3 points so a lightning build exists before level 12.
3. Give Raise Shield a cost that bites (shorter duration, fewer stacks, or mana per stack) so surplus mana is a real trade.
4. Give Magical Specialist a price a caster feels (less health, or no shield), and let Chain Reaction add crit chance or scale with mana cost.
5. Move the danger to bosses: tone down Frost Wolf's Frostbolt and Flame Lord's Inferno, and give each boss one readable move like the Storm Titan charge.
