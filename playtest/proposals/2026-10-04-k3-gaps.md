# Proposals: k3-gaps (2026-10-04)

Sources: the k3 reports (phys, caster, late) and the i2 reports (bleed-s301, caster-s302, world2). No evaluation exists yet. Numbers are sized against the `catalog` output of run `adv1` and the data files as of today. Nothing here was simulated; every "check" below is a reading of the code, not a run.

## Focus

1. **Bosses ask little and their traits are invisible.** k3-caster and k3-phys: "trait not noticed" on six of seven bosses; Storm Titan's Chain Lightning 124 (normal hit 49) and Frost Queen's Frostbolt 123 are "a dice roll, not a fight". i2-caster: "bosses asked nothing". i2-world2: only three enemies asked anything.
2. **Physical builds answer burst with health only, and mana idles.** k3-phys: mana 89% full all run, Iron Constitution was the only answer to Frost Queen, half the drops were mana gear it could not use.
3. **Decisions dry up.** i2-bleed: "Warrior Training five times was automatic". i2-caster: "levels 2-6 were a ratio". k3-late: no level between minute 10 and 20.
4. **Ice does nothing measurable; bleed is 3% of damage on re-clears.** Reading the code explains ice: stacks are flat (never scale), slow 3 speed each, and decay 20% a second, so one Ice Spike (7 stacks) averages about 3 stacks, a 9% slow, and a slow cannot shrink a single hit.
5. **Re-clears are paced by cast time.** Rampage (`onKill -> speedBoost 20`) halved waves 3-4 in k3-phys and was the best-liked piece. i2-caster re-clears stayed at 7 minutes however strong the build got.

One finding that is not a new piece: the physical answers to burst already exist (Disarm, Exhaustion, Fortitude) but cost 6, 5 and 3 spell points in an hour that gives about 8. See Conflicts.

## Top picks

1. **Storm Titan: Thunderhead** (proposal 1). Turns the unreadable 124 into a slow announced cast, which gives ice, Exhaustion and Curse a job in one data change.
2. **Blood Trail** (proposal 9). One trigger that already exists gives bleed a job on trash and carries it into the boss; it is the bleed twin of Epidemic and Wildfire.
3. **Mana Guard** (proposal 5). The one new mechanic I would pay for: it spends idle mana on burst, makes mana gear useful to physical builds, and makes Physical Specialist a real fork.

## Proposals

### Bosses and enemies

#### 1. Storm Titan: Thunderhead (boss rework, enemy-only spell)

- **What it does**: replace `CHAIN_LIGHTNING` in Storm Titan's spells with a new enemy-only magic spell, Thunderhead: cast 20.0s, AoE, dmg 5 x mana cost, crit +0%, lightning 5. Keep `LIGHTNING_BOLT` (cast 8.0s, 2 x) and the health regen trait. Comparison: Inferno is the heaviest existing spell at 12.5s and 3 x; Thunderhead is the same damage per second of cast as Lightning Bolt (0.25 x per second), delivered as one hit of about 2.5 normal hits, which is what the 124 already was (124 against 49), but now on a 20-second clock with no crit roll on top.
- **Combines with**: ice (each stack removes 3 speed from the cast), Exhaustion (speed -30), Curse (speed -12), Frostbite with fast attacks. With base speed near 100, holding 10 ice stacks turns a 20s cast into about 28s: on a 60-second fight that is two Thunderheads instead of three. Also combines with raw damage: a build that kills in under 20s never sees it.
- **What it costs the player**: nothing is given; the boss stops being a coin flip and becomes a clock. The danger stays the same size.
- **How it plays**: three answers instead of one: kill inside the window, slow the cast, or carry enough shield and health for the hit. The cast bar is the tell.
- **Checklist**: combines: ice, Exhaustion, Curse. Existing mechanics: yes. Gives up: n/a for an enemy; it asks for a slot or a talent. Plays differently: yes, control becomes an answer. Real choice: yes, three answers. Archetype: Ice and Control.
- **What it needs**: one spell entry and one boss edit. For readability, the harness `status` and the UI should show the enemy's longest cast in progress; testers could not see traits at all.
- **Risks**: removing the crit removes variance, so if the base hit is survivable the boss becomes easier, not readable. Size it so two Thunderheads kill a build with no answer. Watch: deaths at wave 7 with and without an ice or Exhaustion slot.

#### 2. Orc Warlord: War Cry behind a shield (boss rework)

- **What it does**: replace `FORTITUDE` with `WAR_CRY` (cast 3.0s, +10% damage per stack, max 15, fragile: all stacks lost when he loses health) and give him `baseShield: 30, baseShieldRegen: 3` (Frost Queen has 30 and 2). While his shield holds he loses no health, so the stacks build: about one every 3 seconds, +100% damage after 30 seconds.
- **Combines with**: anything that gets through a shield quickly (Execute, Bludgeon, Shatter), and any status, because statuses tick every second and keep a regenerating shield from closing. Bleed and poison get an early-game job here: a ticking bleed means his stacks drop every second. Ice slows the War Cry casts as well as his attacks.
- **What it costs**: asks the player for either a heavy hit or a damage-over-time piece by wave 3. A build of weak fast hits against 30 shield regenerating 3 a second watches the stack counter climb.
- **How it plays**: the stack count on the boss is the readout. It teaches the player's own War Cry rule from the other side.
- **Checklist**: combines: War Cry, shield, statuses. Existing mechanics: yes, all of it. Gives up: n/a. Plays differently: yes, a race against a visible counter. Real choice: yes, burst or status. Archetype: War Cry, Shield, Bleed.
- **What it needs**: only a data edit, assuming enemy-held fragile auras drop on health loss the same way the player's do (the fragile check should be read once to confirm).
- **Risks**: wave 3 is early; a fresh character with Strike alone may not break 30 shield in time. Start at shield 20, regen 2. Watch: stack count at his first three attacks, and whether Rend changes the fight.

#### 3. Plague Master: Miasma (boss rework, enemy-only aura)

- **What it does**: Plague Master (wave 8, currently two Poison Stabs and no trait) gets a self aura, Miasma: cast 6.0s, lasts 12s, `triggers: [onTakeAttack -> applyStatus poison 4]`, so every attack that lands on him poisons the attacker. Give him mana to cast it (`baseMana: 100, baseManaCost: 36, baseManaRegen: 5`, like his neighbours). Auras already carry triggers (Bleed Shatter does), so this is the route to a boss trait without adding triggers to `BossPreset`.
- **Combines with**: Toxic Blood (poison on you heals 2 per stack: the boss becomes a healing fountain for the one build that poisons itself), Health Regeneration, slow heavy attacks (Bludgeon takes one dose per 14s, Quick Strike takes five), and pure status builds, since statuses ticking on him are not attacks.
- **What it costs**: fast on-hit builds (Quick Strike, Frostbite, Battle Trance) pay per hit. The aura has gaps between casts, and slowing him widens them.
- **How it plays**: the first boss where attack speed is a liability. Poison on the player is already displayed, so the trait reads itself.
- **Checklist**: combines: Toxic Blood, slow spells, statuses. Existing mechanics: yes. Gives up: n/a. Plays differently: yes, inverts fast against slow. Real choice: yes. Archetype: Poison, and a check on speed builds.
- **What it needs**: one aura entry and a boss edit. Confirm that `applyStatus` fired from the defender's `onTakeAttack` targets the attacker (it uses the holder's opponents, so it should for a single player).
- **Risks**: status damage is soaked by shield first, so a Raise Shield build may not notice. If so, the fix belongs with k3-late's suggestion that a share of status damage bypass shield, not here.

#### 4. Ironclad (regular enemy, waves 4+)

- **What it does**: `baseHealth: 18, baseAttack: 5, baseDefense: 12, spells: [SLASH]`, minWave 4. About half the health and four times the defense of its wave neighbours (Orc: 32 health, 2 defense). Status damage ignores defense (`statusActions.ts`), so bleed, poison and fire go straight through.
- **Combines with**: Rend, Lacerate, Poison Stab, Ignite, Curse (defense -30%), Bleed Shatter (defense -1 per bleed stack). Slash and Quick Strike bounce off; Bludgeon's 300% of attack punches through.
- **What it costs**: asks every build for one status, one heavy hit, or a defense shred. Low attack, so it is a time tax and not a killer: it shows up on re-clears as the enemy a one-note build is slow on.
- **How it plays**: bleed's first job on trash. A regular enemy that makes a second damage type worth a slot.
- **Checklist**: combines: any status, Curse, Bleed Shatter. Existing mechanics: yes. Gives up: n/a. Plays differently: yes. Real choice: several answers. Archetype: Bleed, Poison, Control.
- **What it needs**: one preset. Defense is soft (see Conflicts), so "bounce off" above means weak hits are roughly halved, not stopped; it applies to magic hits as well.
- **Risks**: defense scales with wave; at high waves a pure-hit build could deal 1 per hit and stall. Cap it by keeping health low. Watch: seconds per Ironclad for a Strike-only build against a Rend build.

### Physical defense and idle mana

#### 5. Mana Guard (talent, tier 2, cost 1, max 3) - NEW MECHANIC

- **What it does**: 15% of each hit taken (per level, up to 45%) is paid from mana instead of health or shield, at 2 mana per point of damage, while mana lasts.
- **Description line**: "Part of every hit is paid from your mana. Needs a mana pool and regen to refill it; worthless with Physical Specialist."
- **Combines with**: mana gear (the half of k3-phys's drops it could not use), Mana Efficiency, Arcane Boost (mana +50, +20%), Siphon (kills restore mana), and War Cry: damage taken from mana is not health lost, so it protects fragile stacks. Against a 123 hit at level 2, 37 is paid from mana (74 mana) and 86 from health.
- **What it costs**: it forks against Physical Specialist (mana regen set to 0: the guard works once and never refills), and for a caster the mana it burns is casts not made. At base regen 3 a second it covers one large hit about every 25 seconds, so it answers burst and does nothing against a steady beating.
- **How it plays**: a physical build keeps its mana bar full on purpose and reads it as a second health bar. Health Regeneration (25 mana) now competes with it for the pool.
- **Checklist**: combines: mana gear, Siphon, War Cry. Existing mechanics: no, new damage hook. Gives up: Physical Specialist's +60% damage, or caster uptime. Plays differently: yes. Real choice: yes, it is bad for half the builds. Archetype: bridges Mana and physical.
- **What it needs**: a `manaGuard` effect type read in `takeAttack` before shield. It also unlocks enemy pieces that burn mana and an item line ("mana guard +5%").
- **Risks**: with Magical Specialist (mana +50%, regen +50%) and a 300-mana pool it may be a third health bar for casters at no felt cost, like Raise Shield is now. If so raise the rate to 3 mana per point. Watch: caster mana average with it, and physical deaths to Frost Queen.

#### 6. Brittle Armor (aura spell, unlock cost 2)

- **What it does**: cast 3.0s; mana 20; lasts 20s; on self; stacks scale, max 8; fragile (all stacks lost when you lose health); defense +3; defense +20%. Sized against War Cry (cast 3.0s, mana 10, fragile, max 15) and Fortitude (defense +4, +20%, not stacking, 10s). Grows out of the IDEAS.md "brittle armor" entry.
- **Combines with**: Bloodlust and Battle Trance (same fragility: one hit strips both, so a shield in front protects both), Raise Shield, Stone Wall, Mana Efficiency. At 8 stacks defense is +24 and +160%.
- **What it costs**: 20 mana every 3 seconds is 6.7 mana a second against a base regen of 3, so it drains the idle pool and wants regen. It answers the first hit of a fight and nothing after it: a boss that hits every 8 seconds allows two or three stacks between hits.
- **How it plays**: strong against burst after a lull (the boss opener, the re-clear), weak in a brawl. The opposite profile to Health Regeneration.
- **Checklist**: combines: War Cry pieces, shields. Existing mechanics: yes. Gives up: mana, and it resets on every hit. Plays differently: yes, front-loaded defense. Real choice: yes against Raise Shield and Fortitude. Archetype: bridges Shield and War Cry.
- **What it needs**: only data. Confirm defense reduces magic hits; if it does not, this piece does not answer the hits that kill physical builds and should give `shield` on a short timer instead.
- **Risks**: behind a large shield the stacks never drop and it becomes permanent +160% defense. That is the Shield archetype's stated job ("protects fragile stacks") but watch whether it removes danger for Raise Shield builds; if it does, make stacks drop when shield is hit.

### Decisions at levels 2 to 10

#### 7. Leanings (three tier 1 talents, cost 1, max 1 each)

- **What they do**:
  - Heavy Hands: damage +20%, manaCost +20%, speed -10. "Slower, harder hits. Pairs with Rampage, Execute and Bludgeon; bad with on-hit talents."
  - Light Feet: speed +12, damage -10%, manaCost -10%. "Faster, weaker hits. Feeds Frostbite, Battle Trance and every on-hit item."
  - Scar Tissue: health +25%, healthRegen set to 0. "More health, none of it comes back on its own. Pairs with leech, Marrow Drinker and Toxic Blood."
- **Combines with**: named in each line. Sized against Warrior Training (+3, +10% per point), Quickstep (speed +3 per point) and Iron Constitution (+6, +10%).
- **What it costs**: each has a stat it takes away, and a one-point cap so it is a direction and not a ladder.
- **How it plays**: the level 2 point becomes a statement about fast or slow, and the level 3 to 6 points follow from it.
- **Checklist**: combines: yes, named. Existing mechanics: yes. Gives up: yes. Plays differently: partly; these are stat trades, which is the weakest kind of piece here, and I say so. Real choice: yes, they exclude each other in practice. Archetype: fast against slow, which cuts across all of them.
- **What it needs**: only data.
- **Risks**: Heavy Hands' speed -10 may not offset +20% damage, making it an automatic first point. Watch what testers take at level 2; if all take the same one, the trade is too soft.

#### 8. Field Promotion (talent, tier 2, cost 2, needs level 9, max 1)

- **What it does**: one more spell slot that only holds an aura. Sits between Widened Focus (level 8, cost 3) and Split Mind (level 14, cost 4).
- **Description line**: "Needs level 9. One more slot, auras only. Somewhere for mana to go: Battle Cry, Curse, Exhaustion or a shield."
- **Combines with**: every aura, and with the idle mana of physical builds. It is what lets Disarm, Exhaustion or Brittle Armor sit next to a full attack bar.
- **What it costs**: two talent points at the level where tier 2 payoffs (Rampage 3, Bloodlust, Deep Wounds) compete hardest, and auras cost mana the build must now supply.
- **How it plays**: level 9 becomes "a control or defense aura, or two payoff points", which is the choice k3-phys made by default (health) because nothing else was on offer.
- **Checklist**: combines: all auras. Existing mechanics: mostly. Gives up: two points. Plays differently: yes, it is where control gets into attack builds. Real choice: yes. Archetype: Control and Mana.
- **What it needs**: an `auraOnly` flag on the slot; `spellSlots` exists but has no restriction. Small, but it is new.
- **Risks**: a fourth and fifth slot by level 9 may bring everything online too early. If so, drop the flag idea and lower Widened Focus to level 7 instead.

### Ice and bleed payoffs, and re-clear speed

#### 9. Blood Trail (talent, tier 2, cost 1, max 1)

- **What it does**: `onDeath -> spreadStatus bleed`. When a bleeding enemy dies its remaining bleed jumps to every other enemy. Identical in shape to Wildfire (`spreadStatus fire`) and Epidemic.
- **Description line**: "When a bleeding enemy dies, its bleed jumps to every other enemy. Builds through a wave and arrives at the boss with you. Needs a way to bleed."
- **Combines with**: Rend, Lacerate, Hemorrhage, Deep Wounds (a crit doubles the bleed, the kill hands the doubled stack on), Bleed Shatter (defense -1 per stack on everything it spreads to), Marrow Drinker (heals per total bleed on enemies, which this multiplies), Rampage (faster kills, more hand-offs before decay). Bleed decays 10% a second against fire's 20%, so more of it survives the jump.
- **What it costs**: a tier 2 point that does nothing without a bleed spell. Overkilled enemies die before they bleed, so it favours fast light cuts over Execute.
- **How it plays**: bleed gets a job on trash (3% of re-clear damage today), and the wave feeds the boss, which k3-phys asked for ("let Rampage carry into the boss").
- **Checklist**: combines: six pieces named. Existing mechanics: yes, an existing action with a different status. Gives up: only the point; it is dead without bleed. Plays differently: yes. Real choice: for bleed builds it is close to automatic, as Wildfire is for fire. Archetype: Bleed.
- **What it needs**: only data.
- **Risks**: with Hemorrhage and Deep Wounds the stacks could snowball across a 10-enemy wave. Fun-and-broken is allowed, but watch bleed's share of damage on waves 1-5.

#### 10. Cold Snap (talent, tier 2, cost 1, max 1)

- **What it does**: `onKill -> applyStatusAoe ice 6`, and `speed -5` on yourself. Every kill chills everything still standing.
- **Description line**: "Kills chill every other enemy. Keeps a wave slowed and arrives with ice for Shatter to break. Costs you a little speed."
- **Combines with**: Shatter (stacks are waiting on the next target for the first crit: 6 stacks at 3 damage each, scaled by power), Frostbite, Frost Nova, Glacial Ward and Frostbrand, Thunderhead (proposal 1). A sixth of an Ice Spike per kill is small alone; in a wave it holds a floor of ice that single-target ice never reaches because of the 20% decay.
- **What it costs**: speed -5 always, and it does nothing in a boss fight with no kills.
- **How it plays**: ice becomes the archetype that takes less damage on re-clears and opens each enemy with a Shatter, rather than a slow nobody can measure.
- **Checklist**: combines: Shatter, Frostbite. Existing mechanics: yes. Gives up: speed. Plays differently: yes. Real choice: yes, useless to non-ice builds without Shatter. Archetype: Ice.
- **What it needs**: only data.
- **Risks**: goes dead late, because ice is flat and enemy speed is not affected by wave but their damage is; Shatter's power scaling is what keeps it alive. Report seconds of slow in the harness or testers will again say they cannot see it.

#### 11. Flash Freeze (talent, tier 3, cost 2, max 1) - NEW MECHANIC

- **What it does**: `onHit -> freezeStatus ice 20`: if your target holds 20 or more ice, consume all of it and reset its cast closest to finishing to zero.
- **Description line**: "At 20 ice your target's next attack is cancelled and the ice is spent. Competes with Shatter for the same stacks."
- **Combines with**: Ice Spike (7) plus Frostbolt (9) plus Frostbite or Robe of Elements reaches 20 inside the decay window; Cold Snap gives a head start; Thunderhead and every slow boss nuke are what it is for. Quick Strike with Frostbite 3 is the physical route.
- **What it costs**: it spends the slow, and it takes the stacks Shatter wanted: burst or denial, not both. Twenty stacks at 20% decay needs two ice sources committed.
- **How it plays**: the control answer to "one big hit kills me": the hit does not get smaller, it does not happen.
- **Checklist**: combines: all ice pieces. Existing mechanics: no, a new action (a sibling of `shatterStatus`). Gives up: the slow and Shatter. Plays differently: yes. Real choice: yes. Archetype: Ice and Control.
- **What it needs**: a `freezeStatus` action. It would also allow enemy versions and a unique item.
- **Risks**: the obvious runaway is a permanent lock. Guard it with the threshold (raise to 30 if needed) and by resetting one cast, not all. This is the proposal most likely to remove danger; build it only after Thunderhead shows whether plain slows are enough.

#### 12. Follow Through (talent, tier 2, cost 1, max 1)

- **What it does**: `onKill -> castSpell "Follow Through"`, a trigger-only physical spell: dmg 0 + 50% of attack, crit +0%, no status. `speed -8%` on yourself. Same shape as Blood Pact's `onKill -> castSpell "Crushing Weight"`.
- **Description line**: "A kill swings on into the next enemy. Makes slow, heavy attacks clear weak waves; little use on a boss."
- **Combines with**: Execute and Bludgeon (k3-phys: Execute "overkills trash" and made re-clears slower; this gives the overkill somewhere to go), Rampage (the swing can kill, which pushes attacks forward again), Bloodlust, on-hit items such as Plate Armor's speed boost. Slash at 70% of attack is the comparison; this is weaker and free.
- **What it costs**: speed -8% everywhere, including the boss where it does nothing.
- **How it plays**: slow-attack builds get their own re-clear tool, and strong builds chain through waves 1-3: power shows up as lap time, which is what i2-world2 asked for with "Overkill".
- **Checklist**: combines: Execute, Bludgeon, Rampage. Existing mechanics: yes. Gives up: speed. Plays differently: yes. Real choice: yes against Rampage levels. Archetype: generic physical, slow side.
- **What it needs**: only data, plus one check: a kill by the triggered spell fires `onKill` again inside the same pass. `speedBoost` has a guard against that kind of loop; `castSpell` may need one (at most one chain per tick).
- **Risks**: chained kills through a whole trivial wave in one tick. Probably fun; cap it if it breaks the log. A caster twin (magic, 1 x mana cost) is possible, but triggered magic casts spend no mana, so it would be free damage; not proposed.

## Sets

- **Readable bosses**: 1, 2, 3, 4. Smallest useful part: proposal 1 alone, with the cast shown in `status`.
- **Ice has a job**: 1, 10, 11, with the existing Shatter and Frostbite. Smallest: 1 plus 10 (no new mechanic).
- **Bleed has a job**: 9, 4, 2, with Bleed Shatter and Deep Wounds. Smallest: 9.
- **Physical mana**: 5, 6, 8. Smallest: 6 (data only). 5 is the stronger answer but needs the hook.
- **Re-clear by archetype**: 9 (bleed), 10 (ice), 12 (slow physical), next to Rampage, Epidemic and Wildfire.

## Not proposed

- A per-hit damage cap aura (k3-phys's suggestion): simply removes the danger of the hits that are meant to kill.
- A cheaper single-target Disarm: a stat variant; lower Disarm's price instead.
- Bleed decay change or bleed that stops decaying (i2-bleed): a rules change, and DESIGN.md says tune pieces first.
- True overkill carry-over: needs a new damage path; Follow Through gets most of it from an existing trigger.
- Ice that reduces the next hit's damage (k3-caster): changes what ice is; Thunderhead gives the existing slow a job instead.
- "Gain shield equal to mana spent" (IDEAS.md): for casters it is Raise Shield again, which three reports call automatic.
- A boss that drains or locks mana: only asks something of casters, who are already the ones with answers.
- World 2 boss roster: `BossPreset` has no `world` field, so world 2 reuses the ten bosses at higher numbers. Once it has one, the cheapest distinct world 2 is each boss carrying a second trait from another (War Cry plus Miasma, Thunderhead plus shield). Not worked out here.
- Raise Shield changes: a balance fix, not content; the reports already cover it.

## Conflicts

- Proposals 5 and 11 are new mechanics (the limit of two). Proposal 8 needs a small new flag.
- Proposal 7 is three stat trades. DESIGN.md prefers a new angle over numbers; they are here because levels 2 to 6 have nothing else, and I rate them the weakest set.
- Proposals 5 and 6 make the player harder to kill. Both are built to answer one hit and fail against sustained damage, and both cost mana or a talent fork, but they should be playtested against Frost Queen and Storm Titan specifically for whether those bosses still feel earned.
- Disarm (6 points), Exhaustion (5) and Fortitude (3) are existing answers to burst that no tester could afford. Lowering their unlock costs is a tuning change that may close part of gap 2 with no new content; worth trying before proposal 5.
- Code readings checked after writing, which settle the "confirm" notes above:
  - Defense applies to every hit, magic included, through `mitigateDamage` (`damage * damage / (damage + defense)`), so it is soft and never reduces a hit to nothing. Brittle Armor (6) does answer magic burst. Ironclad (4) slows weak hits a lot but does not stop them: a 10-damage hit into 12 defense deals 5, a 60-damage hit deals 50. It asks something of magic builds too.
  - The fragile check in `useCheckAuraEffects.ts` runs on any combatant, so an enemy's War Cry should drop on health loss (2).
  - `applyStatus` targets the holder's first opponent, so Miasma poisons the player (3).
  - `onKill` is called from the death handling in `enemyActions.ts`, not from inside the attack, so Follow Through (12) should chain one kill per pass rather than recursing. Not run.
- Still unverified: the base speed of about 100 behind the slow arithmetic in proposal 1 (inferred from the comment that 7 ice stacks add about 2 seconds to a cast), and whether trigger-cast auras on enemies need mana.
