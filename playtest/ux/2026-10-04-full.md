# UX review: full (2026-10-04)

## Verdict
The fight screen reads well for a player who already knows the game: bars, cast timers and spell cards are clear and the layout is calm. It fails the player the design cares most about, the one who just died. A death in world 2 put the character back on a world 1 Goblin with no message, no cause and no trace in the log, and a new furthest wave changes one small grey number. Today's rule changes are half shown: tooltips state the new formulas, but the equipped physical spell cards compute damage with the old one. The single change that would help most is a death and run summary (what killed you, how far you got, whether it was a best), because it is the moment the game is built around and the screen says nothing.

## Answers to the focus questions
- **Level-locked talents: no.** A level-locked tile looks exactly like a tier-locked one (20% opacity, `TalentPanel.tsx:110-131`, one `isTierLocked` flag for both). The only hint is that the description text starts "Needs level 8." (seen on Widened Focus). There is no "you are level 6, 2 to go", and the tier header only speaks of points. The tooltip also omits cost and current rank.
- **Spell damage: partly, and one card is wrong.** The picker tooltip shows the formula ("Damage: 10 + 220% attack", seen on Execute) but never the result. The equipped card shows a result but ignores the share of attack: `SpellCard.tsx:54` uses `spell.damage.total + floor(caster.attack.total)` while the game uses `attack * attackScale` (`attackActions.ts:74`). On screen Poison Stab read 46 with attack 45, the full attack added. Every non-100% spell (Quick Strike, Slash, Bludgeon, Execute, Poison Stab) shows a wrong number. Magic cards are right (Fireball 44 = 2 x 22).
- **Status damage: no.** Enemies show chips ("Poison x17", "Fire x7") and that is all. Status ticks create no floating number and no log entry (`logDamage` is only called from `attackActions.ts`), so the health bar drops with no visible cause. Nothing says a stack deals 1 per second or fades, nothing shows a shield soaking it, and the gear bonus appears only as unlabelled "Poison: 1 / Bleed: 1" rows on every spell card.
- **Boss traits: only by accident.** A boss differs from a normal enemy by an orange instead of red 1px border (`EnemyCard.tsx:20`). Aura traits (Lifesteal, Fortitude, Curse) show as an ordinary spell card in the boss's list, as seen on a wave 9 Abyssal Horror; shield shows as a bar; health regeneration shows nowhere except the Stats modal. Nothing is labelled as a trait and there is no "Boss" label. Not seen on a live boss (see Not reviewed).
- **Death and new bests: no.** Seen in the browser: the world 2 character died and the next thing on screen was world 1, enemy 1/10, a Goblin. The only death feedback is a 4 second "Respawning in Ns" box (`CombatantCard.tsx`). No killer, no last hits, no "reached world 2 wave 5". A new furthest wave updates the grey "Highest Wave" number (`progressionActions.ts:35`) with no event. A boss kill gets the same floating "killed" text as a Goblin.
- **Costs: shown as plain stats, not as costs.** Raise Shield's description says it slows casts and the tooltip would list "Speed: -1" in the same yellow as a bonus. Talent tooltips list negatives through `EffectsDisplay` with no sign colour; Magical Specialist would show "Damage: =0" and "Health: -25%" with no description text, and Physical Specialist's mana cost shows as "Mana Cost: +0". Adrenaline Surge, seen, has no description at all. Read in code apart from Adrenaline Surge.
- **Numbers: no.** Anything from 1000 up is scientific notation (`utils/format.ts`): "2.3e3 / 2.6e3" experience, "767 / 1.1e3" health, "Mana Cost 2.1e3", "1.6e4 / 2e4". Two significant figures hide progress (2.3e3 covers 2250 to 2349) and mixing "767 / 1.1e3" is hard to compare. Fractions leak too: "XP Reward 5.96 x 3 = 18", "Shield Regen +0.5".

## What I looked at
- Browser, 1440x900, dev server on 5199: a fresh character; `n5-late` (level 10, wave 9, 68 items) with the Spells, Items, Talents and Logs tabs, two spell tooltips and four talent tooltips; `i2-world2` (level 13, world 2) with the Stats modal, through a death and reset.
- Read in code only: boss data and traits, death handling, log and animation sources, the three tooltip components, number formatting, costs on Raise Shield and the Specialist talents.
- No console errors. No runs created, none modified.

## First five minutes
- The first screen is a card called "Character" fighting a Goblin. The spell cards and cast bars explain themselves quickly. This is the strongest part.
- Cast bars read "7 / 60" with no unit. A new player cannot tell it is tenths of a second, and the tooltip's "Time: 75" has the same problem.
- "Points: 0" sits in the Spells tab and separately in the Talents tab. Nothing on the main screen says a point is waiting, so a player on the Items or Logs tab never learns they levelled.
- Spell slots are implied by "Equipped (2):" with no count of how many slots exist or how to get more.
- The "Progress to Next World" bar shows the world number out of 10 (`ProgressionBar.tsx:57-66`), so it reads 1/10 for the whole of world 1. It looks like a progress bar and is not one.
- First death: a red box counts down, then wave 1. A new player will not know they were sent back on purpose.

## Mid-run decisions
- **Talents.** The grid of emoji tiles carries no names; every choice needs a hover. Tooltips open to the left over the enemy cards. Good descriptions exist ("Does nothing without bleed and crit" on Deep Wounds) but several talents have none. Unaffordable, tier-locked and level-locked are three states shown as two opacities. "1 points to unlock" needs a plural fix. Max ranks of "/200" on a tile with 1 point spent look broken rather than aspirational.
- **Spells.** The picker is icons only, split by Auras, Physical, Magic. Unlock cost is not on the tile. The tooltip gives formula, time, mana, crit and status stacks but not the damage against the player's own attack or mana cost, and not damage per second, so Execute against Strike takes arithmetic.
- **Items.** 68 items appear as a 6-wide grid of icons with "Lv 7" and a rarity colour. There is no sort, filter, compare-to-equipped, or sell or clear. Item level dominates rarity in this game (a world 2 common beats a world 1 legendary), yet colour is the loudest signal on the tile. Hover content not reviewed.
- **Stats modal.** Clear and well grouped. It mixes "5.1e3" with plain numbers and shows "Speed -1" with no explanation of what speed does to cast time.

## Combat and death
- Direct hits are readable: a floating "-33" with the spell icon and CRIT. With three enemies and fast attacks the numbers stack at one point and vanish in 0.8s.
- The log shows each hit as Spell Damage, Defense, Final Damage, which explains mitigation well. It omits status ticks, aura casts, shield absorbed and the player's death. Each entry is about 200px tall, so four hits fill the panel. Timestamps are wall-clock ("08:00:00 PM"), meaningless in an idle game.
- Enemy cards spend their top block on "XP Reward 5.96 x 3 = 18" and "Drop Rate +9%", the least urgent facts, above health.
- Enemies with no mana show an empty "0 / 0" mana bar.
- Auras on the player list their effects twice: in the spell card and again as an active aura block below.

## At a glance
- Alive and health: yes, in under a second. Progress: the top-left numbers work, but current and highest are the same size and the highest is greyed, so "is this a new best?" needs reading and comparing six numbers.
- "Highest Enemy 2/10, Highest Wave 6/10, Highest World 2" is one fact split into three boxes.
- Coming back after a death there is no story: no death count, no "last run reached", no time to return to the wall. Rampage-style fast re-clears are the design's growth signal and nothing times them.
- Nothing flags a waiting decision (unspent points, a new rare drop) outside its own tab.

## Faults
- **Wrong damage on physical spell cards.** `SpellCard.tsx:54` ignores `attackScale`. Load `n5-late`: Poison Stab shows 46 with attack 45.
- **"Poison x0" chip.** Seen on Abyssal Horror in `n5-late`: a status at zero stacks is still drawn (`StatusEffectDisplay.tsx` does not filter).
- **Cast bar over its maximum.** Battle Cry read "228 / 79" in `n5-late` page text, likely an aura waiting on mana; the bar gives no reason.
- **World progress bar tracks the world number**, not waves (`ProgressionBar.tsx:57-66`).
- **Possible: Debug button did nothing.** One click after loading `i2-world2` highlighted the button but no panel appeared in the next two screenshots. Not retried; treat as unconfirmed.
- **Possible: stale spell data in old saves.** `i2-world2` showed Raise Shield as "+4, +5%, +0.5 regen" with no speed line, against current data of 4%, 1 regen and -1 speed. Saved spells may not pick up data changes.

## Recommendations
| # | Change | Where | Size | Player gains |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Death and run summary: killer, spell, last five hits taken including status, furthest point this run, best or not | new component fed from `characterActions.ts` death and `logs` | component | Deaths teach; the core loop gets its payoff |
| 2 | Fix physical card damage to use `attackScale`; show computed damage in picker tooltips | `SpellCard.tsx:54`, `SpellTooltip.tsx` | detail | Numbers can be trusted and compared |
| 3 | Log and float status ticks, coloured by status, with shield absorbed | `statusActions.ts` to `logActions.ts`, `CombatLogAnimation.tsx` | component | Status builds see their damage; unexplained health loss ends |
| 4 | Boss header: "BOSS" label, trait line ("Regenerates 3 health/s", "Shield 40"), thicker border | `EnemyCard.tsx`, `bossData.ts` | component | Traits become something to plan for |
| 5 | Lock reasons on talent tiles and tooltip: "Needs level 14 (you are 10)", "Needs 1 more point in earlier tiers", cost and rank in tooltip | `TalentPanel.tsx` | detail | Always something visible to work toward |
| 6 | Replace scientific notation with 1,234 up to 99,999 then 12.3k, 4.56M | `utils/format.ts` | detail | Every large number readable |
| 7 | New-best event: banner and highlight on a new furthest wave or first boss kill | `progressionActions.ts:35`, `ProgressionBar.tsx` | component | Earned moments feel earned |
| 8 | Show costs as costs: red for negative effects, a "Cost:" line, descriptions on every talent | `EffectsDisplay.tsx`, `SpellTooltip.tsx`, talent data | detail | Tradeoffs seen before committing |
| 9 | Compact one-line log entries, game-time stamps, death and aura entries | `LogPanel.tsx` | component | The log becomes the story of a run |
| 10 | Inventory compare, sort and bulk clear | `ItemPanel.tsx` | structural | 100+ item inventories become usable |
| 11 | Unspent-point and new-drop badges on the sidebar tabs | `Sidebar.tsx` | detail | Waiting decisions seen at a glance |

**Structural: run header.** Replace the six-number progress strip with one line, "World 1, Wave 9, Enemy 4/10", a real wave progress bar, and beside it "Best: World 1 Wave 9" that lights up when equalled or passed, plus deaths and time this run. It replaces `ProgressionBar.tsx` only; about half a day. It carries recommendations 1 and 7.

**Structural: inventory.** Split the Items tab into equipped slots and a sortable list (slot, level, rarity), with hover comparing against the equipped piece as green and red stat differences, and a "discard all below level N" control. A day or more; worth it only once the death summary and status visibility are done, since those block understanding and this blocks convenience.

## Quick wins
- `SpellCard.tsx:54`: multiply attack by `spell.attackScale ?? 1`.
- Filter zero-stack statuses in `StatusEffectDisplay.tsx`.
- "1 points to unlock" plural; hide the mana bar when max mana is 0.
- Label cast time in seconds ("7.5s") on cards and tooltips.
- Add "BOSS" text beside the name when `enemy.isBoss`.
- Move XP Reward and Drop Rate below the bars or into the Stats modal.
- Name the player card something other than "Character"; the Stats modal already says "Player".
- Add descriptions to Adrenaline Surge and both Specialist talents.
- Open talent tooltips inside the sidebar rather than over the enemies.

## Keep
- Spell cards with live cast bars on both sides: the fight is legible at a glance.
- The cast bar turning cyan when the caster is iced.
- Damage log's Spell Damage, Defense, Final Damage breakdown.
- Trigger lines in tooltips ("On Crit -> Multiply Bleed 2") and descriptions that name what a talent needs.
- The grouped Stats modal, including its Triggers section.
- Rarity colours on item tiles and the paper-doll layout of equipped slots.

## Not reviewed
- A live boss fight and its traits on screen: the Debug panel did not open and time ran out; boss findings come from `EnemyCard.tsx` and `bossData.ts`.
- Item hover and equip flow, save panel, pause, friends, tier 3 and 4 talent tooltips, the Specialist and Raise Shield tooltips (code only).
- Narrow window: the resize did not change the viewport. From `App.tsx` the sidebar is a fixed 360px and the main column has no breakpoints, so expect cramped cards below about 1100px.
- `k3-phys` and `m4-poison` were not loaded.
