# UX review: Items panel (Inventory / Shop / Smith) and spell/talent unlock clarity, 2026-10-18

## Verdict

The new Items panel fixes the original complaint. A player can now compare, equip, sell, bulk-sell, buy and smith, and every action is one or two clicks. The spell "3 pt" badges and the talent blocker lines answer "why can't I take this?" on hover. Two things still mislead mid-game players. First, the **"Lv N" badge on every item is the wave it dropped on, not its power**: a world-2 "Lv 1" item is about 18x stronger than a world-1 "Lv 1", so players sort, smith and judge by the wrong number. Second, the comparison is a long list of stat deltas with no bottom line, so "is this better?" still takes arithmetic. The change that would help most is to replace the "Lv" badge with a power that can be compared (world-wave, or a single item-power number taken from `item.scale`) and to add a one-line verdict to every comparison.

**Focus answers.**
- *Item vs what I wear:* the per-slot delta lists are correct and fully visible, but they have no summary. Units are unclear for crit multiplier ("+17" means +0.17x), and "Lv" misleads (see above).
- *How to sell:* clear. There is a Sell button on the selected item and "Sell all: Common (31)", and the second click asks "Sell 31 For 386g?".
- *What gold is for:* only the Shop tab says so. The Inventory tab shows gold but never mentions the shop.
- *What the smith gives:* the text says "a random item of the next [rarity], as strong as the average of the four", which matches the code (`shopActions.ts:166-170`). But the panel never shows what that average is, and "Pick weakest 4" picks items whose badges look strongest. The result card has no comparison against what is equipped.
- *Why a spell or talent can't be taken:* spells are good. Talent tooltips are good. But the tier header "2 points to unlock" reads as "you need 2 points", which is exactly the player's complaint, and a level-locked talent looks the same as a tier-locked one until you hover it. Equipping a spell when the slots are full **silently replaces the last-equipped spell**.
- *Rules check:* uniques are priced at 1x sell value (Miser's Ring 4.4K = 150 x 29.1) and rolled wares at 3x (epic Arcane Staff 2.6K = 30 x 29.1 x 3). Restock runs on 1 s ticks of play (`slowTick`), so the "4:42" clock is correct. Kill gold is wired in (`enemyActions.ts:236`). I found no wrong prices.

## What I looked at

- **In the browser** (my own tab, port 5199): `i1-status` (level 12, world 2 wave 3 best, 56 items, 2.9K gold, 6 wares) at 1512x805, and `i2-fresh` (level 10, 3 spell points, 1 talent point, world 2) at 390x780. The window would not resize, so I rendered the game in a 390px same-origin iframe for the phone view; tooltips there were triggered with a mouse, not touch.
- **Done:** hovered and selected items, equipped into Weapon 2, clicked an equipped slot, bulk-sold commons (confirm flow), bought an epic, smithed 4 uncommons via "Pick weakest 4", hovered locked and unlocked spells, unlocked and equipped an aura, equipped with full slots, hovered tier-, level- and point-blocked talents. I checked item `scale` and `level` in the save. No console errors appeared while switching every tab.
- **Read in code only:** touch behaviour of `Tooltip.tsx` (first tap shows, second tap acts), the Restock-now and Hold flows (I saw the buttons but did not use them), and merchant-only item data (`shopItemData.ts`).

## First five minutes

The Items tab is not needed early, and it handles that well: the empty slots are labelled, and the "click one to compare, equip or sell" hint is right where the grid is. What a new player would get wrong:
- **Clicking an equipped item takes it off** (`ItemPanel.tsx:38`), and nothing on the slot or its tooltip says so. On desktop a player who clicks to "look at" their helmet loses it to the bottom of the bag. On touch the first tap shows the tooltip, but it still does not say that the next tap unequips.
- **Spell points:** "You have 0. You get 1 per level." is right, but at level 12 with auras costing 2-6 points, a player is told nothing about how long that is (levels double their XP cost, `characterActions.ts:32`). Add "next point at level 13 (310 XP)".

## Mid-run decisions

**Items: what works.** Hover shows the item and a "vs Slot: Name" delta for every slot it fits. Clicking pins the same view with Equip-per-slot and Sell buttons. Ring and weapon items show both slots side by side. Arrows and colours are consistent, and "Loses X's triggers" is a nice touch.

**Items: problems, most important first.**
1. **"Lv" is not power.** In `i1-status` the equipped "Lv 1" rare Heavy Boots (scale 18.6) beat an inventory "Lv 10" epic Heavy Boots (scale 7.5) on Health by 53. Two "Lv 1" uncommons in the same bag have scale 1.0 and 18.6. The badge is drawn on every icon (`ItemCard.tsx:62`, `ItemPanel.tsx:46`), in the tooltip title and in the shop. A player who keeps "high level" items and sells "Lv 1-2" ones throws away their best gear. Show world and wave ("W2-1") or a power number rounded from `scale`, and colour it against the player's current best.
2. **No bottom line.** The Arcane Staff vs Greatsword comparison has 11 rows: +450 mana and +17 mana regen against -111 damage and -21% crit multiplier. The game already computes final stats. A line such as "Strike hit 427 → 177, crit 988 → 446, Max mana 284 → 734" would answer the question. I equipped it and Strike dropped from 427 to 177, which the list did not make obvious.
3. **No upgrade marker in the grid.** With 56 items the player has to hover each one. A small green ▲ on icons that beat the equipped item on the stat that piece is mainly for (or on the summary above) would turn the grid into a to-do list.
4. **Row order is arbitrary and units are mixed.** Gains and losses are interleaved by map order. Flat crit multiplier shows as "+17" (it is +0.17x, `effectActionMap.ts:22-26`) while the character card says "2.52x". Percentage rows wrap badly at 360px ("Health Leech" / "%" on two lines, with the arrow split from the value). The same stat is "Damage: -12%" in the item block and "Damage %" in the comparison, and "+1769" in one and "+1.8K" in the other. Sort the rows by sign, show multipliers as "x", and write percent stats as "+11% Damage".
5. **The selected panel pushes the grid down.** On desktop, choosing an item lower in the bag moves the grid, so the item you just clicked scrolls away and the cursor lands on another item, whose tooltip pops up. Pin the panel (sticky) or show it in place of the equipment doll.
6. **Bulk sell does not protect upgrades.** "Sell all: Common" sold all 31 commons, including world-2 commons that outclass world-1 gear. Exclude items that would be an upgrade, or say "(2 upgrades kept)".

**Shop.** Prices, Hold and the timer are clear, and "Merchant only" is labelled. Gaps:
- **Merchant uniques are built to be "weak alone, strong with X"** (comment in `shopItemData.ts:3-4`), but nothing tells the player what X is. The template has no `description`. Against a normal ring the Miser's Ring shows seven red rows, so nobody will buy it. A playtester said the same: they did not trust the Chalice or Martyr's Plate without Toxic Blood or a mana build, and they only knew those partners from outside the UI. Add a one-line description ("Pairs with Toxic Blood") as talents have.
- An unaffordable Buy button is only greyed out. Say "Need 1.1K more".
- The shop comparison says "vs Ring 1" without the equipped item's name, while the inventory tooltip includes it. Use the same format in both.
- After a purchase the card vanishes and the list shifts, with no "Added to inventory" feedback.
- At desktop width the wares are in a single column (`lg:grid-cols-1`) of roughly 450px cards. Six wares take three or four screens of scrolling. Collapse the comparison to the summary line and expand it on demand.

**Smith.** Choosing a rarity, the four dashed slots, "→ Rare" and the forged card are clear. Problems:
- "Pick weakest 4" chose Lv 6, 1, 2, 3 and left several "Lv 2" items behind. That is correct by scale, but it contradicts the badges (problem 1 again).
- There is no preview of the outcome. Show "Result: random rare, power ≈ average of inputs (W1-3)" and "Sell value of these 4: 32g" so a player can choose between smithing and selling. My smith turned four world-1 uncommons into a rare with +3 defense and +18 mana, which was worthless for this character, and I could not have known before clicking.
- The forged-result card has no "vs Body" comparison and no Equip button.

**Spells.** The blue badges for affordable spells and the dark ones for unaffordable spells read well at a glance. The unlock tooltip is clear. **Fault-level:** with every slot full, clicking an unlocked spell equips it by popping the last spell (`spellActions.ts:24-28`). In `i2-fresh` Health Regeneration silently replaced the Shield aura I had just equipped, with no warning in the tooltip. Either refuse with "Slots full: click an equipped spell to remove it", or say "Replaces Shield" in the tooltip. The tooltip of an unlocked spell does not say "click to equip" either.

**Talents.** The blocker lines say the right thing in the right order (tier, then level, then cost). Problems:
- The tier header "2 points to unlock" (`TalentPanel.tsx:116`), seen with "Points: 1" showing, reads as "you need 2 points to take this". It should say "Spend 2 more points in tiers 1-2 to open".
- Tier-locked and level-locked tiles both use `opacity-20`, so you cannot tell them apart without hovering, and that opacity makes "0/1 Cost: 2" nearly unreadable. Put a "Lv 14" corner badge on level-locked tiles (as the spell slot shows "Lvl 15") and a lock icon on tier-locked ones.
- When you have points, an affordable talent and an unaffordable one look the same (`opacity-50`). Reuse the spells' blue cost-badge idea.

## Combat and death

Out of scope this time. One thing: on the phone layout I saw the combat cards briefly drawn over the Running/Pause/Debug bar on first paint and when switching tabs, twice. It settled within about a second, and hit-testing found the Pause button on top. I could not pin down the cause.

## At a glance

Gold sits at the top of all three sub-tabs, which is good. But "2.9Kg" reads as "2.9 kilograms". Use a coin icon or a space ("2.9K g"). Nothing outside the Items tab says that something there needs a decision, such as an affordable held ware, a restocked shop or an upgrade in the bag. Show a dot on the Items tab when one of those is true.

## Faults

1. **Equipping a spell into full slots silently replaces the last-equipped spell.** To see it: fill all slots, then click any unlocked, unequipped spell (`spellActions.ts:24`).
2. **Clicking an equipped item unequips it with no hint**, so a player cannot inspect their gear by clicking. To see it: click the helmet slot in Inventory (`ItemPanel.tsx:38`).
3. **"Sell 31 For 386g?"**: the `capitalize` class on the bulk-sell button capitalises "For" (`ItemPanel.tsx:146`).
4. **"Lv" badge misrepresents power across worlds**: the same "Lv 1" appears on items with scale 1.0 and 18.6. To see it: load `i1-status` and compare the two "Lv 1" uncommons.
5. **Comparison rows wrap at sidebar width**: "Health Leech %" splits from its arrow (`ItemCard.tsx:150`, needs `whitespace-nowrap`).
6. **Purchases and sales are not saved until the 5-minute autosave** (`useSaveSystem.ts:6`). A reload after shopping loses the purchase and the gold change. Save after buy, sell, smith and unlock.

## Recommendations

| # | Change | Where | Size | Player gains |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Replace "Lv N" with world-wave or an item-power number from `scale`, everywhere items are drawn | `ItemCard.tsx` (ItemIconBox, ItemDetails), `ItemPanel.tsx` EquipmentSlot | detail | Sorting, selling and smithing by the number on screen become correct |
| 2 | Summary line atop each comparison: change to each equipped spell's hit and crit, max health and max mana | `ItemCard.tsx` ItemComparison (needs a "simulate equip" stat helper) | component | "Is it better?" answered without arithmetic |
| 3 | Spell equip with full slots: refuse with a message, or name the spell it replaces in the tooltip | `spellActions.ts:24`, `SpellItem.tsx` | detail | No silent loss of an aura |
| 4 | Tier header "Spend N more in tiers below"; "Lv 14" badge and lock icon on tiles; cost badge coloured by affordability | `TalentPanel.tsx:113-119, 145-165` | detail | Fixes the exact "I had a point" complaint |
| 5 | Description line on merchant uniques naming their partner | `types/item.ts`, `shopItemData.ts`, `ItemDetails` | detail | Shop uniques become a build choice instead of a trap |
| 6 | Upgrade ▲ marker on inventory and shop icons; bulk sell skips upgrades | `ItemPanel.tsx` grid and BulkSell | component | Inventory becomes scannable; no regret sells |
| 7 | Smith preview (result power, sell value of inputs) plus comparison and Equip on the result | `SmithPanel.tsx` | component | Smith-or-sell becomes an informed choice |
| 8 | Sort comparison rows (gains, then losses), show crit multiplier as x, use one label per stat | `ItemCard.tsx:118-150`, `EffectsDisplay.tsx:74` | detail | Faster reading, fewer misreads |
| 9 | Sticky selected-item panel, or show it in place of the equipment doll | `ItemPanel.tsx` InventoryView | component | Clicking does not move the grid |
| 10 | Items-tab dot when a held ware is affordable, an upgrade is in the bag or the shop restocks | `Sidebar.tsx` | component | The idle glance shows a decision is waiting |

Start with 1, 3 and 4. Each is under an hour and fixes something that actively misleads. Then do 2, which is the largest gain.

**No structural change is needed.** The sub-tab layout of Inventory, Shop and Smith is right. The only near-structural idea is #9, which costs about half a day: in the Inventory tab, swap the equipment doll for the selected item's panel while an item is selected (the doll comes back on close). That keeps the grid fixed and puts the comparison next to the item you clicked.

## Quick wins

- `ItemPanel.tsx:146`: remove `capitalize` from the confirm state ("Sell 31 for 386g?").
- Gold: "2.9K g", or a coin icon before the number (`ItemCard.tsx` GoldAmount).
- Equipment slot tooltip footer: "Click to unequip" (touch: "Tap again to unequip").
- Unlocked spell tooltip: "Click to equip" / "Click to remove".
- Spell unlock line: "Next point at level 13" instead of "You get 1 per level."
- Unaffordable Buy: "Need 1.1K more".
- Shop "vs Ring 1" should include the equipped item's name, like the inventory.
- `whitespace-nowrap` on comparison labels and values.
- Crit multiplier deltas shown as "+0.17x".
- Inventory hint: "Sell for gold, spend it in the Shop."

## Keep

- Hover comparison against every slot the item fits, in both the hover and the pinned view.
- The two-click bulk sell that shows count and total, and resets on blur. It needs no browser dialog.
- The Shop's single explanatory paragraph, the Hold toggle with its tooltip, and the "Merchant only" tag.
- The Smith's four dashed slots, "→ Rare / → Rare boots" result label and forged card.
- Spell "N pt" badges coloured by affordability, and the "Lvl 15" locked-slot box.
- Talent blocker text and its priority order. The touch tooltip rule (first tap reads, second tap acts) is the right model for phone.
- The phone layout of all three sub-tabs fits at 390px with no horizontal overflow.

## Not reviewed

- Real touch input. I used a mouse in a 390px iframe, so the first-tap tooltip behaviour was read in code only.
- Restock now and Hold carrying a ware across a restock. I did not wait 10 minutes or spend 1.5K.
- Smithing legendaries into uniques, and a sold-out shop.
- An early-game save (level 1-4) in the Items tab. Both runs were mid or late game.
