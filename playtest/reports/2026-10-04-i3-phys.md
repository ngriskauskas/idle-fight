# Playtest i3-phys (2026-10-04)

## Run
Seed 77, 60m00s game time, 13 `run` calls. Level 9, highest world 1 wave 6 (standing at wave 6 enemy 10 at the end, Frost Queen just killed). 502 kills (8.4/min), 17 deaths. World 2 not reached.

## Build
- Spells at end: Strike, Rend, Slash, Health Regeneration. War Cry spell used 17m-35m and 35m-53m; Arcane Missile tried 30m-35m.
- Talents (8): Warrior Training 4, Iron Constitution 1, Bloodlust (17m), Deep Wounds (30m), Physical Specialist (53m).
- Gear at end: Greatsword + Warhammer, Plate Armor (onHit speed boost), Iron Helm, epic Heavy Boots, epic Amulet of Shield, Ring of Vitality, Ring of Elements.
- Started with a Shield in one hand for defense; swapped it for the Greatsword at 43m, which doubled damage and doubled deaths.

## How it felt
- 0-10m: brisk. Waves 1-4, 3.2 -> 8.7 kills/min, 1 death (poison at wave 1 enemy 3 with 40 health). Rend at 5m took kills from 4.8 to 8.7/min.
- 10-20m: first wall, wave 4. 8.1 kills/min, 2 deaths, no new wave in 7 minutes.
- 20-30m: still wave 4. Bloodlust added: 6.7 kills/min, 2 deaths. Warhammer (+13 damage): 9.7 kills/min, 2 deaths.
- 30-43m: waves 5 and 6 reached, but slowest part of the run: 6.4 then 4.9 kills/min, 3 deaths. An enemy Curse sat on me 82-90% of the time (speed -26).
- 43-53m: Greatsword. 8.9 kills/min, 4 deaths, bounced between waves 4 and 6.
- 53-60m: Physical Specialist + Slash. 17.6 kills/min, 3 deaths, 4 boss kills in 7 minutes. The only stretch that felt like a spike.
- Hits to kill a typical enemy stayed flat all run: wave 1: 2-3, wave 2: 2.1, wave 4: 2.1-2.6, waves 4-6: 2.6 with shield, 1.8 with Greatsword, 2.3 at the end (fast Slash hits).
- Wall: yes, waves 4 to 6, from 10m to 53m. With the one-wave death rule it is a loop, not a reset: die at wave 5 or 6, redo the wave 4 boss (Orc Warlord killed 6 times, Shadow Assassin 6), climb back. Never hopeless, never dramatic.
- Deaths: 14 of 17 were single enemy spells, mostly magic casters: Frost Wolf Frostbolt 172 and Ice Spike 90 against 91 health, Storm Elemental Chain Lightning 102 and 52, Necromancer Ice Spike 68 against 65 health. Those are explainable: one-shots.

## Fun
- Physical Specialist at 53m: Strike went from 112 to 168 per hit and kills doubled. A real "build clicked" moment.
- Warhammer and Greatsword drops visibly changed the run (Strike 31 -> 61 -> 112 per hit).
- Deep Wounds was measurable: bleed per Rend hit 26 -> 41.
- The death rule: losing one wave stings without wiping the run.

## Not fun
- 43 minutes at waves 4-6 with nothing to spend: levels 8 and 9 took 13 and 22 minutes, one talent point each.
- 70 item drops, about two thirds mana gear useless to this build; inventory ended at 61.
- Enemy Curse (150s, speed -26) cut my attack rate by a third for whole stretches and I had no answer to it.
- Defense did nothing against what killed me; all the killers were spells that hit above my health.

## Too strong
- Physical Specialist: +100% damage and kills 8.9 -> 17.6/min. The cost (no mana regen) is real only if you run auras.
- Slash for 1 point: 42% of direct damage in its stretch, more than Strike or Rend.
- Not Bloodlust. War Cry was up 77-89% and hit 15 stacks, but kills/min did not move when I took it (8.1 -> 6.7) and Strike per hit only went 27 -> 31. Stacks drop on every hit taken. Not a runaway.

## Too weak
- Bleed as a damage source: 33-37% of direct damage early, 12% with Greatsword, 3% (1383 vs 44328) at the end. Enemies die in 2 hits, so the bleed never ticks out. Rend's attack scaling is invisible.
- War Cry spell alongside Bloodlust: uptime 77% without it, 80-89% with it, and its mana cost dropped Regeneration from 94% to 32% uptime.
- Arcane Missile on a physical build: 12 per hit against Strike's 57 (9% of damage for a full slot).
- Gear status stats on every attack: fire, lightning and poison together were about 5% of damage.

## Bugs or oddities
- Killing blows that do not explain the death: "Giant Bat Slash for 9", "Dark Mage Lightning Bolt for 23", "Zombie Poison Stab for 18". Poison was my largest damage source (2735 status vs any single enemy at most 1293) but is credited with one death.
- `status` attack includes live War Cry stacks (20 -> 76 after equipping a +13 weapon), so gear comparisons are unreliable.
- After Physical Specialist, mana regen is 0: Health Regeneration fell to 15% uptime and "too low to cast" read 89%. The slot is dead but nothing says so.
- "Curse on you" reached 100% of a stretch; "Arcane Boost on enemies" shows up as an enemy buff. Level 9 gave no fifth spell slot.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Cap or telegraph enemy spell burst at waves 4-6 (Frostbolt 172 at wave 5 is 1.9x my health). Deaths should come from attrition I can build against.
2. Make bleed matter on short fights: carry leftover bleed to the next enemy, or front-load the first tick. Otherwise it is a boss-only stat.
3. Give Bloodlust a floor (lose half the stacks on damage, not all) so it is worth a point; right now it changes nothing measurable.
4. Shorten enemy Curse (150s) or let kills clear it. A third of my attack speed gone for minutes is a slow grind with no counterplay.
5. Killing-blow line should add status damage taken in the last 5 seconds, so poison deaths read as poison.
