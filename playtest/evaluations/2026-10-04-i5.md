# Evaluation i5 (2026-10-04)

## Verdict

The corrected source structurally bounds SpeedBoost to one accepted activation per combatant per 10 fast ticks, and the valid post-fix evidence supports that the runaway wave-deletion loop is gone. The behavior is not fully confirmed: the only controlled pair is one seed with no activation trace, and the fresh status run did not isolate the trigger. i5 is hard enough in the tested status build, but progression still taxes early level choices and enemy variety fades between boss spikes. Stage 1 exit conditions do not hold yet: status exits wave 1 promptly with readable deaths, but meaningful choices at each level are not established, and the caster sample is too short to certify all builds.

## Evidence base

- Full fresh run: [2026-10-04-i5-status.md](../reports/2026-10-04-i5-status.md), seed 817, 24m54s, status/hybrid build. Used for current death rate, wave-best intervals, enemies, and progression.
- Partial run: [2026-10-04-i5-caster.md](../reports/2026-10-04-i5-caster.md), seed 816, 3m. Weak evidence only: no death, boss kill, or new wave; it cannot establish caster balance or Stage 1 completion.
- Controlled check: [2026-10-04-i5-speedboost.md](../qa/2026-10-04-i5-speedboost.md), one matched 120-second pair, seed 4816. Quick Strike was 56 casts with Plate's trigger vs 49 without (+14.3%); one kill and no deaths in either run. Exact activations and expiry boundary were not observed.
- Comparison: [2026-10-04-r3-new.md](../reports/2026-10-04-r3-new.md) and [2026-10-04-r3-caster.md](../reports/2026-10-04-r3-caster.md). These predate the corrected cooldown and are useful as baseline, not post-fix evidence. r3-new's 4.06 Quick Strike hits/s and waves 7-9 falling in 95 seconds are the runaway contrast.
- Excluded: no i5-fast report exists. The i4-fast report is not post-fix evidence because the cooldown was missing from source during that run. No dedicated post-fix fast-build run, second QA seed, activation trace, or broad archetype coverage exists.

## Difficulty

- **i5 status:** 4 deaths in 24m54s = 9.6 deaths/hour. Fresh bests: wave 2 at 3:59; wave 3 at 6:18 (+2:19); wave 4 at 11:00 (+4:42); wave 5 at 12:29 (+1:29); wave 6 at 20:44 (+8:15). Deaths reset progress. Deaths were attributable to poison at wave 1 enemy 7, Orc Warlord Lightning Bolt (22), Inferno Lord Fireball (29), and Frost Queen Frostbolt (66); the last three were boss encounters. Wave 6 was the lone furthest-wave best after an 8m15 climb, not a chain of free new waves. The run had a brisk 55-kill stretch, but did not reproduce r3's rapid wave 7-9 deletion.
- **i5 caster:** 0 deaths in 3m, still wave 1. A rate or best-wave interval is not meaningful at this duration; Goblin King was reached but not confirmed killed. Do not read this as a safe caster build.
- **r3-new, pre-fix comparison:** 3 deaths in 25m = 7.2/hour; reached waves 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 at 3:01, 5:51 (+2:50), 12:30 (+6:39), 14:45 (+2:15), 15:58 (+1:13), 21:48 (+5:50), 22:29 (+0:41), 23:22 (+0:53). The Plate/Quick Strike stretch killed three bosses and opened waves 7-9 in 95s without a fight, then Dragon Lord killed the build. Not earned after the trigger clicked.
- **r3-caster, pre-fix comparison:** 4 deaths in 24m57s = 9.6/hour, furthest wave 5, then a nine-minute wall; deaths were visible but boss spikes were abrupt. It is not evidence for corrected SpeedBoost behavior.

## Problems, ranked

1. **Early level choices are still taxed by the tier gate.** i5 status spent five ranks in Warrior Training before Epidemic, and the tester described them as mostly prerequisite tax; r3-new likewise called early levels blind or obvious. This conflicts with Builds (choices should not be linear) and Stage 1's per-level-choice exit condition. Confidence: high for this status path, not every build. Fix kind: talent rank costs, tier access, or early talent options.
2. **Regular encounters blur while boss damage supplies most of the readable pressure.** i5 status reports ordinary enemies becoming health/damage and repeats familiar bosses; the run's notable deaths were boss attacks, with Frost Queen's 66-damage Frostbolt ending its furthest push. r3-caster independently called early enemies indistinct once defense rose, while identifying Orc Warlord and Inferno Lord as meaningful checks. This partly misses Builds/Enemies' demand for distinct build responses; the abrupt Frost Queen spike may also undermine earned progress. Confidence: medium, based on two reports with different builds. Fix kind: enemy/boss spell and status numbers or mix, preserving lethal but legible boss checks.
3. **Caster mana choices remain unresolved for the current round.** i5-caster's aggregate first two minutes combine pre- and post-talent states; the final minute changes both talents and is dominated by the boss. Its starvation moves from 21% aggregate to 0%, but cannot attribute the cause. r3-caster found Mana Efficiency could erase starvation and Arcane Affinity added no sustained damage once starved; that is a warning, not a confirmed current-version result. Conflicts with real tradeoffs and distinct builds. Confidence: low for current i5. Fix kind: mana talent/spell costs and payoffs, after a clean caster comparison.

## What is working

- **SpeedBoost no longer has an unbounded feedback path in the corrected source.** [triggerActions.ts](../../src/logic/triggerActions.ts) rejects activation while the combatant cooldown is positive and sets it to 10; [tickActions.ts](../../src/logic/tickActions.ts) decrements it once per fast tick. The QA report identifies the fast tick as 100ms, so the shared lockout is about one second. The accepted boost still advances every equipped spell, but only once per gate interval; its one-tick-before-ready cap also prevents a same-pass free-cast chain.
- The QA pair measured a modest cast-rate increase (56 vs 49 Quick Strike casts in 120 seconds), not r3's runaway rates. The i5 status build wore the trigger for 6m49s and still took 8m15s from wave 5 to its wave 6 best, with four deaths across the run. Together these support bounded behavior, but do not isolate the trigger's contribution or prove exact cadence. Confidence: high in the source gate; moderate in the measured pair; medium overall for post-fix gameplay.
- The poison/bleed combination was meaningful without removing danger: 4,443 poison and 2,142 bleed damage against 26,526 direct damage, while the build still died four times. Epidemic and Deep Wounds created distinct status layers; protect that payoff.
- Death reset and power growth through gear/levels are consistent with DESIGN.md in the tested run. The epic Arcane Shield and later status talents visibly changed the build; no player stat scaling with wave was implicated.

## Against DESIGN.md

- **Pace:** Matched in the full status run: long 8m15 progress gap is acceptable and contained boss decisions; the brief fast-clear stretch did not erase new-wave difficulty.
- **Where power comes from:** Matched in observed progression; deaths reset to wave 1 and gear/talents account for growth. Source confirms `getScaleMulti` scales enemy stats, enemy statuses/triggers/auras, and drops; player statuses scale with player attack/mana power and player aura bonuses are unscaled.
- **Fun over balance:** The poison/bleed spike and trigger's faster-hit identity are worth keeping, bounded by the cooldown.
- **Builds:** Partial. Status play differed from the r3 speed build, but its five-rank gate is a linear tax. Caster differentiation is not established by a three-minute sample.
- **Synergy:** Poison Stab + Epidemic and Rend + Deep Wounds worked together as intended. The trigger + Quick Strike interaction remains recognizable without post-fix evidence of runaway.
- **Progression:** Partly matched: talents, spell unlocks, and gear offered goals, but five generic ranks delayed the first synergy and the available level choices did not feel consistently real.
- **Archetypes:** Poison/bleed have evidence of a working combination; other paths remain partial or untested (see below).
- **Checklist for new content:** No new content judged. The existing status pair combines mechanics and changes damage profile; early rank gating weakens the real-choice test.

- Poison: working in the status build; Epidemic spread payoff is plausible, but spread proc share is unmeasured.

## Enemies

Giant Rat poison felt like an initially opaque killer until its status death was identified. Orc Warlord Lightning Bolt, Inferno Lord Fireball/Inferno, and especially Frost Queen's Frostbolt/Ice Spike forced defensive progress; the run's ending Frostbolt was consequential, though the tester called the late bursts abrupt. The Goblin King, Bone Warden, Orc Warlord, and Shadow Assassin became familiar repeated checks; most regulars blurred once status damage and gear accelerated clears. r3-caster also singled out Orc Warlord and Inferno Lord as effective checks and found Bone Warden forgettable. Source supports different boss kits (e.g. Warlord's Rend/Lightning/Fortitude, Inferno Lord's Fireball/Inferno, Frost Queen's Ice Spike/Frostbolt), but reports say those differences often resolve as hit-size/defense checks rather than counters that change the build.

## Risks in the mechanics

- Exact SpeedBoost activations remain unobservable in harness output. `speedBoost` still advances all equipped spells by the trigger value whenever its shared gate opens; Plate Armor's onHit runs before Adrenaline Surge's onCrit, so Plate can consume the gate on a critical hit. The cap bounds frequency, but no post-fix report measures which trigger wins or how often boosts land.
- No runaway loop is apparent in the corrected source path: positive cooldown returns early, cooldown is decremented in fast ticks, and spell progress is capped one tick shy of ready. This is source-confirmed, not a full behavioral boundary test.

## Open questions

- Does a fresh fast-hit build with the corrected source retain the fun of the r3 trigger spike while remaining boss-gated? The absent i5-fast report leaves this central gameplay comparison to a future round; do not treat i4-fast as post-fix.
- Does the one-second gate yield the intended activation cadence across multiple seeds and with both Plate Armor and Adrenaline Surge equipped? Harness activation/timer telemetry is needed to answer directly.
- Does a clean caster progression make Mana Efficiency and Arcane Affinity competing choices, or does one dominate as in r3-caster? Separate before/after intervals at the same enemy and loadout.
- **Stage 1 exit:** Not met. The status build left wave 1 by 3:59 and its deaths had identifiable causes, but the per-level meaningful-choice condition failed for its early five-rank gate. The only i5 caster run stops at 3m, so the every-build requirements are also incomplete. Stay at Stage 1 next round; the current report set is insufficient to advance.
- Owner rule to note, not change here: death resets to world 1 wave 1. It is consistent with DESIGN.md, but it makes repeated boss laps a large share of a wall; i5 does not show that rule making progress impossible.
