# Playtest: magic-oct04 (caster build)

## Run

- Seed 202. Game time reached: **1h23m**, not the 3 hours budgeted.
- Level 10, highest wave: world 1 wave 4, 36 deaths, 1147 kills, 131 item drops.
- The run ended early for two reasons. From 1h22m the harness crashed on every `run` with `Error: engine did not settle after 500 passes` (details under Bugs), and I spent the remaining session bisecting it. Then the coordinator told me to stop. Everything below is from the 1h22m of real play plus the crash bisection.
- The final build on disk (Slash + Plate Armor + Frostbite + Wand L3 + Plague amulet) was never played: its only `run` crashed.

## Build

Ended with: Fireball, Ice Spike, Arcane Missile, Slash (4 of 5 slots; the fifth was left empty because of the crash). Talents: mana-efficiency 4, arcane-affinity 2, iron-constitution 1, Magical Specialist, Frostbite 1. Gear: Plate Armor (onHit speedBoost 30), Wand L3 and Arcane Staff (mana regen and mana leech), Enchanted Boots, Iron Helm, Plate Leggings, Amulet of Plague.

How it changed:

- 0 to 4m: Strike plus the free Regeneration aura. Five deaths on wave 1.
- 4m: Fireball. 9m: Ice Spike replaced Strike. 13m: Arcane Missile in the fourth slot.
- 20m: Magical Specialist and every "mana cost" item I had. Hits went from about 30 to about 140.
- 33m: dropped the Regeneration aura for Strike because the aura was eating my mana.
- 38m: Plate Armor dropped with an on-hit speed boost. That drop changed the plan.
- 53m to 1h12m: swapped crit and speed gear for mana regen, then removed mana cost gear on purpose.
- 1h12m: Quick Strike plus Plate Armor. The build clicked, then broke the engine ten minutes later.

## How it felt

- **0 to 20m**: slow but readable. Each level gave something obvious to buy. Wave 1 with 25 health is brutal: you die to the third goblin four times before anything else happens.
- **20m to 53m**: stuck on wave 3 for over half an hour. This was the dull stretch. Magical Specialist doubled my hit size and did nothing for progress, because I was already one-shotting everything and was out of mana 61 to 98% of the time. Kills stayed at 10 to 13 a minute whatever I did to damage.
- **53m to 1h12m**: satisfying. I worked out that mana regen was the only stat that mattered and each change showed up in the numbers: 12.4, then 15.9, then 19.3 kills a minute, and wave 4 for the first time.
- **1h12m to 1h22m**: the spike. 26.8 kills a minute, level 10 in ten minutes instead of the forty I expected.
- After that: no play, only crashes.

## Fun

- Finding the Quick Strike plus Plate Armor engine. Quick Strike hit 6274 times in ten minutes (about 10 a second) and Fireball went from 109 to 188 casts. That is exactly the "weak alone, strong combined" moment DESIGN.md asks for.
- Working out that mana cost was hurting me. Swapping a Mage Hood for a plain Iron Helm and gaining 21% kills felt like a real discovery.
- A caster carrying a legendary Greatsword for its +45% speed. Silly and good.
- Plate Armor being worth wearing on a mage because of its trigger.

## Not fun

- Spell points come one per level. At level 10 I had earned 9. Meteor (4) and Arcane Boost (4) were the two things I wanted most and I never got either. I never tried an AoE magic spell in 83 minutes.
- Tier 3 and 4 talents were out of reach. Siphon and Mana Overflow are the mana archetype and need 15 points spent. I had 9.
- Deaths I could not explain. In one ten-minute stretch I took 18 logged damage with 52 health and died twice. Something that is not a direct hit is killing me and the report does not show it.
- Arcane Affinity and Magical Specialist were dead picks for most of the run (see Too weak).
- Inventory fills with 50 items and most are common junk. Discarding them one at a time is a chore.

## Combinations

- **Fireball + Ice Spike + mana regen**: the baseline. Worked, but plays as "wait for mana".
- **Magical Specialist + mana cost gear**: hits 30 to 141, kills 10.5 to 10.2 a minute over five minutes each. Bigger numbers, no gain. With the Greatsword and more cost it fell to 7.8.
- **Dropping the Regeneration aura**: 7.8 to 13.2 kills a minute, deaths 2 to 4 per five minutes. A real tradeoff, which I liked.
- **Mana regen gear (12 to 19 a second)**: 12.4 to 15.9 kills a minute over ten minutes.
- **Lower mana cost (62 to 46)**: 15.9 to 19.3 kills a minute over ten minutes.
- **Strike + Plate Armor speed boost**: Strike went from 66 to 125 hits in five minutes. Changed nothing, since Strike does about 3 damage with Magical Specialist.
- **Quick Strike + Plate Armor**: 19.3 to 26.8 kills a minute. Changed how the build played: cast time stopped mattering and mana became the only limit.
- **Expected and not delivered**: mana leech with the fast hits. Leech is capped by damage dealt and Quick Strike averaged 0.23 damage a hit, so it returned almost nothing.
- **Wanted and never tested because of the crash**: Frostbite and Amulet of Plague on a 10-hits-a-second attack.
- **Wanted and not offered**: anything that makes use of overkill damage, and a cheap AoE magic spell.

## Items

- Mattered: Plate Armor (the trigger), Wand L3 and Arcane Staff (regen), Enchanted Boots, Amulet of Mana.
- Changed my plan: Plate Armor, and the Greatsword for a while.
- Vendor trash for this build: every shield, warhammer, dagger and battleaxe, and four of the five legendaries I saw. Legendary Iron Helms were worse than a common L3 one.
- Gearing did feel like part of the build once I knew regen was the stat. Before that it was just stats.

## Too strong

- Quick Strike with Plate Armor's speedBoost 30. Quick Strike's cast is 2.5s and the boost is 3s, so every hit fully refills it and every other spell. It is a common L1 to L2 body item and a 3-point spell. +39% kills, and it makes cast time meaningless for the whole bar.
- The free Regeneration aura stacks. It reached 28 stacks at 8 health a second each.

## Too weak or useless

- **Mana cost as a stat** (Arcane Affinity, Mage Hood, Silk Leggings, half of Magical Specialist). It raises damage and cost together, so damage per mana never changes. Out of mana and overkilling, it made me slower.
- **Arcane Missile**: 1 damage per mana against 2 for Fireball and Ice Spike. 8% of damage.
- **Crit on a caster** at these waves: all overkill.
- **Strike under Magical Specialist**: 2% of damage.
- Not rated because I never got them: Meteor, Arcane Boost, every tier 3 and 4 talent, every aura except Regeneration.

## Bugs or oddities

- **Engine does not settle (crash).** From 1h22m every `run` threw `Error: engine did not settle after 500 passes` at `settle` / `flush` / `tick` in `playtest/.build/cli.mjs`. Bisection: removing Quick Strike made a 5-second run pass; putting it back crashed again. With Quick Strike equipped, one 5-second run passed after swapping to Amulet of Mana, and the next crashed after a weapon swap. Replacing Quick Strike with Slash still crashed with Wand L3 (+45% speed) equipped.
- My reading of `src/hooks/useCheckCastSpell.ts` and the `speedBoost` trigger in `src/logic/triggerActions.ts`, **not confirmed**: a free physical spell that the boost refills completely recasts in the same pass whenever the spell state changes between passes. A second physical spell (Strike) or Frostbite's ice on the target would supply that change. In the browser this would likely be an endless update loop.
- The same ten minutes with Quick Strike alone ran fine, so the combination is only safe by accident.
- Dead enemies stay on the field at negative health and keep being hit (`Orc L4 -60/128hp`).
- Deaths with almost no logged damage, and "lowest health 30%" in stretches with two deaths.
- A `--no-stop` run still reported "level 9 -> 10" normally, as expected, but Curse showed as an aura on me 2% of the time, which I never cast.
- Curse's description says "Curse yourself, doubling damage" but it targets enemies with defense and speed penalties.

## Against DESIGN.md

- Matches: slow stretches then a sudden spike; a build that emerged from a drop; a real tradeoff in the Regeneration aura.
- Does not match: "Mana: spend it or hoard it" is not reachable in the first 80 minutes. The early caster has one obvious path (regen), and the stat that looks like the caster stat is a trap. Magical Specialist gives up physical damage, which a caster never had, so it costs nothing.

## Suggestions

1. Fix the cast loop: cap a spell to one cast per tick, or stop speedBoost from filling a spell past one tick short of ready. Keep the combination, since it was the best moment of the run.
2. Make mana cost a real choice: let damage scale faster than cost, or carry overkill damage to the next enemy. Big slow nukes would then differ from cheap fast casts.
3. Give more spell points, or cut Meteor and Arcane Boost to 3. A caster should see one AoE spell in the first hour.
4. Put one mana talent in tier 2 (a weak Siphon) so the mana archetype starts before 15 points.
5. Show status and other indirect damage in "damage taken" and on death, so deaths are explainable.
6. Give Magical Specialist a real cost, such as less health or no crit.
7. Let mana leech work on hits that deal 0 damage, or give Quick Strike a minimum of 1, so fast hits plus leech is a build.
8. Raise Arcane Missile to a role: several small hits that each trigger on-hit effects would combine with Frostbite and status amulets.
