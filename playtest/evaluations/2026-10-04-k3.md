# Evaluation k3 (2026-10-04)

## Verdict
The game is harder than in i2 and the danger has moved to the right place (bosses, not mid-wave casters), but it is not yet hard in the right way. The difficulty is binary: a build is either untouched for 10 to 30 minutes or dead to one or two boss casts, and the only answer any tester found was more effective health. Balance is uneven mainly because of Raise Shield; the post-k3 change to it (5 stacks, 4% each, speed cost) cuts its shield by roughly a third to a half, which should help, but is untested. The single most important thing to change: make boss damage arrive as several readable hits a build can answer, by tuning boss spell lists and numbers, so that control, regen and shield size are all answers.

## Is it hard in the right way?
- **Hard enough: physical (k3-phys).** 12 deaths in the hour (4 were the tester dropping Health Regeneration at level 2), wave 8 reached, 14.5 minutes stuck on Frost Queen and 3 deaths each to her and Storm Titan. New bests felt earned and Rampage made the lap back short (31 minutes first time, 3.5 at the end). This is the target feel, except for what the deaths were.
- **Too safe: anything with Raise Shield (k3-caster, k3-late).** Caster: zero health lost for 6m40s across waves 2-5 and three bosses, re-clears at 87-100% lowest health, 4 deaths in the hour. Late build: "average 100%, lowest 100%" from wave 1 to the wave 9 boss for 30 minutes. Why: at 10 stacks it added 81-100 shield to characters with 45-124 health for 15 mana, at 100% uptime, and its damage cost did not reach casters.
- **The kind of difficulty: mostly one-shots.** Phys: every death but one after minute 17 was a boss one- or two-shot (119-125 into 148 health). Caster: both late deaths were the same Storm Titan hit of 124 against normal hits of 49-50, then a 10-11 minute risk-free re-clear to retry it. Late: Dragon Lord Meteor 219 then Inferno 148 through 178 shield and 124 health. Health averaged 98-100% right up to each death. Nothing can be read in advance and nothing but effective health answers it, so regen, leech, ice and War Cry all go dead.
- **Earnable: partly.** Walls broke through items (Tower Shield, hammers, Ring of Poison) and health talents, which is the right source, and a slow wall is allowed. But the choice inside the wall is forced: phys put levels 2-6 and 9 into damage and health with "no decision".
- **Fixes through pieces: yes so far.** The three post-k3 changes are all spell numbers. Several tester suggestions are rules (status bypassing shield, banked Rampage pushes, announced casts); those are the owner's call.
- **Nothing on the player scales with the wave: confirmed.** `getScaleMulti` (`src/logic/progressionActions.ts`) is used in five places: enemy stats (`enemyActions.ts`), enemy trigger values (`triggerActions.ts:35`, gated on `isEnemy`), enemy status stacks (`attackActions.ts:59`), enemy aura bonuses (`useCheckAuraEffects.ts`), and item drops (`itemActions.ts:181`). Only the last reaches the player, as loot, which DESIGN.md allows. Side effect seen in i2-world2: a common world 2 item is about ten times a legendary world 1 item.

## Evidence base
- k3-phys (seed 401, fresh, 60m), k3-caster (seed 402, fresh, 60m), k3-late (seed 403, level 10 start, 40m, first 200s lost to a tester error). Previous round i2-bleed-s301, i2-caster-s302, i2-world2 for comparison.
- Source read: Raise Shield and Execute, boss table, enemy scaling, defense formula, ice slow, speed-to-cast-time, the talents named below. No runs queried.
- Not tested by anyone in k3: the new Raise Shield, Execute at 3 points, wave 10 and Shadow Emperor, world 2, Exhaustion, Disarm, Shatter, Mana Overflow, Lacerate, Bludgeon, Hemorrhage, any poison-self or lightning-crit build, any tier 3 or 4 talent. I did not locate the aura-owner output in the harness and took it as given.

## Problems, ranked
1. **Boss damage is a spike, not a fight.** All three reports. Storm Titan's base hit works out near 50 (mana cost 34 x 1.25^6 x 0.2, spell damage 2), matching the reported 49-50; the 119-125 hits are 2.5x that. The spells' own crits are x1.5-1.6 at 11-12% (`magicSpells.ts`), so I could not confirm in the source where 2.5x comes from. Breaks "the game is hard... earned" and "tradeoffs must be real" (sustain pieces are dead). Confidence high on the effect, low on the cause. Fix kind: boss spell lists, cast times and mana cost, so the same damage lands in more, smaller casts.
2. **Raise Shield decides whether the game exists.** k3-caster, k3-late, i2-caster. Now 5 stacks of 4 flat + 4% shield, 0.5 regen and -1 speed each (`auras.ts:364`). My estimate: caster shield 115 falls to about 68, late build 178 to about 118, so Titan and Frost Queen should reach health again. The speed cost is flat: -5 speed adds 0.5s to every cast (`useRecalcCombatant.ts:10`), 4% on Execute, 8% on a 6s spell, more on fast attacks. The caster called Tower Shield's -8 speed invisible, so the cost probably still does not bite slow casters. Breaks "would every build take it". Confidence medium until retested. Fix kind: its speed and mana numbers.
3. **Bosses ask for effective health and nothing else.** From `bossData.ts` the traits are stat lines: shield 25-40 (Bone Warden, Frost Queen, Dragon Lord), regen 3 (Storm Titan), Fortitude, Lifesteal, Curse; Goblin King and Inferno Lord have none, Plague Master is Poison Stab twice. Testers noticed one trait in three runs (Shadow Assassin's curse or lifesteal). Titan's regen was outrun without being seen. Breaks "enemies ask something of a build". Confidence high. Fix kind: boss numbers large enough to be felt (a shield a burst build cannot crack, regen a slow build cannot outrun).
4. **Control does not answer what kills.** Ice is 3 speed per stack (`statusActions.ts`), about 2s on a cast at 7 stacks; it delays a 124 hit, never shrinks it. Caster measured no benefit and died faster with Ice Spike. Ice, Control and Frostbite are decoration while problem 1 stands. Confidence medium (noisy comparison). Fix kind: follows from 1; otherwise Frostbite and Exhaustion numbers.
5. **Sustain pieces are dead for shield builds and compulsory without one.** Health Regeneration: mandatory for phys (4 deaths without it, 87% uptime), useless with a shield (3-5% uptime in k3-late), and the Regeneration talent likewise. Not a choice either way. Confidence high. Fix kind: Health Regeneration and early enemy numbers.
6. **Bloodlust has no job on bosses.** 89-90% uptime, 15 stacks, no change in clear times because trash already died in one hit and stacks go on the first boss hit. In i2 it was the best piece because 20 shield protected it. The War Cry archetype now works only with a shield. Confidence medium. Fix kind: War Cry stack numbers, or shield items for physical builds.
7. **Decisions dry up from level 8.** No level in 37 minutes (k3-late), 10+ minutes per level and spell points with no slot (k3-caster), forced points (k3-phys). Mana sits at 87-95% unused in two builds; Mana Overflow is 15 points away. Breaks "always something to work toward". Confidence high. Fix kind: unlock costs and tier placement.
8. **Magical Specialist's cost is erased by a shield.** Health 60 to 45, then Raise Shield made it irrelevant. The smaller shield may restore it. Confidence medium. Fix kind: retest first.
9. **Execute has no role.** Now 3 points (`physSpells.ts:125`), 220% at 12s against Strike's 100% at 6s: about the same damage per second, more overkill, less for Rampage to chain. Cheaper, not better. Confidence medium. Fix kind: its scale or cast time.
10. **Drops are mostly noise.** About 20 of 119 equipped, half mana gear on a physical build; a level 1 legendary loses to a level 5 common. Confidence medium. Fix kind: rarity and drop numbers.

## What is working
- Rampage: re-clears became speed-clearing, which is exactly "growth shows as clearing early waves faster". Protect it.
- Danger moved from unreadable mid-wave casters to bosses since i2.
- Item clicks: Robe of Elements, Ring of Poison, Plate Armor's trigger, hammers.
- Critical Toxin with crit gear made poison a real build (status 45k against direct 41k).
- Bleed has a defined job: 24-29% of damage on bosses, 4-7% on re-clears.
- Mana: one Mana Efficiency point per step keeps a caster fed; the cliff is gone.

## Against DESIGN.md
- **Pace**: matched. Walls of 14-32 minutes are fine; the fault is the caster's wall being a coin flip with nothing to decide.
- **Where power comes from**: matched in code. "Hard, death common, earned" matched for phys, not for shield builds.
- **Fun over balance**: matched. Rampage and Robe of Elements are strong and should stay.
- **Builds**: partly. Phys, ice caster and poison-shield play differently, but all three answer danger the same way. Tradeoffs fail on Raise Shield and Magical Specialist.
- **Synergy**: partly. Critical Toxin + crit and Rampage + cheap attacks work; Bloodlust, Frostbite 1 and Wildfire + Fireball do not.
- **Progression**: not matched after level 8.
- **Checklist**: Raise Shield fails "would every build take it"; Execute fails "plays differently".

## Archetypes
- Bleed: partial; a boss tool beside direct damage.
- Poison: working (k3-late); self-poison untested.
- Fire: partial; Wildfire has no cheap source; Immolation last seen in i2-world2.
- Ice: partial; damage fine, slow unfelt; Shatter untested.
- Lightning: untested in k3; kit out of reach in hour one (i2).
- War Cry: partial; lost before the boss.
- Shield: too strong in k3; changed since, untested. Hit-back untested.
- Mana: spending works; hoarding and overflow untested.
- Control: missing as a build; Exhaustion and Disarm untested.

## Enemies
- Memorable: Dragon Lord (for killing in two casts), Storm Titan (for one hit), Shadow Assassin (curse slowed wave 4 and made a tester want speed, the one good example).
- Flat: Goblin King, Bone Warden, Inferno Lord, Plague Master. Boss shields of 25-40 base and regen 3 are too small to notice.
- Regulars that register: Dark Mage, Frost Wolf, Storm Elemental, Berserker. Wave 8 "Storm Lord" and "Frost Lord" read as bosses by name and damage.

## Risks in the mechanics
- Speed is flat against cast cost with a floor at 30% of base (`useRecalcCombatant.ts`): flat speed penalties fade on slow spells and stack hard on fast ones, so Raise Shield plus Tower Shield plus enemy ice could cripple a fast-attack build while a caster shrugs.
- Raise Shield's 4% per stack multiplies item shield, and item shield grows with the world (865 on one world 2 piece), while the 4 flat and the speed cost do not. In world 2 the aura is +20% shield for a fixed half second per cast.
- Defense halves a hit equal to it (`attackActions.ts:109`), so it does little against the spikes that kill; shield and health are the only real stats against bosses.
- Enemy mana cost, and so caster damage, grows 1.25x per wave with a 4x world jump; boss spikes grow with it.

## Open questions
- Where do the 2.5x boss hits come from? Next round should log whether a hit was a crit and its raw value.
- With the new Raise Shield, does a shield build take partial damage (lowest health between 20% and 80%), and does anyone feel the speed cost?
- Does Execute at 3 points earn a slot over Strike on a Rampage build?
- Wave 10, Shadow Emperor, and a control build (Exhaustion, Disarm, Frostbite 3) against Storm Titan.
- Rules that look like causes, for the owner: shield absorbing all status damage (makes regen dead); War Cry lost on any health loss (dead on bosses); death always costing a full re-clear when the wall is a single random hit.
