# r1-late: end of world 1 from a late start (2026-10-04)

## Run
- Seed 603. NOT a fresh start: level 11 at world 1 wave 8, 10 talent + 10 spell points, 20 drops. 30m00s game time, 11 `run` calls, still level 11 (3500/5120 xp).
- My mistake, not the game's: my first batch of `act` calls failed (shell quoting, harness printed its usage error) and the first 3-minute stretch ran with nothing spent or equipped. That stretch is a naked character dying to Frost Lord (Frostbolt 70) at wave 8 enemy 1. So only 27 minutes are the real build, and death 1 of 4 says nothing about balance.
- Highest: world 1 wave 10, enemy 10 (Shadow Emperor). World 2 not reached. Deaths 4 (3 with the build). 597 kills, 19.9/min overall.

## Build
- Spells: Strike, Slash, Rend, Bludgeon, Poison Stab (5/5). No aura equipped by choice: Physical Specialist sets mana regen to 0, so a paid aura is a few casts per life.
- Talents: Warrior Training 4, Iron Constitution 1, Physical Specialist, Bloodlust, Rampage 3. Widened Focus skipped: 2 points would have cost Rampage 3 plus a damage level, and I had no sixth spell point to fill the slot.
- Items at end: rare Battleaxe (dmg +19, crit x +65), Arcane Shield L8 (shield 37, dmg +17), Plate Armor L10 (def 39, hp 81, onHit speedBoost 6: my trigger item), Plate Leggings L10, Swift Boots, Amulet of Life Leech (leech +9), two defensive rings. Final: 200 hp, 106 defense, 37 shield, leech 13.
- Changes: at 12m added legs, a def +23 ring and leech boots after dying to status damage; at 18m swapped the fire body and plague amulet for Plate Armor and the leech amulet. That second swap is what stopped the deaths before wave 10.

## How it felt
- Re-clears are the best part. Bloodlust held War Cry at 15 stacks 100% of the time through waves 1-7, and Rampage 3 chained Bludgeon into the top damage source (29% of all damage, 187 hits). Kills per minute by stretch: 29.3, 18.7, 30.0, 18.3, 16.7, 22.7, 18.0, 18.7, 23.3. About 23-30 in waves 1-6, about 17-19 in waves 7-10.
- Full re-clear: death at about 16m50s, back on wave 10 at 23m34s, so about 6m45s for waves 1-9. The earlier one, wave 1 to wave 9, took about 4m45s. Waves 1-7 took no damage at all (health lowest 100% in four stretches); that part is pure waiting.
- Plague Master: 3 attempts, 3 wins, never a threat (about 55s for the whole wave, 22-75 poison taken). Not earned.
- Dragon Lord: 2 attempts, 2 wins. First (15m39s) cost real health: Inferno 76, Meteor 45, fire 61. Second, with the plate and leech, I stayed at 100% health. My wave 9 death was before the boss, at enemy 8, to bleed plus lightning ticks with zero healing. That death taught me something (statuses ignore defense, bring leech), which felt fair.
- Shadow Emperor: 1 attempt, 1 loss. He dealt 448 before shield in one fight; a single Inferno hit 157 against my 237 health plus shield, then Chain Lightning 45, Poison Stab 36, killing blow Inferno 93. I had no time to try an answer. It reads as a burst check that my sustain gear does nothing for.
- Wave 10 trash also killed me once (enemy 5, Storm Elemental Chain Lightning 22 after Archmage 72 and Shadow Fiend 46 wore me down).

## Enemies
- Earned: Dragon Lord first kill, and the wave 9 bleed/lightning pack that forced leech gear. Traits showed in what hurt: lightning and bleed ticks (185 and 114 taken) mattered more than hits once defense passed 78.
- Not earned: Plague Master, and every boss in waves 1-6, which did under 10 damage each. Cursed Knight's Curse was on me 16-35% of the time and I never noticed a result.
- Possibly unfair: Shadow Emperor's 157 Inferno is over twice anything else in the world (Dragon Lord's best is 76). One sample only.

## Too strong
- Bloodlust plus any shield. "Lost when you lose health" never fires while 37 shield (+5 to 9/s) soaks waves 1-7, so it is +150% damage for 1 point with no downside until wave 9.
- Rampage 3 with Bludgeon: 14s cast landed 17-26 times per 3 minutes, about 610 damage per hit.

## Too weak
- Poison Stab: 9% of damage, poison 5371 total across 30 minutes. A filler slot. Rend was only 14% and bleed 8377; neither status mattered next to raw hits.
- Percent leech with no flat leech: Battleaxe +20% and legendary ring +13% gave leech 0.

## Bugs or oddities
- Status showed "leech 0" with +33% leech equipped; it only became 1 then 13 after flat leech items. A legendary ring whose headline stat does nothing alone looks like a bug to a player.
- Physical Specialist makes mana a dead stat, yet mana items kept being my best defensive drops (Ring of Mana def +23, Mage Hood def +11).
- Harness: a mistyped `act` prints only the usage line, and `run` still proceeds on an unbuilt character with no warning about 10 unspent points.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make Bloodlust's stacks drop when shield is hit too, or cap Bloodlust stacks at 8. The fragile tradeoff should be felt before wave 9.
2. Give Shadow Emperor's Inferno a tell or trade its 157 peak for more frequent 80s, so the answer is sustain or a defensive aura rather than a bigger health bar.
3. Let physical builds use one aura: a mana-free shout (cooldown only), or have Physical Specialist keep 1 mana/s. Fortitude or Disarm against Shadow Emperor would be a real choice and I could not make it.
4. Make percent leech grant a small flat base (or show the item as inactive), so Battleaxe plus leech ring is a combination, not a trap.
5. Raise Poison Stab and Rend status stacks or let Rampage kills carry the dying enemy's bleed/poison to the next one, so statuses matter in a fast-kill build.
