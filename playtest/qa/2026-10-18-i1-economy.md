# QA 2026-10-18, batch i1: item economy and merchant-only uniques

Code under test: `src/logic/shopActions.ts`, `src/data/shopItemData.ts`, `src/logic/itemActions.ts`, plus the triggers they use in `src/logic/triggerActions.ts`.

## Summary

21 items checked: **16 pass (7 of them with a note), 2 fail, 1 exploit found, 2 could not be tested fully.**

The results that matter most:

1. **Smith exploit (fail).** The smith rolls its result at the level and scale of the *best* input, so one high-wave item plus three wave-1 junk items of the same rarity gives a full-scale item of the next rarity. One world 2 wave 10 common (sells for 278) plus 3 wave-1 commons (2 each) gave an uncommon that sells for 694. Chained up through every rarity with wave-1 junk, 650 gold of inputs became a world 2 wave 10 unique that sells for 20,817.
2. **Overflowing Chalice works backwards.** It fires every second that mana is full, so a fighter who never spends mana triggers it on 147 of 180 seconds and deals 7,466 lightning (more than all its direct damage). A caster who spends mana triggers it on 10 of 180 seconds. Its -15% damage cost lowers `attack` only, and magic damage never reads attack, so a caster does not pay the cost at all.
3. **Pyre Censer death loop.** A normal level-12 character (260 health, no regen) wearing it died 7 times in 3 minutes, 6 of them at world 1 wave 1, every killing blow its own fire. Martyr's Plate with Toxic Blood goes the other way: health averaged 98% (lowest 69%) at waves 4 to 6, against 62% without the plate.

Also: shatter damage (Rimewalker Boots, and the Shatter talent, which uses the same code) is not logged, so it is missing from every `run` report. Legendary-to-unique smithing does not keep the slot for boots and legs.

## Details

### Common setup

- `qa-ec-base`: `new --seed 11 --level 12 --wave 6` (20 drops).
- Fighter base `qa-ec-phys`: forked from base, `qa wave 1 4`, `qa points 11`. Battleaxe + Dagger, Leather Leggings, Ring of Poison; spells Strike + Rend; talents warrior-training 5, iron-constitution 10, stone-wall 5. 200 health, 63 attack, 24 defense. Body, helmet, boots, amulet and ring2 left **empty**, so each pair adds one item into an empty slot and changes nothing else. All six merchant items were granted at wave 4 (item level 4, so percentages are x1.3) and sit in the inventory of both copies.
- Caster base `qa-ec-cast`: forked from base, `qa wave 1 4`, `qa points 10`. Arcane Staff x2, Mage Hood, Cloth Robes, Leather Leggings, Ring of Poison; Fireball + Lightning Bolt; arcane-affinity 5, iron-constitution 10, stone-wall 5. Mana cost 33, regen 7/s, so it really spends mana.
- Sturdy base `qa-ec-bossbase`: the fighter plus `qa points 100` into iron-constitution and stone-wall (5,800 health, 196 defense), used where the item has to act for a full minute without the character dying.

All pairs are forks with the same seed. Wave changes during a stretch are shared by both copies unless a death splits them, which I note.

### 1-3. Martyr's Plate (180 s pairs from `qa-ec-phys`)

Expected self-poison per enemy attack (`triggerActions.ts:194` and `triggerStacks`): ceil(2 x (1 + 63 x 0.01) + 10/3) = 7 stacks. Poison deals about 5x its stacks before it wears off, so about 35 damage per enemy attack, which is more than most enemy hits at wave 4. Toxic Blood heals 2 per stack per second, so it should net about +1 per stack per second.

| run | change | poison taken | health avg / lowest | deaths | furthest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| qa-ec-m0 | none | 15 | 62% / 0% | 1 | w5 |
| qa-ec-m1 | + plate | 811 | 55% / 0% | 2 (both own poison) | w4 |
| qa-ec-m3 | + Toxic Blood | 24 | 55% / 1% | 0 | w5 |
| qa-ec-m2 | + plate + Toxic Blood | 1,113 | **98% / 69%** | 0 | w6, killed 2 bosses |

The plate also raised poison dealt to enemies (172 -> 924 with Toxic Blood) through its +7 poison stat. When m2 continued for 300 s more, it pushed the furthest wave from 6 to 8 and then died to a Frost Wolf Ice Spike, so it is not invincible. But for three waves it made damage close to irrelevant: the harder you are hit, the more you heal. A clean, large result. It is worth flagging against DESIGN.md's "the game is hard".

### 4-5. Overflowing Chalice

`tickActions.ts:37-42` calls onFullMana on **every** 1 s tick while mana is full, and the Chalice does not spend mana. Expected stacks per enemy per firing at L4 for the fighter: ceil(6 x (1 + 51 x 0.01) + 5) = 15.

- Fighter (`qa-ec-c1` vs `qa-ec-m0`, 180 s): lightning dealt 7,466 vs 0. Total damage 12,307 vs 5,540. I counted seconds with full mana by stepping 1 s at a time: **147 of 180**. Direct damage fell 4,873 -> 3,940 and attack 63 -> 51, so the -19% cost is real for this build.
- Caster (`qa-ec-k1` vs `qa-ec-k0`, 180 s): full mana on **10 of 180** seconds. Lightning dealt 703 vs 577, and some of that comes from the +5 lightning stat riding on each spell. Direct damage was 4,478 vs 4,373: **no damage cost**. Lightning Bolt averaged 105 per hit with the Chalice and 102 without.
- Why: the `damage` effect sets `attack.total` (`effectActionMap.ts:57-62`), but magic damage is `spell.damage x manaCost` (`attackActions.ts:86-90`) and never reads attack. Glass Pendant's +damage% and Miser's Ring's -damage% behave the same way for casters.

The trigger and the cost both do what the code says, so this is a pass. But the item pays off most, and costs least, in exactly the build its description does not name.

### 6. Glass Pendant

Fighter, L4 (`qa-ec-g1` vs `qa-ec-m0`, 180 s): direct damage 7,857 vs 4,873 (+61%), deaths 1 vs 1, health pool 120 vs 200. In `status`: attack 63 -> 75. That is +19%, not +36%, because percentage bonuses add to warrior-training's +75% rather than multiplying (`effectActionMap.ts:31-41`). Crit multiplier 1.94 -> 2.49. On the caster (`qa-ec-k2`) only the crit multiplier and the health penalty apply: direct damage 5,396 vs 4,373, with 17 crits against 14, so that comparison is a lead only.

### 7-8. Rimewalker Boots

Shatter goes straight to health with no `logDamage` (`triggerActions.ts:227-245`), so I measured it by accounting. Setup: `qa-ec-bossbase`, `qa wave 1 10 10` (Shadow Emperor, 4,693 hp, no shield), 60 s, boss still alive at the end.

- Without the boots (`qa-ec-bossb`): HP lost 2,446; logged direct + status 567 + 1,879 = 2,446. Exact match, which validates the method.
- With the boots (`qa-ec-bossr`): HP lost 3,422; logged 1,122 + 1,804 = 2,926. **496 unlogged = shatter**, over 7 crits (about 71 per crit). Expected: about 8 ice stacks x 2 x (1 + 63 x 0.05) = about 66 per crit.

Speed in `status`: 7 -> 4. Strike and Rend still landed 11 and 7 hits in 60 s, so -3 is cheap at this speed. The boots work alone, without a frost spell, because their +ice rides on every attack. The crit counts differ (0 vs 7) by chance.

### 9. Pyre Censer

- Normal fighter (`qa-ec-p1`, 260 health, no regen, 180 s): own fire taken 1,960, fire dealt 2,085. **7 deaths, all killing blows status damage (fire), 6 of them at world 1 wave 1.** Self stacks at L4 are ceil(1 x 1.63 + 7/3) = 4 per second, which settles near 20 damage per second, so a character with no sustain dies in about 15 s whatever wave it is on. Death sends it to wave 1, where it dies again. Unequipping is the only way out.
- Sturdy fighter at the Shadow Emperor, 60 s (the no-item copy took 145 fire from the boss itself):
  - L4 (`qa-ec-bossp4`): about 1,030 own fire per minute vs 2,911 fire dealt.
  - L10, fire +29 (`qa-ec-bossp10`): about 3,200 own fire per minute vs 7,562 fire dealt.
  - Self-damage grows with the item's fire stat (1/3 share), which grows with the wave the item was rolled at.

It does what it says. The note is that without about 20 to 55 health per second of regeneration or leech it is a death loop, and nothing on it says so.

### 10. Miser's Ring

Fighter, sturdy base at wave 3 (`qa-ec-d0` vs `qa-ec-d1`), 3,600 s each.

- Drops: 60/415 kills (14.5%) without the ring, 76/414 (18.4%) with it.
- Drop chance in the state: 0.10 -> 0.17. I expected about +7 points; I saw +3.9, about 1.5 standard errors from either the expected gain or no gain. Treat the drop increase as a lead, not a measurement.
- Direct damage over the first 900 s: 27,898 -> 23,484 (-16%), with attack 63 -> 55.

### 11. Level growth

All percentage and ratio stats use `ratioLevelMulti` (`itemActions.ts:130`), 1 + 0.1 per level above 1. Item level is the wave number (`enemyActions.ts:72`; the shop uses `highest.wave`), so it caps at 10 and x1.9.

| item | L1 | L10 |
|---|---|---|
| Glass Pendant | +29% damage, +38 crit multiplier, -30% health | +62%, +75, -57% |
| Miser's Ring | +6 drop, -10% damage | +11, -18% |
| Overflowing Chalice | -16% damage | -30% |
| Martyr's Plate | -26% defense | -45% |
| Rimewalker Boots | ice +6, speed -3 | ice +11, speed -5 |

The cost and the benefit grow together. Nothing absurd: penalties multiply (`effectActionMap.ts:35-36`), so -57% health is the worst a single item does. Flat stats (health, poison, fire) grow with the wave scale as on any drop: Martyr's Plate health is +28 at L1 and +225 at L10 of world 1.

### 17. Restock timer pauses while dead

`useGameLoop.ts` runs `slowTick` (which holds `tickShop`) only while `currentRespawnTime === 0`, and the harness does the same. In `qa-ec-econ`, 601 s after a restock, with 2 deaths, the timer still read 7 s. It restocked on schedule once the lost time passed. This is fine if "600 s of play" means time alive. Noted only because the UI countdown will stall during respawns.

### 19. Smith slot at unique tier

`getRandomItem(rarity, slot)` (`itemActions.ts:157-161`) falls back to a random slot when the pool has nothing in that slot. `UNIQUE_ITEMS` has no boots and no legs (Rimewalker Boots is merchant-only and not in the pool). In `qa-ec-w2`, smithing 4 legendary Heavy/Swift Boots gave `item_39`, a unique **Robe of Elements** (body). Every other tier, and unique tier for weapon, body, helmet, ring and amulet, keeps the slot.

### 20-21. Smith scale lift (exploit)

`smithItems` takes `level = max(levels)` and `scale = max(scales)` (`shopActions.ts:135-136`). Since death sends the player to wave 1, wave-1 junk is plentiful, and three pieces of it plus one good item make a full-strength item of the next rarity. Setup: `qa-ec-w2` = `new --seed 12 --level 30 --world 2 --wave 10`, then `qa wave 1 1` and `qa grant-item heavy-boots <rarity>` for the junk (highest stays world 2 wave 10).

| step | inputs (sell value) | output (sell value) |
|---|---|---|
| common -> uncommon | 1 x w2w10 common (278) + 3 x w1 common (2 each) | uncommon boots L10 (694), health +1,543 |
| -> rare | that + 3 x w1 uncommon (5 each) | rare L10 (1,665 by formula) |
| -> epic | + 3 x w1 rare (12 each) | epic L10 |
| -> legendary | + 3 x w1 epic (30 each) | legendary L10 |
| -> unique | + 3 x w1 legendary (75 each) | unique Robe of Elements L10, **20,817** (mana +5,198, fire +227, lightning +213) |

Total inputs: 278 + 372 = 650 gold of value, turned into 20,817 gold and a top-scale unique. Even one step pays: each high-scale common smithed with 3 wave-1 commons is worth +410 gold over selling it. Smithing items of one scale always loses gold (4 x 2 = 8 -> 5, ... 4 x 75 = 300 -> 150), and buy/sell loses two thirds, so this is the only loop that creates value. The code comment says the max rule is intended, but it makes three of the four inputs free. A fix could use the lowest or the average scale and level. That is a design call; I only report it.

### Economy pace (context, not a verdict)

In `qa-ec-econ` the character earned about 115 gold from 11 drops in 10 minutes (with 2 deaths). Merchant uniques cost 1,374 at wave 6, and the opening 20-item stash sold for 192. So buying one merchant unique takes about an hour or more of selling, unless the player uses the smith lift above.

## Bugs

1. **Shatter damage is never logged.** `shatterStatus` (`src/logic/triggerActions.ts:227-245`) subtracts health directly, without `logDamage`. It does not appear in `run` output, kill attribution or the game log; 496 damage in 60 s was invisible. This also hides the Shatter talent, which uses the same action. Repro: `new X --from qa-ec-bossbase; qa X wave 1 10 10; qa X grant-item rimewalker-boots; act X equip-item <id> boots; run X --seconds 60 --no-stop`, then compare (4,693 - boss hp) with direct + status damage dealt.
2. **Unique smith ignores the slot for boots and legs** (above). Repro: grant 4 legendary `heavy-boots`, `smith` them, and the result is not boots.
3. **Harness cosmetic:** `act X sell-item A A` prints "sold 2 for N gold" but sells one item (the gold is correct). `act X smith A B C D D` (5 ids, one repeated) is accepted and consumes the 4 distinct items. Both are harmless.

## Could not test

- **Merchant items with their named partners other than Toxic Blood** (Plaguebearer's Mask with Martyr's Plate, Mana Overflow or Leyline Band with the Chalice, Raise Shield or Eternal Aegis with the Pendant, Frostbolt with Rimewalker). Time went to the base effects. Note that Mana Overflow empties mana when it fires, so with the Chalice the Chalice would fire once per fill instead of every second; worth a pair.
- **Saves from before the shop existed** (no `gold` or `shop` in the save, items with no `scale`). By reading the code, `useGameStore.setState(data)` merges over the defaults, so gold 0 and an empty shop survive, and `itemScale` falls back to world 1 at the item's level. The harness cannot load a browser save, so this is untested.
- **Miser's Ring drop rate** needs more kills, or several seeds, to measure precisely (above).
