# i1-status: poison/fire status build, item economy test

## Run
Seed 1303, 45m game time, 9 `run` calls. Not a fresh start: level 12 at world 2 wave 1, 60-drop stash, 11+11 points unspent. Ended level 12 (9913/10240 xp, no level gained), highest world 2 wave 3, 6 deaths, 1045 kills (23.2/min).

## Build
Spells: Strike, Health Regen, Poison Stab, Poison Gas, Immolation (on for 10 of 45 minutes, see below). One slot stayed empty all run. I spent all 11 spell points (2+4+5) before taking Widened Focus, so the 6th slot had nothing to fill it and no level came. That one is my mistake, but the game makes it easy to make: nothing warns that a talent's extra slot will be empty. I never got a magic spell.
Talents: Warrior Training 3, Iron Constitution 2, Regeneration 1, Widened Focus, Epidemic, Critical Toxin, Wildfire. I held Toxic Blood back for Martyr's Plate, which I never got.
Items: Dragon Scale Mail (fire +17), Ring of Elements (fire +13), Ring of Poison, then a smithed legendary Greatsword (leech 37 +24%) and a smithed Warhammer (damage +173).
In the end it was a melee bruiser with incidental poison: Strike did 67% of direct damage. Status damage was 247k against 390k direct. Fire 145k came mostly from the +30 fire my gear puts on every hit, not from Immolation.

## Immolation and self-burn (the main status finding)
- With fire gear (+30 fire on every hit), Immolation's self-burn takes its share of your own fire stat. The burn was brutal: 16.6k and 18.6k fire taken per 5-minute stretch (about 60/s) against 6/s regen. Average health was 26%, then 9%, and I died to world 1 wave 7's Storm Titan with a Lightning Bolt for 11.
- Without it: 0 deaths, average health 63%. Then I added the leech Greatsword: average health 99%, and I set a new furthest wave (world 2 wave 3).
- I put Immolation back with 45 leech to test the pair. It still dropped me to 22% average health, with 18k self fire, and I died twice. Enemy fire damage went from about 11k to 26k per stretch and kills/min went up (38.6 against about 20), but most of those kills were back in world 1.
- Verdict: it's a real tradeoff, but it tips into a trap. Fire gear and Immolation fight each other, which is the opposite of synergy. The self-burn is far from negligible. It decides the whole build.
- I never saw Martyr's Plate or Pyre Censer at a price I could pay. Pyre Censer (fire +79) would have made the self-burn about 3x worse through the same share rule, so I doubt it could work with Immolation.

## Gold and the shop
- Gold over time: 1204 from selling the starting stash. After that +114 (5m), +468 (15m), +243 (20m), +849 (25m), 0 (35m), and 2878 at the end. Play earned about 1674 in 45 minutes, about 37/min.
- First purchase: none. Every unique cost 8382 at my furthest world 2 wave 1, and 13098 once I reached world 2 wave 3. Pyre Censer (10m) and Martyr's Plate (30m) were each listed for one 10-minute window while I held 1800 and 2900. Selling everything I owned would have reached about 5–6k.
- Gold after a death does feel worthless. World 1 drops sell for 2–8 gold, and a world 1 legendary Leather Armor sold for 75 (its stats are +1 to +3). In the same run the shop priced off world 2 wave 3. Two of my 45 minutes in world 1 earn less than one world 2 common. Each death also raised the gap, because the reroll price follows the furthest wave (931 → 1455).
- Prices: 3x sell value is fair for rares and epics, which I could afford but which were worse than smithed gear. For the merchant uniques it is out of reach on a 45-minute horizon. That makes the shop a window to look through, not a goal.
- Wares: the merchant-only uniques read well (Glass Pendant, Chalice, Martyr's). The shop's Ring of Lifespan (health +1095, leech 132) next to my dropped Ring of Lifespan (health +38) shows how hard scale dominates.
- Oddity: the shop was empty at game time 0 ("restocks in 0s") until the first tick. Each restock replaces all 6 wares, so a chase item vanishes with no way to hold it.

## Selling against smithing
Smithing was the best thing in the economy. 4 epics → legendary Tower Shield (damage 73, def 113). 4 commons (one strong) → uncommon Battleaxe, damage +101. Later 4 commons → Warhammer, damage +173, my best weapon. 4 epics → legendary Greatsword (leech, speed +23%). "As strong as the best input" means one strong item plus three junk items is a free upgrade. That made smithing worth more than selling every time. Junk sells for 2–37, while smithed output sold for 93–1397. Slot-keeping worked (4 bodies → body, 4 helmets → helmet). The forged item's "L" label (L9/L10) does not match its strength, which follows the strongest input. 4 world 1 commons gave world 1 trash, as expected.

## How it felt / progress
Kills/min by stretch: 29 (Immolation, died), 35 (Immolation, world 1, died), 17, 13 (new furthest world 2 waves 2 and 3 at 17–19m), 39 (Immolation, 2 deaths), 21, 14, 24 (died), 18. Both new furthest waves came within the first 19 minutes, then 26 minutes with nothing new. Orc Warlord (world 2 wave 3 boss) is a hard wall: Rend for 301 plus bleed, and a Lightning Bolt for 172, against my 1080 health. He killed me 2 of 2 times without Immolation. Death costs about 8 minutes to walk back from world 1. Re-clearing world 1 was quick and boring (13 bosses with no threat).

## Enemies
Orc Warlord is the memorable one. He asked for health or defense, not more damage, and I had a Tower Shield in my bag I should have tried. Storm Titan killed me only when Immolation had me at 0–10% health. Otherwise he was nothing. Bone Warden (Ice Spike 109) and Shadow Emperor (Inferno 62) hit hard but never threatened me once I had leech. World 1 enemies blur together.

## Suggestions
1. Cut the self-burn share of your own fire stat for Immolation (e.g. self stacks from base value only), or let fire gear lower self-burn. Then fire gear and Immolation combine instead of cancelling out.
2. Price shop wares by the wave you are on now, or let gold carry a scale bonus. Otherwise world 1 runs after a death earn nothing toward the shop.
3. Bring merchant-unique prices to about 2–3x a world's typical hourly income, or let the player reserve one ware across a restock.
4. Warn (or block) when a talent adds a spell slot you have no spell points to fill.
5. Smithing "as strong as best input" is great fun but very generous. Consider the average of the inputs, or best minus one step.
6. Fill the shop at time 0.
7. A cheap fire-resistance or "burn heals shield" piece would make Pyre Censer and Immolation a real build rather than a death sentence.
