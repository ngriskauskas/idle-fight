# Ideas

## Shop System

## Progression

### Tournament/League Mode

- Add a "Tournament" mode where your character can participate in a season against interesting generated characters.
- Fight through a bracket (8 or 16 NPCs per season) to win and move up to the next league.
- If you lose, you must go back to grinding enemies to improve your character before re-entering.
- This mode leverages your existing RPG mechanics (stats, spells, etc.) but changes the context to a sports-sim/competition.

### The "League" Loop

- **Season:** A bracket of 8 or 16 generated NPCs. The app simulates their matches while you play yours.
- **Struggle:** Lose a match and you're out. Prompt: "You should go back to grinding enemies to improve."
- **Loop:** The "Normal World" is your training ground, the "League" is the test.
