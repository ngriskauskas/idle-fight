# n5-caster: safe caster, first hour, after the shield fix
## Run
- Seed 501, fresh character, 60m00s game time, 13 `run` calls. Level 8 (1 spell point unspent). Highest wave 5 (world 1), Inferno Lord not beaten. 4 deaths. m4-caster on the same seed: level 9, wave 7, 4 deaths.
- Not tested: any trigger piece (a legendary Plate Armor with onHit speedBoost dropped at 54m51s, too late), a third spell kind beyond magic and aura after Strike left, a real two-piece talent combination beyond Arcane Affinity plus Magical Specialist. Nothing below rates those.
- My mistake that hurts the comparison: the first stretch ran with `--no-stop`, so I fought wave 1 with Strike only for 5 minutes (wave 2 at 5m13s, m4 had it at 2m20s). Everything after is about 3 minutes behind m4 for that reason alone.
## Build
- End: Arcane Missile, Fireball, Raise Shield, Health Regeneration. Talents: Arcane Affinity 3, Mana Efficiency 1, Shield Bearer 2, Magical Specialist. Stats: health 52, shield 78 (+2.5/s), mana 201 (+21/s), mana cost 66, speed -11.
- Gear: Dragon Scale Mail, Tower Shield L5, Spellbook L1, Mage Hood, Silk Leggings, Enchanted Boots, Ring of Vitality, Ring of Mana. No amulet dropped until the last minute.
- Order: Missile and 2 Affinity at 5m00s, Raise Shield and Shield Bearer at 8m21s (level 5), shield off on purpose 18m21s to 23m21s, Magical Specialist and Fireball at 34m13s (level 7), Tower Shield at 40m43s, second Shield Bearer and Scale Mail at 47m43s.
## How it felt
- Kills per minute by stretch: 3.6, 4.6, 6.5 (no shield, waves 1 to 3), 2.4, 2.0 (shield on, waves 3 to 4), 1.2 (shield off, wave 4 boss), 3.8, 6.7, 8.8, 8.1, 9.6, 11.2 (re-clears after Specialist).
- New furthest waves at 5m13s, 7m48s, 14m03s, 22m57s, then nothing for the last 37 minutes. Wave 5 is a wall for this build: 2 deaths to Inferno Lord, 2 to wave 5 trash.
- Deaths: about 27m, wave 5 enemy 7, Dark Mage Poison Stab 7 (shield up, mana too low to cast 19% of that stretch); about 39m, Inferno Lord Inferno 81 (I had 49 health plus 44 shield); about 48m, Inferno Lord Fireball 30 after Inferno 70 (62 health plus 78 shield, he dealt 230 in the fight); about 57m, wave 5 enemy 9, Frost Wolf Frostbolt 27 after I traded 10 health for damage.
- Re-clear of waves 1 to 4 after each death: about 9 min, 6.5 min, 6 min. That is the growth I could feel, and it was the best part of the hour.
- Long safe stretch: still there, but shorter and less total. No deaths from 0 to 27m. With the shield on waves 3 to 4 my lowest health was 64% and 55% (m4: 94% and 100%). So safe, not immune. It still had nothing to decide in it: 12 and 10 kills in two 5 minute stretches.
- Raise Shield as a choice, measured: Missile casts per 300s were 43 and 42 with it, 49 without (about 15% fewer, m4 measured 6%). Damage per 300s 1,905 and 1,858 with it, 2,469 without (Strike back in the slot). Shield off on wave 4, including Shadow Assassin: 0 deaths, lowest health 43%, 1,182 damage taken from the boss and survived on health regen and 12 defense. Shield back on for wave 5: died at enemy 7 anyway. So it is now a real trade through wave 4, and I would defend skipping it there. From wave 5 it is not a choice again, because one Inferno is bigger than my health.
## Fun
- Magical Specialist plus Fireball at 34m: damage per stretch went from about 3,500 to about 10,800, kills from 6.7 to 8.8 per minute, and waves 3 and 4 fell in 5 minutes instead of 15.
- The Inferno Lord gear puzzle. After the first death I knew the number to beat (81 plus 38) and went looking for it: Tower Shield L5 at -7 speed, Scale Mail over Cloth Robes. That felt like building against a boss. It was not enough, which is fine.
- Tower Shield is a good item: +25 shield for -7 speed is a cost I hesitated over twice.
## Not fun

- Losing to Inferno Lord the second time told me nothing. I do not know if he had 5% or 60% health left, so I could not tell whether to add damage or health. I guessed damage and died to a Frost Wolf before reaching him.
- Level 6 slot filled with Strike again (attack 3, 10% of damage), then deleted by Specialist. A banked spell point and a dead slot for 11 minutes.
- 44 drops, 36 unused. Three Tower Shields, five body armours with health or leech, four weapons with damage.
## Enemies
- Goblin King (w1): Rend 16, Slash 11 against 40 health with no shield. Lowest 63%. Typical 8 to 11, hardest 16. No trait seen. Fair, slight threat only because I was on Strike.
- Bone Warden (w2): Ice Spike 16, later 11 and 8; Poison Stab 10. Took me to 51% unshielded. The weaker Ice Spike shows (m4 saw 22 to 38). Asked nothing; poison 47 that stretch was the real damage.
- Orc Warlord (w3): Fortitude on itself 25% of the first fight, Lightning Bolt 35 hardest against 17 to 28 typical, Rend 20. With a third more health the first kill took about 6 minutes (7m48s wave start to 14m02s). Trait showed and made it long, not dangerous: lowest 64%.
- Shadow Assassin (w4): Lifesteal up 69% of the fight with my shield off, Slash 25 against 15 to 21 typical. The fight took most of 5 minutes because he healed against my low damage. First boss whose trait I felt. Asked for burst; after Specialist he died in under 2 minutes with Lifesteal up 3 to 6%.
- Inferno Lord (w5): Inferno 81 and 70, Fireball 38 and 30. Completely readable now, no spike: hardest to typical is about 1.15. Asks for more than about 110 effective health per cycle and enough damage to end it before the third cycle. Fair, and the first boss that was a fight. Unbeaten.
- Wave 5 trash is the other half of the wall: Storm Elemental did 282 in one stretch, Dark Mage Curse was on me up to 27% of the time (-12 speed, about -8 defense), and poison, bleed and lightning statuses did 861, 346 and 291 over the run straight past the shield.
- Bosses 1 to 4 were first-try kills again, but two of them now took real health off me. Still not fights. I did not reach Frost Queen.
## Too strong
- manaCost as the only damage stat. Mana averaged 72% for the run and 81 to 83% after Specialist; every manaCost item was free damage (Boots +7 and Leggings +6 took cost 41 to 66, damage per minute 1,540 to 2,350).
## Too weak
- Raise Shield's regen at wave 5: +2.5/s against 100 per Inferno cycle. The shield is a one-time buffer in boss fights now, which may be the intent, but then 5 stacks for 78 shield and -10 speed is thin.
- Health Regeneration aura: up 92% of the time, 7/s, irrelevant from wave 5. Strike on a caster. Magical Specialist's health cost showed this time (65 to 49 and it mattered at Inferno Lord), so I would no longer call it free.
## Bugs or oddities
- `status` stats include temporary enemy auras: speed read -26 and defense 18 mid-fight, -14 and 26 before it. Hard to check gear swaps. "Curse" on me still does not name its owner.
- Killing blow "Dark Mage Poison Stab for 7" again hides the cause (poison 130, fire 48 that stretch).
- Dragon Scale Mail's "fire +1" put fire on my attacks ("status bonus on every attack: fire 1"). I read it as fire resistance and equipped it for the fire boss.
- Fire status damage fell 852, 301, 131 over three stretches with 40, 35, 33 Fireball casts. Probably faster kills, but it looks wrong.
- A level-up inside a `--no-stop` stretch gave no hint in the first line that points were waiting.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Give the caster a wave 5 answer in build pieces: a tier 2 talent or item that turns mana into shield (for example shield regen equal to 5% of mana regen). Mana sat at 80% unused while shield regen was 2.5/s; this links the Mana and Shield archetypes and gives spare mana a job.
2. Print the boss's remaining health on a death at a boss. Without it the second Inferno Lord loss gave me nothing to build against.
3. Keep Raise Shield's 2 speed per stack, but let stacks decay one at a time or add shield regen per stack (1 instead of 0.5), so it is a boss tool as well as a trash tool. Right now it is a fair trade on waves 1 to 4 and mandatory but insufficient on 5.
4. Add a status defence piece (poison or bleed reduction on an amulet or boots, or a cleanse on Health Regeneration). Statuses did about 1,570 damage past the shield and caused 2 of 4 deaths; a shield build has no answer to shop for.
5. Give Goblin King and Bone Warden one build-testing hit each, and give the 8m to 23m stretch a decision: a 1 point magic spell at level 4 or 5 worth slotting, so the level 6 slot is not Strike.
