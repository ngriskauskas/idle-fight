# i2-world2: poison into Pyre Censer, world 2 economy

## Run
- Run name `i2-world2b`, seed 2303. A stale `i2-world2` run from Oct 4 (seed 303) already existed and `new` refuses to overwrite, so I used a new name.
- Not a fresh start: level 12 at world 2 wave 1, 60 stash drops, 11 talent and 11 spell points unspent.
- 45m game time, 13 `run` calls. Ended at level 13 with a furthest wave of world 2 wave 4. 6 deaths, 1076 kills (23.9/min).

## Build
- Spells: Strike, Poison Stab, Poison Gas, Poison Pool, Health Regen, and Arcane Missile (6/6 slots after Widened Focus).
- Talents: Warrior Training 5, Iron Constitution 3, Epidemic, Critical Toxin, Widened Focus.
- Gear at the end: Pyre Censer, Amulet of Sustenance, Ring of Ancient Wisdom, Ring of Poison, two damage weapons, Dragon Scale L3.
- How it changed: I started as a pure poison build and went for Martyr's Plate. Martyr's killed me, so I switched to poison with a Pyre Censer fire layer.
- Damage over the whole run:
  - Strike 304k (66% of direct damage), Poison Stab 124k, Gas 33k, Arcane Missile 1.5k.
  - Status damage: poison 268k, fire 88k (all of the fire after minute 35).

## The changes, one by one
- **Kill gold: landed, but small.** About 80-117 gold/min while in world 2 and about 37/min while re-clearing world 1. Most of my gold still came from selling. The big sales were a legendary helm (2728), a legendary wand (1397), legendary legs (1397) and an epic staff (559). Gold went 0 -> 1056 at 5m, 2914 at 25m, 5693 at 35m.
- **1x unique price: landed hard.** I bought Martyr's Plate at minute 5 (2794) by selling the starter stash. Later I bought Ring of Ancient Wisdom, Pyre Censer and Amulet of Sustenance (5457 each), all after a death had sent me back to world 1.
  - Problem: a unique sells back for exactly what it cost. I sold Martyr's for 2794 and bought the ring for 2794 the same second, so uniques are free rentals. Either swapping is meant to be free or sell-back should be lower.
- **hold-item: useful.** I held the Ring of Ancient Wisdom and then Martyr's Plate. Two catches:
  - A higher-level copy of the same unique (Martyr's L4, 5457) showed up next to my held L3 copy, which reads like a duplicate.
  - The held ring did not track my furthest wave. It stayed L1 while L4 copies (mana +1910) appeared.
- **Smith averaging: landed.** 4 world-2 commons (L1-L3) gave an L2 uncommon Cloth Robes. 4 world-1 rares gave an L9 epic helm with 28 health, which was trash. 4 world-2 uncommons gave an L1 rare Leather Armor that was worse than what I had. It is fair now, but I never smithed anything I equipped. Selling paid more.
- **Pyre Censer regen: works, and fire can work in this shape.** Over 5m it dealt 36k fire and burned me for 25k. With 89 regen/s my average health was still 74%, and after adding the Sustenance amulet (178 regen/s) it stayed at 100%.
  - Self-burn still grows with my fire stat. Dragon Scale's +30 fire feeds it. It was survivable only because the Censer and the amulet stacked regen.
  - It roughly doubled my status damage for one item slot, which felt great. Cost: a 5457-gold helmet slot and 13-25k self-damage per stretch.

## Martyr's Plate (+ Toxic Blood)
- Martyr's without Toxic Blood is a suicide item. Equipped through world 1 for 5 minutes, I took 6 damage from attacks and 3966 from my own poison, and died twice (both killing blows were my own poison).
- The self-poison scales with my own poison stat (+88 with the plate), not with the attacker. Fast, weak world-1 enemies stack it fastest. So the item is deadliest on the trivial waves you re-clear after every death.
- I never tested Toxic Blood. It costs 2 points, level 13 gave 1, and I had spent all 11 at the start. That is partly my mistake.
- Even so, one point per level means the item is a trap for anyone who reaches it a point short. The shop should at least warn about it.

## How it felt
- 0-5m: fast. I took w2w2 at 0:57 and w2w3 at 2:10, then the Orc Warlord (w2w3 boss, Lightning Bolt 152) killed me.
- 5-20m: two Martyr's deaths, then a slow world-1 re-clear. Each re-clear takes about 5m and is risk-free (lowest health 86-99%). It shows growth, but it is boring.
- 19:06: I beat the Orc Warlord and reached w2w4 for the first time. That one felt earned. He hits 250+ with Rend and Lightning Bolt and is the first enemy I learned to dread.
- 19-45m: no new furthest wave for 26 minutes. I died three times in w2w4 (Dark Mage, a Stoneback while bleeding from Orc Rends, and the Shadow Assassin boss with a 258 Slash).
- Hard enough? Yes in world 2. Gear upgrades were mostly sidegrades until the uniques. The world-1 bosses were all handed over.

## Enemies
- Orc Warlord is the standout: two heavy hits and a self-Fortitude, and he gates w2w3.
- Orcs' Rend bleed (763 bleed taken in one stretch) wore me down before the boss.
- Shadow Assassin one-shots 258 out of 1619 health. It was readable, and it pushed me toward health and regen.
- Enemies cast Curse on me 9-20% of the time. I never saw much effect from it.
- World 1 waves blur together. Nothing there asks anything of a level-13 build.

## Too weak / bugs / oddities
- Arcane Missile: 161 damage over 21 hits with 0 mana cost. Magic spells are dead in a physical build because they scale on mana cost.
- Epidemic and Critical Toxin: no visible output in the run log, so I can't rate them.
- Martyr's Plate self-poison scaling with its own +poison is the same trap as last round's Immolation.
- After selling the plate, two Martyr's copies (L3 held, L4 new) were in the shop at once.

## Suggestions
1. Make unique sell-back about 50% of the buy price. Free swapping turns the shop into a rental counter.
2. Martyr's Plate: base the self-poison on the attack, not on the wearer's poison stat. Or put "needs Toxic Blood" in its tooltip, or make Toxic Blood cost 1.
3. Raise kill gold in world 1 re-clears, or show gold per wave. Right now selling legendaries outpays killing 5:1.
4. Held items should re-roll up to the furthest wave on restock. Otherwise holding means falling behind.
5. Give magic spells a small flat base, or an "attack% to magic" item, so a 6th slot in a physical build is not wasted.
