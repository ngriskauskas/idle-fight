# m4-caster: safe caster, first hour
## Run
- Seed 501, fresh character, 60m00s game time, 16 `run` calls. Level 9 (1 talent and 2 spell points unspent: the level came in the last stretch). Highest wave 7 (world 1). 4 deaths.
- Time-boxed: I did not test a trigger piece, a two-piece talent combination or a second ring (none useful dropped until the end). Nothing below rates those.
## Build
- End: Arcane Missile, Fireball, Raise Shield, Health Regeneration. Talents: Arcane Affinity 3, Mana Efficiency 2, Shield Bearer 1, Magical Specialist. Stats: health 42, shield 84 (+5/s), mana 244 (+24/s), mana cost 66.
- Gear: epic Leather Armor (shield %), Arcane Shield, Amulet of Shield, Ring of Vitality, Spellbook, Mage Hood, Silk Leggings, Heavy Boots.
- Path: Missile at level 2, banked three spell points for Raise Shield at level 5 (5m31s), Strike as slot filler, then Magical Specialist plus Fireball at level 7 (27m15s). Spellbook L5 at 35m took mana cost from 34 to 66.
## How it felt
- 0 to 5m: fine. 6.6 to 8.6 kills/min, no deaths, a level every minute or two.
- 5m to 20m, Raise Shield on: I stopped being in danger at all. 0 deaths, lowest health 63%, then 94%, then 100% for a full stretch on waves 4 to 5. Also the dullest part: 2.4 to 2.8 kills/min, three new waves in 15 minutes, nothing to decide.
- 20m to 25m, Raise Shield off on purpose: died at wave 5 enemy 7 within a few minutes.
- 27m on, Magical Specialist plus Fireball: the build took off. 8.6, 7.3, 11.3, 9.0 kills/min; damage per stretch went from about 3,200 per 5 min to about 22,000 per 8 min.
- New furthest waves at 2m20s, 5m32s, 10m53s, 18m12s, 44m36s, 56m38s. The 26 minute gap at wave 5 was partly my shield-off test, partly Inferno Lord.
- Deaths: Giant Bat Slash 4 (w5 e7, shield off), Inferno Lord Fireball 40 after an Inferno for 83 (w5 boss), Frost Queen Frostbolt 133 (w6 boss), Berserker Rend 20 (w7 e8). Re-clear of waves 1 to 5 took about 10 min after the second death and about 7 min after the third.
- Real choices: levels 2 to 4 and 6 were not (Arcane Affinity or Mana Efficiency, either is fine). Level 5 (first shield point) and level 7 (Magical Specialist) were the only ones I thought about.
## Fun
- The level 7 spike. Specialist's +60% mana cost on top of Affinity turned Missile from about 60 to about 180 per hit, and it showed up immediately as faster re-clears.
- Inferno Lord and Frost Queen. First bosses that one-shot through a full shield, and beating each on the second try, after a gear swap, felt earned.
- Spellbook drop: one item nearly doubled my damage and briefly broke my mana. A drop that changes the plan.
## Not fun
- 5m to 20m. Completely safe and completely passive. Goblin King, Bone Warden, Orc Warlord and Shadow Assassin were handed to me: first-try kills, health never under 63%.
- Magical Specialist's health cost did not register: 52 to 39 health behind an 84 shield. I never felt it.
- Gear: 53 drops, 45 unused. Every damage, crit and leech item is dead for this build; the choice was only "does it say shield, mana or manaCost".
## Enemies
- Goblin King (w1): Rend and Slash, hardest hit 10. No trait visible. A speed bump.
- Bone Warden (w2): Ice Spike 22 to 38 and Poison Stab. Took me to 25% health before the shield, nothing after. Trait not visible in the output.
- Orc Warlord (w3): Fortitude on itself 20% of the fight, Lightning Bolt 53. Trait showed; it made the fight long (about 5 min), not dangerous.
- Shadow Assassin (w4): Lifesteal on itself 27% of the time, Slash 39. 568 damage soaked without losing health. Trait showed, asked nothing.
- Inferno Lord (w5): Inferno 83 plus Fireball 40 back to back. First boss that asked for effective health over about 125. Fair, readable.
- Frost Queen (w6): Frostbolt 133 in one hit, which was more than my shield plus health (123). Second try the hardest hit was 64, so it is a crit or a variance spike. Felt like a coin flip, not something I answered.
- Wave 7 Berserkers and Cursed Knights: bleed 122 in the stretch. Bleed and poison ignore the shield plan and are the caster's real weakness.
## Too strong
- Raise Shield is still the automatic pick. With it: 0 deaths in 20 minutes before wave 5 boss, lowest health 100% for whole stretches. Without it, same waves: dead in minutes. The slow costs about 6%: 47 Missile casts per 300s with it, 50 without. I could not feel that.
- manaCost as a damage stat. Mana sat at 92% average for most of the run, so every manaCost point was free damage. Spellbook's +12 became +32 after multipliers.
## Too weak
- Magical Specialist's downside (see above). Strike as a filler: 2% of run damage. Health Regeneration at 7/s once hits are 40 or more: up 93% of the time and irrelevant.
## Bugs or oddities
- "Auras on you: Curse 11%" does not name the owner; auras on enemies do.
- Killing blow "Giant Bat Slash for 4" at wave 5 enemy 7 hides the real cause (poison 119 that stretch). The death line should name the damage that did the work.
- 35m to 43m: mana averaged 17% and Fireball got 11 casts against 52 the stretch before and after. One Mana Efficiency level (+3/s) fixed it entirely. That cliff is unreadable from the output.
- Speed read -13 in `status` at 15m with 5 shield stacks and +4 speed from gear; I expected -1.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make Raise Shield's cost bite: 3 speed per stack, or make each stack add to the mana cost of other spells. At 1 speed per stack it is 6% for immunity through wave 4.
2. Make Magical Specialist cost something a shield does not cover: reduce shield by 25% too, or give up health regen. Health alone is free for shield builds.
3. Give the first four bosses one hit each that tests the build, the way Inferno does. Their traits (Fortitude, Lifesteal) only lengthen the fight.
4. Cap or telegraph Frost Queen's Frostbolt (133 against 64 typical). A boss one-shot should be something a piece can answer; an ice resist or "first hit after full shield is halved" item would do it.
5. Add a caster piece that turns spare mana into something (shield from overflow below tier 4, or an aura that burns mana for speed). At 92% mana for half the run I had nothing to spend it on.
