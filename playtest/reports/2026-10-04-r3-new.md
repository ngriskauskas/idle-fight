# r3-new: fresh character, 25 minutes, speed/fast-attack build with poison on crit
## Run
- Seed 801, fresh start, 25m00s game time, 12 `run` calls. Ended level 9 (level 9 points unspent, it arrived in the last stretch), highest world 1 wave 9 (died to its boss, Dragon Lord). Deaths 3. 326 kills (13.0/min), 36 drops.
- Not tested, so not rated: any magic spell, shield talents, War Cry, tier 3+ talents, enemy-targeted auras. Only two player auras used (Regeneration, Battle Cry).
## Build
- Idea: many fast weak hits that feed on-hit and on-crit pieces. Picked because r1 was War Cry and r2 was shield, and Quick Strike's text ("triggers on-hit effects often") promised a build that plays differently.
- End: Strike, Poison Stab, Quick Strike, Battle Cry. Talents: Quickstep 3, Warrior Training 2, Critical Toxin, Adrenaline Surge. Gear: rare Plate Armor (onHit speedBoost 6), Battleaxe, Arcane Shield, Swift Boots, Ring of Speed, epic Plate Leggings.
- Course change at 14:28: a common Plate Armor with "onHit -> speedBoost 6" dropped. That one item became the build; poison became a side dish (15-20% of damage all run).
## Level-by-level choices
- L2 (0:50): Quickstep over Warrior Training. A real choice on paper, but I could not see what "+3 speed" does. Measured: Strike every 4.7s instead of 5.0s. Felt like nothing.
- L3 (2:05): Quickstep 2 + Poison Stab (2). Weighed Slash (1). Poison did 121 status on top of 276 direct in its first stretch, so it paid.
- L4 (3:11): Quickstep 3. Weighed Iron Constitution; health was not at risk yet (lowest 73-80%) so I stayed greedy. Strike down to 4.4s.
- L5 (5:18): Warrior Training. Gave up on Quickstep: +2/+15% on attack 8 is about +40% damage, +3 speed was about +5%. Not a choice once you know the numbers.
- L6 (10:03): Warrior Training 2 + Quick Strike (3 points saved since L4). The best pick of the run: kills 5.3/min -> 12.3/min, and Quick Strike did 53% of direct damage at once.
- L7 (14:28): real choice between Critical Toxin, Adrenaline Surge, Frostbite and Rampage; all four fit a fast hitter. Took Critical Toxin for the Poison Stab pair. This was the only level where I wanted three things.
- L8 (21:08): Adrenaline Surge, and swapped Regeneration for Battle Cry (2). Weighed Widened Focus (2 points, would have needed saving). Verdict: 3 real choices in 7 levels (L3, L7, L8); L2 and L4 were blind, L5 and L6 obvious.
## How it felt
- 0-10m: still dull, but no longer safe. 4.8-9.1 kills/min, one spell doing 73-100% of damage. Lowest health per stretch 80%, 73%, 75%, 40%, then death at 9:00 (Orc Warlord, Lightning Bolt 21 into 62 health). Health at risk from wave 2 on (Skeletal Crawler Ice Spike 12 into 40 health). Better than r1/r2 here.
- 10-14m: satisfying. Quick Strike landed, re-cleared waves 1-3 in 2m30s and beat Orc Warlord on the retry. Wave 4 mobs took me to 15% health. This felt earned.
- 14:28-25m: the game fell over. With the Plate Armor trigger, Quick Strike went from one hit per 0.9s to one per 0.5s, then 0.25s after Adrenaline Surge + Battle Cry (942 hits in 232s; Strike 476 hits, one per 0.49s against a 5.0s cast). Damage per stretch 4188 -> 6618 -> 7386 -> 28,511. Health average 99-100%.
- New furthest waves at 3:01, 5:51, 12:30, 14:45, 15:58, 21:48, 22:29, 23:22. Waves 7, 8 and 9 fell in 95 seconds, first try, without a fight. Not earned.
- Bosses: Goblin King formality (lowest 75%, 10s kill; the doubled health did not show). Bone Warden formality (first try at level 4). Orc Warlord a real wall at level 5 (one death, Fortitude up only 5-7%, no stalemate), then a fight on the retry. Shadow Assassin and Inferno Lord formalities, both first try inside 90s (Inferno 63 into 80 health + 10 shield was close to a one-shot, but only one landed). Frost Queen, Storm Titan, Plague Master: formalities. Dragon Lord: Inferno 101 into 108 health, a stat check.
## Enemies
- Asked something: Orc Warlord (burst before I had health), Dark Mage + Necromancer on wave 4 (Curse's -8 speed was on me 68% of one stretch and slowed Quick Strike from 0.9s to 1.5s; the only enemy effect that hit my build's actual strength, and I liked it), Dragon Lord (needs health or fire defence).
- Only numbers: Goblin, Bat, Rat, Slime, Spider, Zombie, Goblin King, Bone Warden, and every boss from wave 4 to 8 once the speed loop ran. Poison was the steady tax again (202 status damage taken).
## Too strong
- Plate Armor "onHit -> speedBoost 6" (a common drop) with Quick Strike: every hit pushes every spell forward, so hits make hits. Status showed speed 6-16 while real cast rates were 4x to 10x the listed times. Adrenaline Surge and Battle Cry (+20 crit, 94% uptime) stack onto the same loop: 24.1 kills/min and three new bosses in 95s.
- Speed is a flat cut to cast time (about 0.08s per point), so it is worth 3x more on a 2.5s spell than on a 5s one. Quick Strike at speed 17 cast every 0.9s.
## Too weak
- Quickstep alone, levels 2-4: three points for a 12% faster Strike. Critical Toxin: poison 1440 -> 1977 per 200s (+37%), about +6% total damage; it worked but did not change how the build played.
- Starting Regeneration: 25 mana per 7s against 2/s regen left me "too low to cast" 94-100% of the time for 20 minutes, for 2-3 health a second. Mage gear was 12 of 36 drops on a build with no use for it.
## Bugs or oddities
- Status speed (6, then 16) says nothing about the real rate once speedBoost triggers exist; the catalog never says speed is flat seconds off a cast, or what "speedBoost 6" means.
- The level 8 Plate Armor trigger is the same strength at common L3 and rare L5; the trigger does not care about rarity.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. (Important) Break the speedBoost loop in the pieces: make the Plate Armor trigger and Adrenaline Surge push only the spell that hit or crit, or give speedBoost a short internal cooldown (once a second). Keep the idea; it was the most fun moment of the run for about four minutes, then it deleted waves 5-9.
2. (Important) Give Quick Strike a floor, such as a minimum 1.0s cast, or make speed a percentage. Flat speed on a 2.5s spell is the root of item 1.
3. (Important) Make Quickstep readable and worth a point early: show "Strike 5.0s -> 4.7s" in the talent text, and either raise it to +5 or give rank 1 a small on-hit rider. Levels 2-4 were blind picks.
4. (Minor) Regeneration at 25 mana eats the whole mana bar for a fresh character. Drop it to 15 mana or make it last 14s so Battle Cry at level 5-6 is a choice and not a rescue.
5. (Minor) Critical Toxin needs poison worth multiplying: let Poison Stab apply 8, or have Critical Toxin also add 2 poison on crit, so the pair can be a build and not a 6% bonus. Beyond these I am not short of things to change: the first ten minutes are improved, the problem has moved to minutes 14-25.
