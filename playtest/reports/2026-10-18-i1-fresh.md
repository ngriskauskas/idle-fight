# i1-fresh: physical bleed/crit, economy focus

## Run
Seed 1101, fresh character, 40m00s of game time in 12 `run` calls. Ended at level 9, highest wave W1-10, with 5 deaths and 555 kills (13.9/min overall).

## Build
Final setup: Strike, Slash, Rend, Battle Cry and Ice Spike. Talents: Warrior Training 4, Iron Constitution 1, Deep Wounds, Widened Focus. Gear: a bought rare Battleaxe (+66 crit multi), a Greatsword, a legendary Iron Helm, Heavy Boots, Plate Leggings from the smith, the Amulet of Plague from the smith and a legendary Ring of Elements. I started with Strike/Slash/Regen and added Rend at level 5. Deep Wounds came at 11m. At 16m Battle Cry replaced Regen. At 28m I put Regen in the Widened Focus slot, and that was a mistake: Battle Cry uptime fell from 97% to 12% and kills/min dropped from 18 to 13. I replaced it with Ice Spike to test a magic spell.

## How it felt
- 0-11m: steady growth. New furthest waves at 2m, 7m, 8m and 14m. I died to the Bone Warden (W2) and to the Shadow Assassin (W4) poison stab. Both deaths felt fair.
- 11-24m: stuck at W5. The Inferno Lord hit me with a 70-73 Inferno when I had 63-77 HP, so I died twice. That was a gear wall, and more HP from drops (130 HP) is what got me past it at 24m. Waves 6 and 7 then fell within 90s, until the Storm Titan's 83 Lightning Bolt killed me.
- 28-33m: re-clearing W1-6 with no new furthest wave, and Regen was eating my mana. This was the dull stretch.
- 33-40m: buying the Battleaxe was the moment the build clicked. Attack went from 43 to 80 and crit multi from x1.5 to x2.16. Direct damage per 5 minutes went from 8.3k to 27.4k. W8, W9 and W10 fell in 3 minutes with no deaths, beating the Storm Titan, Plague Master and Dragon Lord. That felt great, but it came from one shop item and not from a choice I made in the build.
- Kills/min by stretch: 5, 9, 9, 10, 12.5, 15, 13, 24 (Greatsword equipped), 18, 14, 13, 13, 8 (W10).

## Economy (the focus)
- **Gold over time:** 0 (2m), 0 (4m), 3 (7m), 45 (11m), 82 (16m), 103 (18-23m), 203 (23m), 218 (33m), 80 after the purchase, 183 (38m), 254 (40m). Every coin came from selling. Gold arrives in bursts when I clean out the inventory, never from fighting.
- **First purchase:** the rare Battleaxe for 138 gold at 33m. It was the single biggest power spike of the run. Rares (36-138) and epics (90-342) are affordable. Legendaries (438-858) were barely out of reach, and merchant uniques (450, then 879, 1098, 1716) were never close. The unique price rises every time I set a new furthest wave, so I fell further behind as I improved.
- **Missed item:** the epic Battleaxe (177) I was saving for vanished at the 10-minute restock. Restocking a wanted item away is frustrating unless the restock timer is visible, which it is. OK.
- **Sell vs smith:** commons sell for 2-8, which is basically nothing, so smithing them is always right. I ran 9 smiths. Six of them were useful: the forged Arcane Shield (+13% damage) beat my drop, and I also got the Iron Helm, the Amulet of Plague (it filled an empty slot and added bleed), the Greatsword (damage 19) and the Plate Leggings. Three were junk: a Mage Hood and two items I sold. Taking the item level from the best input feels good. It is a low-stakes gamble that is always worth it for commons. Rares and above sell for 15-47 and legendaries for 286-447, so I sold those for gold.
- **Merchant uniques:** Deathbringer Plate (+bleed 3, +14 damage) was exactly my build, but at 1098 gold I could never test it. I don't trust the Martyr's Plate or the Overflowing Chalice without Toxic Blood or a mana build. The Chalice's -23% damage is a trap for a physical build. The Miser's Ring at -14% damage is a trap too. I bought none, so I can't rate how they play.
- **Shop wares:** wares were relevant to my stage. They were mostly weapons, at 4 of the 6 slots across restocks.

## Combinations
- **Rend + Deep Wounds:** bleed went from 980 to 2342 over similar 4-5 minute stretches (+140%). It is a real pairing.
- **Deep Wounds + Battle Cry + crit-multi Battleaxe:** bleed went from 1.7k to 4.8k and direct damage from 8.3k to 27.4k. The pieces stack, but the build plays the same, just with bigger numbers.
- **Missing:** I wanted Rampage or Blood Trail. Widened Focus took my 2 talent points instead.

## Enemies
- **Readable bosses:** the Inferno Lord and the Storm Titan are one big caster hit (70-83) against my HP. They taught me "stack HP", which is a clear lesson but always the same one.
- **Shadow Assassin:** the poison stab and Lifesteal made it more distinct.
- **Filler:** Curse on me (8-27% uptime) never felt noticeable.
- **Blur:** waves W1-W4 blur together on re-clears.

## Too strong / too weak
- **Too strong:** the shop Battleaxe for 138 gold, a rare that tripled my damage. Rare weapons with two crit-multi lines (+51 and +15) are very cheap for what they do.
- **Too weak:** Ice Spike did 770 damage over 51 casts (1% of damage). Health Regen in a 5th slot starves Battle Cry of mana. Legendary "speed +21%" did nothing, because base speed is 2.

## Bugs or oddities
- **Repeated stats:** items still show the same stat twice even after this change. Greatsword "speed -1, speed +2, healthLeech +2, healthLeech +1"; forged Iron Helm "health +9, health +10"; Battleaxe "critMultiplier +51 ... +15"; Heavy Boots "defense +7 ... defense +12, health +11 ... health +35". Either the base stats and secondaries collide, or the change is not working.
- **Doubled line:** one `status` printed "equipped:" and the body line twice (at 33m29s).
- **Speed shown differently:** speed showed 2 in `status` but -6 in `summary`, on the same gear.

## Suggestions
1. Let kills drop a trickle of gold, so the shop is something to work toward between sell sprees.
2. Cut merchant unique prices, or stop scaling them with furthest wave, so one of them is reachable in about 40 minutes. Right now they are display pieces.
3. Fix the duplicate stat rolls, or label base and bonus stats.
4. Smith 4 rares into a choice of 2 results, so smithing feels like building and not only a gamble.
5. Give Ice Spike or a cheap magic spell a physical-hybrid hook, such as Rimewalker-style shatter on crit, so it isn't just dead weight in a slot.
