# k3-phys: physical, bleed and kill-chaining, first hour

## Run
Seed 401, fresh character, 60m00s game time, 16 `run` calls. Level 10 (1021/2560 xp), highest world 1 wave 8 (enemy 7), 12 deaths, 960 kills (16.0/min), 119 drops.
## Build
- Spells (5/5): Slash, Rend, Execute, Strike, Health Regeneration. Talents (9): Warrior Training 3, Iron Constitution 3, Rampage 2, Bloodlust 1.
- Gear: two Warhammers (+20, +18), Plate Armor (on-hit speed boost 6), legendary Ring of Vitality, Ring of Poison (bleed +1), leech amulet. End: 148 health, 86 attack (149 with War Cry stacks), 24 defense.
- Path: Slash L2, Rend L5, Rampage at L7 and L8 (before Bloodlust, to measure it cleanly), Execute at L9 replacing Strike, Strike back in the L10 slot, Bloodlust at L10. No Rampage 3: the L9 point went to health because bosses were one-shotting me.
## How it felt
- 0-5 min: I dropped Health Regeneration for a second attack at L2 and died 4 times on wave 1 (40 health, Goblin Slash 7). With it back: zero deaths. It is mandatory, not a choice; up 87% of the run, mana 89% full with nothing else to spend it on.
- 5-17 min: steady, a new wave every 2 minutes, one death (Dark Mage Lightning Bolt 32 into 59 health, wave 4).
- 17-31 min: stuck on the wave 6 boss for 14.5 minutes, 2 deaths. Rampage arrived here, so each lap was visibly quicker and the wall felt fine.
- 31-60 min: wave 7 boss killed once (42m), then it killed me 3 more times. Wave 8 seen for 90 seconds. The last 18 minutes were laps with no new ground.
- Kills/min per stretch: 3.8, 10.2, 12.0, 9.5, 14.8, 10.0, 21.0, 17.5, 15.8, 8.8, 18.6, 9.6, 20.1, 27.6, 27.0, 21.9. About 2.4 hits per kill throughout; waves 1-3 were one-hit kills by minute 50.
- Rampage, seconds per wave on re-clears (w2/w3/w4/w5/w6): none 45/80/116/99; level 1 43/42/68/79; level 2 28/38/57/85; level 2 with Execute replacing Strike 42/52/66/100/114; level 2 with 5 slots and +12 weapon damage 24/26/38/51/54; plus Bloodlust 27/26/40/36/55. Gear changed between laps, but waves 3-4 roughly halved across the two Rampage points. Waves 1-6 took 31 minutes the first time and about 3.5 at the end. Yes, it feels like speed-clearing. It does nothing on bosses, which is where I was stuck.
## Fun
- Rampage is the best thing in the run. Dying stopped feeling like a punishment once the lap was under 4 minutes.
- Rampage or Bloodlust at L7-8 was a real choice. The 50m lap (27.6 kills/min, no deaths, Frost Queen down in 54s) was the moment the build clicked.
- Bleed has a clear job: 24-29% of my damage in the three stretches spent on wave 6-7 bosses, 4-7% in pure re-clear stretches (12% overall). Slow and sustained, as DESIGN.md says.
## Not fun
- Execute made re-clears slower when it replaced Strike (w2 28s to 42s, w3 38s to 52s): it overkills trash, and one 12s attack gives Rampage less to chain than a 6s one. It only paid off as a fifth attack. Four spell points for that felt bad.
- Bloodlust did not change clear times. War Cry showed 89-90% uptime and 15 stacks, but trash already died in one hit and the stacks are gone after the first boss hit.
- Every death after minute 17 but one was a boss one-shot or two-shot. Points at L2-6 and L9 were forced into damage and health; no decision.
- 119 drops, about 20 equipped. Roughly half were mana gear a physical build cannot use.
## Enemies
- Goblin King, Bone Warden, Orc Warlord: fair, forgettable; I noticed no trait on any. Bone Warden's Ice Spike 21 was the only number I registered.
- Shadow Assassin (wave 4): the one trait I noticed. Curse sat on me 14-25% of each stretch (speed -11) and wave 4 was the slowest early wave (116s). Fair; made me want speed.
- Inferno Lord (wave 5): Inferno 80, Fireball 37. Hurt, never killed me. Trait not noticed.
- Frost Queen (wave 6): Ice Spike 90, Frostbolt 123 into 103-120 health. 3 deaths; beaten at 151 health. Asked only for more health; a dice roll on her crit, not a fight.
- Storm Titan (wave 7, 1287 health): Lightning Bolt 119, Chain Lightning 125 into 148-151 health. 3 deaths, 1 kill. Same complaint: nothing physical answers it except more health.
- Regular enemies that mattered: Dark Mage (202 damage in one stretch, one kill), Bandit, and Berserker (Whirlwind got the wave 8 kill). The rest blurred.
- Enemy auras are listed (Lifesteal 1-4%, Fortitude 0-1%, Arcane Boost 1-8%) without an owner, so I could not tie traits to bosses.
## Too strong
- Rampage at 1 point per level in tier 2: two points roughly halved waves 3-4. Fun, keep it.
- Flat weapon damage: two hammers took attack from 49 to 86 and cut w3-w5 by about a third on their own.
## Too weak
- Execute at 4 points as a replacement attack. Bloodlust on a build that takes boss hits. Rend's direct damage (21% of the total over 628 hits); only bleed on bosses justifies it.
- Low-level legendaries: legendary Leather Armor L1 (defense +3) loses to a common Plate Armor L5 (defense +12, health +22).
## Bugs or oddities
- No bugs, no harness errors. Mid-wave Storm Lord and Frost Lord at wave 8 have boss-like names and boss-like hits (Chain Lightning 62).
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Give Bloodlust a boss payoff: keep half the War Cry stacks on health loss. Today it is spent before the fight that needs it.
2. Give physical builds an answer to magic burst other than Iron Constitution: a cheap aura that spends the idle mana on a per-hit damage cap or a magic shield.
3. Make Execute want Rampage: refund part of its cast on a kill, or bonus damage to targets above 50% health so it opens on bosses instead of overkilling trash. Or cost 3.
4. Let Rampage carry into the boss: bank one push per kill in the wave, released when the boss spawns, so kill-chaining and bossing are one build.
5. Name the owner of each enemy aura in the run output and show boss traits in `status`. Bias drops away from mana gear once several damage talents are taken.
