# r2-mid: the same caster after the mana regen cut
## Run
- Seed 602, NOT a fresh start: level 8 at world 1 wave 5, 7 talent and 7 spell points, 20 drops, all spent and equipped before the first run (every `act` printed `ok:`). 25m00s game time, 11 `run` calls. Ended level 9, highest wave 8 (r1: wave 9), 2 deaths (r1: 1).
- r1 lost its first 2m30s to an unbuilt character, so r2 had 2m30s more built time and still got one wave less far.
## Build
- Same as r1 for 17m20s: Arcane Affinity 4, Mana Efficiency 2, Magical Specialist; Arcane Missile, Fireball, Raise Shield, Health Regeneration; Arcane Staff + Shield L5, Dragon Scale Mail, Iron Helm, Plate Leggings, Enchanted Boots, Amulet of Mana, Rings of Power and Defense. Mana 247, regen 24/s (r1: 41/s), mana cost 60.
- At 10m00s, after the first death: Iron Helm L6 and Tower Shield L7 (defense 37 to 54, shield 12 to 73, speed 2 to -11).
- At level 9 (17m20s): Arcane Affinity 5 (cost 69) and Ice Spike in place of Health Regeneration, to see if a third nuke would finally spend the mana.
## How it felt
- With r1's four spells the regen cut changed nothing I could feel. Mana averaged 83 to 90% (r1: 92 to 96%) and was too low to cast 0% of the time in all 8 stretches. Damage per 150s was 5130 to 6278, the same band as r1 (5400 to 6660). Mana cost is still free damage on that loadout.
- The price only appeared when I added a third damage spell. Direct damage jumped to 8341, 8550 and 8760 per stretch (+55%), mana fell to 49%, 47% and 36% average, and I was too low to cast 1%, 1% and 3% of the time. That is close to the edge but not over it: a fourth nuke or Arcane Affinity 6 would starve. So the stat now has a price, but only for a build greedier than r1's.
- That swap was the one good decision of the run and it cut both ways: Storm Titan died (20m23s) and wave 8 opened, but with no regen my health averaged 29% (lowest 14%) and I died at wave 8 enemy 9 to poison and lightning ticks, not a hit. Trading sustain for damage felt like a real choice. Not dull, not starved.
- Raise Shield still costs this build almost nothing in mana: 97 to 100% uptime at 5 stacks in every stretch, including the 36% mana one. Its slow is invisible next to gear: Tower Shield's speed -8 cost about 8% damage (5677 to about 5240 average).
- Pace: wave 5 to 7 in 4m32s with no fight (Inferno Lord and Frost Queen first try), then Storm Titan was a real wall for 13 minutes, then wave 8 killed me in two minutes. Re-clear of waves 1 to 5 took about 5 minutes at 100% health both times; 43 kills in the last stretch with huge overkill.
## Enemies
- Inferno Lord (wave 5): Inferno 56 then 49 (r1: 64), 98, 67 and 18 total damage in three fights. Lowest health 83%, then 100% twice. Still asks nothing; the nerf made a non-event smaller.
- Frost Queen (wave 6): Frostbolt 85, 255 damage, lowest 32% in the first fight. With the Tower Shield: 132 damage, lowest 85%. The answer was plain defense and shield.
- Storm Titan (wave 7): the boss of the run. First try survived at 47% but did not finish it inside the stretch; it then killed me with Chain Lightning 78 at full Raise Shield stacks. Lightning status did 142 through the shield. Second visit, with Tower Shield, helm and the third nuke, won in under a minute of boss time. My answer was killing it faster, and I changed three things at once again, so I cannot split the credit.
- Plague Master (wave 8): never reached. Wave 8 regulars (Cursed Knight 107, Dark Mage 72, poison 110, lightning 116 in status) drained me with no regen. Status damage ignoring defense is the real counter to shield stacking, and I liked that.
- Curse was on me 14 to 39% of the time in waves 5 to 7; still no source named.
## Too strong
- Raise Shield: 100% uptime, 5 stacks, 15 mana, even on a build at 36% mana.
- Arcane Affinity: one point took cost 60 to 69, about +15% on every spell, with no visible mana strain on the two-nuke loadout.
- Tower Shield L7 on a caster: +17 defense and +61 shield for 8% damage turned Frost Queen from 32% lowest to 85%.
## Too weak
- Mana Efficiency 2 and the mana pool: at 83 to 90% mana on r1's loadout they bought nothing. They only start to matter with three nukes.
- Fireball next to Ice Spike: 8.5s cast for 2560 against Ice Spike's 2841 at 7.5s; its fire added only 168 to 951 per stretch.
- The 11 new drops after wave 5 were almost all physical or shield pieces; Mage Hood L5 (manaCost +7) was the only caster option and cost 15 health and 9 defense.
## Bugs or oddities
- Slow from enemy ice and the source of Curse are still not in the run output; I cannot tell whether Ice Spike's ice 7 did anything to enemies either.
- A death by status prints "killing blows: status damage (poison, lightning)" with no enemy named.
- At level 9 I had 2 spell points and full slots; the only use was a swap.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. (Matters most) Raise Raise Shield's cost so it competes for mana: 15 mana per cast to about 30, or a mana regen penalty per stack. Then shield versus a third nuke is the choice the regen cut was meant to create; right now both fit.
2. Make spell mana use scale harder with the mana cost stat for auras too, or give Arcane Affinity a small regen penalty per level, so the two-nuke loadout sits near 50% mana and not 88%.
3. Give Mana Efficiency a reason: a tier 2 talent that turns spare mana regen into cast speed or shield, so regen is a build direction and not just a tax you pay when greedy.
4. Add a caster answer to status damage (an aura that cleanses or converts poison, or more value on Cloth Robes' healthRegen) so wave 8 asks for a piece and not just Health Regeneration back.
5. Name the source of auras and status kills in the run output, and give re-clear an overkill outlet (excess kill damage splashing on) so the 5 minutes back to wave 5 are not dead time.
