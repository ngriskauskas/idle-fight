# Playtest i3-poison (2026-10-04)

## Run
- Seed 99, 59m57s game time, 13 `run` calls. Level 9. Highest world 1 wave 8 (never beat its boss, Plague Master). World 2 not reached.
- 282 kills (4.7/min), 6 deaths, 41 items dropped. Damage: 51,951 direct, 43,891 poison (46% of all damage).

## Build
- Spells at end: Strike, Poison Stab, Raise Shield, Battle Cry. Regeneration was in the slot until 43m, then dropped for Battle Cry.
- Talents (8): Warrior Training 3, Shield Bearer 2, Epidemic, Critical Toxin, Adrenaline Surge.
- Gear: Warhammer L7, Tower Shield L7, Amulet of Shield L7, Plate Armor L2 (on-hit speed boost), rare Leather Leggings, Iron Helm, Enchanted Boots. Ring slots stayed empty: no ring dropped in 41 drops.
- Not reached: Poison Gas and Poison Pool. I had 9 spell points all run and spent 7 (Stab 2, Shield 3, Battle Cry 2). So the "poison" build was one spell plus two talents.

## How it felt
- 0-10m: waves 1-3, 3.6 to 6.0 kills/min, 0 deaths, about 3 hits per enemy. Poison Stab at 4m made poison 33% of damage straight away.
- 10-20m: waves 3-5. One death at 14m before the shield: wave 4 boss Shadow Assassin, one Slash for 69 against about 53 health. Raise Shield at 14m: 6.2 kills/min, 0 deaths, health never left 100%, 3.5 hits per enemy.
- 20-30m: waves 5-8, 4.8 then 3.4 kills/min, 0 deaths, 4.4 then 6.5 hits per enemy. Epidemic at 19m.
- 30-40m: stuck on wave 8. 2.8 kills/min and 7.3 hits per enemy (enemies 400 to 950 health). First loss to Plague Master (Poison Stab for 175).
- 40-50m: shield removed on purpose for 4 minutes: 3 deaths, slid from wave 8 to wave 5. Shield back plus Battle Cry: 6.0 kills/min, 2.6 hits per enemy on waves 5-7.
- 50-60m: back to wave 8 boss, died again (killing blow: poison), sent to wave 7, spent the last 5 minutes re-clearing it.
- The wall moved from waves 5-8 to exactly the wave 8 boss. The new death rule makes it feel fine: a boss loss costs about 5 minutes, not the whole climb.

## Fun
- Raise Shield coming online: shield went from 0 to 692 (+63/s), and the run changed from careful to unkillable in one purchase.
- Epidemic: poison went from 303/min to 653/min with direct damage flat (3,698 vs 3,388 per 5 minutes). Poison share rose from 29% to 49%, then 56% on bigger waves. This is the piece that makes groups melt.
- Critical Toxin: poison 948/min to 1,440/min on the same wave with 18 crits in 4 minutes; share hit 67%. Battle Cry (crit 10% to 30%) then felt like a real poison piece, not just an attack buff.

## Not fun
- With the shield up there is no danger at all for 25 minutes (health 100% average and lowest in six stretches), then one boss that kills outright. Nothing in between.
- Mana was starved 75-83% of the time from 14m to 35m (50 mana, +3/s) and Regeneration dropped to 40-75% uptime. I only fixed it by luck with boots.
- Drops were mana weapons, Arcane Shields and Spellbooks. No ring, no status amulet, no poison stat in 41 items, so "every attack applies your status stats" was never visible.

## Too strong
- Raise Shield: 1,126 to 1,307 shield and +127 to +153/s on a character with 74 health, for 3 points and 15 mana. Same gear and waves without it: 85 shield, 3 deaths in 4 minutes versus 0 or 1. Shield gear adds little on top (amulet +32, Tower Shield +38).
- Battle Cry for 2 points: direct damage per hit about 57 to 86, 96-100% uptime, and it feeds Critical Toxin.

## Too weak
- Regeneration aura and health generally: status damage is soaked by shield, so health only matters when the shield is gone, and then you are already dead.
- Warhammer swap: +26 damage but I lost the Battleaxe crit multiplier and speed; hits fell from 80 to 64 per 4 minutes and poison from 1,440/min to 938/min. Flat damage is worse than crit for this build and nothing says so.

## Bugs or oddities
- At 35-39m I died to "Plague Master Poison Stab for 175" while the stretch reported health lowest 100%. The final stretch reported lowest 0% for the same kind of death. The lowest-health figure looks unreliable.
- "Curse" shows under auras on you 35-83% of the time, and "Arcane Boost" under auras on enemies. Those two lists look swapped for enemy-cast auras.
- Raise Shield is said to stop recasting at its cap, yet mana was too low to cast 75-83% of the time while it sat at 30 stacks.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Cut Raise Shield scaling (or its regen) by roughly half, or let it decay when hit, so waves 5-8 threaten a shield build a little instead of not at all.
2. Make poison pieces reachable in the first hour: Poison Gas at 3 points, or one more spell point by level 6. One poison spell is not an archetype.
3. Add status stats to the drop table for early rings and amulets, or guarantee a ring by wave 5. Two empty slots for an hour feels broken.
4. Give Plague Master a counter the player can build toward (poison resistance on gear, or Toxic Blood earlier). Right now the shield build wins everything and then loses to one enemy.
5. Print shield lowest and average per stretch, and fix the lowest-health figure, so shield deaths are as explainable as the rest.
