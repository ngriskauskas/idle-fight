# k3-caster: ice caster, first hour (2026-10-04)
## Run
Seed 402, fresh character, 60m00s game time, 16 `run` calls. Level 9 (573/1280). Highest: world 1 wave 7, Storm Titan killed at 59m59s. 4 deaths. 493 kills (8.2/min), 64 drops.
## Build
- End: Arcane Missile, Ice Spike, Fireball, Raise Shield. Talents: Arcane Affinity 3, Mana Efficiency 2, Iron Constitution 1, Magical Specialist, Frostbite 1. Gear: unique Robe of Elements (ice +3, fire +2, lightning +2 on every hit), Tower Shield L7, Spellbook L3, epic Mage Hood, defense/health filler.
- Path: Strike -> Arcane Missile at L2, Ice Spike at L4, Magical Specialist + Raise Shield at L7 (14m), Frostbite + Fireball (replacing Health Regen) + Robe + Tower Shield at 49m.
- Stats at end: health 61, shield 115, defense 32, mana 231 (+24/s), mana cost 38. Hits about 105 each; wave 7 enemies (~330 hp) take 3 casts, Storm Titan (1287 hp) about 12.
## How it felt
- 0-14m: brisk. A level every 1-5 minutes, each with an obvious next buy. Kills/min 3.8, 7.4, 8.3, 6.8, 11.6, 11.1. Died to the wave 3 boss (Orc Warlord Lightning Bolt 56 into 44 hp) and the wave 4 boss (Shadow Assassin Slash 20 after a Poison Stab 30).
- 14-27m: Raise Shield turned the game off. The next 6m40s had health at 100% average and 100% lowest across waves 2-5 and three bosses. Kills/min fell from 9.2 to 4.5 at waves 5-7 because enemies got fatter, not because anything threatened me.
- 27-60m: stuck on the wave 7 boss for 32 minutes. Two deaths, both the same blow: Storm Titan Chain Lightning 124 (its normal hit was 49-50). Each death cost a 10-11 minute re-clear with no risk at all (lowest health 87-100%), so most of this half hour was waiting to retry one coin flip.
- Out of mana: 21% of the time at L2, 0% after one Mana Efficiency, 31-33% for 11 minutes after Magical Specialist raised mana cost 22 -> 38, then 0% again after a second Mana Efficiency (+14/s -> +24/s). One point fixes it completely; after that mana sat at 87-95% doing nothing.
- Re-clear times: death at 7m30 -> back past wave 3 in about 4m; death at 13m50 -> past wave 4 in 5m40; death at 33m20 -> wave 7 in 10m40; death at 48m20 -> Titan dead in 11m40.
## Fun
- The Robe of Elements drop was the best moment: one item made every cast apply ice, fire and lightning. Fire status went from 0 to 1384 damage in the next 8 minutes (about 10% of my damage) for free.
- Magical Specialist plus Raise Shield in the same level-up felt like a real power spike.
- Finally killing Storm Titan in the last second after swapping to Tower Shield.
## Not fun
- Ice did nothing I could measure. Storm Titan without Ice Spike: I absorbed 754 damage before dying. With Ice Spike: died after 174 (Lightning Bolt 50 + Chain Lightning 124). Wave 6-7 stretches: about 385 damage taken/min with Ice Spike, about 330/min without (different enemies, so noise). What kills me is one big hit, and a slow does not make a hit smaller. Frostbite 1 + Robe (4 extra ice per cast) showed no visible change either.
- Magical Specialist's health cost was not a cost. Health went 60 -> 45, then Raise Shield added 81 shield and I took zero health damage for 6m40s. The only cost I felt was the mana cost jump, fixed by one point.
- Level 9 gave 2 spell points and no slot to put anything in. I swapped out Health Regen for Fireball just to use them. From L8 on a level takes 10+ minutes, so there was no decision for long stretches.
- Re-clears are 10 minutes of nothing. Waves 1-5 never touch me.
## Enemies
- Goblin King (w1): Slash 15, Rend 10. Trait not noticed. Fair, a non-event.
- Bone Warden (w2): Ice Spike 22, Poison Stab 8. Its ice on me was not visible in my numbers. Fair.
- Orc Warlord (w3): Lightning Bolt 56 one-shot me at 44 hp; made me buy Iron Constitution. Fortitude showed on enemies around here, probably its trait; did not slow the kill noticeably. Good first wall.
- Shadow Assassin (w4): Poison Stab 30 then Slash 20-39; Lifesteal aura seen on enemies. Killed me once; trivial after shield.
- Inferno Lord (w5): Inferno 75-80, once 153. Never killed me because of the shield, but the 153 took me to 3% health. Trait not noticed.
- Frost Queen (w6): Ice Spike 85, Frostbolt 60; the most total damage of any enemy (1425). Long fight (933 hp), survivable every time. Trait not noticed.
- Storm Titan (w7): the wall. Chain Lightning 124 twice, both fatal; normal hits 43-50. It asked for effective health above about 175, which Tower Shield (shield 81 -> 115, defense 19 -> 32) gave. Unfair in that the 124 looks like a crit I cannot see coming or counter except with more shield.
- Regular enemies: Frost Wolf (Frostbolt 30-54) and Storm Elemental (Lightning Bolt 18-40, 748 lightning status over the run) were the only ones I noticed. Mid-wave casters are no longer the killers: all 4 deaths were to bosses. Curse was on me 6-25% of the time and I never saw what it changed.
## Too strong
- Raise Shield: 100% uptime at 10 stacks for 81-115 shield on a 45-61 health character, mana cost irrelevant. Still an automatic pick. Its -2% damage per stack did not show in my numbers at all (Arcane Missile averaged 62 before, 106 after with mana cost 22 -> 38); I suspect the penalty is on `damage`, which Magical Specialist already sets to 0, so it is free for casters.
- Tower Shield L7 (defense +21, shield +37, speed -8): the speed loss was invisible, kills/min unchanged (11.1 vs 10.1 on the re-clear).
- Robe of Elements: three statuses on every cast for one slot. Fun-strong, keep it.
## Too weak
- Ice Spike's slow, Frostbite 1: no measurable effect (see above). Ice Spike is fine as damage (40% of my total) but the ice is decoration.
- Health Regen aura once a shield exists: removed it, nothing changed.
- Mana past the second Mana Efficiency: 90% full with no way to spend it; Mana Overflow is 15 points away.
## Bugs or oddities
- Hits average about 105 where 2 x (5 + 38) = 86 plus crits predicts about 90. Something adds roughly 15-20% that I cannot find in `status`.
- Run output lists "killed boss Storm Titan" at 59m59s while `status` still shows "Storm Titan L7 11/1287hp" in front of me.
- Boss traits are not named anywhere in the run output; I could only guess from "auras on enemies" (Fortitude, Lifesteal, Arcane Boost) and never saw a boss shield, regeneration or which boss cursed me.
- Storm Titan and Inferno Lord occasionally hit for 2-2.5x their normal damage (124 vs 49, 153 vs 75). If those are crits the output does not say so.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make Raise Shield cost a caster something: charge the per-stack penalty on mana cost (which is caster damage) as well as damage, or make each stack cost mana regen. Then shield versus damage is a choice for every build.
2. Make ice reduce what kills you: let ice stacks also cut the damage of the frozen enemy's next cast (say 3% per stack), or have Frostbite delay the cast by a flat amount per hit. A slow that cannot stop a 124 hit is not control.
3. Make Magical Specialist's health cost bite: also halve shield from auras, or take the 25% from health and shield both. Right now 3 spell points erase it.
4. Give bosses a visible tell and name the trait in the output. Storm Titan's big Chain Lightning should be a slow, announced cast that ice or Exhaustion can push back, so control is the answer and not only more shield.
5. Give spare points and mana somewhere to go early: a cheap tier 2 talent that spends surplus mana (for example, casts cost 50% more and hit 50% harder while above 80% mana), and let Widened Focus be reachable at L6 so spell points never sit without a slot.
