# Playtest i1-magic (short early-game caster test)
## Run
Seed 22, 25m00s game time, 12 `run` calls. Level 8. Highest: world 1 wave 6. 13 deaths, 405 kills (16.2/min), 46 drops. Out of mana 56% of the run.
## Build
Ended: Arcane Missile, Fireball, Ice Spike, Battle Cry. Talents: Arcane Affinity 3, Mana Efficiency 3, Magical Specialist. Gear: Arcane Staff + Wand, Silk Leggings, Enchanted Boots, Plate Armor L2, Iron Helm, Amulet of Shield. Mana 172 (+18/s), mana cost 32, health 60, attack 0. Not tested: War Cry, Raise Shield, any AoE spell, tier-3 talents, rings (none dropped).

- Minutes 0-5 were the worst: 4 deaths on wave 1 with 40 health and no gear, 2-3 Strikes per 15hp Goblin, first boss at 5:39.
- Minutes 6-16: stuck between waves 1 and 3 (6 deaths), each death about a minute of re-clearing waves I one-shot.
- Minutes 16-19 took off: waves 3 to 6, no deaths. Then died twice at exactly wave 6 enemy 6 (Necromancer 104, Berserker 62, Knight 57 damage against 60 health) and never passed it.
- Hits to kill: 2-3 at the start, 1-2 by minute 8 (Fireball ~40 vs 18-27hp), 1 at the end (Fireball/Ice Spike ~110, Missile ~51 vs 28-50hp). Late on, survival and re-clear time were the limit, not damage.
- Verdict on the rebalance: a caster can progress in 25 minutes, but only because a Staff dropped at about minute 14; before that it was no better than Strike with a Battleaxe. L1-L2 items stayed useful through wave 6. Plate Armor's speed boost of 6 was not noticeable. Battle Cry held 84% uptime at 1 stack without draining mana on recasts.
- Mana cost is no longer a pure trap, but it is conditional. Cost 9 -> 18 with regen 6 -> 9 was flat. Cost 18 -> 32 with regen 15 gave +62% damage and +42% kills/min. It only pays when cast time, not mana, was the limit.
## Fun
- The Staff + Wand swap: 88% out of mana to 7%, and three new waves in under three minutes. A real "build clicks" spike. Mana cost as a damage dial: each point trades uptime for hit size, which is a real tradeoff.
## Not fun
- Out of mana most of the time (up to 91%). The caster mostly stands there. Dying on wave 1 at level 2 with nothing to do about it, then re-walking trivial waves after every later death.
- Magical Specialist was a non-event the day I took it: damage 2856 -> 2673, kills 20.3 -> 18.8/min.
## Too strong
- Arcane Staff (common L2: mana +32, regen +2, mana leech +3). With the Wand it was worth more than all 7 talent points.
- Battleaxe crit multiplier: +47 to +60 on L1-L2 commons, x1.50 -> x2.60 with two, and it applies to spells. Not a small percent affix.
## Too weak
- Mana Efficiency: +2 regen per point against spells costing 37-40 mana. Starting regen of 2 feeds one 5-mana Missile and nothing else.
- Strike after Magical Specialist: 30 hits for 45-65 damage, a dead slot until a spell point frees it. Caster drops: 4 Battleaxes and 4 Daggers before the first mana weapon.
## Bugs or oddities
- "damage taken" does not add up to the deaths: 8:34-11:14 had 2 deaths with every enemy listed at 0 damage; 4:26-5:53 had 1 death with 3 damage recorded. Cause not found.
- Arcane Affinity and Mana Efficiency list only a flat amount in the catalog, no 4% per level like the other tier-1 talents. A "Curse" aura showed on me for 1% of one stretch; I never cast it.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Start casters at 3-4 mana regen, or give Mana Efficiency a percent part like the other tier-1 talents.
2. Weight an early caster weapon (a Wand by level 3). The whole build hinged on one late drop.
3. Make Magical Specialist a spike on its own: +100% regen, or mana back on kill.
4. Soften minutes 0-5 and death: drop back one wave instead of to wave 1, or more starting health.
5. Fix damage-taken tracking so deaths can be diagnosed, and cut Battleaxe crit multiplier to match the small-affix rule.
