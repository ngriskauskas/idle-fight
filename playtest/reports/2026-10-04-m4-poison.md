# m4-poison: poison and crit, first hour
## Run
- Seed 502, fresh character, 60m00s game time, 15 `run` calls. Level 10, highest world 1 wave 8, 7 deaths, 776 kills (12.9/min), 117 drops.
- New furthest wave at 2:49, 8:05, 10:30, 20:56, 29:27, 47:01, 53:54 (gaps 2:49, 5:16, 2:25, 10:26, 8:31, 17:34, 6:53).
- Per stretch, kills/min (deaths): 3.3, 5.6, 8.5, 8.3 (1), 11.2, 5.8, 15.7 (1), 9.2, 11.8 (1), 11.3, 12.5 (1), 11.1 (1), 17.6 (1), 22.0 (1), 15.4.
## Build
- End: Poison Stab, Slash, Quick Strike, Battle Cry, Arcane Missile. Talents: Warrior Training 3, Iron Constitution 2, Critical Toxin, Epidemic, Rampage 2. Crit 34% x2.31, attack 36, health 146, no Raise Shield.
- Order: Poison Stab (L3), Slash for Strike (L4), Battle Cry (L6), Critical Toxin (L7), Rampage (L8), Epidemic + Quick Strike replacing Health Regeneration (L9), Rampage 2 + Arcane Missile (L10, last 70s only).
- Gear: Battleaxe (crit multiplier +33) all run, Dagger until a legendary Arcane Shield (crit +8, multiplier +11, speed +3) beat it at 42m. No poison ring or amulet dropped in 117 items.
## How it felt
- 0 to 10m: quick and gentle, three bosses down, one death (Bone Warden Ice Spike 44 into 40 health).
- 10m to 47m: the wall. Waves 5 to 7 took 36 minutes and 4 deaths. I sat on 2 unspent spell points for 16 minutes (26:50 to 42:50) because all slots were full and nothing at 2 points beat what I had. That stretch was plain waiting.
- 42:50 on: took off. Dropping Health Regeneration for Quick Strike took kills from 11.1 to 17.6/min, then 22.0. Mana "too low to cast" fell from 77% to 1% and Battle Cry uptime rose from 85% to 97%. Waves 7 and 8 fell within 11 minutes.
- Levels 2 to 6 were not choices: Warrior Training (+3 and +10% on 3 base attack) beats everything in tier 1. Level 8 (Rampage, Epidemic or Widened Focus) was the first real one.
## Fun
- Quick Strike clicking: 336 hits and 269 crits in 8 minutes against 105 crits the stretch before. The build finally played like "many small hits".
- Re-clears getting visibly shorter. Goblin King to Shadow Assassin kill (waves 2 to 4): 5:28, 4:05 (no Rampage), 3:40 (Rampage 1), 2:49 (better gear), 1:58 and 1:35 (with Quick Strike). Rampage alone was worth about 10%; Quick Strike plus Rampage halved it.
- The legendary Arcane Shield with crit on it: a real "do I leave daggers" moment.
## Not fun
- Poison never felt like the build. It was 18 to 22% of my damage all hour (23,097 poison against 103,528 direct). Slash and Quick Strike kill trash before stacks matter.
- Critical Toxin was invisible when I took it: poison per minute 183 before (9:01 to 14:01), 194 and 168 in the two stretches after. Later rises (358 with Rampage, 443 with Quick Strike and Epidemic, 972 at waves 6 to 8) arrived with other changes, so I cannot credit them to it.
- Deaths were mostly one caster spell landing on a no-regen health pool: Frostbolt 34, 52, 78 as killing blows; Inferno Lord Inferno hit 151 against 157 max health. With no shield my only answer was health and defense gear, which is a stat check, not a decision.
## Enemies
- Goblin King: Slash 15, Rend 10. Trait never showed. A speed bump after level 4.
- Bone Warden: Ice Spike 41 to 44 at wave 2 is a one-shot on a fresh character. First death, and it felt cheap rather than earned.
- Orc Warlord: Fortitude up 1 to 7% of the time, Lightning Bolt 21 to 47. The defense aura never slowed a kill noticeably; I could not tell if poison ignored it.
- Shadow Assassin: Lifesteal up 2 to 15%, Slash 37 to 39. Killed me once at 79 health. Fair, and the first boss that felt earned (10:26 gap).
- Inferno Lord: Inferno 78 one time, 151 another. That swing is unreadable. Frost Queen: Frostbolt 48 to 52, killed me once, took 17:34 to pass. Storm Titan: Chain Lightning 54, fell first try.
- Curse sat on me 14% of the run (up to 41%) and the output does not name who cast it. Frost Wolf and Frost Lord (trash) hit as hard as bosses.
- I did not see a boss shield or regeneration clearly enough in the output to judge poison against them.
## Too strong
- Quick Strike: 13,339 damage in 8 minutes, level with Slash, and it feeds every on-hit and on-crit piece. 3 points is cheap.
- Status bonuses on gear with fast hits: Dragon Scale Mail "fire +3" did 2,413 fire damage in 8 minutes; Ring of Elements "+1" did 957 fire and 957 lightning. A common body armour out-damaged half my poison.
- Arcane Missile with an Amulet of Mana (mana cost +21): 81 per hit, 30% of direct damage for 1 point, in a 70 second sample. It starved mana (27% average).
## Too weak
- Poison Stab: 6 poison on a 7.5s cast, 12 to 25% of direct damage. Critical Toxin on trash (see above). Epidemic not isolated; taken together with Quick Strike.
- Health Regeneration: cost 25 mana per 10s, fought Battle Cry for mana, and removing it raised average health to 97% once kills sped up.
## Bugs or oddities
- Deaths have no timestamp in the timeline, so re-clear time from wave 1 has to be inferred from boss kills.
- Boss traits other than auras (shield, regeneration) do not show in the run output.
- Plate Armor's on-hit boost was equipped only for the last stretch along with two other items; not judged.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make Poison Stab stack faster (cast 5s, or poison 10) so poison is the damage on bosses, not a 20% side line.
2. Give Critical Toxin something on trash: for example crits also add 2 poison, so it works before stacks exist.
3. Add poison gear. Fire, ice and lightning ride on armour and rings; poison had nothing in 117 drops.
4. Add a 2 point non-shield answer to caster burst (a spell-damage ward aura) so unshielded builds have a choice besides health gear.
5. Cap or telegraph Inferno's range (78 to 151) and lower Bone Warden's Ice Spike at wave 2; name the caster of Curse in the output.
