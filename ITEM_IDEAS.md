# Item System Ideas

## Shop System

- Ability to sell items for currency
- Combine/craft items from components
- Merge duplicates for enhanced versions

## Item Types

- **Physical Weapons**
  - Dagger (fast, low damage)
  - Axe (medium speed, medium damage)
  - Hammer (slow, high damage)
  - Shield (defense, reduces incoming damage)

- **Magic Weapons**
  - Wand (medium mana cost)
  - Staff (high mana cost, high power)
  - Magic Shield (defense, boosts shield regeneration)

## Spell Scaling

- Magic spells and auras scale off mana spent
- More mana = more damage/effect
- Encourages high mana builds

## Item Properties

- Mana cost modifier property
  - Increases/decreases spell mana costs
  - Used to balance spell builds
  - Example: "Increases spell mana cost by 20%"
  - Allows scaling spells by increasing their cost (more powerful versions)
