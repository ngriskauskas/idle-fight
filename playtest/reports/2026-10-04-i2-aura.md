# Playtest i2-aura (2026-10-04)

## Run
- Seed 66, 60m00s game time, 13 `run` calls. Level 8 (566/640 xp). Highest world 1 wave 8, ended on wave 7. 4 deaths. World 2 not reached.
- One stretch (4m00s to 6m36s) was played with no gear or talents because my own shell loop failed to apply them; its death is my error, not the game's.

## Build
- Spells: Strike, Raise Shield, Health Regeneration, Fireball. Battle Cry unlocked and tried twice, then benched.
- Talents (7): Warrior Training 2, Mana Efficiency 2, Shield Bearer 1, Arcane Affinity 1, Wildfire.
- Items: Tower Shield L2, Greatsword L6, Dragon Scale Mail L4 (fire +3), Mage Hood L8 (mana cost +15), Leather Leggings L8, Enchanted Boots L3, Amulet of Luck L7.
- I was asked for shield plus poison. Poison was not reachable: 8 spell points in the hour, Raise Shield took 3, and Poison Stab is 4, Poison Gas 5, Immolation 7, Poison Pool 9. I went fire instead (Fireball at 2, Wildfire, a fire body armour). Epidemic and Critical Toxin were never testable. No ring dropped in 60 drops; the only status gear was three Dragon Scale Mails.

## How it felt
- 0 to 10 min: wave 1 to 2. Strike only, 3.1 to 3.3 kills/min, 2 to 3 hits per kill. 2 deaths, both on wave 2. The second came right after adding Raise Shield: it recast to 30 stacks, left me out of mana 84% of the time and Regeneration up only 18%.
- 10 to 20 min: wave 2 to 4, 4.8 kills/min, 0 deaths. Two points of Mana Efficiency fixed mana (out of mana 4%), Raise Shield at 100% uptime and 30 stacks (shield 270, +30/s).
- 20 to 30 min: wave 4 to 6, 4.8 kills/min, 0 deaths. Fireball added: direct 2888, fire status 1642 (36% of all damage).
- 30 to 40 min: wave 6 to 8, 3.6 then 2.7 kills/min, 0 deaths. Wildfire added: direct 5554, status 4794 (46%). Enemies 267 to 498 health, about 6 to 9 hits each.
- 40 to 50 min: swapped Regeneration for Battle Cry and died twice in 6m40s (wave 8, then wave 4 on the walk back). Back to wave 1, then 12.9 and 8.6 kills/min re-clearing.
- 50 to 60 min: wave 6 to 7 again, 2.4 to 3.5 kills/min, 0 deaths.
- Pace is a slow grind, not a runaway. About 4 minutes per wave throughout. Never truly stuck, never took off.
- Deaths are only half explainable. Attack damage is listed "before your shield" and was small (under 300 total in the stretch with two deaths), so what kills you is status damage, which seems to skip the shield. The output does not say which hit or status finished you.

## Fun
- Wildfire was the one moment the build changed: status share went from 36% to 46% and packs of two or three melted together.
- Fireball with mana cost gear: Mage Hood L8 took Fireball from about 17 to about 73 damage per hit, overtaking Strike (53% of direct damage).
- Watching Raise Shield climb to 30 stacks felt good the first time.

## Not fun
- Poison build is locked behind 7 to 12 spell points; in an hour you get 8. The build I was told to chase could not start.
- Re-walking waves 1 to 7 after a death took about 13 minutes with nothing to decide.
- Levels 7 and 8 gave a spell point but no new slot, so the point bought a spell I had to swap out.
- 40 items in the bag, mostly level 1 to 3 commons dropped during the re-walk. A legendary Battleaxe dropped at L2 and was worse than a common L6 Greatsword.

## Too strong
- Health Regeneration (free, 25 mana): 8/s at wave 1, 38/s at wave 8 on 64 to 76 health. It refills me every 2 seconds. Removing it caused 2 deaths in 7 minutes; with it, 0 deaths in 40 minutes.
- Enemy statuses against me: 3021 poison in one 3.5 minute stretch on wave 7 (about 15/s) versus 1425 from attacks. Statuses are the whole threat.

## Too weak
- Raise Shield (3 points): 270 shield and +30/s did not stop either Battle Cry death, and the shield value stayed 268 to 270 from wave 3 to wave 8, so it does not grow with the wave like other auras. It soaks attacks only, and attacks are the minor threat.
- Battle Cry: with it instead of Fireball, 2.4 kills/min; with Fireball, 3.5 kills/min one wave higher.
- Status gear without a matching spell: fire +3 with Strike only gave 0 status damage in 34 hits (and 0 in 14 hits earlier). With Fireball it is lost in the noise.
- Shield Bearer (+3 shield) and Warrior Training (+3 damage) are flat and tiny next to wave scaling.

## Bugs or oddities
- "status bonus on every attack: fire 3" is shown on the character but Strike applied no fire at all. Either the label or the behaviour is wrong.
- Raise Shield shield stayed 270 at every wave while Regeneration grew 8 to 38.
- A death was logged in a stretch whose "lowest health" read 45%; the health sampler misses deaths.
- Raise Shield at 30 stacks keeps recasting and burning mana with no gain (84% out of mana before mana talents).
- Enemies cast Arcane Boost and Curse; Curse on me reached 72% uptime on wave 7 (speed -32 at one point).

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make one poison source cheap (Poison Stab at 2, or a 3 point poison aura) so a poison or aura build can start before level 8.
2. Let shield absorb status ticks, or at least part of them, and make Raise Shield scale with the wave. Right now regen is the only defense that matters.
3. Make item status stats apply on every attack as the status line says, so a fire or poison ring can start a build on its own.
4. Stop stacking auras from recasting at the cap, and stop spending mana on it.
5. Name the killer in the death line (which enemy, which status, how much) and drop items near the highest wave reached rather than the current one so the re-walk is not pure filler.
