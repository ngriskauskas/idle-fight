# i5-status

## Run

Fresh character, seed 817; 24m54s, level 8, highest world 1 wave 6, four deaths. No setup or carry-over.

Kill rates: 0:00-0:45, 4 kills (5.3/min); 0:45-2:09, 6 more (4.3/min); 2:09-2:58, 11 (13.5/min); 2:58-5:20, 19 (8.1/min); 5:20-9:29, 33 (8.0/min); 9:29-12:09, 55 derived from cumulative counts (20.6/min); 12:09-20:04, 99 (12.5/min); 20:04-24:54, 55 (11.4/min). Overall: 282 kills (11.3/min).

New furthest waves: wave 2 at 3:59, wave 3 at 6:18, wave 4 at 11:00, wave 5 at 12:29, wave 6 at 20:44. Deaths reset progress each time.

## Build

Combat-tested ending build: Health Regeneration, Poison Stab, Strike, Rend; Warrior Training 5, Epidemic, Deep Wounds. Dragon Scale Mail, rare Iron Helm L4, Silk Leggings, Enchanted Boots, Battleaxe, epic Arcane Shield, Amulet of Shield.

I started with Strike and Regeneration, tried Arcane Missile, then shifted to Slash and Poison Stab. At level 7 I took Epidemic; at level 8 I replaced Slash with Rend and took Deep Wounds. After the time cap I equipped an L6 helm and Ring of Elements/Ring of Power to fill the last gear slots; those final swaps were not tested.

## How it felt

The early magic stretch was scrappy: Arcane Missile dealt 128 damage over 12 hits, but mana averaged 41% and was too low to cast 22% of the time in the first 2m09. At 2m09 I had 10 kills, one poison death, and 20 poison damage taken. The next 49 seconds jumped to 13.5 kills/min; then the 2m21 stretch slowed to 8.1/min, with mana averaging 18%, starvation 25%, and Regeneration up 51%.

Poison Stab plus gear steadied the run: 5:20-9:29 produced 33 kills, 665 poison damage, 12% starvation, and 90% Regeneration uptime. The next interval cleared 55 enemies in 2m40 with no death; bosses fell rapidly. After the shield drop, 12:09-20:04 had 99 kills, 2,415 status damage, 91% mana, and no starvation. New waves fell quickly in that stretch, but the long climb from wave 5 to wave 6 took 8m15.

The final 4m50 dealt 3,375 status damage and reached wave 6 again after a reset. Overall health averaged 94%, mana 75%, starvation 7%, and Regeneration uptime 83%. Four deaths: poison at wave 1 enemy 7; Orc Warlord Lightning Bolt for 22 at wave 3 enemy 10; Inferno Lord Fireball for 29 at wave 5 enemy 10; Frost Queen Frostbolt for 66 at wave 6 enemy 10.

## Fun

The build clicked when poison became a spreading background threat, then clicked again when Rend and Deep Wounds made bleed the leading status source. Reaching wave 6 after the Inferno Lord kill felt earned; the 26-shield/3-per-second shield setup still could not erase the Frost Queen threat.

## Not fun

Five Warrior Training ranks were mostly a prerequisite tax to reach the interesting poison talent. Bosses and waves repeated enough that the 55-kill, 2m40 stretch felt like re-clearing rather than discovery. Early mana pressure made the magic spell and aura compete before the later shield/mana gear arrived.

## Combinations

- Poison Stab + Epidemic: poison dealt 2,167 damage during 12:09-20:04; total poison damage was 4,443. The spread payoff felt meaningful, but the harness does not report spread proc counts, so I cannot isolate Epidemic's share from level and gear growth.
- Rend + Deep Wounds: 40 Rend hits dealt 1,706 direct damage; bleed added 2,142 during the final 4m50. That is a distinct sustained-damage layer, not just a bigger hit.
- Dragon Scale Mail's fire-on-hit bonus bridged into the status build: total fire damage was 344. The final Ring of Elements raised the displayed fire bonus from 1 to 3 after the cap, so that extension is untested.
- Regeneration with mana-cost gear: mana cost reached 9 while regeneration was 3/s. With the Arcane Shield's larger mana pool, the late stretches had 0% starvation and 90-91% aura uptime. Early Arcane Missile plus aura was much tighter.

## Items

The epic Arcane Shield was the standout: +19 mana, +14 shield, +1 shield regen, +5 crit chance, and +12% speed. Together with the amulet it brought the displayed shield to 26 (+3/s). Silk Leggings and Enchanted Boots raised mana cost by 9 but also supplied mana, regen, and leech; the cost was manageable after the shield drop. Plate Armor's on-hit speedBoost 6 trigger was naturally encountered and worn from 5:20-12:09. Later Dragon Scale Mail replaced it for fire and shield synergy. I equipped drops across every available slot; the final ring choices were post-cap.

## Enemies

Giant Rat poison was an early invisible-feeling killer until the report named the status death. Orc Warlord's Lightning Bolt and aura, Inferno Lord's 68-damage Inferno, and Frost Queen's 66-damage Frostbolt/Ice Spike were the attacks that asked for defense; the last two were abrupt spikes, and Frost Queen ended the run. Goblin King, Bone Warden, Orc Warlord, and Shadow Assassin became familiar repeat fights. Enemy auras such as Fortitude, Lifesteal, and Arcane Boost added texture, but I did not change spells to counter them. Most regular enemies blurred into health/damage once the build accelerated.

## Too strong

Epidemic poison and Deep Wounds bleed paid off strongly: 6,929 total status damage (4,443 poison, 2,142 bleed, 344 fire) against 26,526 direct damage. The build still died four times, so it felt powerful rather than invulnerable.

## Too weak or useless

Arcane Missile was useful to test magic but accounted for only 430 of 26,526 direct damage overall and coincided with the worst mana pressure. I did not retest it after the mana gear arrived, so I would not call it universally weak.

## Bugs or oddities

The speedBoost item trigger was naturally present and equipped for 6m49, but the harness exposes no proc count or cooldown timing. I cannot verify the corrected shared one-second cooldown from aggregate kill rates. No other description mismatch stood out.

## Against DESIGN.md

The poison/bleed/fire layering matched the combination-first goal, and the mana-cost gear initially traded casting comfort for other stats. Later gear opened the build rather than merely raising damage. The five-point tier gate made the early choices feel linear, and the rapid boss re-clears briefly removed the intended sense of earned progress.

## Suggestions

1. Show trigger proc counts and cooldown suppression in run output; it would make speedBoost item choices testable.
2. Add a clearer tell or a counter window for Frost Queen's burst, or tune the paired 66-damage hits; I died at its Frostbolt after already reaching wave 6.
3. Let early poison/bleed builds reach one synergy talent with fewer generic ranks, so the interesting build arrives before the first several levels are spent on prerequisites.
4. Vary repeated boss waves with status-specific enemy mixes; the 12:09-20:04 stretch cleared 99 enemies and several repeated bosses without a death or much adaptation.
