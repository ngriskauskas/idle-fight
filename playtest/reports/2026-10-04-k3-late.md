# k3-late: sturdy status build against the late bosses of world 1

## Run
- Seed 403, 40m00s game time, 13 `run` calls of 200s. NOT a fresh start: level 10 at world 1 wave 7, 9 talent and 9 spell points, 20 drops. Ended level 10 (xp 1653/2560), highest wave 9, 3 deaths.
- My mistake, not the game's: the first 200s ran with nothing spent or equipped (a zsh quoting error made every `act` fail with `ERROR: actions: talent <id> | unlock-spell <id> | ...`). That naked character died at once (Bandit Slash 20) and is death 1. Real testing covers 3m20s to 40m00s, starting from wave 1, not wave 7.
- Wave 10 and Shadow Emperor were not reached. Bosses met: Frost Queen (w6), Storm Titan (w7), Plague Master (w8), Dragon Lord (w9).

## Build
- Spells: Strike, Poison Stab, Fireball, Raise Shield, Battle Cry (Health Regeneration for the first 10 minutes and one test stretch).
- Talents: Shield Bearer 2, Stone Wall, Regeneration, Warrior Training, then Epidemic, Critical Toxin, Wildfire, Rampage 1.
- Items: rare Plate Armor (shield +28, on-hit trigger), Battleaxe, Arcane Shield, Ring of Vitality, Ring of Poison, Amulet of Plague, Iron Helm, leggings, epic Heavy Boots. End stats: health 124, defense 48, shield 178 (+8/s), attack 39, crit 34% x2.20.
- Setup choices: spells were interesting (Immolation at 5 points against Poison Stab plus Fireball at 4 was a real fork). Tier 1 talents were not: five filler points to reach the fun ones. The stash was rich in weapons (11 of 20) and body armor, but had no boots and one useful amulet, so most slots had exactly one candidate. About right in power, poor in choice.

## How it felt
- Kills per minute by stretch: 17.4 (w1-4), 7.5 (w4-5), 5.4 (w5-6), 3.6 (w6-7), 2.4 (w7-8), 5.7 (w8-9, after a gear swap), 15.9 (death, w1-4), 11.4, 5.4, 4.8, 14.7 (death, w1-2).
- With Raise Shield at 10 stacks my health read "average 100%, lowest 100%" in every stretch from wave 1 to the wave 9 boss. Nothing touched me for 30 minutes, then Dragon Lord killed me in two casts. It is untouchable or dead, with no middle.
- Minutes 10 to 20 dragged: mana-starved (unable to cast 26 to 53% of the time), kills falling to 2.4/min at wave 7-8, and no level to spend. Level 11 needs 2560 xp; I earned 1653 in 37 minutes.
- At a rough 45 direct damage per hit, a wave 9 Storm Lord (768 hp) is about 9 hits with poison doing the other half.

## Fun
- The Ring of Poison drop (poison +3, mana regen +3) was the click. Same wave band, next stretch: kills 2.4 to 5.7/min, poison damage 2620 to 5367, Battle Cry uptime 0% to 99%, mana average 21% to 80%. A better Battleaxe went on at the same time, so not all of it is the ring.
- Status carried the build: 45372 status damage against 41159 direct over the run, poison alone 33601. Critical Toxin with 34% crit is why.
- The re-clear felt good: 15 to 17 kills/min through waves 1-3 against 3 to 5 at the frontier. Rampage plus Epidemic shows there.

## Not fun
- No level-up in 37 minutes, so after setup the only decisions were item swaps.
- Raise Shield at 15 mana every 4s outspends the base 3 mana/s on its own. Until the ring dropped it starved Fireball and Battle Cry, and nothing in my build could fix that without a talent point.
- Health Regeneration (spell and talent) did nothing: I was at 100% health or dead. Regeneration uptime was 3 to 5% because it never needed casting.

## Enemies
- Frost Queen (w6): the hardest hitter before the dragon, Frostbolt 110 and Ice Spike 71, 588 and 432 damage per fight, all soaked. Wave 6 took 3m55s first time, 2m25s on the re-clear. Her shield never showed in the output.
- Storm Titan (w7): Chain Lightning 48, Lightning Bolt 42, 576 and 296 damage per fight, plus lightning status on me (164 that stretch). Wave 7 took 4m11s then 2m12s. I could not see his regeneration; poison simply outran it. Weakest boss as a puzzle: just a long health bar.
- Plague Master (w8): Poison Stab 72, 174 and 256 damage per fight, poison on me 223 and 133. Wave 8 took 3m55s then 2m06s. Readable and fair, but poison into a 178 shield with +8/s is a non-event.
- Dragon Lord (w9): the only memorable one. Meteor 219 beats my 178 shield in one cast, then Inferno 148 beats my 124 health. Dead with 367 damage taken. I never saw his shield matter because I never got far enough.
- Danger now sits with bosses, but only the last one. Mid-wave casters only kill a build without Raise Shield: Flame Lord Inferno 141 at wave 9 enemy 7 killed me inside a minute of unequipping it. Frost Wolf (241) and Storm Elemental (231) were the regulars that mattered at wave 9.
- I could not time boss fights alone: the output gives kill times only, so the lengths above are whole waves.

## Too strong
- Raise Shield. Shield 78 to 178 and regen 3 to 8/s for 20% damage. With it: zero health lost across waves 1-8. Without it: dead at wave 9 enemy 7. The 2% per stack is a real cost on paper (Strike averaged 59 per hit with it, 82 without) but it is not a choice, because the alternative is death.
- Critical Toxin plus any crit gear: poison was 74% of all status damage.

## Too weak
- Fireball: 16% of direct damage and fire was 20% of status damage. Wildfire had little to spread from one single-target fire spell.
- Health Regeneration and the Regeneration talent for any shield build. Bleed from item bonuses (2716 total) is noise.

## Bugs or oddities
- Battle Cry (30 mana) had 0% uptime in the 16m40s to 20m00s stretch while "too low to cast anything" read 0%. Cheap spells drain mana before an expensive one can ever fire, and the readout hides it.
- Curse was on me 14 to 30% of the time from wave 4 onward. The output never says who cast it, and it is meant to be Shadow Emperor's trait.
- Boss traits are invisible in the run output except Shadow Assassin's Lifesteal (4 to 9%) and Orc Warlord's Fortitude (1%, he died too fast for it to matter).

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Give Storm Titan a trait that asks something: have his regeneration scale down per status stack on him, or print it. Right now a status build beats him without noticing he heals.
2. Raise Shield: lower the mana cost to about 8, or make stacks last longer, and raise the damage cost to 3 to 4% per stack. The cost should be damage, which is a choice, not mana starvation, which is a gear lottery.
3. Make partial damage exist for shield builds: let a slice of status damage (say 25%) bypass shield. Then Health Regeneration and the Regeneration talent have a job and Plague Master and Storm Titan become threats.
4. Dragon Lord's Meteor (219) then Inferno (148) is a two-cast kill with no counter in reach at level 10. Keep the damage but widen the gap between the two casts so shield regen and Fortitude can answer it.
5. Wildfire needs a cheap fire source that is not Immolation's 5 points: let Fireball splash 1 or 2 fire onto the other enemies, or give Dragon Scale Mail's fire bonus an area version.
