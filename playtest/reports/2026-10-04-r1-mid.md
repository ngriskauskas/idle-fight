# r1-mid: caster through the middle of world 1
## Run
- Seed 602, NOT a fresh start: level 8 at world 1 wave 5, 7 talent and 7 spell points, 20 drops. 25m00s game time, 11 `run` calls. Ended level 9, highest wave 9, 1 death.
- My mistake: the first 2m30s ran with nothing spent or equipped (my shell loop passed every `act` wrongly and the harness rejected them). So the built character played 22m30s. As an accidental baseline, the naked level 8 did 105 damage to a 234 hp Orc in 150s: 0 kills.
- I ended with 2 spell points unspent: all 4 slots were full and I had no runs left to test a swap.
## Build
- Spells: Arcane Missile, Fireball, Raise Shield, Health Regeneration. Talents: Arcane Affinity 5, Mana Efficiency 2, Magical Specialist.
- Gear: Arcane Staff + Shield L5, Dragon Scale Mail, Iron Helm, Plate Leggings, Enchanted Boots, Amulet of Mana, Ring of Power, Ring of Defense. Final stats: health 90, defense 44, shield 60 (+6/s), mana 247 (+41/s), mana cost 69.
- The way through is the mana cost stat, not mana. Arcane Affinity x4, Magical Specialist's +60%, amulet +13 and boots +5 gave mana cost 60, so each cast hit for about 157 (Missile) and 174 (Fireball): roughly 39 direct damage a second, every stretch.
## How it felt
- No wall. Inferno Lord (wave 5) died on the first attempt at 4m06s, 96 seconds after I was built, and again on the second visit. Frost Queen (wave 6) also 2 for 2. Wave 5 to wave 9 took 20 minutes with one death.
- It was too smooth to feel earned. Health averaged 96 to 100% in every shielded stretch; the lowest I saw in a win was 48%. I made one real decision after the initial build.
- Kills per minute by stretch: 8.0 (wave 5-6), 5.2 (6-7), 6.8 (7, death), 15.2 (re-clear 1-3), 11.6 (3-5), 7.2 (5-6), 5.2 (6-7), 5.6 (7-8), 5.3 (8-9). Damage output never moved (5400 to 6660 per 150s), so kills per minute is just enemy health divided by 39.
- Re-clear of waves 1 to 4 took about 5 minutes (death near 9m20s, Shadow Assassin dead at 14m39s) while taking zero damage (lowest health 100%). The limit is cast time: every enemy dies to one hit of roughly 5x overkill, but I have two single-target casts at 6.0s and 8.5s and nothing to spend the excess on.
- Raise Shield is not a choice, it is free. With it: 17m30s, no deaths, 4 new bosses. Without it (slot left empty): died to Storm Titan on the first try, killing blow Chain Lightning 46. Its "slows your casts" cost did not show: direct damage per 150s was 5467 and 6094 without, 5402 to 6659 with.
## Enemies
- Inferno Lord: Inferno 64, Fireball 26, 90 to 116 total damage per fight, 15 to 24 fire status on me. The trait (burning me) barely registered against 6/s regen and a shield. No answer needed.
- Frost Queen: the hardest hitter of the run (Frostbolt 51 then 78, Ice Spike 29 then 51; 269 and 318 per fight). Lowest health 68% and 50%. If her ice slowed my casts, the output gave me no way to see it; my damage for the stretch was normal.
- Storm Titan (wave 7): the only boss that asked something. Lost without Raise Shield (174 damage taken, dead), won with it plus a level and a shield body piece (274 taken, lowest 48%). Those three changes were made together, so I cannot say which one won it.
- Plague Master (wave 8): won first try; poison was the largest status of the run (129 in the stretch), lowest 54%.
- Curse was on me 39% of the time in waves 6-7 and the second Frost Queen hit 50% harder. I assume a Necromancer cast it; the output does not name the source.
- Regular waves blurred together. Nothing made me change a spell.
## Too strong
- Mana cost stacking with Magical Specialist. Cost is supposed to be the price of damage, but regen was 41/s against roughly 26/s spent: mana averaged 92 to 96%, never too low to cast. Damage with no downside.
- Plate on a caster. Magical Specialist's health -25% still left 90 health and 44 defense because heavy armor costs a caster nothing.
- Raise Shield at 5 stacks, 98 to 100% uptime, for 15 mana.
## Too weak
- Mana pool and mana regen for this build: Cloth Robes (mana +38) and the second Arcane Staff were trash, and my 2 Mana Efficiency points were probably wasted (untested without them).
- Fireball beside Arcane Missile: 44% of direct damage from a slower cast; its fire added 5253 over the run, about 9% of total.
- Legendary Plate Leggings rolled itemDropChance three times and was worse for me than the common pair. Ring of Power's damage +4 does nothing under Magical Specialist.
## Bugs or oddities
- Arcane Staff rolled manaLeech three times (+7, +3, +3); the legendary leggings rolled the same affix three times too.
- The run output does not show enemy-applied slow or the source of auras on me, so ice and Curse are unreadable.
- A leftover spell point with full slots has nowhere useful to go at level 8 to 9.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make mana cost bite: lower Magical Specialist's manaRegen +100% or raise its manaCost bonus so a cost-stacked caster runs dry in boss fights and has to choose between regen and damage. That is the missing decision.
2. Give Raise Shield a real price, for example speed -3 per stack or 25 mana, so shield versus a fourth damage spell is a choice.
3. Add a cheap AoE or an overkill piece for casters (a talent: excess damage on a kill splashes to the next enemy). Re-clear is 5 minutes of 5x overkill.
4. Make heavy armor cost a caster something (mana cost or mana regen penalty on plate), so Cloth Robes are worth wearing.
5. Show slow and aura sources in the run output, and stop affixes rolling twice on one item.
