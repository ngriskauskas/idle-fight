# QA: r2 recheck (2026-10-04)

Quick re-test after: extra enemy health following the wave inside a world (1 at wave 1 to 3 at wave 5), world scale jump 2 (was 2.5), Shadow Emperor attack 14 and mana cost 19, Inferno Lord mana cost 26, War Cry 8% to 12 stacks, Magical Specialist +25% mana regen (was +100%), Mana Efficiency 3 regen per level.

Builds: level 11 caster and level 11 physical, both in world 1 wave 10 gear with Raise Shield. Bosses via `qa wave <world> <wave> 10`, stepped 5 s at a time (times to the nearest 5 s, boss health left read up to 5 s before death). None is a fair playthrough.

**Important setup note.** Forking an old run keeps the old talent numbers (see Bugs 1). `qa-se` still had Magical Specialist +100% and regenerated 77 mana/s. The caster was therefore rebuilt from scratch as `qa-se2` (`new --seed 6 --level 11 --wave 10`, same talents, spells and items; 47 mana/s) and every caster number below is from `qa-se2` unless marked. The physical build `qa-sep` was forked as is; its talents (Warrior Training, Iron Constitution, Rampage) are not on the changed list, but they were not re-read against current data. War Cry and Inferno Lord were not on the test list and were not run.

## Summary

4 items: 1 about right (Shadow Emperor, slightly easy for the caster and still shut for the physical build), 1 pass with a note (Goblin King: win rate on target, but it is now easier than Shadow Emperor and over in 10 to 15 s), 1 split (world 2 waves 1 and 2 trash are a breather, wave 3 trash is a wall), 1 fail against its target (caster mana: the regen cut changed nothing that matters).

1. **World 2 wave 3 trash kills both builds every time**: 8 deaths in 8 runs, to Orc Rend 88 to 93 (163 to 170 on a crit) plus bleed.
2. **The caster's mana is still free**: regen 77 -> 47 per second, mana average 95% -> 86%, too low to cast 0% -> 0%, damage identical to the last digit.
3. **Goblin King is no longer a wall** (0 of 19 before; now caster 6 of 10, physical 3 of 9), but with 1117 health it is a 10 to 15 s coin flip, not a fight a little harder than Shadow Emperor.

## Details

### 1. Goblin King

Seeds 51 to 58, `qa wave 2 1 10`.

| Build | Wins | Time to kill | Time to die | Boss health left on a loss | Lowest health on a win |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Caster `qa-se2` | 4/8 | 10 s | 10 to 15 s | 100%, 100%, 19%, 19% | 2%, 27%, 44%, 44% |
| Physical `qa-sep` | 2/8 | 15 s | 10 to 15 s | 91%, 47%, 47%, 47%, 34%, 32% | 9%, 9% |

Also beaten on arrival from wave 1 trash in all three 150 s runs (caster at 1m15s and 1m25s, physical at 1m11s), with shield and Raise Shield stacks already up. Hits: Slash 69 to 74 (crit 110 to 116), Rend 71 to 75 (crit 134 to 140), one about every 2 s; it was 94 to 100 and 147 to 185.

Reading: the win rate is where the target asks, but the shape is wrong for "a little harder than Shadow Emperor". Its health fell to about a quarter (extra health 1 instead of 3, jump 2), so the caster kills it in two or three casts or dies in three hits; the result is decided by one crit in the first 10 s. Its damage per second is still about twice Shadow Emperor's (about 35/s against about 18/s). The physical build does better here (3/9) than against Shadow Emperor (0/8). Something like twice the health and two thirds of the attack would give a fight of the same odds that lasts. Medium confidence (seeds 54 and 56 gave identical fights, so the 8 are fewer than 8 independent samples).

Expected regen from the data: (3 base + 6 Mana Efficiency + 24 items) x (1 + 0.20 + 0.25) = 47.9; `status` shows 47, so the change itself works. But the same seeds produce the same fight to the last point of damage, so mana never gated one cast. This build has 670 mana and mana cost 123 from gear and Magical Specialist, plus mana leech 34 and +27% from the staff and shield. The pool never drops far enough for regen to matter. The earlier "starved caster" finding (16% average mana) was a different, lower level character; this one is not starved and not close. To make mana cost bite here regen would need to fall to somewhere well under half of 47, or leech would need to come down; where exactly was not measured. High confidence in the numbers, one build only.

## Bugs

1. **Forked and saved runs keep old talent numbers.** A run file stores each talent's `effects` and the character's applied effects, and nothing refreshes them from `src/data/talents`. Steps: `new x --from qa-se`, `status x` shows mana `+77/s`; the file has Magical Specialist `manaRegen 100` and Mana Efficiency `4` flat per level where the source now says 25 and 3. Unequipping and re-equipping an item does not refresh it. If `src/utils/saveSystem.ts` loads talents the same way, a player's existing save keeps pre-patch talent values too; that was not checked. Any re-test of a talent change on a fork is silently testing the old talent.
2. **Fractional stat in `status`**: the rebuilt caster shows `shield 61 (+5.5/s)`.
3. **Seeds are not independent**: Goblin King seeds 54 and 56, and Shadow Emperor seeds 23 and 26, gave identical fights. Lead only.
4. A `run` that continues past a death does not say which enemy or wave the death happened on.
