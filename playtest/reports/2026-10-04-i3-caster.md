# Playtest i3-caster (2026-10-04)
## Run
- Seed 88, 60m00s game time, 14 `run` calls. Level 10, highest world 1 wave 8 (did not reach world 2), 17 deaths, 581 kills (9.7/min), 77 item drops.
- Whole run: direct damage 162k, status damage 53k (25% of the total, almost all fire). Out of mana 20% of the time.
## Build
- End: Meteor, Fireball, Arcane Missile, Raise Shield, Health Regeneration. Talents (9 points): Arcane Affinity 4, Mana Efficiency 3, Magical Specialist, Wildfire.
- Gear: Wand, Arcane Shield, rare Mage Hood, Silk Leggings, Enchanted Boots, Ring of Mana (mana cost 127, mana 506, +35/s), epic Plate Armor (on hit: speed boost), two health pieces.
- Path: Strike only until 3m, Fireball at 3m, two melee weapons at 6m, Meteor at 11m, Magical Specialist at 15m (Strike dead), Arcane Missile at 19m, Wildfire at 23m, shield gear at 42m, Raise Shield at 55m.
- Physical test: Strike with Greatsword and Warhammer (attack 18) did 1443 over 53 hits in 6m to 11m, more than Fireball (1270 over 37). After Magical Specialist it did 173 over 28 hits.
## How it felt
- 0 to 10m: slow but fine. 3.6 kills/min with Strike alone (2.8 hits per kill), 6.6 with Fireball, 9.6 with weapons. Wave 3 by 6m49s. 0 deaths until the wave 3 boss (Orc Warlord Rend for 60 on 56 health).
- 10 to 20m: best block. Meteor took kills to 12.7/min and direct damage from 2713 to 5834 per 250s, but I was out of mana 93% of the time. Magical Specialist fixed mana (2% out) and reached wave 5. 3 deaths, all one hit from a boss or mage.
- 20 to 30m: wave 6 at 23m, then stuck. 8.4 to 9.8 kills/min, 3 deaths (Berserker Rend 73 and Whirlwind 50 on 84 health).
- 30 to 40m: wall. Bounced between waves 5 and 7, 4 deaths. Frost Wolf Frostbolt for 165 and Storm Elemental for 160 on 104 health. Defense 29 did nothing against these.
- 40 to 50m: took off again. Shield gear plus mana cost 51 to 94 doubled direct damage (50/s to 84/s), 12 kills/min, wave 8 at 47m28s, 1 death.
- 50 to 60m: stuck at wave 8 enemy 10, 4 deaths (Flame Lord Inferno 278, Plague Master Poison Stab 100 and 87). Poison was 906 to 982 damage taken per stretch here.
- Hits per kill stayed at 1.4 to 2.2 casts from wave 2 to wave 8 (Meteor about 490, Fireball about 320 against 228 to 579 health at level 7). Enemy health never felt like the wall. Enemy burst did.
- New death rule: a death costs about one to two minutes instead of the whole run, so the wall is a yo-yo between two waves and not a reset. Much less punishing, also less dramatic: I stopped caring about a single death.
## Fun
- Meteor at 3 points is the moment the caster starts. Direct damage doubled in one stretch.
- Wildfire: fire share of total damage went from 15% (before) to 26% and then 33% (5813 fire in 5m). Packs melting after a Meteor kill is the build's identity.
- The mana puzzle is real now: twice I had to choose between bigger casts and being able to cast at all.
- Killing blows make deaths readable. Every death was one named hit bigger than my health.
## Not fun
- First 3 minutes: one Strike every 6s, 3.6 kills/min, nothing to decide.
- Level 9 to 10 took 17 minutes with no point to spend. The wall and the point drought landed together.
- Defense is worthless for a caster: mage and boss hits of 124 to 278 ignore it in practice, so the only answer is more health or shield.
- 77 drops, 9 worn. About 20 were melee weapons that Magical Specialist turns into trash.
## Too strong
- Raise Shield: within 5 minutes it was at 30 stacks, shield 970 with +136/s, up 97% of the time, for 3 points. Health never dropped below 100% in that stretch. It dwarfs every shield item (30 to 38 shield).
- Mana cost gear when mana is spare: cost 51 to 94 gave +68% direct damage per second with out-of-mana time only rising from 2% to 20%.
## Too weak
- Mana cost gear when regen is short: at 23m a Spellbook (cost 35 to 62, regen still 14/s) cut Meteor casts from 30 to 12 per 250s and kills from 9.8 to 8.4/min. It only pays once regen is ahead, so regen still comes first; cost is now a good second stat, not an equal one.
- Arcane Missile: 11% of damage for a slot. Strike after Magical Specialist: 2%.
- Status stats on gear for a caster: fire +3 to +5, lightning +2 gave 925 lightning damage in the whole run (under 1%).
- Defense talents and helms against waves 5 to 8.
## Bugs or oddities
- Last stretch reports "health: average 100%, lowest 100%" and 2 deaths (Bandit Rend 53, Plague Master Poison Stab 87) with shield 970. Either the health sampling misses the death, or something kills through a full shield. Plague Master also shows 2209 damage in that stretch.
- An earlier stretch shows "lowest 0%" with 3 deaths, so the two readings are not consistent.
- Enemy Curse was on me 44% of the run (speed 9 to -18, defense 30 to 6). The status screen gives no hint why stats moved; I first read it as a gear bug.
- "auras on enemies: Arcane Boost" appears although I never unlocked it; it is an enemy self-buff listed as if it were mine.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Cap Raise Shield lower or make stacks decay faster. 970 shield from one 3-point spell removes the wave 8 wall on its own and makes shield gear pointless.
2. Give casters a defence against big single magic hits (a magic resist stat, or defense applying partly to spells). Right now every death from wave 5 on is one mage hit above max health.
3. Hand out a point between levels 8 and 10 (a boss first-kill reward, for example). 17 minutes with nothing to spend during the wall is the dullest part.
4. Start the caster with a magic spell or give Fireball at 1 point. Three minutes of Strike is not a caster opening, and melee weapons beat Fireball until Meteor.
5. Make gear status stats scale with mana cost for magic builds, or drop fewer melee weapons once Magical Specialist is taken. Both are dead stats for this build today.
