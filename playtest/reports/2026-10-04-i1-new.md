# Playtest i1-new (short early-game test after the rebalance)
## Run
Seed 33, 24m58s game time, 12 `run` calls. Level 8 (197/640 xp). Highest: world 1 wave 5. Deaths: 5 (first at 5m-7m30s). 364 kills, 34 item drops.
## Build
Spells: Slash, Fireball, Regeneration aura, Battle Cry aura (Strike dropped at level 4 for Fireball). Talents (7 points): Warrior Training 2, Mana Efficiency 1, Shield Bearer 1, Stone Wall 1, Iron Constitution 1, Adrenaline Surge 1.
Items at the end: Battleaxe L3, Tower Shield L3, Iron Helm L2, Plate Armor L1 (on-hit speed boost), Plate Leggings L3, Enchanted Boots, Ring of Power, Amulet of Plague.
I wanted shields and stacking auras but never got them: Raise Shield and War Cry cost 4 spell points, and I had spent my first 5 points on Slash, Fireball and Battle Cry. I ended with 2 unspent points and nothing useful to buy.
## How it felt
- 0-1m: 3.6 kills/min, 7 Strikes per Goblin (15 hp, 7 per hit). No deaths, health never under 80%. Slow, but level 2 came at 1m06s.
- 1-3m: Slash plus one Warrior Training: 7.0 then 10.2 kills/min, 2 hits per kill. First boss down at 2m40s. Two drops at 2m (robes and helm, 5 defense) cut Goblin damage from 74 to 2 per minute.
- 3-5m: Fireball and Mana Efficiency. 8.4 kills/min, wave 3 at 4m43s. Mana went from "too low 100% of the time" to 2%.
- 5-11m: Battleaxe (+7 damage) took Slash from 10 to 22 per hit, 15.5 then 17.1 kills/min, one hit per kill on waves 1-2. First two deaths, both on wave 3 (enemy 6 and 9).
- 11-16m: Tower Shield and four more slots filled, defense 7 to 19. Waves 4 and 5 for the first time, died at wave 5 enemy 2 to Frost Wolf (208) and Necromancer (144). 12.0 kills/min.
- 16-21m: 15.8 then 19.4 kills/min, but two more deaths (wave 4 enemy 5, wave 2 enemy 7) and no new wave. This was the stuck stretch: 5 minutes re-climbing waves 1-3 three times.
- 21-25m: no deaths, back to wave 5 enemy 6. 17.5 then 14.0 kills/min.
The first 10 minutes work: a level every 1 to 2.5 minutes, 6 levels, 3 bosses, 2 deaths, a decision at every stop.
## Fun
- Levels 2 to 6 arrive fast and each one changed the numbers visibly (Slash, then the Battleaxe, each roughly doubled kill speed).
- Items now matter and read clearly. Gearing six slots between 10m and 12m was the best moment of the run.
- Dying to wave 1 of the same world is fair: about 1 minute per wave to climb back.
## Not fun
- I could not see what killed me. 4 of 5 deaths came in stretches where attacks did 0 to 94 total damage against 50-65 health and 8/s regen.
- Stack auras and shield spells are out of reach in the first 25 minutes unless you know to save 4 points. Level 8 to 9 costs 640 xp, about 10 minutes.
- Both auras sat at 88-98% uptime with no input from me, so they were flat stat sticks, not something to play around.
- From level 7 on there was nothing to buy: 2 spell points, full slots, no 2-point spell worth a slot.
## Too strong
- Flat defense. 5 defense at 2m zeroed wave 1-2 attackers; 19 defense zeroed everything through wave 3 (damage taken 0 in three stretches).
- Battleaxe L3: +7 flat damage was worth more than all my talents together (attack 9 to 16; 8.4 to 15.5 kills/min).
- Battle Cry: +3 damage, +20% crit, crit x2.34 with the axe. Slash went from 22 to 38-41 per hit for 30 mana per 30s.
## Too weak
- Shield without regen. Shield Bearer (+3) and Tower Shield gave 20 shield at +0/s; I saw no change in deaths. The only regen source is Fortitude or Raise Shield.
- Regeneration aura: 8/s for 25 mana kept health at 90-100% but did not stop a single death.
- Plate Armor on-hit speed boost 6: Slash hits went from 15.0 to 16.0 per minute. Unnoticeable now; 30 was too much, 6 is too little.
- Adrenaline Surge with 30% crit: 16.0 to 17.5 Slash hits per minute.
- Fire from Fireball: 59-81 stack-ticks per 4 minutes against 3000+ direct damage, under 3%. Status never matters while enemies die in 1-2 hits.
## Bugs or oddities
- Amulet of Plague shows "poison 1, bleed 1, fire 1 on every attack", but in 9 minutes no poison or bleed stack was ever recorded on enemies, only fire. Either it only adds to spells that already carry that status, or it does nothing.
- The harness "damage taken" list only counts attacks, so deaths to enemy status effects are invisible (died at wave 2 enemy 7 with 0 damage taken listed).
- "Too low to cast anything" read 100% for minutes 1-3 while the Regeneration aura was up 78-83%. The number seems to count waiting for the aura's cost as being out of mana.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Show what killed you, including status damage, in the death line. Right now the main early wall is unreadable.
2. Give a new player one stacking or shield piece early: drop Raise Shield or War Cry to 2 points, or put 1 shield regen on Shield Bearer so a shield item does something by itself.
3. Make defense a percentage or give enemies some penetration; flat defense turns waves 1-3 into zero damage and then a cliff at wave 4-5.
4. Let status effects count early: apply them on killing blows to the next enemy, or give wave 2-3 enemies enough health to burn (currently 1 hit).
5. Raise Plate Armor's boost to about 12-15 and fix or explain the Amulet of Plague, so the first trigger items a player finds do something visible.
