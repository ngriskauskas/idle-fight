# r3-caster: early caster, seed 802

## Run
Fresh character, seed 802, 24m57s game time, 12 `run` calls. Level 8, highest world 1 wave 5 (died at its boss), 4 deaths, 281 kills (11.3/min). Ended with 2 spell points unspent (nothing at 2 points I wanted over saving for Meteor/Arcane Boost) and helmet, amulet and ring2 empty (no drops for them).

## Build
Arcane Missile, Fireball, Ice Spike, Health Regeneration aura. Arcane Affinity 3, Mana Efficiency 3, Magical Specialist. Legendary Spellbook L2 (+6 mana cost, +26 mana), rare Wand, plate body/legs/boots (defense 14), Ring of Vitality. Trigger piece: Plate Armor's on-hit speed boost. Tested physical only as Strike (levels 1 and 3). Plan never changed; the Spellbook at 7m13s just confirmed it.

## Level-by-level choices
- L2: Missile + Arcane Affinity, dropped Strike. Kills 4.4 -> 5.4/min, but starved 36% and lowest health 25%: the aura (25 mana per 7s = 3.6/s) alone outspends regen 2/s. Real tension, felt good.
- L3: Mana Efficiency + Strike back in the new slot. Starved 36% -> 3%. One point erased the problem. Obvious pick.
- L4: Fireball + Affinity 2. Starved 48%, first death (Slime, wave 2 enemy 9), 9.2 kills/min.
- L5: Mana Efficiency 2 + first armor. Starved 3%, lowest health 82%. With the Spellbook and Wand: 0% starved, regen 10/s. Mana was not a limit at all here.
- L6: Affinity 3 + Ice Spike. Starved 0% -> 82%, then 94%. Yet kills went UP to 14.1/min.
- L7: Magical Specialist. Damage 18 -> 27.5 per second, starved 75%. Health 63 -> 53.
- L8: Mana Efficiency 3 (regen 18/s). Damage 39/s, starved 57%, 17.3 kills/min.

## How it felt
Mana is now a real limit, but it flips on and off: 38, 36, 3, 48, 3, 0, 82, 94, 75, 77, 72, 57% starved per stretch. Each Mana Efficiency point switches it off; each Affinity point or new spell switches it back on. That see-saw was the most interesting part of the first ten minutes. After level 6 it stopped being a tension and became a formula: once starved, damage per second is exactly 2 x mana regen (regen 10 -> 20 dps, 13 -> 27.5, 18 -> 39). Arcane Affinity then adds nothing to sustained damage and Mana Efficiency is the only damage talent, so the choice collapses. Being starved never felt slow, because spells still fire as mana arrives; "too low to cast 94%" reads alarming but was my fastest-growing stretch.
The regen aura does compete: at the end it cost 53 mana per cast, about a third of my spend. Dropping it for a same-length stretch gave +14% damage (4129 -> 4701) and 8.0 -> 15.6 kills/min (partly re-clearing easy waves), then Inferno Lord's 47 Fireball killed me from 53 health. Good tradeoff, the best real choice in the run.
Pace: new furthest wave at 3m, 8.6m, 14.2m, 16.1m; stuck at wave 5 for the last 9 minutes. Deaths about every 6 minutes, re-clear to wave 3 in about 2.5 minutes. Hard enough, not hopeless.

## Enemies
- Goblin King: a fight the first time (72 damage into 40 health, lowest 40%, only the aura kept me up). With defense 5 it dealt 21: trivial from then on.
- Bone Warden: not a fight. Dead first try, 32 damage taken, lowest 78%.
- Orc Warlord: best boss. Lightning Bolt for 36 killed me first try; second try won at 33% health. Still the top damage source at the end (221 total).
- Shadow Assassin: beat first try at 30% health; its Curse on me (47% uptime, defense 11 -> 8) was the only enemy effect I noticed changing my stats.
- Inferno Lord: one Fireball for 47 of 53 health. Asks for health or a shield, which Magical Specialist had just taken away. Fair, and the wall I'd build toward.
- Poison was the real early killer (224 status damage, ignores my defense; rats and spiders). Everything else in waves 1-3 blurred once I had 11 defense.

## Too strong
- Mana Efficiency: +3 flat on a base of 2 means the first point is +150% regen, and regen is damage. 1 point took starved 36% -> 3%.
- Plain defense gear on a caster: 3 common armor pieces took wave 1-3 damage to near zero (lowest health 82-99%) at no cost to the build.

## Too weak
- Arcane Affinity once starved: third point changed starved 0% -> 82% with no lasting damage gain over what regen allows.
- Magical Specialist's +60% mana cost: same reason. Its value was the +25% regen (10 -> 13). The health cost is real (see Inferno Lord).
- Strike next to Missile: 28% of damage at level 3, 1% of the run. Fine as a filler.

## Bugs or oddities
- "Too low to cast anything" hit 94% while kills rose; it seems to count any moment mana is below a ready spell, so it overstates the problem. A "mana-limited casts lost" figure would read better.
- Spellbook mana leech (+2, +8%) showed no visible effect in any number the harness prints.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Important: make Arcane Affinity worth taking when starved, e.g. small crit chance or status amount per level, or cost-scaled burst from a full pool. Right now Mana Efficiency strictly wins.
2. Important: soften Mana Efficiency's first point (e.g. +1.5 flat, +15% per level) so mana stays tight through levels 3-6 instead of vanishing with one pick.
3. Important: give casters an early reason to hoard, a cheap tier 2 talent like "+damage while mana is above 50%", to combine with Affinity and oppose the spend-as-it-comes formula before Mana Overflow at tier 4.
4. Minor: heavy armor should cost a caster something (e.g. plate reduces mana regen or raises cast time more than -2 speed) so Cloth Robes are a real choice; I never wanted the robes.
5. Minor: Bone Warden needs something that asks a question (it died first try); the other four bosses are in a good place and I would not change them.
Beyond these I am running out of things worth changing in the first 25 minutes; the early caster is in decent shape.
