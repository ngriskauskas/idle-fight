# i2-caster (run i2-caster-b, because i2-caster already existed)

## Run
Seed 2202, 45m04s game time, 6 `run` calls. **I did not start fresh:** I started at level 8 on world 1 wave 7 with 40 stash drops. I ended at level 10 with a highest wave of 1-9 and 5 deaths.
Deaths: Storm Titan (w7 boss, Chain Lightning 63), Plague Master (w8 boss, Poison Stab), Dragon Lord twice (w9 boss, Meteor 68 / Inferno 118), and Abyssal Horror at w9 enemy 8 (I was worn down, with poison 122 taken in that stretch).
New furthest waves came at 8m45 (w8) and 19m15 (w9). After that I hit a 26-minute wall at w9. The game was hard enough, and each new wave felt earned.

## Build
- Spells: Arcane Missile, Fireball, Ice Spike (added at level 10), Arcane Boost, Health Regen, Strike.
- Talents: Arcane Affinity 3, Mana Efficiency 3, Widened Focus, Chain Reaction 1.
- Gear: stacked manaCost (Amulet of Mana +34, Mage Hood +18, Spellbook +19, Silk Leggings +15, Enchanted Boots +14). Body was unique Dragonscale Mail.
- Mana cost went 93 -> 105 -> 171 -> 181.
- Damage/min went 6.0k (stretch 1-2) -> 8.0k (amulet + boots) -> 9.2k (with Ice Spike) -> 9.1k.
- Kills/min stayed flat from stretch to stretch: 17.2, 14.9, 16.2, 15.9, 17.2, 16.9. More damage did not show up as faster kills.
- Mana starvation: at mana cost 105, mana averaged 91% and was never too low to cast. At 171, mana averaged 15-17% and was too low to cast 1-2% of the time.
- Lesson: since magic damage scales with mana cost, damage per mana stays fixed at about 2, so mana regen is the real throughput stat. Stacking manaCost only buys burst. I liked that tension.

## Economy (the main question)
- **Gold over time:** 0 -> 97 (5m) -> 149 (8m50) -> 447 (18m50) -> 572 (25m) -> 1272 (35m) -> 1357 (45m), counting spending in between.
- **Kill gold** was steady at about 19-22/min (about 920 over the run). Last round a caster earned 3-10/min. **This change landed.**
- **Selling** brought in about 2240 over the run, or about 50/min. Selling still makes about 70% of income, but kill gold now pays for something every 10 minutes.
- **First purchase** came at 5m00, after selling my stash for 373: a rare Silk Leggings (138; health +20%, speed +18%) and an epic Spellbook (342). That took health from 81 to 121. On the next stretch I beat Storm Titan, which had killed me, and reached w8.
- **Second purchase** was epic Enchanted Boots (429) at 18m50: manaCost +14, health +46.
- **Uniques are reachable now.** They cost 572 at L7 and 894 at L9. I bought Dragonscale Mail (894) at 35m. It took health from 207 to 344 and defense from 9 to 76. Dragon's hits fell from 71 to 25. It was the right answer to the Dragon Lord wall, and buying a counter felt great.
- At 45m I had 1357 gold again. Ring of Lifespan (health +238, leech 31) was on sale, and I would have bought it next. **This change landed**, maybe a bit generously, since a unique now arrives every ~15-20 minutes.
- **Smith:** 4 rares (worth 135 gold) gave an epic Arcane Staff L6 (worth 84) that I never equipped. Averaging levels is fair, but smithing is still worse than selling unless you want the gamble. I tried it once and stopped.
- **hold-item:** it worked. The held legendary Plate Leggings (1341) survived 3 restocks. I ended up spending the gold on a unique instead, so holding was a cheap option rather than a commitment. It's useful, low drama.
- **Restock** at 191-298 gold felt too pricey next to 20/min kill gold. I never used it.

## Item notes for a caster
- **Miser's Ring** showed health -25% (L7) and -23% (L8), **not the -15% in the change notes**. Either the change did not land or it rolls in a range; please check. I never bought it because a caster with 81-207 health cannot give up a quarter of it.
- **Overflowing Chalice** never appeared in my shop, so I cannot rate it. With mana averaging 16%, it would be dead for me.
- **Speed% is weak at low base speed.** Spellbook +19% and leggings +18% moved my speed from 10 to 13.
- **Some shop uniques were dead for a caster:** Glass Pendant (damage +54%) and Deathbringer Plate (damage +36, bleed) do nothing for magic. Rimewalker Boots (shatter 2/stack) is tiny next to a 360-damage Fireball.
- **Body slot** was the hardest choice: Robes (speed 15, defense 9) against Plate (speed 6, defense 41, health 262). Until the unique, it was a real tradeoff between casting faster and staying alive.

## Combinations
- **Chain Reaction + crits:** lightning status did 1399 of 60k (2.3%) in its first stretch and 6304 over the run (1.9%). At 15% crit it is not worth a point for a caster. I kept it only for its tier-2 slot.
- **Arcane Boost + manaCost stacking** was the real engine. Arcane Boost was up 99% of the time and is cheap. It was an obvious pick, not a choice.
- **Ice Spike** was worth 24-29% of damage the moment I slotted it, so the 6th slot paid off.
- I wanted a regen-for-cost tradeoff talent, because regen is the real cap.

## Enemies
- The bosses are memorable because they are elemental casters: Storm Titan (Chain Lightning), Frost Queen (Frostbolt 67), Dragon Lord (Inferno 118).
- **Dragon Lord** asked something of me (health or defense), and the shop let me answer it. That was the best loop of the run.
- **Plague Master and poison generally** were the biggest status threat: I took 527 poison damage, which ignores defense. My defense purchase did nothing against it. That is a good ask, but a caster has no cleanse or leech answer, so the answer is just more health.
- **Waves 1-6 blurred together** on re-clears. They took about 5:30-6:50 from the w1 boss to the w7 boss, which only improved from 6:49 to 5:23.
- **Attrition death at w9 enemy 8:** no single big hit, just being worn down. That felt unreadable.

## Suggestions
1. Keep kill gold at 0.25x. Consider lowering restock to about 100 so it gets used.
2. Make smithing beat selling, for example with a minimum rarity floor or by letting the player pick the slot.
3. Fix or verify the Miser's Ring health value. Make damage-only uniques rarer in a caster's shop, or give them a magic hook.
4. Raise Chain Reaction's lightning per crit for magic hits, or add a caster crit source.
5. Add a mana-regen-per-cost talent, such as "manaRegen +X% of manaCost", to make the burst-vs-sustain choice explicit.
