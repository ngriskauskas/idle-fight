# Playtest: phys-oct04 (physical build)

## Run

- Seed 101. Stopped at 1h28m of game time on the caller's instruction (budget was 3h).
- Level 129, highest world 38 wave 11 (current position world 38 wave 10).
- 12 deaths, all in the first 17 minutes, all in world 1 waves 1 to 4. Zero deaths after that.
- 6,374 kills, 3,846 items dropped, 77 talent points and 91 spell points left unspent because nothing was worth buying.

## Build

Ended with:

- Spells (6/6): Rend, Lacerate, Hemorrhage, Quick Strike, Whirlwind, Bleed Shatter (aura).
- Talents (46 spent): Warrior Training 38, Physical Specialist, Deep Wounds, Bloodlust, Battle Trance, Adrenaline Surge, Blood Pact, Spiked Shield, Rampage.
- Items: Battleaxe L10 + Dagger L10, Plate Armor L10 (onHit speedBoost 30), Leather Leggings, Iron Helm, Swift Boots, Ring of Power L10, a world-4 Ring of Mana kept for its +4605% damage and +5570% crit multiplier, Amulet of Plague (bleed, poison and fire on every attack).

How it changed:

- 0 to 12 min: Strike, then Slash, then Rend, with the free Health Regeneration aura in the spare slot. Five points into Warrior Training.
- 12 to 15 min: tried Arcane Missile in place of Strike as the magic test, then dropped it.
- 15 min: Physical Specialist. 17 min: equipped a common Plate Armor with onHit speedBoost 30 and a crit ring. The run took off here and never slowed.
- 26 min onward: the plan was a bleed and crit build (Deep Wounds, Rend, Lacerate, Hemorrhage) with War Cry stacking from Bloodlust and Battle Trance. I took all of it, but by then every enemy died in one hit, so the plan was decoration.

## How it felt

- **0 to 17 min: the real game.** 25 health, dying to Goblins on wave 1 enemy 2 four times in the first two minutes. Each level mattered. Slash over Strike, saving three points for Rend, a Warhammer that took attack from 12 to 22: all small, all felt. Slow and a little brutal, in a good way. This matched DESIGN.md.
- **17 to 21 min: the spike.** In one 4-minute stretch I went from wave 4 to world 2, hits went from 58 in 114s to 520 in 240s, damage from 49 per second to 219 per second, and I stopped dying. For about two minutes this was the best moment of the run.
- **21 min to the end: nothing.** Every stretch after that was the same line: about 90 kills per minute, 0 deaths, one world every minute or two. Damage dealt went 772 thousand, 263 million, 18 trillion, 1.4e21, 1.9e73. Kills per minute did not move (86, 92, 91, 90, 98, 93, 80, 95). I ran 30 minutes straight on world-8 gear and went from world 17 to world 33 without touching anything. No decision I made after minute 21 changed the outcome.

So: 17 minutes of grind I liked, 2 minutes of a great spike, 70 minutes of watching exponents.

## Fun

- The first Goblin King kill at 6m50s after six deaths on wave 1.
- Rend arriving and the first bleed ticks showing up.
- The Plate Armor moment. A common item with a trigger turned three slow spells into a chain. That is exactly the kind of drop that should change a plan.
- Seeing "War Cry max 444 stacks" the first time. It looked like a build coming together.
- Unique drops with triggers (Butcher's Cleaver: onCrit multiply bleed 1.5; Thornmail: bleed attackers and gain shield) read as build-defining.

## Not fun

- After minute 21 there is no challenge and no choice. Kill rate is capped by how fast enemies appear, not by anything I own.
- Gearing became a search through 4,000 inventory lines for the highest exponent. I could not bulk discard.
- The uniques were dead on arrival. Butcher's Cleaver dropped in world 21 with 6e21 damage; by the time I looked, commons had 5e32. I never equipped either unique.
- Talent and spell points piled up. By level 129 I had 77 and 91 unspent. Slots cap at 6, so levels stopped giving anything.
- Mana has no base regen, so the starting aura is castable about twice and then sits dead ("too low to cast" 55 to 91% of the time early on).

## Combinations

- **Plate Armor (onHit speedBoost 30) + three fast physical spells.** Equipped at 17 min together with a crit ring and a regen hood. Hits per second went from about 0.5 to 2.2. This changed how the build played. When I swapped the armor out at world 7 for 2 minutes, kills per minute did not drop (about 91 before, about 105 whirlwind hits per minute after), because by then the speed stat alone had hit the spawn cap. It mattered early and not at all late.
- **Battleaxe crit multiplier + crit chance over 100% + Ring of Mana's +5570% crit multiplier.** Damage per hit went from thousands to 1e18. Numbers only, but this is what removed the game.
- **Bloodlust + Battle Trance (War Cry).** 100% uptime, 444 stacks after 3 minutes, 22,286 by the end. Never lost, because nothing ever hit me. Average damage per hit rose about 47% in the first stretch (attack 5.8e9 to 11.1e9). The stacks appear to add to the same pool as other percent damage, so 444 stacks next to a +4605% ring is under a doubling. The "lost when you lose health" tradeoff never came up.
- **Deep Wounds + Rend/Lacerate/Hemorrhage + Bleed Shatter.** Could not measure. Enemies died on the first hit, so bleed never ticked: 399 bleed stacks in the whole run, almost all before minute 24. Bleed Shatter never registered any uptime on enemies.
- **Rampage (kills push attacks forward).** No visible change: 97.7 kills per minute before, 92.9 after.
- **Spiked Shield (Riposte).** Riposte never appeared in the damage list. Nothing attacked me.
- **Blood Pact.** Crushing Weight did 10% of damage. The -50% health did nothing visible (health 1.103e15 before and after).
- **Wanted but missing:** a reason for bleed to exist when direct damage is high, such as enemies that cannot be one-shot.

## Items

- Mattered: Warhammer L1 (attack 12 to 22), Plate Armor L3 (the spike), Battleaxe (crit multiplier), Ring of Mana L4 with rolled percent damage and percent crit multiplier (still equipped 34 worlds later), Amulet of Luck L5 (crit over 100%).
- Vendor trash: everything else. All caster items for this build, and every item more than one world old.
- Item stats grow about tenfold per world. One Leggings roll gave health +502,177,740%.
- Percent rolls on low-level items outlive flat rolls forever, which is why a world-4 ring beat every world-33 ring.

## Too strong

- **Item scaling against enemy scaling.** World-8 gear cleared through world 33 with no deaths. At world 33 an enemy had 8e24 health and a common Battleaxe gave 5e32 damage and a 5e33 crit multiplier.
- **Crit multiplier on Battleaxes**, flat and percent, stacking with percent damage.
- **Physical Specialist.** One point, +100% damage, and its cost (mana cost set to 0) costs a physical build nothing. Damage per second went 21.7 to 49.
- **onHit speedBoost 30 on a common armor** in the early game.

## Too weak or useless

- **Arcane Missile on a physical character.** 359 damage over 24 hits in 3 minutes (9% of damage) and it starved the regen aura of mana (uptime 85% to 23%). Fair enough as an off-build pick, but there is no cheap way to dabble.
- **Health Regeneration aura** early: no mana regen to sustain it.
- **Speed past the cap.** Speed 2,823 and speed 1e14 both gave about 90 kills per minute.
- **Everything defensive** after minute 21. I took no recorded damage for over an hour.
- **Bleed, Deep Wounds, Bleed Shatter, Rampage, Spiked Shield** in this run. Not rated as designs; they never got a chance to act.

## Bugs or oddities

- Regeneration aura reported "max 11001 stacks" although it is not marked as stacking.
- After equipping a huge max-health item, health showed "average 0%, lowest 0%" for over 40 minutes with 0 deaths. Current health does not rise with max health and regen could not fill it.
- Enemy health shows far below zero ("-5.5e26/8.1e24hp").
- Enemy level resets to 1 at the start of each world ("Giant Rat L1 18/18hp" at world 2 wave 1), and L1 drops in a new world are tiny (defense +6 in world 4) next to L2 drops from the same world (defense +46).
- "highest wave 11" is shown for worlds that have 10 waves.
- Battleaxe shows "critMultiplier +102" for what is +1.02.
- Early deaths at wave 1 enemy 7 and 8 with only 12 damage recorded as taken in that stretch.
- "Mana too low to cast anything 100% of the time" in the same stretch that Regeneration was up 87%.
- Bleed Shatter was equipped for 50 minutes and never appeared under auras on enemies.
- Every kill dropped an item for a while (457 drops in 457 kills) once item drop chance rolled into the thousands.

## Against DESIGN.md

- "Grindy and slow, goals take a long time": true for 17 minutes, then false. 37 worlds in 70 minutes.
- "Sudden power spikes are fine": the spike was great, but it never ended, so it stopped being a spike.
- "Tradeoffs must be real": not in this run. Physical Specialist, Blood Pact and War Cry's fragility all cost me nothing. Percent penalties vanish next to percent bonuses in the millions.
- "No single obvious path": Physical Specialist was obvious at 5 points. After that, "equip the biggest exponent" was the only path.
- "Always something to work toward": no. Slots cap at 6 and points pile up.
- "Builds emerge from combinations": the early Plate Armor chain did this. Nothing later did.

## Suggestions

1. **Make enemies keep pace with items.** Either cut item growth per world or raise enemy growth to match. This one change would give every other system in the game a chance to matter.
2. **Cap or flatten percent rolls on items** (crit multiplier, damage, health). A +5570% roll on a world-4 ring should not be best in slot at world 38.
3. **Make percent penalties multiply rather than add**, so Blood Pact's -50% health and Speed Specialist's -50% damage are real costs at any gear level.
4. **Give uniques stats that scale with the world you are in**, or make their triggers strong enough to wear at any level. I wanted to build around Butcher's Cleaver and Deep Wounds and could not justify it.
5. **Give bleed something to do against one-shots**: bosses with a damage cap per hit, or armor that only bleed shreds. That would make "slow, sustained, shreds defense" a reason to pick the archetype.
6. **Make enemies actually hit**, so War Cry's fragility, Spiked Shield and Bloodletting come into play. A Riposte build needs something to riposte.
7. **Add something to spend late points on**: more slots, or tier 5 talents that change rules rather than add numbers.
8. **Add bulk discard or auto-salvage by rarity**, and a small base mana regen so the first aura is usable.
