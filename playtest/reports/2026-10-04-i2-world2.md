# i2-world2: shield, Immolation and fire at the start of world 2

## Run
- Seed 303. NOT a fresh start: level 12 at world 2 wave 1, 11 talent and 11 spell points, 40 drops. 40m00s game time, 11 `run` calls.
- Ended level 13, highest world 2 wave 6, 3 deaths, 783 kills (19.6/min overall).
- Yes, a sensibly built level 12 can fight at world 2 wave 1: first 3 minutes were 42 kills, no deaths, wave 3 reached.

## Build
- Spells: Raise Shield, Immolation, Fireball, Arcane Missile, plus Regeneration and Strike as dead filler.
- Talents: Shield Bearer 5, Arcane Affinity 2, Shield Specialist, Magical Specialist, Wildfire, Widened Focus.
- Items: Tower Shield (865 shield), Spellbook, Dragon Scale Mail (fire +42, shield regen +7), Mage Hood, mana legs, boots, ring, amulet, Ring of Elements. End stats: health 1, shield 3083, defense 852, mana cost 2106.
- Setup choices: talents were interesting. Shield Specialist (health set to 1) was a real gamble and it killed the 587 to 1195 health on every Dragon Scale Mail. Items were not: any world 2 item beats any world 1 item tenfold, so I just picked the world 2 one per slot.
- The stash felt too rich. Seven or eight world 2 pieces put me at one hit per kill on wave 1.
- Widened Focus did not earn its 2 points. With 11 spell points I could afford four useful spells, and I had five slots already. Spell points were the limit, not slots.

## How it felt
- Minutes 0 to 5: strong. World 2 waves 1 to 3 at 14.0 kills/min, Arcane Missile averaging 3116 against 1676hp goblins, so one hit each. Died on wave 4 to the Shadow Assassin boss (Slash 510).
- Minutes 5 to 12, 14 to 21, 27 to 35: re-clearing world 1. About 6.5, 6.3 and 7.7 minutes. Half the run (roughly 20 of 40 minutes).
- The re-clear is a chore, not a speed-clear. Enemies have 33 to 1564hp and I hit for 3000 to 5700, yet each wave takes 38 to 50 seconds whatever I do. Cast time (6s and 8.5s) sets the pace, so my power does not show.
- World 2 stretches: 14.0, 16.0, 15.2, 12.3 and 14.4 kills/min. By wave 4 to 5 (5600 to 6100hp) it is two hits per kill.

## Fun
- Immolation plus Wildfire on packs. Fire was 33% of all damage (818k of 2.49M) with no targeting.
- Fire gear cutting both ways. Dragon Scale fire +78 raised my self-burn from about 125/s to about 210/s, and I died on wave 3 to a Goblin Slash for 11 because the burn had eaten the shield. Swapping back to fire +42 with shield regen got me to wave 6. That was the one real gear decision of the run.
- Mana cost as both damage and price is a neat knob. I was never out of mana (0% too low), sitting at 22 to 28%.

## Not fun
- Half the run in world 1 with nothing at stake: hardest hit taken there was 1 to 9.
- 106 drops, about 12 worth reading. World 1 drops after a death are pure trash.
- Two of six slots held dead spells. Regeneration does nothing at 1 health; Strike did 702 damage over 334 hits.

## Enemies
- World 2 is world 1 with bigger numbers. Same names, same boss order, same spells.
- Only three things asked anything of me: Shadow Assassin (Poison Stab 794, Slash 472 to 510, killed me once), Orc Rend (up to 391 plus bleed), and Arcane Specter (Arcane Missile 712, killing blow on wave 6). The Specter's hit seemed to ignore my 852 defense, which is the first time defense stopped being a full answer.
- Everything else hit for under 110 through defense and blurred together. Enemy Curse was on me 11% of a stretch and I never felt it.
- My biggest threat was myself: 377k self-burn against about 21k from every enemy status combined.
- One change to make world 2 a new place: give it its own roster rule, for example every pack has one caster whose damage ignores defense, so shield size and not defense is the stat that matters there.

## Too strong
- Mana cost stacking. Five common items took mana cost from 0 to 2106 and both nukes scale 2x with it, with regen keeping up.
- Shield soaking self-burn. 200/s of fire for minutes against +15/s shield regen and I only died to it once. Something refills the shield faster than the stat says.

## Too weak
- Widened Focus at this level (see Build). Regeneration with Shield Specialist. Strike with Magical Specialist.
- Rarity. A common world 2 Tower Shield (865 shield) replaced an epic one (383); a legendary world 1 Wand L10 was never worth a look.
- Immolation in world 1 is only a modest speed-up: 20.3 kills/min without it against 24.6 to 27.7 with it.

## Bugs or oddities
- Item level labels restart in world 2, so "L1" items are ten times stronger than "L10" ones. Unreadable in a stash.
- Shield shows +15/s but survives about 200/s of self-burn at 99% Immolation uptime. I could not see where the refill comes from (Raise Shield recasts are my guess, untested).
- With Immolation unequipped I still took 763 fire, so some enemy applies fire without it showing in hits taken.
- Raise Shield uptime 98 to 100% at 10 stacks, Immolation 94 to 99%: no upkeep decision at all.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Let power show in the re-clear: overkill damage should carry to the next enemy, or kills should refund cast time. A talent version ("Overkill") would fit Rampage.
2. Make Shield Specialist change dead stats into live ones: convert health from gear into shield at some rate, so health items are a choice and not trash.
3. Show item world in the label (W2 L1) and let rarity add a build-shaping affix rather than more of the same stat.
4. Give world 2 a defining enemy rule (defense-ignoring casters, or shield-breakers) so it asks for a different build than world 1.
5. Cut Widened Focus to 1 point or give it a spell point with the slot; and make self-burn scale visibly with fire gear in Immolation's tooltip, since that tradeoff is the best thing in the build.
