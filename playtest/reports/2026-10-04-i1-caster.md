# i1-caster: magic caster, first 25 minutes

## Run
Seed 201, fresh character, 24m57s game time, 12 `run` calls. Level 8, highest world 1 wave 7, 3 deaths, 251 kills (10.1/min), 30 drops.
## Build
- End: Arcane Missile, Fireball, Ice Spike, Health Regeneration aura. Talents: Arcane Affinity 4, Mana Efficiency 1, Magical Specialist, Wildfire. Two Arcane Staves (one legendary), Enchanted Boots, Plate Armor (onHit speedBoost trigger), Leather Leggings, Amulet of Luck, two filler rings.
- Order: L2 Missile + Affinity, L3 regen aura + Affinity, L4 Fireball replaces Strike + Mana Efficiency, L5 Affinity, L6 Ice Spike + Affinity, L7 Magical Specialist, L8 Wildfire. 2 spell points left unspent: slots were full and Lightning Bolt (3) was never affordable at a moment I had a free slot.
## How it felt
- 0-3 min: clear and fast. Missile went 10 -> 20 -> 32 damage with two Affinity points against 15-18 hp enemies: one cast per kill. Kills/min 4.3 (Strike only) -> 9.0 -> 10.3.
- 3-8 min: comfortable. Waves 2-4, 1-2 casts per kill at wave 2, 2-3 at waves 3-4 (hit 45-60 vs 62-136 hp), Orc Warlord (203 hp) about 5 casts. Kills/min 8.9, 8.0, 6.6. Mana averaged 72-79%, out of mana 0%.
- 8-21 min: the mana cliff. The 4th Affinity point plus Ice Spike took me from 0% to 82% of the time out of mana in one level. Dropping Ice Spike did not help (79%). Magical Specialist: 78%, then 92%. Kills/min 13.8 (mostly re-clearing waves 1-3 after a death), 7.0, 9.6, 12.8. It still progressed, so a slow grind and not a stall, but nothing I picked moved the needle.
- 21-25 min: two Arcane Staves and mana boots took regen from 12/s to 20/s. Out of mana fell to 58-64%, kills/min 14.3 at waves 4-5, and waves 5, 6 and 7 fell in 4 minutes. Wave 7: hit about 95 vs 171-343 hp, 2-4 casts.
- Deaths: 1 in stretch 2 (Goblin Slash for 3, no healing yet), 1 in stretch 7 (Arcane Specter Arcane Missile for 57 against 56 max health), 1 in stretch 9 (Storm Elemental Chain Lightning for 25 after 232 damage from it). 0 in the other nine stretches.
## Fun
- The first two Affinity points: visible doubling, one-shot kills. Best level-ups of the run.
- The legendary Arcane Staff from Orc Warlord at 19m56s. It fixed exactly what the build was starving for, and the next stretch showed it.
- Wildfire with Fireball: fire status damage went from 136 in 67s to 991 in 160s (about 4% -> 11% of damage). A real two-piece payoff, even if small.
## Not fun
- Once starved, damage per second is just 2 x mana regen. Affinity raises cost and damage together, so points 4+ did nothing, and nothing tells you that. Affinity vs Mana Efficiency looks like a choice at each level and is not: Affinity while mana is full, regen after.
- Magical Specialist was a non-event: +60% cost against +50% regen, out of mana 78% before and after. The tier 2 capstone the build aims at should feel like something.
- The Health Regeneration aura is free, on from level 3 and held health at 97-99% average for the whole run. Level-up choices for defense never came up; I ended on 64 health and it only mattered against two enemies.
- Four of thirty drops were for my build until minute 20. Warhammers, greatswords and shields were instant trash with attack set to 0.
## Enemies
- Asked something: Storm Elemental (306 damage taken in total, 100 lightning status that ignores my 17 defense) and Arcane Specter (215, including a 57 one-shot). Both punish low max health, which is the caster's real weakness. Those were the only two I learned to fear.
- Only bigger numbers: Goblin King, Bone Warden (10 damage to me across three kills), Orc Warlord, Shadow Assassin, Inferno Lord, Frost Queen. I killed the last two without noticing they were bosses.
- Unreadable: Necromancer or Dark Mage put Curse on me 10-39% of the time and I could not see what it cost me. Enemies also had "Arcane Boost" 1-4% of the time with no visible effect.
- Unfair: Arcane Specter hitting for 57 at wave 4 when a caster with no health gear has 56. One hit from full to dead, with 12/s regen irrelevant.
## Too strong
- Health Regeneration aura: 0 points, +12 health per second on a 40-64 health character, up 81% of the run.
- Arcane Affinity levels 1-3: +3 cost each is +60% Missile damage per point at level 2.
## Too weak
- Arcane Affinity from level 4 on, and Magical Specialist, while mana-starved (see above).
- Ice Spike: 21% of damage and I never saw the slow do anything in the output. Damage taken did not visibly drop.
- Plate Armor's onHit speedBoost 12: no measurable change in casts per minute.
## Bugs or oddities
- The mana cost stat is added to auras too: Health Regeneration went from 25 to about 57 mana with no stronger effect (regen stayed 12/s). It became my most expensive spell per second and hit 68% uptime in the starved stretches.
- Swapping wands and boots dropped speed from 11 to 4 and I could not tell what that changed.
- manaLeech +9 from the staves: no line in the run output shows mana gained from it.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Do not add the mana cost stat to auras (or let it lengthen or strengthen them). Paying 57 mana for a 25 mana effect quietly taxes the caster for its own core talent.
2. Make Magical Specialist change play, not ratios: for example +60% cost, +100% mana regen, or "magic spells cost nothing for 3s after a kill". Right now it nets slightly negative mana.
3. Give Arcane Affinity a diminishing flat part (+3, +2, +1...) or give Mana Efficiency +15% per level, so the regen side keeps pace and the pick stays a choice past level 5.
4. Charge 1 spell point for the Health Regeneration aura or cut it to +4/s. Then early defense is a decision, and Iron Constitution has a reason to exist for casters.
5. Show status effects in the run output and tooltips: what Curse and ice slow actually did, and mana gained from manaLeech. Also cap Arcane Specter's hit around 60% of a no-gear health pool at its wave.
