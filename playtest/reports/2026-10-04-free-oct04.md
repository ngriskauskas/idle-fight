# Playtest: free-oct04 (2026-10-04)

## Run

- Seed 404, no assigned build. Stopped early at **23m33s of game time** on the coordinator's instruction (budget was 3 hours), so nothing here covers hours two or three.
- Level 31, world 9 wave 2 (the harness prints "highest: world 9 wave 11", see Bugs). 7 deaths, all in the first 7 minutes on world 1 waves 1 and 2.
- 1515 kills, 339 item drops, 19 talent levels taken.
- One harness crash at 23m28s ("engine did not settle after 500 passes"), details under Bugs.

## Build

Ended as a physical crit/War Cry build that items had made irrelevant:

- Spells: Battle Cry, Quick Strike, War Cry, Whirlwind, Raise Shield, Immolation (Strike, Slash and Health Regen were used and dropped).
- Talents: Warrior Training 3, Iron Constitution 2, Physical Specialist, Adrenaline Surge, Critical Toxin, Chain Reaction 1, Bloodlust, Deep Wounds, Epidemic, Battle Trance, Blood Pact, Spiked Shield, Venomous Entrance, Siphon 1, Rampage, Mana Overflow.
- Items: epic Battleaxe, epic Arcane Staff, epic Dragon Scale Mail, unique Plaguebearer's Mask, epic Plate Leggings, Heavy Boots, Ring of Elements, Ring of Poison, rare Amulet of Plague.

How it changed: I started Strike + Slash + Battle Cry. Venom Fang (unique, poison on every attack, 4m12s) made me plan a poison-crit build with Quick Strike and Critical Toxin. That plan never happened because enemies died before poison ticked (5 poison stacks ticked in the whole run). An epic Plate Armor with "onHit -> speedBoost 30" turned it into an attack-speed build at 7m36s. A Battleaxe with a big crit multiplier turned it into a crit build at 9m54s. From 11m28s on the build was "whatever dropped last".

## How it felt

- **0 to 7 minutes: rough and unclear.** I died twice before my first level, and 7 times before leaving wave 1/2. Starting health is 25 and a Goblin did 102 damage to me in the first two minutes. Mana does not regenerate (0/s), so the free Health Regen aura was out of mana 63 to 74% of the time. There was no hint of what to do except "take damage talent, buy the cheap spell". The first spell slot at level 6 sat empty for a level because nothing I wanted cost 2 points.
- **7m36s to 9m17s: the hook.** Plate Armor's speed boost took me from 17.5 to 33 kills/min, then Quick Strike plus Physical Specialist took it to 104 kills/min and five waves in 23 seconds. That was a great moment.
- **10 to 23 minutes: no game left.** Kills sat at 90 to 105 per minute whatever I did, which looks like the spawn rate. Every wave took about 10 seconds. I took no damage at all after 12m28s. Nine worlds in 23 minutes is not "grindy and slow".

I cannot say how the late stretch plays; by minute 12 the decisions had already stopped mattering.

## Fun

- Equipping Plate Armor and watching hit counts double (55 hits in 2m24s, then 118 in 1m18s).
- Bloodlust with War Cry: a clear, readable combination that I could feel.
- Seeing a unique drop at 4 minutes and planning around it.
- The first Battleaxe: crit went to x4.26 and damage per minute jumped about 4x.

## Not fun

- Dying on wave 1 for seven minutes with nothing to change.
- Inventory flood: 31 drops in 36 seconds, 49 in a minute, 157 items in the bag at 14m28s. I had to discard them one by one.
- Gear choice collapsed to "equip the newest item". A common Battleaxe from world 2 beat my rare one by 5x damage and 7x crit multiplier.
- Talents stopped mattering: +3 damage per level next to a weapon with +5182 damage and +50300%.
- Auras were starved all run: mana "too low to cast anything" 83% of the time overall.

## Combinations

- **Plate Armor (onHit speedBoost 30) + fast attacks (Slash, Quick Strike):** 17.5 to 33 to 104 kills/min. Changed how the build played. Best thing in the run.
- **War Cry + Bloodlust:** War Cry alone was up 48% of the time with max 2 stacks (no mana). With Bloodlust: 100% uptime, 86 stacks in a minute, later 1428. Damage per minute went 2.8M to 7.6M at the same gear. Works exactly as described.
- **Shield + War Cry:** health stayed at 100% so stacks were never lost. Felt right, but nothing was hitting me anyway.
- **Siphon + auras:** average mana 4% to 10%, Raise Shield uptime 0% to 18% over equal 2-minute stretches. Helps, not enough with a 50 mana pool.
- **Venom Fang + Critical Toxin + Epidemic + Venomous Entrance:** did nothing. Enemies die in one hit.
- **Chain Reaction:** 99 lightning stacks ticked in the whole run. No visible effect.
- **Spiked Shield:** Riposte never appeared in the damage list; nothing attacked me after I took it.
- **Blood Pact:** Crushing Weight did 12 to 14% of damage. Halved health cost nothing.
- **Rampage, Mana Overflow, Immolation:** taken at 23m28s, only 5 seconds of play after. Not rated.
- Wanted: a way to make poison matter against things that die fast (for example poison carrying over to the next spawn).

## Items

- Mattered: epic Plate Armor (trigger), rare Battleaxe (crit multiplier), then anything from a later wave.
- All Plate Armors have the speed-boost trigger, including commons. It read like an epic affix; it is a base property.
- Item stats grow far faster than enemies. A world 1 Greatsword L8 has +51 damage; a world 4 Battleaxe L10 has +5182 and +50300%; a world 8 Battleaxe has +3,965,545. Enemies went from 448 hp (world 2) to 11,264 hp (world 9). My attack was 2 billion.
- Percent affixes are huge: "damage +31045%", "health +5204149275%", "critMultiplier +225213415%".
- Uniques were outclassed within a minute. Venom Fang (L1) and Deathbringer Plate (L2) were trash on arrival once I was past world 2.
- Item level resets each world (L1 to L10) while power keeps growing, so level tells you nothing.

## Too strong

- Item scaling, as above. This is the whole run.
- Physical Specialist: +100% damage for 1 point with no cost to a physical build. Attack 19 to 38.
- Plate Armor's speedBoost 30 on every hit.
- Battleaxe crit multiplier: +1944 on a common at world 2 wave 6 (crit x20.96).

## Too weak or useless

- Health Regen aura at the start: no mana to keep it up.
- War Cry and Raise Shield as cast spells without a mana source.
- Tier 1 flat talents after the first world.
- Poison and lightning status effects in this run (see Combinations).

## Bugs or oddities

- **Crash:** `run free-oct04 --seconds 300 --no-stop` at 23m28s threw `Error: engine did not settle after 500 passes` at `settle` / `flush` / `tick` in `playtest/.build/cli.mjs`. It came right after I equipped an Arcane Staff (mana 120,878,309, regen 8,868,473/s) and took Rampage, Mana Overflow and Venomous Entrance, with Immolation equipped. I do not know which piece caused it. A 5-second run afterwards completed.
- Tier gates count talent levels, not points spent. With 16 points spent I was told "tier 4 needs 15 talent points spent"; tier 3 talents cost 2 but count as 1.
- "highest wave" always reads 11 from world 2 onward ("highest: world 9 wave 11").
- Battle Cry showed 100% uptime and up to 112 stacks while mana was "too low to cast anything 100% of the time" and mana regen was 0.
- Health never refills: it sat at exactly 79%, then 51% (after Blood Pact) for minutes. In the last stretch it read "average 0%" with 4.4e16 max health.
- Enemy health is shown negative ("Bandit L7 -774/1666hp").
- Curse's description says "Curse yourself, doubling damage" but it lowers enemy defense and speed.

## Against DESIGN.md

- "Grindy and slow": no. Worlds took about 100 seconds each from minute 10.
- "Sudden power spikes when a build clicks": yes, once, at 7 to 9 minutes, and it was good.
- "Tradeoffs must be real": no. Blood Pact's health cost, Physical Specialist's mana cost and negative speed on hammers cost me nothing.
- "No single obvious path": the path was "equip newest drop".
- "Builds emerge from combinations": true for about three minutes, then items buried it.
- Archetypes: War Cry and Shield showed up as intended. Poison, lightning, fire, ice and mana never got a chance.

## Suggestions

1. **Flatten item scaling to match enemy scaling.** If a drop is 10 to 30% better than the last, gear becomes a choice and talents keep their meaning.
2. **Cap or rework percent affixes.** Tens of thousands of percent make every other number noise.
3. **Soften the first seven minutes.** More starting health or a weaker Goblin, and some base mana regen so the free aura works. Dying five times on wave 1 is not a hook.
4. **Give enemies enough health to live a few hits past world 1.** Status builds (poison, bleed, fire, lightning) cannot exist when everything dies in one hit.
5. **Make Plate Armor's speed boost a rare affix or a talent.** It is the most fun piece I found; it should be something you chase.
6. **Cut drop volume or add bulk discard and auto-compare.** 49 drops a minute is a chore.
7. **Give Physical Specialist a real cost**, for example no auras, so it is a choice.
8. **Find the "engine did not settle" loop** and count tier gates in points spent, or reword the message.
