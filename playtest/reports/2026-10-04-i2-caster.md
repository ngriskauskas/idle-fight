# Playtest i2-caster (2026-10-04)

## Run
- Seed 55, 60m00s game time, 15 `run` calls. Level 9 (1252/1280 xp), highest world 1 wave 7, 14 deaths. World 2 not reached.
- Whole run: 861 kills (14.3/min), 111,246 direct damage, 8,390 status damage (7.5% of direct), out of mana 9% of the time.

## Build
- Spells at end: Fireball, Ice Spike, Lightning Bolt, Health Regeneration aura. Strike (min 0-4) and Arcane Missile (min 1-39) were dropped.
- Talents (8 points): Arcane Affinity 3, Mana Efficiency 2, Iron Constitution 1, Wildfire, Magical Specialist.
- Gear at end: two Arcane Staffs (L6 +6 regen, L5 +4 regen), Dragon Scale Mail (fire +3), Mage Hood, legendary Plate Leggings, Enchanted Boots, Ring of Elements, legendary Ring of Vitality, Amulet of Mana. 381 mana, +37/s, mana cost +67, 107 health, 16 defense.
- Not tested, for lack of points: Frostbite, Chain Reaction, any AoE spell. Eight talent points and 9 spell points in an hour did not stretch that far.

## How it felt
- 0-10 min: rough. 4 deaths on wave 1 (enemy 3-4) as a Strike plus Arcane Missile caster with 40 health; poison did more than attacks (51 of 173 taken). 3.6 to 7 kills/min, 2.3 to 2.9 hits per kill. Health Regeneration aura plus the first body armor ended the deaths (0 deaths min 3-11). Fireball at 4m took damage from 264 to 650 per stretch.
- 10-20 min: out of mana 51% of the time (min 7-11) until an uncommon Arcane Staff L3 dropped at wave 3; that one item took it to 0% and kills from 7.0 to 9.4/min. Ice Spike added here. First boss death, wave 4 enemy 10.
- 20-30 min: 12.5 to 13 kills/min, 1.7 hits per kill. Two deaths at wave 5 (enemy 1 and 6).
- 30-40 min: Wildfire. 16 to 17 kills/min, one death per stretch, always wave 5. Adding +11 mana cost gear put me out of mana 34% of the time and total damage did not move (12,150 then 12,409).
- 40-50 min: Magical Specialist plus Lightning Bolt for Missile: damage per hit about 110 to about 175, stretch damage 12,409 to 21,026, first wave 6. Kills/min flat at 16.3 because fights were already 1.1 hits per kill. 3 deaths.
- 50-60 min: Staff L6 and L5 put mana at 95-97% full. 19 to 22 kills/min, wave 7 at 58m, one death per stretch (wave 5 enemy 7, wave 5 enemy 3, wave 7 enemy 4).
- Stuck at wave 5 from minute 18 to 57: nine of 14 deaths were on waves 5-7. Each death costs about 4-5 minutes of one-shotting waves 1-4 to get back. Pace is neither grind nor runaway; it is a loop.
- Deaths were hard to read. In death stretches average health was 95-100%, so they were bursts: one enemy type dominates each (Storm Elemental 203 at min 51-55, Frost Wolf 124 to 140 three times, Necromancer 128) against 100-116 health.

## Fun
- Magical Specialist is a real commitment (attack went to 0) and an obvious jump: the one moment the build clicked.
- The first staff drop fixing mana felt great; drops of caster gear were frequent (142 items) and readable.
- Three elements rotating with near-equal shares (35/35/30%) looked like a caster.

## Not fun
- Waves 1-4 after every death: one hit per enemy, zero danger, then a sudden burst death on wave 5.
- Talent points are scarce (8) and tier 1 stat talents eat the first five, so the interesting tier 2 pieces arrive after 25 minutes and I could afford two.
- Mana cost gear is a trap without regen: more cost, same damage, more idle time.

## Too strong
- Health Regeneration aura (free unlock): 23/s against 107 health, up 87-94% of the time. Average health 96-100% all run.
- Magical Specialist: +69% stretch damage for one point with no cost a caster feels.
- Arcane Staff regen: one L3 uncommon removed 51% mana downtime.

## Too weak
- Status damage. Per stretch, status as a share of direct: 12%, 19%, 21%, 7%, 2%, then with Wildfire 11%, 9%, 8%, 3%, 9%, 5%. Fire did 6,748 all run, lightning 376 (2% of Lightning Bolt's direct), ice 1,266.
- Wildfire: fire went from 358 per 400s to 1,223 per 400s, still about 10% of direct. Enemies die to the hit before fire ticks.
- Arcane Missile: 1x mana cost multiplier makes it the worst use of mana once mana is short. Iron Constitution: +6 health did nothing against 120-200 bursts.

## Bugs or oddities
- Output header says "ice only slows" but ice is listed with status damage (271 in one stretch, 1,266 total).
- "Lowest health" was 33%, 52%, 36%, 54% and 16% in five stretches that each contained a death; it showed 0% in others.
- Fire status dealt 0 in a stretch with 11 Fireball hits (min 23-26).
- Stats showed speed -21 while cursed; Curse appears under "auras on you" 13% of the run and Arcane Boost under "auras on enemies", presumably enemy casts.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make status matter for casters: fights at 1.1 hits per kill give fire no time. Raise status stacks relative to direct damage, or let leftover stacks carry to the next enemy by default.
2. Soften burst on waves 5-7 (Storm Elemental, Frost Wolf, Necromancer) or show the killing blow in the run output; deaths from 95%+ health are not explainable.
3. Do not send a death back to wave 1; one or two waves back would cut the 4-5 minute replay of trivial waves.
4. Give Health Regeneration a real cost, and give Magical Specialist a drawback casters feel (for example less health).
5. Give a starting caster one regen source that is not a drop: minutes 7-11 were half spent waiting on mana, and Mana Efficiency alone (2 points) did not fix it.
