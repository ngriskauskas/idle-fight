# n5-late: sturdy status build against the late bosses of world 1, after the Raise Shield nerf

## Run
- Seed 403, 40m00s game time, 12 `run` calls of 200s. NOT a fresh start: level 10 at world 1 wave 7, 9 talent and 9 spell points, 20 drops. All points spent and items equipped before the first run (checked with `status`). Ended level 10 (xp 2272/2560), highest wave 9 (boss reached once), 3 deaths, 443 kills.
- Bosses met: Storm Titan (w7) 4 times, Plague Master (w8) twice, Dragon Lord (w9) once, Frost Queen (w6) 3 times on re-clears. Wave 10 and Shadow Emperor not reached.

## Build
- Start: Strike, Poison Stab, Fireball, Raise Shield, Battle Cry. Talents: Shield Bearer 2, Stone Wall, Mana Efficiency, Warrior Training, Epidemic, Critical Toxin, Rampage 1, Adrenaline Surge. Rare Plate Armor (on-hit trigger), Battleaxe, Arcane Shield, Iron Helm, Ring of Vitality, Ring of Power, Amulet of Plague. Stats with 5 stacks: health 121, defense 42, shield 118 (+5.5/s), speed -10, crit 34% x2.23.
- At 20m I swapped Raise Shield for Health Regeneration as a test and never went back. At 23m20s: rare Plate Armor L8 and rare Enchanted Boots L8 (health +44, mana cost +14). At 30m: Arcane Shield L9 (damage +21%). End: health 155, defense 51, shield 108 (+5/s), health regen 12/s, speed +7.
- Difference from k3-late: Mana Efficiency instead of Regeneration, Adrenaline Surge instead of Wildfire.

## How it felt
- Stretch by stretch (kills/min, lowest health, deaths). With Raise Shield: 1) w7, 5.7, 40%, 0. 2) 18.9, 0%, death to Storm Titan, then w1-4. 3) w4-6, 8.4, 100%. 4) w6-7, 5.4, 8%. 5) w7-8, 8.4, 0%, death at w8 enemy 8. 6) w1-4, 18.6, 100%.
- Without Raise Shield: 7) w4-7, 9.0, 69%. 8) w7-8, 9.3, 64%. 9) w8-9, 8.7, 0%, death to Dragon Lord. 10) w1-5, 22.5, 100%. 11) w5-7, 11.1, 100%. 12) w7-9, 6.9, 71%.
- Health no longer sits at 100%. The shield breaks now: 40% on the first Storm Titan attempt, 8% against Frost Queen (432 damage in the fight), and two deaths with five stacks up. The "untouchable or dead" feel from k3-late is gone below wave 9. That is a clear improvement.
- Deaths: Storm Titan Lightning Bolt 39 (hardest hit Chain Lightning 44) at w7 boss; Berserker Rend 35 at w8 enemy 8 (hardest hit that stretch Flame Lord Inferno 87); Dragon Lord Inferno 239 after Meteor 85 at w9 boss.
- Re-clear from death back to a dead Storm Titan: about 10.5 min with Raise Shield (3m40s to 14m09s), about 8.5 min switching off it midway (16m30s to 25m04s), about 7 min without it and with better gear (29m50s to 36m57s). Waves 1-4 take about 3m20s either way.
- No level-up in 40 minutes again (288 xp short). All decisions after setup were item swaps and one spell swap.

## Fun
- The second Storm Titan attempt (14m09s) felt earned: he killed me at the first try, I came back with nothing changed but boots and won with health dipping low. A grind fight, not a wall.
- The Raise Shield test was the best decision of the run: dropping a "mandatory" spell made me both faster and safer, which I did not expect.
- Enchanted Boots L8: mana cost +14 tripled Fireball (577 to 2991 per stretch, 13% to 33% of direct damage). An item that changed what a spell was worth.
- Poison still carries: 34487 of 45560 status damage. Critical Toxin with 34% crit, and Epidemic on the re-clear.

## Not fun
- Raise Shield now costs more than it gives. Same start point (w4 enemy 10), 200s each: with it I reached w6 enemy 8 at 8.4 kills/min; without it w7 enemy 1 at 9.0, Frost Queen included. Against Frost Queen I took 432 damage (lowest 8%) with it and 229 (lowest 69%) without, because -10 speed makes every fight longer.
- Dragon Lord is still a two-cast kill: Meteor 85 plus Inferno 239 is 324 against 248 health plus shield. No answer in reach at level 10.
- Rampage 1 and Adrenaline Surge: I could not see either in the numbers. Waves 1-4 took the same 3m20s as k3-late.

## Enemies
- Storm Titan (w7): typical hit 39 to 44, one Lightning Bolt for 70. 122 to 288 damage per fight plus lightning status (up to 172 a stretch). Now a real fight for a slowed build: killed me once. Still no visible trait in the output.
- Plague Master (w8): Poison Stab 31 to 32, about 177 damage per fight, poison on me 175 to 196. Fair and readable; Health Regeneration at 12/s is the answer and I had it. Fights took about 2m10s.
- Dragon Lord (w9): the only memorable boss, for the wrong reason. Inferno 239 is 2.8 times his Meteor and 3.4 times any other hit in the run.
- Frost Queen (w6): Frostbolt 75, Ice Spike 49. The best fight below wave 9: she punishes a slow build and is fine for a fast one.
- Regulars that mattered: Flame Lord (Inferno 87), Berserker (190 damage and the killing blow at w8), Knight (561 over the run), Frost Wolf (550). Wave 8 mid-wave is now more dangerous than its boss.

## Too strong
- Health Regeneration plus Ring of Vitality and Plate Armor regen: 12/s for 25 mana, 94% uptime. Health averaged 98 to 100% from wave 4 to 9. It does Raise Shield's old job with no cost.
- Mana cost on items as a Fireball multiplier while mana sits at 88 to 92% full: the cost never bites.

## Too weak
- Raise Shield at 5 stacks: +40 shield and +2.5/s for -10 speed and a spell slot. Not mandatory any more; I would not take it.
- Bleed from Amulet of Plague: 1493 damage in 40 minutes.

## Bugs or oddities
- Dragon Lord numbers flipped against k3-late (Meteor 219 and Inferno 148 there, Meteor 85 and Inferno 239 here). Storm Titan Lightning Bolt hit 70 once against 36 to 39 otherwise; about 1.8 times, more than the stated 1.5 crit.
- Curse was on me 7 to 30% of every stretch and the output still does not name its owner (owners show only for auras on enemies).
- Death 1 reads oddly: Storm Titan dealt only 83 attack damage that stretch, so I entered it already broken. The shield does not seem to recover mid-fight at +5.5/s.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Raise Shield: cut the slow to 1 speed per stack or raise it to shield +6 and +6% per stack. It should be a tradeoff a sturdy build wants, not a trap.
2. Health Regeneration: shorten to match its 10s more strictly or raise the cost to 35 mana, so regen and shield are two real sturdy paths.
3. Dragon Lord: cap Inferno near 150 or give Meteor a burn that Inferno consumes, so a fire-resist or regen piece is an answer.
4. Give Storm Titan and Plague Master a printed trait. Both are health bars with a status attached.
5. Add an xp or talent source between level 10 and 11: 40 minutes with no point to spend twice in a row.
