## How it felt
- 0-10 min: slow and fine. 3 to 4 kills/min, 2.5 to 3.3 hits per kill, no item dropped until 5m18s. First death at wave 2 enemy 9.
- 10-20 min: stuck at waves 3 to 4. Two deaths at wave 4. Poison was the main thing hurting me (572 poison against about 680 from attacks in 10:17-16:44).
- 20-30 min: War Cry as a spell reached 21 stacks but ate the mana Regeneration needed (Regeneration up 41%, too low to cast 29% of the time): 3 deaths at waves 3 to 4.
- 30-40 min: Bloodlust turned it into a runaway. Wave 4 to wave 8 in about 7 minutes, 33 kills/min, War Cry at the 30 cap and up 96% of the time.
- 40-60 min: hard wall at wave 8. Five of the 11 deaths were at waves 7 to 8, and each one costs about 4 minutes of re-clearing waves 1 to 7. Nothing I changed in gear moved the wall.
- Deaths are only partly explainable. Health averaged 95 to 100% and then went to 0, so they are bursts. The output names damage sources per stretch (Frost Wolf, Storm Elemental, poison ticks) but not what landed the killing blow.
## Fun
- The Bloodlust stretch. Kills per minute went from 14.4 to 33.3 and four new waves fell in a row. That is the "build clicks" spike DESIGN.md asks for.
## Not fun
- Bleed never mattered: 4228 status damage against 202700 direct over the run (2.0%). Enemies die in 1.2 to 1.8 hits, so a bleed has no time to tick.
- Fights do not last several hits after the first five minutes. Only the ungeared opening was 2.5 to 3.3 hits per kill.
## Too strong
- Bloodlust: one talent point for a permanent 30 stacks (+300% damage) with no mana cost. Kills/min 14.4 to 33.3.
- War Cry's fragility is not a real risk with Bloodlust. Uptime was 77 to 96% and it hit the cap in every stretch, because each kill hands a stack back. The harness does not print how often stacks were lost, so I cannot give a count.
## Too weak
- Rend as a bleed source: 6 base stacks is noise next to 100 to 200 damage hits. It was my weakest attack in every stretch (24 to 27% of direct damage).
- Deep Wounds: bleed share was 5 to 6% before it and 1.3 to 3.0% after. Other things changed at the same time, so this is not a clean test, but I never saw it do anything.
- Physical Specialist's cost. With a mana leech item (Shield, mana leech +10) mana averaged 89% and Regeneration stayed up 92% with regen set to 0. Without mana leech mana averaged 4% and Regeneration was up 3%, yet kills/min rose (19.3 to 22.9) and deaths went 1 to 2. Losing mana cost me almost nothing.
- Epic Tower Shield (shield 39, speed -8): slowest late stretch (16.7 kills/min) and health average fell to 80%.
## Bugs or oddities
- "status bonus on every attack: fire 14, poison 2, lightning 5" from rings, but the only status damage ever reported dealt was bleed. Either those bonuses do nothing on physical spells or they are not counted.
- "auras on enemies: Arcane Boost" appeared twice, and "Curse" showed under auras on me up to 27% of the time. Enemy casts seem to be logged under the wrong side's label, or enemies buff themselves with my catalog's aura.
- Status snapshots swing without any gear change (attack 118 to 79, speed 11 to -21, then speed -40). Probably the enemy Curse, but its catalog text says defense -5 and speed -25, not an attack cut.
## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make bleed scale with the hit or with attack, not a flat 6 stacks, so it keeps pace with War Cry and gear. Right now direct damage outgrows it 50 to 1.
2. Give Bloodlust a cost or a slower gain (a chance per kill, or stacks that must be earned again from zero after any health loss with a short lockout).
3. Make Physical Specialist block mana leech too, or it has no cost for anyone holding one leech item.
4. Lengthen fights from wave 2 onward. 1.2 to 1.8 hits per kill leaves no room for any status build.
5. Soften the wave 7 to 8 wall or the death penalty: returning to wave 1 costs about 4 minutes per attempt, which reads as punishment, not grind. Also print the killing blow in the run output.
