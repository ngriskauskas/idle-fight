# i5-caster

## Run

Fresh character, seed 816; stopped at 3:00 game time after harness output began interleaving other runs. Level 3, highest world 1 wave 1, 0 deaths. I did not reach a confirmed boss kill or a new wave.

## Build and test

Started with Strike and Health Regeneration. At 1:00 I took Mana Efficiency 1 (+3 mana/s, total +5/s), unlocked Arcane Missile, and replaced Strike while keeping the aura. At 2:00 I added Arcane Affinity 1 (status showed manaCost +3). No items dropped. I never got to test another magic spell or aura.

The first two minutes together: 10 kills (5/min), 220 direct damage: Arcane Missile 10 hits/124 damage; opening Strike 12 hits/96. Mana averaged 66%, with 21% of the time too low to cast; Regeneration uptime was 86%. The summary did not split this interval around the 1:00 build change, so it cannot measure Mana Efficiency alone.

From 2:00 to 3:00 with both talents and the same Missile/aura loadout: 3 kills (3/min), 10 Missile hits for 177 damage, 2 crits, no deaths. Mana averaged 82%; too low to cast 0% of the time; Regeneration uptime 90%. The Goblin King hit for 117 total attack damage in that stretch; my lowest health was 60%. I reached enemy 10 of wave 1, but no new-wave timestamp or boss kill was recorded. Its Slash/Rend demanded survival, though Health Regeneration kept the encounter from feeling dangerous; it read more like a slower health check than a build-specific counter.

## Caster choices and feel

Mana Efficiency plus the healing aura and Missile still showed 21% starvation in the two-minute cumulative snapshot, but that number includes the pre-Missile opening minute. After Arcane Affinity was added, starvation was 0% in the next minute and mana averaged 82%. This points toward Affinity helping sustain casting, but the comparison is not clean: both talents were active together, and the later stretch was dominated by the Goblin King. I cannot say which talent is better from this run.

The aura/magic tradeoff was clear in intent but not severe after both investments: the aura stayed up 90% while Missile landed 10 hits in the final minute. The uncomfortable choice would have been dropping healing for another attack spell, but the run ended before I tested it. Progress slowed from 5 kills/min over the first two minutes to 3/min in the boss stretch. No enemy forced a spell swap, and no wave fell so quickly that the pacing felt like a free clear.

## Against DESIGN.md / suggestions

The spell-plus-aura setup made a recognizable caster, and Mana Efficiency versus Arcane Affinity looked like a meaningful regeneration-versus-cost-efficiency choice. But this short run did not establish a winner or a distinct playstyle difference. The Goblin King was the only memorable enemy; its high Slash/Rend damage created pressure, but there was no readable mechanic asking for a different spell or aura.

For a follow-up, snapshot each one-minute interval separately before and after each talent with the same enemy/wave and loadout; then test removing Regeneration for a second magic spell. Add a caster-relevant enemy interaction only if existing spells can answer it, so the choice becomes more than mana throughput. Harness output began mixing in unrelated runs after 3:00, so I stopped rather than report unverified progression or combat data.
