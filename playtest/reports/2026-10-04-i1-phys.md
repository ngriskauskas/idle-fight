# Playtest i1-phys (short early-game test, physical)

## Run
Seed 11, 25m00s game time, 14 `run` calls. Level 9, highest world 1 wave 10, 9 deaths, 574 kills (23.0/min), 87 items dropped.

## Build
Spells: Quick Strike, Slash, Rend, Health Regeneration (Strike dropped at 16m for Rend). Talents (8 points): Warrior Training 4, Iron Constitution 1, Physical Specialist, Deep Wounds, Bloodlust. Items: Greatsword L6 + rare Battleaxe L4, Plate Armor L6 (on-hit speed boost), Iron Helm L7, Plate Leggings L7, Heavy Boots L5, Ring of Vitality, Ring of Defense L8. No amulet dropped. War Cry and Battle Cry were never unlocked: 4 spell points for War Cry never lined up, and Bloodlust gave me War Cry for free.

## How it felt
- 0:00-2:42: 4.3 then 8.5 kills/min, 3 deaths in 2 min once I swapped the regen aura for Slash. 2-3 hits per enemy (6-8 damage into 15-18 hp). 40 health with no regen dies to wave 1 trash; the regen aura is mandatory, not a choice.
- 2:42-6:42: 8.5 then 13 kills/min, 1 death per stretch. First 4 drops arrived at ~4m; +6 defense took damage taken from ~170 to 4 per stretch.
- 6:42-8:42: Quick Strike plus shield and ring: 15 kills/min, 0 deaths, waves 1 to 3. Average hit 15.
- 8:42-10:42: 9 kills/min, wave 5. Enemy Curse sat on me 68% of the time (defense 8 to 3, speed 2 to -23) and health dipped to 10%.
- 10:42-12:42: rare Battleaxe (attack 17 to 27, crit x1.59 to x2.12): 28 kills/min, damage per stretch 1391 to 2924. Died once to Storm Elemental (112 damage into 63 health).
- 12:42-16:02: Physical Specialist (attack 27 to 51, Quick Strike hit 37 to 64), then Plate Armor: 26 then 40 kills/min, one-hit kills up to wave 4.
- 16:02-19:22: Rend, then Deep Wounds: ~31 kills/min, waves 6 to 8 first try, one death to Frost Lord (197 damage).
- 19:22-25:00: gear swap (attack 71, defense 26): 40 kills/min, damage 6232 to 10904. Bloodlust: 15597, then 21641 with Greatsword. Wave 10 Dragon did 315 into 143 health and sent me to wave 1.
- Never stuck for more than one stretch. Every death was from full health (average health 95-100% in every stretch after 6m): things either do 0 or kill me.

## Fun
- The Battleaxe drop and Physical Specialist were real spikes: each roughly doubled damage.
- Bloodlust ramping to 30 War Cry stacks while Quick Strike chains kills is the best feeling in the run.
- Plate Armor plus Quick Strike is a readable two-piece combination: Quick Strike hits went 31 to 51 per 100s.

## Not fun
- Minutes 0-4: no drops, 4 deaths, nothing to decide. The first item took ~4 minutes.
- Bleed did nothing. 63 bleed ticks all run against 78124 direct damage. Enemies die in 1-2 hits, so Rend and Deep Wounds (18 vs 21 ticks per 100s) are wasted points at this stage.
- Drops are mostly caster gear (staffs, wands, spellbooks, robes, hoods); inventory hit 68 with no reason to look at most of it.
- Too low on mana 42% of the run with only one 25-mana aura equipped. 2 regen cannot sustain Health Regeneration alone, so a second aura is out of the question.

## Too strong
- Bloodlust: 1 talent point, no spell unlock, no mana, no slot. War Cry was up 98% of the last stretch at 30 stacks (attack 141 to 211 shown, damage per stretch +43% then +39%). "Lost when you lose health" barely applies when defense zeroes most hits.
- Physical Specialist: +100% damage, and its cost (mana cost set to 0) is no cost to a physical build. Everyone physical takes it at point 6.
- Flat defense: 6 defense at 5m and 26 at 20m both reduced nearly all trash damage to 0.
- Plate Armor on-hit speed boost: at 6 it still gave +34% total hits (64 to 86 per 100s). Fine, but still the best body item by far; three more dropped.

## Too weak
- Rend, Deep Wounds and bleed in general (above). Strike once Quick Strike exists (17% of damage).
- Legendary Heavy Boots L2 (defense 4, health 5, regen 4) lost to a common L5 pair. Legendary means nothing if level dominates.
- Tier-1 talents after the rebalance: Warrior Training was +3 attack per point, invisible next to a +9 weapon or Physical Specialist. Iron Constitution +7 health did not prevent a single death.

## Bugs or oddities
- Bloodlust casts War Cry without the spell being unlocked (saves 4 spell points and a slot). Intended?
- At 4:42-6:42 I died (lowest health 0%) while logged damage taken was 4 in total. Something that kills is not being counted in "damage taken".
- Battleaxe "critMultiplier +62" is a flat +0.6 to the multiplier at item level 4; not a small affix.
- With mana cost 0 from Physical Specialist the report still says "too low to cast anything 100%"; aura costs seem unaffected, which the talent text does not say.
- Slots stayed 4/4 from level 6 to 9; I ended with an unspendable spell point.

## Top 5 suggestions
1. Make bleed matter early: let it tick faster or carry over to the next enemy on kill, so Rend and Deep Wounds beat a third direct attack somewhere before wave 10.
2. Make Bloodlust require War Cry to be unlocked and equipped, or have its stacks cost mana. Free +300% damage is the obvious path.
3. Smooth the damage curve between "0 after defense" and "one-shot": more enemies that chip (percent or status damage) and bosses that take two or three hits to kill me, so War Cry's fragility and health talents become real.
4. Give the first item by ~1 minute and bias early drops toward the equipped spell types; start mana regen at 3 or drop Health Regeneration to 20 mana so the starting aura is sustainable.
5. Give Physical Specialist a real cost for physical builds (for example auras cost double, or no crit) and raise tier-1 talents or cut early weapon damage so a talent point is felt.
