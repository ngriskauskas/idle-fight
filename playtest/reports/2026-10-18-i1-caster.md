# Playtest i1-caster (run file `i1-caster-b`), 2026-10-18

## Run
I used seed 1202 and played 45m of game time over 9 `run` calls. I finished at level 9 with a highest wave of W1 W8 and 5 deaths. **This was not a fresh start**: I began at level 8 on wave 7 with 40 stash drops and all points unspent. The name `i1-caster` was already taken by an old Oct 4 save, and `new` has no overwrite option, so the run is saved as `i1-caster-b`. Also, the old save crashes `status` because it has no `shop` field (`state.shop.restockIn` is undefined, cli.ts:230).

## Build
- **Talents:** Arcane Affinity 4, Mana Efficiency 2, Magical Specialist, Wildfire.
- **Spells:** Fireball, Lightning Bolt, Ice Spike, plus Health Regen in the fourth slot for the first 15m and Strike after that.
- **Gear:** Arcane Staff, Spellbook, Silk Leggings, Ring of Mana, Ring of Elements (rare, made by the smith), epic Mage Hood (smith), epic Dragon Scale Mail (bought), rare Amulet of Health.
- **End stats:** mana cost 134, mana regen 18/s, 130 health, 25 defense.
- **Damage over the whole run:** Fireball 58%, Lightning Bolt 31%, Ice Spike 10%, Strike 1%. Fire status added another 22k on top of 119k direct damage.

## Economy (the focus)
- **Gold only comes from selling.** Kills pay nothing.
  - 0m: 85 gold, from selling the leftover stash.
  - 5m: 85. 15m: 92. 23m44: 128.
  - Selling the Soulrender unique pushed that to 539. Buying armor dropped it to 197.
  - 33m44: 321. 45m: 324.
  - Junk earns about 3 to 10 gold per minute. One unique drop (366) was worth more than 20 minutes of junk.
- **First purchase:** epic Dragon Scale Mail for 342, at 23m44. It took that long because I needed to sell a unique to afford it. It was the best decision of the run: health went from 70 to 130 and defense from 7 to 23. Kills went from 8.8/min to 12.2/min, because I stopped dying mid-wave, and that stretch killed Inferno Lord and Frost Queen for the first time.
- **Prices:** rare (138) and epic (342) feel fair. Legendary (858) is a stretch. **Uniques at 1716 are out of reach.** My entire sellable stash was worth about 870 at its best, so no merchant unique could be bought within 45 minutes. **Overflowing Chalice was in the shop at 23m44 and I could only look at it.** It was gone at the next restock.
- **Wares are mostly physical.** Most of what was on offer was daggers, shields, a Greatsword, and Arcane Shields with damage and leech rolls. The useful caster wares were the epic Dragon Scale Mail and one epic Mage Hood.
- **Shop timing:** the shop is empty on a `--level` start until the first tick ("restocks in 0s", no wares). The restock price went from 191 to 238 when my furthest wave became 8, while the uniques on offer stayed L7 until the next restock.
- **Smith results** (6 smiths):
  - 4 Mage Hood/Iron Helm commons → uncommon Iron Helm. Physical, even though 3 inputs were caster hoods.
  - 4 uncommon weapons → rare Battleaxe. Useless to a caster.
  - 4 common bodies → uncommon Plate Armor.
  - 4 uncommons of mixed slots → **rare Ring of Elements** (+2 fire/ice/lightning, +19% speed). I kept it all run.
  - 4 rares → **epic Mage Hood** (+24 health). Kept.
  - 4 mixed commons → uncommon Tower Shield. Junk.
- **Sell or smith?** The inputs of each smith would have sold for 30 to 50% more than the result. Smithing below rare was a loss: rolls are random within the slot and mostly physical. Rare and above, smithing paid off twice (an epic out of rares worth 37 each). My verdict: sell commons, smith uncommons and rares. Smithing is a gamble, not a crafting choice, because a caster cannot steer the item type. Letting the result keep the inputs' item family when all four share it would fix that.
- **No bugs found.** Gold totals matched the listed sell values every time, nothing vanished and no action failed.

## How it felt
- **0-15m (W1-W7, 8.1-8.4 kills/min, 2 deaths): starved and fragile.**
  - Mana averaged 14-15%. Fireball did over 80% of the damage because it is first in the rotation. Lightning Bolt managed 4-5 casts per 5 minutes.
  - I died to the Arcane Specter's Arcane Missile on W7 and was one-shot on W5 by the Inferno Lord's Inferno (75 damage against 70 health).
- **Regeneration was secretly eating my mana.** Aura costs add the character's mana cost (`spell.manaCost.total = base + character.manaCost`), so Regeneration cost 25+128=153 mana every 10s. That is about 15 mana/s, against regen of 13/s. Swapping it for Strike raised damage from 10,445 to 11,997 per 5 minutes (+15%) and crits from 8 to 20. Strike itself did 1%, because Magical Specialist sets attack to 0. In practice a mana-cost caster cannot run an aura, which makes aura plus magic a non-choice.
- **15-24m (8.8-11 kills/min, 1 death on W5): stuck.** It was the same Arcane Specter and Inferno Lord loop.
- **24-34m (12.2 kills/min):** after the armor, Inferno Lord and Frost Queen fell on the first try. Storm Titan's 52-57 lightning hits ended the climb.
- **34-44m (9.5 kills/min):** Storm Titan fell at 43m13 with my health at its lowest point of 27%. That was a new furthest wave (W8) after 43 minutes, and it felt earned.
- **W8:** the Flame Lord (Inferno for 60) and a Storm Elemental killed me within a minute.
- **Difficulty felt right.** Bosses gave a few tries' worth of resistance before falling.

## Fun / Not fun
- **Fun:** the Storm Titan kill. The Ring of Elements appearing out of a junk smith. Selling a unique I could not use to buy armor I needed felt like a real trade.
- **Not fun:** "too low to cast anything" read 94-100% for every stretch while Strike was equipped, even though Strike landed 155 hits per 10 minutes. The metric seems to count Strike as uncastable, or the stat is wrong.
- **Mana is the whole game.** Damage per mana is fixed at 2x for every bolt, so sustained damage is about 2 × regen. Mana cost gear only buys burst, so picking spells barely matters.

## Combinations
- **Magical Specialist + Arcane Affinity + mana cost gear.** This is the build. Fireball hits for about 390, which overkills a 33-health rat, and those wasted casts on early waves are why I cleared them so slowly.
- **Wildfire + Fireball.** Fire status did 22k (19% of all damage). It is the only combo that clearly paid, and the run was too short for a before/after number.

## Enemies
- **Arcane Specter** was the most damaging enemy overall (465) and killed me twice with Arcane Missile. It is recognisable, but nothing in my kit counters it.
- **Inferno Lord, Flame Lord and Storm Titan** are all burst casters. They demanded health, and that is what made me buy armor. Good.
- **Waves 1-4** blurred together. Poison (453 taken) quietly did the most status damage to me and never changed a decision.

## Too strong / too weak
- **Too strong:** epic body armor at 342 gold changed the run more than any talent did.
- **Too weak:** Strike under Magical Specialist (1%). Self-auras for a mana-cost caster. Ice Spike gets crowded out by mana and did 10%.

## Against DESIGN.md
- **"Tradeoffs must be real":** the -damage% on Overflowing Chalice and Miser's Ring is free for a Magical Specialist caster. Magic damage never reads the damage stat (attackActions.ts:91), so the drawback costs this build nothing.
- **Hoard versus spend:** the gold economy adds a real choice. The 1716 unique price stops it from opening any builds within a session.

## Suggestions
1. **Cut merchant-unique prices** to about 600-800, or give 1-3 gold per kill. As it is, the "for Mana Overflow" items are window shopping.
2. **Make Chalice and Miser's Ring cost a caster something** (-mana regen% or -mana cost%), not -damage%.
3. **Stop auras inheriting the character's manaCost**, or cap the inherited part. Right now auras plus a mana-cost build is a trap.
4. **Weight shop wares and smith results toward the equipped item families.** Let 4 same-family inputs keep the family.
5. **Fix "too low to cast" when a free spell is equipped.**
6. **Stock the shop on `new`.** It is empty until the first tick.
