# Playtest report: aura-oct04

## Run

- Seed 303. Build chased: aura / status / trigger.
- Game time reached: **1h07m** of a 3 hour budget. The run was stopped early by the caller, so everything below covers 67 minutes only.
- Level 28. Highest: world 8 ("wave 11", see oddities). Ended in world 5 wave 3, climbing back after a death.
- 13 deaths: 12 in world 1 during the first 35 minutes, 1 in world 8 wave 6.
- 2330 kills, 1509 items dropped. 15 talent points and 1 spell point were still unspent when I was stopped.

## Build

Ended with:

- Spells (6/6): Battle Cry, Raise Shield, Immolation, Poison Pool, Strike, Rend.
- Talents (12 spent): mana-efficiency 4, iron-constitution 1, stone-wall 1, quickstep 1, shield-bearer 1, deep-wounds, bloodlust, critical-toxin, epidemic.
- Items: Wand L10 (crit chance +53776), Dragon Scale Mail L7 (fire +245239), Mage Hood, Silk Leggings, Enchanted Boots, Ring of Elements. Second weapon, one ring and the amulet deliberately empty for the last test.

How it changed:

1. 0 to 23m: Strike + Regeneration + Battle Cry. Auras ate all the mana (too low to cast 80 to 99% of the time).
2. 23m: Plate Armor (onHit speedBoost 30) dropped and Rend went in. Kills went from 3.5/min to 17/min.
3. 31m: Bloodlust (kills cast War Cry). This was the click. Damage per 4 to 5 minutes went from 3,063 to 26,749 to 328,756.
4. 40m: Raise Shield in slot 5, plus world 2+ gear. From here every enemy died in one hit.
5. 48m: Immolation, then Poison Pool, then a pure-aura test with Arcane Missile as the only attack.
6. 54m on: took off all damage gear (attack 6) to force damage through statuses. Rend + Deep Wounds + guaranteed crits became the whole build.

## How it felt

- **0 to 23m, slow and a bit hopeless.** Stuck in world 1 wave 1 to 2 at 3 kills/min, dying to Giant Bats. Mana regen 0 at the start means the free Regeneration aura burns the pool and then nothing casts. The only sensible talent was mana-efficiency, three times. This part matched "grindy and slow", but I had no interesting choice to make.
- **23 to 35m, the good part.** Plate Armor's speed trigger, then Rend, then Deep Wounds, then Bloodlust. Each piece visibly changed the numbers and I was choosing between real options.
- **35 to 48m, runaway.** World 1 wave 6 to world 6 in 13 minutes. Levels 9 to 22. Bosses died every 10 seconds. Health average 100%, lowest 100%. Nothing I chose mattered any more.
- **48m to 1h07m, I had to invent my own challenge.** I stripped my damage gear to see whether statuses could carry. They can, through one talent.

## Fun

- Plate Armor's onHit speedBoost. A common L1 body armour doubled my hit rate (51 hits in 2m36s against 99 hits in 10m). First drop that changed my plan.
- Bloodlust. War Cry hit 81 stacks in the first stretch, then 260, then 936. Watching the stack count was the best moment of the run.
- The attack-6 bleed build. With every hit critting, Deep Wounds doubles the bleed each hit. In world 8 it dealt 50.6 million bleed against 9,496 direct damage. That is a real "damage from statuses" build and it felt clever.
- Dying in world 8 with that build. It was the first time since minute 35 that an enemy got to attack me, because statuses need a tick to kill.

## Not fun

- The first 20 minutes with an aura build: mana starvation with one answer.
- Everything after world 2. Enemies die in one hit, so statuses never tick, auras do not matter and comparisons are meaningless.
- Gearing became "equip the newest drop". 1509 drops, one item per kill once itemDropChance rolled in the tens of thousands. I ended with 1106 items in the inventory.
- Flat talents. By world 3 my health was in the thousands and iron-constitution offers +6. I had 15 points unspent at the end and nothing I wanted except multiplicative triggers.
- One death in world 8 sent me to world 1 wave 1, not to wave 1 of world 8. Seven minutes later I was only back to world 5.

## Combinations

- **Battle Cry + Strike**: kills 2.9/min to 6.2/min. Just bigger numbers.
- **Plate Armor trigger + Rend**: 3.5 to 17.1 kills/min. Changed how the build played (many fast hits).
- **Rend + Battle Cry + Deep Wounds**: early on, bleed went 425 to 861 over similar stretches. Modest. With a +53776 crit chance wand it became the whole build: 50.6M bleed in 3 minutes at attack 6.
- **Bloodlust + shield (War Cry never lost)**: direct damage 3,063 to 26,749 in one stretch, 328,756 the next. Far too strong, see below.
- **Immolation + Bloodlust**: anti-combination. War Cry uptime went from 100% to 0 to 2% the moment Immolation went on, even with 42,507 shield. The self-burn strips fragile stacks through the shield.
- **Pure auras (Immolation + Poison Pool) + Arcane Missile**: kills dropped from about 92/min to 19/min, and all 57 kills were Arcane Missile. The auras ticked 1,344 fire and 784 poison in 3 minutes against enemies with 11 million health.
- **Poison Pool + Critical Toxin + Epidemic**: expected an exponential poison chain. Got 16,950 poison against 830,760 bleed over 7 minutes. Not a fair test: enemies in worlds 1 to 5 died to bleed first.
- **Fire gear + physical attacks or Immolation**: does nothing. Fire +245239 on my armour produced 79 fire stacks in 3 minutes. Item status bonuses only add to spells that already carry that status.
- Wanted but missing: a way for item status bonuses or aura stacks to feed aura ticks.

## Items

- Mattered: Plate Armor L1 (trigger), Arcane Staff L3 (mana), Amulet of Shield (kept War Cry safe), Wand L10 (crit chance made Deep Wounds exponential).
- Item power grows far faster than enemies. Examples: Amulet of Life Leech L9 with damage +268925%; Heavy Boots L10 with speed +53099; legendary staff with speed +630%. Meanwhile a world 8 wave 5 enemy had about 11M health against my 328M attack.
- Rarity meant little. A legendary Leather Leggings L2 was worse than common leggings one world later.
- Three uniques dropped (Soulrender, Robe of Elements, Shadow Mantle). None had a trigger; they were stat sticks. Only Plate Armor carried a trigger in 1509 drops.

## Too strong

- **Bloodlust**: one tier-2 point. 936 War Cry stacks at +10% each, 100% uptime, because one-shot enemies never hit back.
- **Raise Shield stacking**: 1,746 stacks gave shield 14,067 at level 18. Stacks never fall off while it is recast.
- **Item percent rolls and world scaling**: the real cause of the runaway.
- **Deep Wounds with over 100% crit**: doubles bleed every hit, at several hits per second.

## Too weak or useless

- Immolation (7 points) and Poison Pool (9 points) as damage: flat 3 and 2 per tick, never scale.
- Battle Cry's "critMultiplier +1.5": crit multiplier went from x1.50 to x1.51.
- Every flat tier-1 talent after about minute 40.
- Ring of Elements and Dragon Scale Mail fire for a build with no fire spell.
- Not tested, so not rated: lightning, ice, shatter, spiked-shield, battle-trance, all tier 4, most magic spells. Arcane Missile was the only magic spell I cast.

## Bugs or oddities

- Highest wave reads "wave 11" in every world, though worlds have 10 waves.
- Deaths happen with "health lowest 85%" or "lowest 100%" in the same stretch, so the health sample misses the killing blow.
- Non-stacking auras show thousands of stacks (Battle Cry 8,268, Regeneration 8,034) with no effect. Early on this re-casting is what starved my mana.
- "Curse" showed on me 15% of one stretch and "Arcane Boost" on enemies, though I owned neither. Curse's description says "curse yourself, doubling damage" but its numbers are an enemy debuff.
- After dying, world 3 wave 1 showed Goblin L1 with 15 health, the same as world 1.
- The harness line "status bonus on every attack" is misleading; it only applies to spells that already have that status.
- Unequipped items (amulet, warhammer, ring) did not appear at the top of the inventory list; I did not confirm where they went.

## Against DESIGN.md

- "Grindy and slow": true for 23 minutes, then world 1 to world 8 in 16 minutes. Not a power spike, a loss of the game.
- "Tradeoffs must be real": Immolation against War Cry is a real one, and I liked it. Most others vanished because nothing could hurt me.
- "Weak alone, strong combined": Plate Armor + Rend, and Deep Wounds + crit, are exactly this.
- "No single obvious path": Bloodlust plus any shield is the obvious path. Aura damage is not a path at all.
- "Poison: stacks multiply and spread" and "Fire: burns groups": the aura versions do not deliver this at any scale I saw.

## Suggestions

1. Rein in item scaling per world (or scale enemies to match). Fixing this alone would make every other test meaningful.
2. Cap or decay War Cry stacks from Bloodlust, or let it stack only on kills that took more than one hit.
3. Make aura ticks scale: add the caster's item status bonus, or scale with aura stacks. Then Immolation + fire gear + Wildfire is a build.
4. Stop non-stacking auras recasting while active. It would fix early mana starvation and make stacking auras a visible choice.
5. Make tier-1 talents percentages, or they are dead after 40 minutes.
6. Give uniques triggers. Plate Armor proved one trigger on an item is the most exciting drop in the game.
7. On death, return to wave 1 of the current world, or make the cost clear.
8. New piece: "Kindling" talent, War Cry stacks are not lost to your own burns. Combines Immolation with Bloodlust and Raise Shield.
